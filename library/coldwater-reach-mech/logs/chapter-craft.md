
## 01-the-breakwater
**Ending mode:** elliptical
**Opening texture:** routine-procedure
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Procedural anchor sustained through the entire chapter — body discovery and blank log entry embedded in harbormaster routine, never extracted into separate emotional register
- Short declarative clustering for the body examination: 'A man.' as a one-sentence paragraph; single-fact statements in sequence without connective tissue
- Displacement object handled in three distinct physical moments: reading the October 2 blank, writing the weather notation into it, reading it back alone at dusk
- Post-storm sensory grounding in the opening sequence: harbor sound (what is NOT there — no wind in stays, no swell against seaward face) as the primary phenomenological register
- Owen's coat described as physical object with specific physical detail (size, button mechanics) rather than as symbol — grief carried in a maintenance habit
- The log entry reproduced in italics as a document, then immediately contrasted with the non-entry at 2300 — the juxtaposition does the work without commentary
**Recurring constructions:**
- `each one a little less than the last`
- `she did not touch it`
- `no papers`
**POV register:**
- **Eira Bowman:** Fully spare procedural register established: short declaratives, 10-15 word average, sea and harbor as workplace vocabulary throughout. Interior is minimal and factual — 'She marked the fact and moved on' is the closest thing to interiority. Grief arrives only in the coat's physical description and the detail of learning to button it. The word 'grief' never appears.
**Craft notes:** The chapter ends before any chapter heading hints what the blank means; the reader must feel it through the juxtaposition of the complete October 3 entry and the thin weather notation at 2300. Do not explain the blank in any subsequent Eira chapter; let it accumulate. Owen's coat is now a recurring object anchor — next Eira chapter should reference it in some physical way without staging it. The ending's 'each one a little less than the last' recurs once within the chapter (breakwater swell) and again in the closing line (harbor against breakwater) — one repetition was structural; two risks becoming a tic; avoid a third.

**Recurring constructions:**
- `not from X but from Y`
- `the character of`
- `had been, by Marcus's`
**POV register:**
- **Marcus Klein:** Fully established: precise, subordinate-clause syntax, 14-20 word average sentences with periodic structures for suppressed intensity. Wary of his own expertise: he answers Graves with more than is strictly necessary but no more than is professionally correct. The control of the register is the point; what slips through is visible precisely because of how hard the control is held.
**Craft notes:** The chapter's object rhyme with ch01 is built into the scene-log but should NOT appear in the prose. Let the reader assemble it. Next Marcus chapter (05-the-name) should pick up the bottom drawer as the primary object anchor; the drawer should not be 'checked on' casually. - `certain of the practical details`

Next Marcus chapter (11-the-crow-table) should not re-examine the drawer — when it opens again it should be consequential. The chapter breaks the recent observation-opening pattern with an arrival texture; the second elliptical ending in three chapters is appropriate but should be monitored for ch09 (Hannah chapter, likely road or dialogue texture).

- `did not ask`
- `from before, from another time`
**POV register:**
- **Marcus Klein:** Taxonomic interiority at full extension: the Carroll student error analyzed in category terms (sequence of tenses, accusative avoidance, the gap between available clause and correct clause) as the thought occupying Marcus in the moment before the disclosure. Opening texture is arrival, breaking the consecutive routine-procedure pattern of ch09 and ch10; ending mode is resolved-beat, breaking the elliptical/turn/literary-fade sequence of ch08–10. Next Marcus chapter (ch14) should make the outward movement — the letter to Josef's sister — and should probably open with transit or a handling of the object.
