# Continuity — Coldwater Reach

Hard facts that must not break across the draft. Numbered for reference.

1. The story runs from autumn 1943 to spring 1945, ending just before VE Day (May 8, 1945). Resolutions precede the war's end; they are not caused by it.
2. The body of an unidentified man washes up against the seaward face of the Coldwater Reach breakwater after a three-day blow in October 1943. He carries no papers. He cannot be identified from the body alone.
3. Eira Bowman is 52 years old at the story's start (October 1943). She has been harbormaster for eight years.
4. Owen Bowman, Eira's husband and the previous harbormaster, drowned in the November storm of 1935.
5. Eira saw something on the dock the night before the body came in. This is the load-bearing secret of her thread. What she saw must not be fully disclosed until the braid's assembly demands it.
6. Hannah Pfeiffer is 17 at the story's start. Her parents, Werner and Marta Pfeiffer, immigrated from Bavaria to Maine in 1922.
7. Four German POWs work the Pfeiffer farm under one Coastal Patrol guard (Carvell Oakes, 61, arthritic). The arrangement is authorized under the federal POW labor program.
8. Karl Reinmar is 19 at the story's start. He is from a town approximately fifty kilometers from the Pfeiffers' home village in Bavaria. This proximity is meaningful to both Hannah and Werner.
9. Dr. Marcus Klein is 47 at the story's start. Born Vienna; interned in Quebec under the War Measures Act for fourteen months before arriving in Coldwater Reach in September 1942.
10. Marcus boards with Henry Crow (school custodian, Black) and Lillian Crow on Prospect Street. This is the only household in town where Marcus is treated as a guest rather than a specimen.
11. The three POV characters — Eira, Hannah, Marcus — **never meet as a group**. They may intersect in pairs; they do not form a trio.
12. At least one thread ends in deliberate, unresolved withholding. Not every question the novel raises is answered.
13. **DISPLACEMENT OBJECT RULE (operative)**: Every chapter built on withholding must give the withheld thing a concrete physical object that the POV character handles, keeps, hides, fails to write, returns to, or destroys. A withholding chapter without a physical object is categorically forbidden. "A thing she could not name" without an object to hold it is a failure.
14. **ACCRETION RULE (operative)**: Every chapter must either (a) change a named fact about the breakwater body, or (b) recontextualize a fact the reader already holds, or (c) advance a displacement object's meaning. A chapter that re-establishes atmosphere the reader already has — however well-written — is an "orbiting" failure.
15. The harbor log is a legal document. What Eira enters and what she does not enter are consequential acts with real-world consequences (reporting to the District Commander at Rockland).
16. Real Maine place names may appear. Real historical persons may not appear by name.
17. Period-accurate historical facts must be respected: the Maine POW labor program, Geneva Convention POW regulations, V-Mail procedure, Coastal Patrol duties, harbormaster reporting chains.
18. Race and class are facts of the town — present in who boards where, who is watched, who is trusted — and are rendered as texture, not subject lectures.
