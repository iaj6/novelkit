# Editor — Pacing Pass
## Macro arc assessment

### Overall shape

The arc divides cleanly into four phases: Setup (chs 1–8), Complication (chs 9–16), Convergence (chs 17–22), Resolution (chs 23–24). The proportions are roughly 33/33/25/8 percent of the book. The outline knows what it is doing with that shape — the brief explicitly permits dead air in a haunting — but the weighting concentrates risk in the first and middle thirds and earns its release in the last quarter.

### Midbook trough — the most serious structural concern

Chapters 12, 13, and 14 form the book's deepest dead-air pocket. Ch 12 reinforces Della's wrong belief with effort. Ch 13 (a flagged stall, ⊘) has Ellen firming the false account on paper — no epistemic movement. Ch 14 watches the clock approach 9:38 — no movement toward P. All three chapters maintain ¬P without any membrane crossing that advances Thread 1 or Thread 3.

The brief explicitly asks for stalling chapters and the outline correctly flags ch 13 as one of three designated stalls. The problem is concentration: three consecutive no-advance chapters, sandwiching the only Ellen stall between two consecutive Della-holding-the-mercy chapters, creates the longest dead-air run in the book. Each of these chapters individually is defensible. Together they risk losing forward momentum precisely at the midpoint where readers are most likely to flag. The per-chapter pass should pay particular attention to whether chs 12 and 14 have enough internal forward energy — accreting genuinely new physical detail, not restating previously established texture — to carry the trough without sagging.

### Setup length (chs 1–8)

The investigative engine (the inquest file) does not engage until ch 9. Chs 3, 4, and 6 are largely atmospheric: Ellen notes the house, Della circuits the house, Della tries the stove. These are correct according to the brief's dead-air mandate, and they are doing necessary register work. The concern is whether chs 4 and 6 are sufficiently differentiated in the draft: both are Della circling-and-maintaining in literary-fade mode, both accrete physical facts of the same night, neither moves belief. The specific physical content distinguishes them on paper (latch vs. flue), but the per-chapter pass should verify that the accretion in each feels genuinely new in texture rather than returning the same atmospheric room in different words. If these two chapters share too close a sensory register, the setup will feel like six chapters, not eight.

### Convergence (chs 17–22) — no structural concern

This section is the book's strongest movement by design. Six chapters, each a clear increment: the last stall (ch 17) → Della's knocking (ch 18) → Ellen enters the back room (ch 19) → Della holds the ledger at January 31 (ch 20) → Ellen reads Record B (ch 21) → Della's correction (ch 22). The escalation is clean and the belief-movements are distinct in each chapter. No sag risk here.

### Climax and coda balance

The twin revelations land at chs 21 (Ellen knows P) and 22 (Della knows P). The coda is chs 23 and 24, totaling ~5,600 target words. This is appropriate: ch 23 is a genuine scene (the formal assembly of both records, the proceeding closed) rather than recap, and ch 24 carries the book's deliberately unanswered question about Della's fate. The three-Ellen-chapter run (21, skip 22 Della, 23, 24) is structural, not a fault — the asymmetric close is the brief's explicit intention. The only watch item is ch 23: at ~3,200 words, it risks feeling like a long ceremony over a conclusion the reader reached in ch 21. The per-chapter pass should verify ch 23 is doing genuine new work (Cale confirmed, both documents formally assembled, Ellen's trade-as-instrument made visible) rather than narrating the reader's existing knowledge back to them.

### Thread weight and the trade theme

Thread 4 (the trade and the exact word) has its most explicit articulation in ch 17 (the Elkins deposition — the attorney's comment on digital audio). Its resolution at chs 21 and 23 is structural and unpronounced, as the brief requires. The risk is that ch 17, as a stall chapter whose primary cargo is the trade theme, must land the trade beat without over-stating it. An over-articulated ch 17 would pre-empt the quiet structural irony of ch 21. The per-chapter pass should watch that ch 17's trade beat stays oblique.

### Ending-mode distribution

The chapter map's distribution (6 literary-fades, 5 resolved-beats, 4 turns, 3 mid-action-cuts, 3 ellipticals, 2 declarative-closes, 1 coda) is well-varied. No two consecutive chapters share an ending mode per the outline's own verification. Literary-fade dominates Della's chapters (5 of 11), which is apt. No structural concern here — the variety is genuine.

### Summary of watch items for the per-chapter pass

1. **Chs 12–14 (the midbook trough):** Verify each has sufficient internal forward texture to sustain the dead air without sagging. Particular attention to ch 14's clock-watching: is the temporal imminence rendered as pressure, or as marking time?
2. **Chs 4 and 6 (early Della chapters):** Verify the physical accretion in each is genuinely distinct — not the same atmospheric room re-entered in parallel words.
3. **Ch 17 (trade-theme stall):** Verify the attorney's comment lands obliquely, not as a declared irony.
4. **Ch 23 (resolution ceremony):** Verify it does new work rather than summarizing the reader's knowledge back at them.

## 04-the-latch

**Overall verdict: clean. One small soft spot worth flagging for the line/voice pass.**

**What the chapter does well.** The room-by-room circuit is purposeful rather than mechanical: each sub-beat accretes a genuinely new physical fact (the latch mechanism and the father's three-test rule; the travel clock at 9:10; the temperature at 44°F; the pantry inventory with its precise "I will not starve — this is not the danger"; the window gap in Della's room unfixed as the father left the step unfixed). The opening and closing are structurally mirrored (latch tested → "The front door is latched") in a way that reinforces Della's circling psychology without feeling mannered. The back-room door beat — the chapter's emotional climax — earns its placement after the full house has been declared sound.

**The one soft spot: three-sentence over-articulation at the back-room door.** After Della takes her hand off the handle, the narration offers three consecutive sentences that record the same psychological fact:

> "I am not afraid of this room. I am simply not at the part of the night where this room is what I am doing. The back room is not tonight."

Each sentence approaches the same truth from a slightly different angle (emotional denial; scheduling frame; crystallized declaration), but the effect is a slight hesitation — the narration audibly working to convince itself of what it just said. The middle sentence is the most precisely in Della's voice: it describes her cognitive system (the night divided into parts, each part with its task) rather than her emotional state. The first and third sentences are redundant around it. Cutting the first ("I am not afraid of this room.") would tighten the passage and keep Della in the register she holds elsewhere in the chapter — ordering and inventorying, not reporting emotional states. This is borderline pacing/voice; flagged here for the line pass.

**Macro arc watch item: register differentiation with ch 6.** The macro assessment asks whether chs 4 and 6 are sufficiently distinct in sensory register. Ch 4 passes its side of the test: its physical content is the full house circuit (latch, clock, inventory, stairs, back-room door) after the stove has already failed; the governing sensation is temperature in rooms, not the stove itself; the time-stamp is 9:10. Ch 6 is set before ch 4 in story-time (the stove failure itself) — but both land as literary fades in a cold house with Della at a table. The per-chapter pass for ch 6 should verify that ch 6's dominant sensory register (the chimney, the torch-paper method, the two remaining matches) is foregrounded enough to distinguish it from ch 4's register (the room-by-room inventory, the latches, the counting). The differentiation exists in subject matter; the question is whether it registers as distinct texture in prose.
