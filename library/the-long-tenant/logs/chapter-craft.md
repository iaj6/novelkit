
## 01-verrill-street
**Ending mode:** literary-fade
**Opening texture:** arrival
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Anomaly-as-inaudible-passage: the uncanny noted the way a court reporter notes an unintelligible word — 'marked, bracketed, the proceeding continuing' — then the chapter moves on without adjudicating
- Professional shorthand as interiority: Ellen's legal-pad notes (*desk / maker's mark / stays*, *third stair from bottom / tread*, *back-room door / latch / monitor*) carry emotional and epistemological weight without interiority being named
- Compression bolt of attention: each room given only its load-bearing facts; the back room given more time not by elaboration but by the absence of an account for what it is
- Counter-rhythm in the diner: after the house's dense physicality, the diner section deliberately loosens to exterior social observation — the three people at the far end, the television, the plant building ghost-letters — then snaps back to the house on return
- Fade via learned eccentricity: the chapter ends not on the anomaly but on Ellen mastering a domestic detail (the stair's step-left rule) — the house teaching her its ordinary self on the way to bed
**Recurring constructions:**
- `she noted this without stopping`
- `she noted it`
- `she did not go back up to check it`
**POV register:**
- **ellen-vance:** Third-person limited, past tense throughout. Vocabulary from court reporting (noted, marked, bracketed, examined, filed, the proceeding) bleeds into domestic observation without announcing itself. Sentences average 18-22 words; none exceed 35. Interior life surfaces only as professional reflex — Ellen attends to facts and files them; she does not name feelings or interpret what the house might mean. The professional loss enters only in the thin calendar note: 'nothing confirmed until the end of the month' — present but unnamed.
**Craft notes:** This chapter establishes the Ellen register at full speed: the opening observation from across the street is a court-reporter's site survey, not an aesthetic appraisal. The back-room anomaly is handled exactly twice — first walk-through and return from diner — which gives it weight without escalation; a third instance would tip into gothic clichÃ©. The diner excursion is load-bearing as a Renick-texture beat (plant ghost-letters) and as a decompression before the second anomaly, not mere scene-setting. The 'step left' close is the register's fade: Ellen wins a small procedural insight from the house; the chapter ends on mastery, not unease. Next chapter (Della) must not open with any similar 'learning the house' beat — the two POVs must distinguish immediately.

## 02-the-stove
**Ending mode:** declarative-close
**Opening texture:** set-piece
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Father's-teaching as technical manual: the chimney explanation is rendered entirely through remembered instruction — flat, step-by-step, with the father as a teaching presence rather than an emotional one; this carries the exposition without lyricism and keeps it in Della's register
- Millwright's end-of-shift inventory: the move through cord wood / kindling / matches / oil tin is structured as a checklist, not a scene — each item named and assessed in ledger-column shorthand; the method itself characterizes Della without a word of interiority
- Ledger-entry as chapter spine: the physical act of writing the account-book entry organizes the second half of the chapter; the column's declarative syntax (Power out since six / Temperature fifty-one degrees) bleeds into the narrative voice, making the narrative register and Della's document register indistinguishable
- Withheld ledger entry: the kept sentence appears at 8:42 PM — before the knocking at 9:40 — so it floats free of its actual occasion; the reader receives it in a stove-failure context and cannot yet hear its weight; the chapter does not signal that anything is wrong with this
- Declarative close on the clock face: the chapter ends on a time-stamp (8:44 on the travel clock) rather than on atmosphere or interiority; the clock is the chapter's recurring object and the closing on it has no comment attached — pure flat-declarative, Della's register at its most characteristic
**Recurring constructions:**
- `he did not need to`
- `I am not burning it`
- `the column is not for`
**POV register:**
- **della-markey:** First-person present tense throughout, no slip. Sentences average 9-12 words; only the chimney-explanation section pushes slightly longer as she works through the physics. Vocabulary from the millwright household: damper, flue, firebox, draft, cord wood, kindling, stove, the column, figure if applicable. Her father is rendered entirely through his functional speech (He said: feel that? He said: once you lose it in real cold) with no emotional gloss. The kept sentence appears without announcement and without aftermath — written into the ledger column and treated as equivalent to the oil-level entry. Zero lyricism: even the frost on the inside of the window glass is a flat observation, not an image.
**Craft notes:** Della's father is the chapter's hidden structural engine — every beat of technical knowledge she demonstrates (chimney physics, wick-trimming, inventory discipline) comes back to him without being named as grief or loss. The chapter avoids the temptation to make the father's absence an emotional subject; he is present only as transferred competence. The pine-scraps memory (she was wrong about the wood supply, he did not need to say so) is the chapter's one beat of acknowledged fallibility, rendered flat. Next Della chapter should open from a different angle of the night — not the stove, which has been fully inhabited here; the latch, the draft, or the clock face would be appropriate re-entry points per the outline's circling structure.

The three-test latch ritual opens the chapter deliberately to shadow Ellen's three-test door check in Chapter 3 — the same act, opposite relationship to what's behind the door. The closing confirmation formula ('the front door is latched') mirrors the opening beat without restating it; the chapter closes on the object it opened on, which is the only lyricism the register permits. Next Ellen chapter (Ch05) opens the deed search; it should not re-use the observation/confirmation move as its dominant texture — the professional-procedural archival mode will be the right contrast to this chapter's domestic inventory.
