# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-verrill-street** — Ellen arrives at the Verrill Street house, takes possession, notes the desk's maker's mark, and finds the back-room door ajar — twice — without committing to an interpretation.
- **02-the-stove** — Della takes inventory of a failed stove and a cold house on the night of February 11, 1983, records the facts in her ledger, and writes "Nobody came to that door."
- **03-the-third-step** — Ellen takes the house's measure room by room, reads the maker's mark on the desk and the ghost-letter brick of the plant building, buys a twelve-dollar chair from the resale shop, and decides — procedurally — to find the deed history of the house.
- **04-the-latch** — Della circuits the house room by room — testing latches, counting the pantry, standing at the back-room door without opening it — and reads the kept sentence back from the ledger at the kitchen table.
- **05-chain-of-title** — Ellen pulls the deed and probate records at the county courthouse, learns Della Markey died in the house on February 12, 1983 (hypothermia, accidental), finds a brother who never responded, and discovers the inquest file is still on paper.
- **06-the-flue** — Della makes her last sustained attempt to relight the stove; the torch-paper method fails; she is left with two matches, the cord wood held back, and the cold already in the walls.
- **07-manner-of-death** — Ellen reads the coroner's inquest file — death certificate, investigation report, pathologist's opinion, inquest verdict — and photographs the witness statements unread before being cut off.
- **08-the-oil-lamp** — Della tends the oil lamp, circuits the house in the dark, and perceives Ellen's presence as warmth at the wrong angle outside the back-room door — filed under her category for the living, and left unresolved.
- **09-before-the-scanner** — Ellen returns to the inquest file, reads the pathologist's time-of-death range and the mild-hypothermia note, and discovers—in Deputy Harman's scene description—that a household ledger was open on the kitchen table when Della was found, inventoried, and left in the house.
- **10-footfall** — Della hears Ellen's footfall on the floor above, finds a wrong light under the back-room door, almost-thinks about the ledger on the shelf, turns away, and descends to the front room as the tenant's light goes out.
- **11-no-visitors** — Ellen reads Mabel Duff's full inquest statement, assembles the complete documentary account of Della's death, and wrong-believes nobody came — the false crossing lands.
- **12-what-she-kept** — Della, alone at the kitchen table with the ledger, writes the kept sentence twice — the second time with deliberate effort — while the tenant's presence in the back room presses into the night without breaching it.
- **13-the-account-as-it-stood** — Ellen writes the false account as a professional rough draft, identifies the back room as the likely location of the ledger, goes to the door, and does not open it.
- **14-near-quarter-to-ten** — Della watches the travel clock move toward 9:38 — two minutes before the knocking — and holds the kept sentence like a pin, refusing to think past the second hand.
- **15-the-probate-record** — Ellen reads the probate file, learns Cale Markey was never found, and discovers the gap in her false account: Mabel Duff's observation covered daylight only, not the dark of February 11.
- **16-his-name** — Della tests the front-door latch, turns in the ledger to October and holds her thumb over his name without reading it, writes the kept sentence a third time, and watches the clock at 9:36 without looking through the transom window above the door.
- **17-a-deposition-in-elkins** — Ellen drives to Elkins for a deposition — an attorney mentions his firm uses digital audio — she returns to find the back-room door ajar again and closes it; the question in the notebook sits unanswered.
- **18-the-sound-of-the-knocking** — Della stands at the foot of the stairs at 9:42 PM while someone knocks at the front door — three at a time, deliberate — and holds the kept sentence against what her body already knows.
- **19-the-back-room** — Ellen enters the back room at last, finds the household ledger in the closet, reads through Cale's appearances and disappearance, and stops just short of February 1983.
- **20-the-account-book** — Della takes the ledger to the back room, reads through to January 31 1983, and holds the page there — she knows exactly what February 11 contains and will not turn to it.
- **21-the-final-entry** — Ellen reads Record B in full — the knocking, the recognition, the refusal — and the last five words invert the kept sentence; Ellen knows P; the false crossing is disproved by the bracket she draws around "daylight hours."
- **22-what-she-wrote** — Della reads her own final ledger entry in the back room, understands the mercy she wrote, and puts her hand on the latch — the alternation severs.
- **23-the-two-records** — Ellen sets both records side by side at the desk and produces the complete certified account of Della Markey's death — the proceeding formally closed.
- **24-the-door-open** — Ellen brings the travel clock downstairs to the desk, reads the ledger one last time, moves through the house, and stays — the back room door open, the proceeding closed, the book's final image held without explanation.
