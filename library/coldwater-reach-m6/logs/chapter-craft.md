
## 01-the-breakwater
**Ending mode:** turn
**Opening texture:** routine-procedure
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Short declaratives clustered throughout — three declaratives before any subordination, Eira's rhythm established in the first paragraph
- Inventory-as-revelation: the damage survey becomes the chapter's structural model for how Eira processes everything, including the body and the dock image
- Delayed interiority: the dock image surfaces a full section after the log entry is written, after her attention has returned to the physical world
- Physical action substituted for affect throughout — Owen's absence is his handwriting on a logbook spine, not a named feeling
- The pen held and set down without explanation as the chapter's climactic gesture — the turn is in the body, not the prose commentary
- One-beat-past-resolution ending: after the turn lands (she sets the pen down), the final sentence is tidal and physical, not declarative
**Recurring constructions:**
- `She noted it for`
- `She wrote it down`
- `No papers, no`
**POV register:**
- **eira-bowman:** Established: spare, procedural, 10-14 word sentences, working vocabulary of the waterfront. Interior passages limited to 2-3 sentences before attention returns to the physical. No affect naming. Grief is Owen's handwriting on a spine. Deductions rendered as professional assessments, not labeled as deductions.
**Craft notes:** No pattern pressure (first chapter). The chapter front-loads Eira's procedural register so heavily that the dock-image section — which is her only sustained interior passage — arrives with the weight of the contrast rather than announcing itself as significant. The structure of the damage survey (note, write, move on) is the structural model for how she processes the body and eventually the dock image. Owen is established through his logbook placement alone; his name is used once, without context. The CG scene is kept brisk to match Mercer's 'thorough not curious' character note — the twelve-minute visit feels right for the register and sets up the gap between official handling and Eira's private knowledge.

- The English crack as structural pivot: Albrecht's one sentence in English ('The count is right. You miscounted.') is the chapter's turn; it is given without gloss; the chapter then sits inside Hannah's surprise rather than explaining it
- Evening negative space: the six-feet passage (the coat on the hook, the paper in the pocket, the distance Hannah does not cross) renders the central not-act of the chapter through physical geometry rather than declared restraint
**Recurring constructions:**
- `the way you know a person`
- `the stillness of a man who`

- **hannah-pfeiffer:** Consistent with established Hannah register: 12-20 word sentences with wider variance; sensory grounding (hot dishwater, dark farmyard, lamp guttering); German phrases in italics (Ich hoffe, Ich weiß nicht) embedded without translation; interior passages associative and specific, always returning to physical world; signature move of accurate observation held at one remove — she sees the stillness on her father's face and names its category (confirmation-not-discovery) without naming what it confirms; no contemporary psychological vocabulary; the cost-of-restraint register is felt rather than declared.

The act of not crossing to the coat on the hook is the chapter's most deliberate not-act; it comes without editorial reach. Future chapters should honor that Hannah has not seen the letter's contents — only that a German letter exists, was delivered by Klein, and changed something in her father for one sentence before settling back into the usual.
