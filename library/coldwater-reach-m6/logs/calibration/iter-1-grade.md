## Compared sample against brief anchors

**Brief register anchor**: Literary adult fiction, restrained, period-exact. Comparables: *The Remains of the Day* (repression as texture, the load-bearing quality of what is not said); *Nora Webster* (a widow holding a post, grief arriving late in physical idiom); *Gilead* (moral weight in quiet sentences).

**Eira-specific targets from guidance**: Spare, procedural, observant. Average sentence 10–16 words. Opens in task. Sparsest in dialogue. No affect named. Working weather only. Literary-fade ending preferred.

**Sample measured against each anchor**:

- **Ishiguro repression / what is not said**: Present. "She was not thinking about this" is a single sentence that names the habit of suppression without explaining it — exactly the Stevensian technique. "She stood." as the beat after discovering the body is the briefed mode at its best. The harbor log for 1935 beside the radio (the year Owen drowned) arrives without comment: the weight is entirely in the object, not named. ✓
- **Tóibín / grief in the language of work**: Consistently honored. She inventories the breakwater damage; she notes the body's coat as "town-cut, not oilskin"; she writes down the position the same way she wrote down the bent mooring rings. Grief and professionalism are indistinguishable. ✓
- **Sentence-length distribution**: The large majority of sentences fall in the 4–16 word range. Roughly 10–12 sentences run 20–30 words — these carry operational data (the storm stats, the breakwater description, the body's details). Mean is likely 12–15 words: within the 10–16 target. ✓ (One outlier at 33 words addressed below.)
- **Opens in task**: "Eira did the breakwater first." — Exactly the guidance's prescription. The chapter begins in her body and her work, not in weather, not in abstraction. ✓
- **Working weather**: The storm statistics arrive in sentence two as operational context — recorded facts that explain what she is going to find — not mood. "Fifty-three miles per hour at the outer head, which was honest for October but not a record" is weather doing logbook work. ✓
- **Dialogue density**: Zero dialogue in the sample. This honors the guidance ("Eira's chapters are the sparsest in dialogue") and is correct for the scene she is walking. ✓
- **Ending mode (literary fade)**: "She put on water for coffee and went back to the breakwater to wait." — One physical beat past the chapter's action resolution (the phone call to the station). No summary, no naming. The exact mode described. ✓

## What the sample does well

- **Procedural objectivity as emotional register**: The examination of the body (pockets turned out, handkerchief with no initial, no papers) uses Eira's working vocabulary to do everything the scene needs emotionally. Nothing is named. Everything lands.
- **The Owen callback**: Two instances — the compare to his November sixty-one record, the 1935 harbor log on the shelf — connect the body on the breakwater to Eira's own loss without once naming grief. This is the technique the guidance asks for and the comparables demonstrate.
- **Sentence rhythm in Eira's register**: Short declaratives cluster correctly. "Wrote it down." / "She stopped." / "Nothing." / "She stood." — the guidance's "three short declaratives is the rhythm; vary at the fourth" is present throughout.
- **Period texture earned, not decorative**: "Dragger," "oilskin," "oxford-type," "the appropriation," "Sprucehead" — every period marker is there because it belongs to Eira's working day, not to signal authenticity.
