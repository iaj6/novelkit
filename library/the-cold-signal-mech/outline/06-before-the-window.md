# Chapter 06 — Before the Window

**One-line summary:** Elena builds the data packet at the fax terminal and does not send it; the relay window opens in under twelve hours; she has not decided.

**POV:** Dr. Elena Hartmann  
**Tense:** Third-person limited past  
**Setting:** Winslow Station — equipment building (fax terminal), then receiver hut. Day 6.  
**Target words:** ~1,900  
**Chapter shape:** Interiority-heavy — first of the two permitted interiority chapters; anchored to the physical task of building the data packet at the terminal  
**Ending mode:** Elliptical

---

## Escalation (from Chapter Map)

- **(a) Day 6**
- **(b)** Tape integrity: passive cold degradation has extended the five-second dropout to eight seconds (visual measurement of reel timing marks; no protocol run burned today). Hours to gate: ~48 h → under 24 h. Power reserve: 51 % → 47 %.
- **(c)** Step closer: Elena has completed the data packet at the fax terminal. It exists, ready to transmit. The relay window opens in under twelve hours. The only remaining question is whether she initiates the uplink. Everything she needed to do in order to send is done; all that remains is the act.

---

## Goal

This is the book's first dedicated interiority chapter, and it arrives at the right moment: the eve of the gate, when action is suspended and thought is the only available work. The chapter must be anchored to a physical task (building the data packet) and to a physical object (the terminal's cursor, the tape housing in the hut) so that the interiority does not become abstract. Elena is a scientist who thinks through her hands; even when she is thinking, she is holding something.

The chapter is short because the compression is the point. Elena does not have a lot of words for this. Neither does the chapter.

---

## Obstacle

The obstacle is entirely interior: Elena has all the information she needs to decide, and she cannot decide. Not because she lacks information but because neither option closes cleanly. She is spending this chapter doing the one thing her career has not prepared her for: sitting with an unresolved question that will resolve itself whether she acts or not.

---

## Action Beats

1. **Elena does not run the protocol today.** She knows this. She decided it last night, implicitly. She goes to the receiver hut only to check the tape housing temperature (within range) and to look at the tape without touching it. She notes, by examining the reel against the timing marks on the housing, that the edge binder stress has extended. She estimates the affected zone by eye: what was five seconds two days ago is now approximately eight seconds. She does not run the protocol to measure it precisely. She cannot afford the power. She notes "~8s dropout, visual estimate" in the log.

2. **She goes to the fax terminal.** She has done this before — used the terminal for routine maintenance data transmissions when the relay window was active. The terminal's interface is familiar: a text input field, a packet-formatting utility, a status indicator for the relay carrier signal.

3. **She builds the data packet.** This is a concrete, physical task. She formats the signal's compressed metadata according to the USAP data transmission protocol: frequency band, timestamp, duration, amplitude profile header, the first parametric segment summary (the only complete one; the second segment is still intact at this point but the first is now partially dropout-affected, which she documents). She adds her chain-of-custody notes, her verification protocol summary from Runs 1-3, and a cover statement that she writes and rewrites twice before settling on a version.

4. **The cover statement** (the reader sees Elena writing it, not the text itself). She is aware that this cover statement, if transmitted, will be the first document anyone outside Winslow sees. It will frame what they look at before they look at the data. She has spent her career arguing that the frame matters as much as the content. She writes it. She reads it back to herself. She changes two phrases.

5. **The data packet is complete.** The cursor is blinking. The packet is formatted, addressed to the USAP relay buffer, waiting for the initiation command that will transmit it when the window opens. She has not initiated it. She is looking at the cursor.

6. **She leaves the terminal.** She puts on her outer layer and goes to the receiver hut. She checks the tape housing one more time. She reads through her analysis notebook from Run 1 to Run 3. She sits in the work chair in the cold hut for a while.

7. **The interiority.** (Not labeled as such in the prose; rendered through free indirect interiority in Elena's scientific vocabulary.) She thinks about what it means to send this: the agencies will receive a compressed data packet from a scientist they trust. They will ask for the instrument logs. They will want the tape. They will schedule a verification run using equipment she does not have here — on the assumption that the tape can be transported and the signal analyzed again. She will be the source, not the author. She knows what that means; she has watched it happen to others.

8. She thinks about what it means not to send: she will have three runs and a degrading tape and five days and no institutional channel. She will have to hand it to Walczak on Day 12 with no preparation, no framing, no ability to control what happens next.

9. She thinks about Lars. His reading, filed as noise, sixty kilometers east. She does not pick up the radio.

10. **End of chapter.** She goes back to the equipment building. The data packet is still on the terminal screen. She does not delete it. She does not initiate it. She goes to the hab and lies down on her bunk, which is not sleeping. The cursor is blinking at the unattended terminal. The window opens in less than twelve hours.

---

## Outcome / Elliptical

The chapter ends on the cursor and the absence of a decision. It is deliberately unresolved — not as a technique for suspense but because this is where Elena actually is. She has done everything required to send the data except send it. She has not decided not to send it either. Both options are still open. The window will open regardless.

---

## New Question / Pressure Raised

What will she do when the window opens tomorrow morning?

---

## Craft Notes for the Drafter

- This chapter must be anchored to the **physical act of building the data packet** throughout; if Elena floats free of the task, the interiority becomes abstract and violates the style guidance.
- The cover statement she writes: the reader should sense its weight without seeing its content. The signal's content is never described; the cover statement covers a signal, not a message.
- **Do not resolve the decision in this chapter.** The elliptical ending is structural, not a failure of commitment. Elena is genuinely undecided; the prose must be undecided with her.
- **Do not use this chapter to editorialize on the ethics.** Both options must feel real and costly, not one obviously right and one obviously wrong.
