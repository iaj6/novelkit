## Escalation Ladder

Every chapter must worsen at least one **named quantity**. Mid-book chapters (Days 4–7) worsen two or three simultaneously. The ladder below lists (a) the day, (b) the quantity worsened and by how much, (c) what option is permanently foreclosed or one concrete step closer to foreclosure.

**Named quantities:**
- **Power reserve** — percentage of usable kilowatt-hours above life-support threshold
- **Tape integrity** — qualitative + measured state of the magnetic tape record
- **Instrument run margin** — whole-number count of full protocol runs remaining in power budget
- **Hours to gate** — hours until the Day 7 relay window closes (counts to 0; then replaced by days to foreclosure)
- **Days to foreclosure** — days until the LC-130 lands on Day 12 (counts to 0)

---

### Chapter 1 — Day 1 (night)
**(b)** Power reserve: 100 % → 78 % (brownout during Run 1 + warm-up cycle costs extra draw). Instrument run margin: 8 → 7.  
**(c)** Step closer: Elena has initiated the verification sequence; the signal's existence is no longer ignorable. The clock is running.

---

### Chapter 2 — Day 2
**(b)** Tape integrity: first binder stress observed at reel edge (temperature threshold breached overnight when heater cycled low). Instrument run margin: 7 → 6 (Run 2). Power reserve: 78 % → 71 %.  
**(c)** Step closer: heater and instruments cannot run simultaneously at full capacity from this point; one must be rationed every day forward.

---

### Chapter 3 — Day 3
**(b)** Hours to gate: ~144 h → ~96 h (four days remaining). Power reserve: 71 % → 67 % (overnight passive draw; no run today).  
**(c)** Step closer: Lars Engström's offhand mention of an anomalous reading at Mather Station (~47 seconds, filed as artifact) means the signal may not be entirely Elena's to control — independent detection may already exist sixty kilometers east.

---

### Chapter 4 — Day 4
**(b)** Tape integrity: a five-second dropout confirmed in the edge of the first parametric segment during Run 3 (first measured degradation). Instrument run margin: 6 → 5. Power reserve: 67 % → 60 %.  
**(c)** Step closer: the tape is actively degrading at a measurable rate. Elena calculates that at this rate the core record will be compromised in eight to twelve days. She stops scheduling runs until the gate question is resolved.

---

### Chapter 5 — Day 5
**(b)** Power reserve: 60 % → 51 % (diesel fuel burn 15 % above baseline; cold has thickened the diesel and the generator is compensating). Instrument run margin: revised downward from estimated 5 remaining → confirmed 3 remaining (the original 8-run estimate was based on faulty baseline; true budget is ~6 total; with 3 runs used, 3 remain). Hours to gate: ~96 h → ~48 h.  
**(c)** Step closer: the instrument margin is two runs lower than Elena believed yesterday. Two options she was holding as available are now foreclosed by the revised math.

---

### Chapter 6 — Day 6
**(b)** Tape integrity: passive cold degradation has extended the five-second dropout to eight seconds (visual measurement against reel timing marks; no run burned). Hours to gate: ~48 h → under 24 h. Power reserve: 51 % → 47 %.  
**(c)** Step closer: Elena completes the data packet at the fax terminal. It exists, ready to transmit. The only remaining question is whether she initiates the uplink. The window opens in under twelve hours.

---

### Chapter 7 — Day 7 (0800–0848)
**(b)** Hours to gate: 0 (window opens at 0800, closes at 0848 without transmission). Days to foreclosure: 5 (new operative quantity). Power reserve: 47 % → 41 % (terminal draw and warm-up during the window).  
**(c)** **PERMANENTLY FORECLOSED:** the institutional relay transmission channel. The window will not recur before Day 12. Elena now holds the data and must decide what to do with it in five days.

---

### Chapter 8 — Day 7 (afternoon/evening)
**(b)** Generator fault: voltage variance widens noticeably (generator drops to 90 % output during fault; manual stabilization required). Tape dropout: eight seconds confirmed by visual check while power is reduced. Power reserve: 41 % → 38 %. Days to foreclosure: 5 (same calendar day as Ch 7).  
**(c)** Step closer: Elena calculates the new arithmetic — 3 runs, 5 days, degrading tape — and recognizes she has no escape mechanism. The only exits now are: hand the data to the team, destroy it, or publish it in some form she cannot yet execute.

---

### Chapter 9 — Day 8
**(b)** Tape integrity: Run 4 reveals a twelve-second dropout in the **core** record — the second parametric segment is gone. The dropout is no longer edge-only. Instrument run margin: 3 → 2. Power reserve: 38 % → 30 %. Days to foreclosure: 4.  
**(c)** **PERMANENTLY FORECLOSED:** full parametric verification. The complete parameter set her protocol was designed to prove is no longer achievable with this tape. What remains is the first parametric segment — sufficient for a provisional claim, not a peer-reviewed one.

---

### Chapter 10 — Day 9
**(b)** Generator voltage variance: +/- 8 % (was +/- 5 %). Each remaining run now requires a pre-run stabilization procedure (fifteen minutes of generator draw, no analysis). Days to foreclosure: 3. Power reserve: 30 % → 24 %.  
**(c)** Step closer: Lars reveals Mather Station recorded a forty-seven-second anomalous reading in the UHF secondary band, fourteen minutes before Elena's detection. He filed it as artifact and does not know what he had. The option to be the sole holder of knowledge about the signal is foreclosed — but the corroboration is inaccessible unless Elena tells Lars what he detected.

---

### Chapter 11 — Day 10
**(b)** Printout integrity: two columns are ink-degraded from cold contraction (timestamp column and calibration reference column partially illegible; three data points unreadable). Tape dropout: visual measurement confirms 18 seconds. Days to foreclosure: 2. Power reserve: 24 % → 20 %.  
**(c)** Step closer: the printout's chain-of-custody timestamp is partially illegible. The printout can no longer independently verify the signal's receipt time. The photograph record (twelve frames, station camera) exists but is not instrument-verifiable.

---

### Chapter 12 — Day 11 (morning)
**(b)** Tape dropout: confirmed at 20 seconds by instrument (Run 5; more precise than visual measurement). Instrument run margin: 2 → 1. Power reserve: 20 % → 13 %. Days to foreclosure: 1.  
**(c)** Step closer: with one run remaining and one day left, Elena documents the final state of her evidence in her analysis notebook. What she has is fixed; it will not improve. She must decide how to present this to Walczak.

---

### Chapter 13 — Day 11 (night)
**(b)** Days to foreclosure: under 24 hours. Power reserve: 13 % → 10 %. Generator voltage variance further widening (she cannot safely run a stabilization procedure at current reserve levels).  
**(c)** Step closer: McMurdo confirms the LC-130 departs McMurdo 0700, arrives Winslow ~1300. The timeline is fixed. Fourteen hours remain in which Elena holds the choice and the tape alone.

---

### Chapter 14 — Day 12 (pre-arrival)
**(b)** Tape dropout: 23 seconds (Run 6). Instrument run margin: 1 → **0**. Power reserve: 10 % → ~3 % (at life-support threshold). Days to foreclosure: 0 (this is Day 12).  
**(c)** **PERMANENTLY FORECLOSED:** all further instrument verification. The record is what it is. Elena picks up the radio handset as the chapter ends.

---

### Chapter 15 — Day 12 (arrival)
**(b)** Days to foreclosure: 0 — the LC-130 has landed. Power reserve critical (generator running at minimum safe output). The chain of custody passes from Elena's sole possession.  
**(c)** **FINAL FORECLOSURE:** the choice is no longer Elena's alone. Whatever she has done with the data, the team knows or does not know. The outcome of the signal — whether it survives peer review, whether Mather's corroboration is ever examined, what the institutional machinery does with what she hands them — remains unresolved at chapter's end.
