## Action Beats

**Stabilization procedure**

1. Elena wakes on Day 11 with a clear agenda. She has one day before the LC-130. She needs to know the tape's current state before she decides anything about tomorrow. She will run the protocol this morning.

2. She goes to the equipment building for the stabilization procedure. The morning voltage variance: she checks the fault log — five additional minor excursions overnight. The variance is at +/- 8 %, same as yesterday. She runs the stabilization: heater shed, reduced instrument load, fifteen minutes. She watches the gauge. The variance settles to +/- 5 % under reduced load. Good enough.

3. She restores the instrument load slowly. The variance holds. She initiates the run sequence.

**Protocol Run 5**

4. The run begins. The warm-up cycle: familiar, steady. The tape loads. Elena watches the analyzer's output.

5. **Opening segment:** The beginning of the signal — the calibration lead and the start of the first parametric segment. The opening seconds are silence: the binder failure at the reel edge. She measures it mentally against the timing: she estimates it at seven or eight seconds. Slightly worse than the eight she had on Day 7.

6. **First parametric segment (body):** Still intact. The frequency distribution reads cleanly. The primary anomalous parameters — the ones that make the signal non-terrestrial and non-natural — are present. She does not let herself feel anything about this yet; she is watching the display, not interpreting.

7. **Gap:** The twelve-second silence at the transition to the second parametric segment. Same as Run 4. The second segment begins at twelve seconds: silence. Elena notes it. The instrument's precise measurement: **twenty seconds** of silence across the gap (from the start of the expected second segment to the point where the calibration tail begins). This is two seconds more than the twelve seconds she measured at Run 4 two days ago. The dropout is growing: from 12 on Day 8 to 20 on Day 11, approximately 2.7 seconds per day.

8. At that rate: the core signature region — the first parametric segment's body — will be reached by the dropout in approximately four to five days. It will not be reached by tomorrow. The core signature will survive through tomorrow's final run.

9. **Calibration tail:** Intact. Forty-seven-second duration confirmed.

10. **Run complete.** Power reserve: ~13 %. Instrument margin: 1 remaining.

**The evidence summary**

11. Elena goes to the main hab. She makes tea, not because she wants it but because her hands need something to do. She brings her analysis notebook to the galley table and opens it to a blank page.

12. She writes the evidence summary. She takes her time. This is the most important document in the station; she writes it in the language she would use in a paper, not in shorthand. The summary covers:

    - **What exists:** the magnetic tape (physical condition as of Day 11: dropout at 20s, core signature intact); the printout (physical condition: two columns partially degraded, core parameter columns intact); the analysis notebook (this document); twelve photographic frames (not yet developed); instrument run logs for Runs 1–5.
    - **What the instrument runs established:** the signal's duration (47 seconds), primary anomalous parameters (from the first parametric segment), confirmation of non-terrestrial and non-natural character of the signal pattern. What the runs have NOT established: the complete parametric profile (second segment lost to binder failure as of Run 4).
    - **What the corroborating data shows:** Mather Station (Lars Engström), forty-seven-second event in UHF secondary band, received fourteen minutes before Winslow's detection. Filed as unresolved artifact. Not independently verified; not yet communicable without revealing the signal's existence to Engström.
    - **What can be claimed:** a provisional statement that a signal of specific anomalous character was received for forty-seven seconds on [date/time] at Winslow Station; the primary anomalous parameters; the absence of terrestrial or natural explanation within the scope of the verification protocol; corroborating data at Mather Station (unverified, requires follow-up). What cannot be claimed: a peer-reviewed complete parametric characterization from this tape.
    - **What one additional run (Run 6) can do:** confirm the current state of the tape and the core signature's continued readability, and produce one final verification of the primary anomalous parameters before the chain of custody passes.

13. She reads the summary back to herself, paragraph by paragraph. She makes two small corrections. She does not add or remove any assessments. The summary is honest.

14. She closes the notebook. She puts it with the tape and the printout in the equipment cabinet.

15. **One run remaining.** Tomorrow: the LC-130 arrives at approximately 1300. She has the morning.
