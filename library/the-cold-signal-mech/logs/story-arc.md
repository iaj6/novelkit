# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-forty-seven-seconds** — Elena receives a forty-seven-second anomalous signal in the receiver hut, runs Protocol Run 1 through a brownout, confirms the result, and counts what she has left.
- **01-forty-seven-seconds** — Elena receives a forty-seven-second anomalous signal in the receiver hut, runs Protocol Run 1 through a generator brownout, confirms the result, and begins counting what remains.
- **02-the-second-run** — Elena inspects the tape at dawn, finds first binder stress at the reel edge, runs Protocol Run 2, confirms the signal intact, and locks in the heating tradeoff that will govern every run forward.
- **03-anomalous** — Elena checks the generator (67%), visits the receiver hut without running the protocol, then takes Lars Engström's SSB contact and learns — in passing — that Mather detected an anomalous one-minute transient last week, filed as instrument artifact.
- **04-the-inventory** — Elena completes a systematic inventory of all depleting resources on Day 4; Protocol Run 3 reveals the first confirmed five-second tape dropout at the start of the first parametric segment.
- **05-worse-than-calculated** — Elena discovers the fuel burn rate is 15% above baseline, collapsing her remaining run margin from 5 to 3; Walczak calls from McMurdo and she says nothing about the signal.
- **06-before-the-window** — Elena estimates the tape dropout at eight seconds by visual inspection, builds the data packet at the fax terminal, writes and revises the cover statement, and does not send it; the relay window opens in under twelve hours.
- **07-the-window** — The relay window opens at 0800 and closes at 0848; Elena writes the timestamp, moves between terminal and receiver hut, and does not transmit; the institutional channel closes permanently.
- **08-after** — Post-gate afternoon: a generator fault forces Elena through the stabilization procedure and outside; she calculates three runs, five days, and a degrading tape, and sits with the arithmetic and no decision made.
- **09-core-dropout** — Run 4 reveals a twelve-second core dropout in the second parametric segment; full parametric verification is permanently foreclosed; Elena writes the honest accounting of what remains.
- **10-sixty-kilometers-east** — Lars reviews his monthly log and unknowingly hands Elena independent corroboration — forty-seven seconds at 432.275 MHz, fourteen minutes earlier — and she tells him it was probably RFI.
- **11-the-physical-record** — Elena examines the printout column by column, finds the timestamp and calibration reference ink-degraded, photographs the damage on 35mm film, and measures 18 seconds of tape dropout — the record diminished, documented, nothing fixed.
- **12-one-remaining** — Run 5 confirms the tape dropout at twenty seconds; power reserve falls to 13%; Elena writes the complete evidence summary in her analysis notebook, knowing exactly what she has and what she has lost.
- **13-fourteen-hours** — McMurdo confirms the LC-130 at 1300 Day 12; Elena assembles the evidence package, carries the tape into the hab for the night, writes a page she sets aside face-down; fourteen hours remain.
- **14-the-last-run** — Protocol Run 6 confirms the core signature intact at a 23-second dropout; instrument margin reaches zero; Elena assembles the evidence package and picks up the SSB handset.
- **15-the-plane** — The LC-130 lands; Walczak is first off; Elena brings him to the equipment building in the first private moment and opens the analysis notebook to Run 1; the chapter ends before he looks up.
