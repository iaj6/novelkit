# Continuity

Hard facts that must not break in any chapter. These are the load-bearing assertions the rest of the pipeline must respect exactly as stated.

1. The signal lasted exactly **forty-seven seconds**.
2. The signal was received on **night three of polar darkness** at Winslow Station — this is Chapter 1.
3. Elena Hartmann is **sixty-four years old** at the time of the story (June 1991).
4. She has **thirty years** of career in astrobiological research.
5. Her professional reputation — "if Hartmann calls it, it's real" — is the exact formulation used by her colleagues as shorthand for her record of refusing to announce prematurely.
6. The team left on the night of the signal or the following morning; Elena is **alone** for the full duration of the story except for radio contacts.
7. There are **twelve days** until the LC-130 Hercules arrives with her returning team.
8. The scheduled mid-winter communications gate (SSB net to Mather Station + relay uplink window) falls **around Day 6–7** — this is the book's structural hinge.
9. After the Day 6–7 window passes without transmission, **no equivalent uplink opportunity exists before Day 12**.
10. The LC-130 lands on **Day 12**. The final chapter ends on that day. There is **no coda beyond it**.
11. **At least one thread must remain deliberately unresolved** in the final chapter.
12. The signal's **content is never described to the reader** — not the message, not what it encodes. Only shape, duration, and the parameters that make it unambiguous.
13. There is **no second transmission**. The signal came once. It will not repeat.
14. The **magnetic tape and printout are the sole copies** of the signal data. There is no backup, no digital duplicate transmitted in real time.
15. The **signal analyzer is a proprietary instrument** — the verification protocol cannot be replicated on any other device at the station. Each re-run costs power; the number of runs remaining is finite and decreasing.
16. The **generator's voltage regulator is faulty**, causing intermittent brownouts. It is the limiting resource for everything else.
17. The **decision must stay live throughout** — the story may not reveal, imply, or foreshadow that Elena has "already decided" on night one or at any point before she acts. The choosing must be happening in real time until it is done.
18. **Extra time must increase pressure, never relieve it.** Any delay, weather event, or scheduling change must be rendered as tightening the screw — more cold exposure for the tape, more generator cycles burned, more time for a third party to also detect something.
19. **Each chapter must measurably worsen at least one named quantity** — power reserve, tape integrity, instrument run margin, hours-to-gate, or days-to-foreclosure. A chapter that does not advance the depletion is a structural failure.
20. Winslow Station has **no internet, no e-mail, and no real-time digital uplink**. Communications are HF single-sideband radio and a once-per-fortnight fax/data relay window.
