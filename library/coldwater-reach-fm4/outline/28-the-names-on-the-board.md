# Chapter 28 — The Names on the Board

**POV**: Marcus Klein  
**Tense**: Third-person limited past  
**Season / time**: December 1944–January 1945  
**Target words**: ~2,800  
**Chapter shape**: Interiority-heavy (school, the news, the kitchen; the weight of accumulation)  
**Ending mode**: Coda  
**Opening texture**: Interior

---

## One-line summary

Two Coldwater men are killed in the Ardennes counteroffensive; one — Calvin Wilkes — was in Marcus's Latin class in 1942; he and Henry sit with it in the kitchen.

---

## Setting

Coldwater High School, then the walk home, then the Crow house kitchen. December 1944–January 1945. The school in deep winter: the furnaces working hard, the days very short, the holiday break and then the new year.

---

## Goal / pressure

This is the chapter in which the war's losses arrive at Marcus's own level — not Horak, not the vague weight of the European news, but a student: a specific person whose Latin declensions he corrected, who is now dead in Belgium. The pressure is the particular pressure of carrying losses when you are already a person who has been carrying losses for eight years.

---

## Action beats

**Beat 1 — The news:**
1. Early January. Henry tells him in the kitchen one morning — he has heard from one of the school parents. Two Coldwater men are confirmed dead in the German counteroffensive in the Ardennes. One name Marcus knows: Calvin Wilkes. Henry says: "He was in your Latin class. First year you were here."
2. Marcus knows him. He remembers: weak ablatives but a real facility for the rhythm of the sentences. He could recite the Virgil passages with more feeling than most students manage; he just couldn't explain the grammar.
3. He says: "Yes. I remember him." He does not say what he remembers, yet.

**Beat 2 — At school:**
4. He goes to the school and teaches. The school is aware of the losses — someone has posted the names in the hallway by the main entrance, where the names of the school's servicemen have been maintained since 1942.
5. Calvin Wilkes's name has been there since he enlisted in 1943. Now there is a small change — a black border has been placed around it. Standard practice; Bertrand Hatch did this for every name when the telegram arrived.
6. Marcus walks past the board on his way to his classroom. He looks at Calvin's name. He taught him: fall 1942, spring 1943. One full school year. The boy could do Virgil by ear.

**Beat 3 — Teaching through:**
7. He teaches his classes. Third-year Latin — they are doing Caesar's *Gallic Wars*, which is the wrong text for today and the only text he has. He teaches it correctly. He does not make any remark about the war or the losses.
8. After school: he stays in the classroom longer than usual. He is grading papers but he is also sitting with the knowledge of Calvin Wilkes's death and what it means to have taught a person and then to have them die.

**Beat 4 — The walk home:**
9. He walks home in the January dark. He is thinking about accumulation — the losses he has been carrying since Vienna. His brother (who stayed in Vienna; Marcus has no word since 1940). A colleague from Paris (arrested 1942; status unknown). The friends from Sherbrooke who were released but had nowhere to go. Horak.
10. And now Calvin Wilkes — the first loss from this specific life, this town, this classroom. A student he taught. A person who was in his care in the small way that students are.
11. He thinks: he has been here for almost three years. He has a classroom, a room, a kitchen table. He has students whose names he knows. One of them is dead now in Belgium. This is not the same as the other losses. It is the same as the other losses.

**Beat 5 — The kitchen:**
12. He arrives home. Henry is already there. He has also heard about the other man — a fisherman's son, a boy Henry knew. They eat dinner. The kitchen is warm.
13. After dinner, Henry says: "You think about your students long after." Marcus says: "I suppose that's what teaching is." Henry says nothing for a moment. Then he gets up and does the dishes.
14. Marcus sits at the table and watches Henry do the dishes. He is thinking about Calvin Wilkes and Virgil and the rhythm of the sentences. He is also thinking: he has not moved. He has been here nearly three years. He has this kitchen, this table, this man doing the dishes. He cannot yet say what this is, but it is not nothing.

---

## Outcome / coda

The coda is the kitchen after the dishes — the lamp, the winter on the clapboard, Henry putting away the last plate. Marcus sitting at the table. Both of them holding their losses separately in the same room. The chapter doesn't resolve the grief; it ends on the fact of the kitchen, which is its own kind of answer to the question Marcus has been carrying since he arrived: whether displacement ends, and what is left of a person when it does. He has this kitchen. He has not yet said that it is home. He is sitting in it.

---

## New question raised

What happens to Marcus when the war ends? Not to the war — to him. He has been living in transit for eight years. The transit has created this kitchen, this table, these students. When the war is over, is he still in motion, or has the motion already stopped and he just hasn't known it?

## Continuity notes

- Calvin Wilkes: named here for the first time, retroactively established as a student in Marcus's Latin class in 1942 (his first year). He dies in the Ardennes in December 1944–January 1945.
- Two Coldwater men dead in the Ardennes — continuity fact #13 (the town loses two more men in the Battle of the Bulge). The second is unnamed in this chapter; Marcus doesn't know him personally.
- Marcus's brother: in Vienna, no word since 1940. This is referenced here for the first time explicitly. Do not elaborate; it is one item in the accumulation.
- Henry doing the dishes: a physical act of companionship. He doesn't say the thing he's thinking (about all the names on all the boards he has seen in twenty years of living in this town); he does the dishes. This is his register.
- "He cannot yet say what this is, but it is not nothing" — this is Marcus's arc resolution beginning to form. It should feel earned but not announced. Chapter 29 and 30 will complete the arc.
