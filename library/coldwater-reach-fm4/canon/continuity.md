# Continuity

Hard facts that must not change between chapters or drafts. Numbered for citation.

---

## Dates and timeline

1. The story opens in **October 1943** and closes in **spring 1945**, just before VE Day (May 8, 1945). Approximately eighteen months of narrative time.
2. The body washes up on the Coldwater Reach breakwater in **October 1943**, after a three-day blow.
3. Owen Bowman drowned in the **November storm of 1935**. He was the previous harbormaster. Eira found him after three days.
4. Eira has held the harbormaster post for **eight years** when the story opens (i.e., since approximately 1935).
5. Eira is **52** in October 1943.
6. Hannah turns **18 in March 1944** (she is 17 at story's opening in October 1943).
7. Marcus arrived in Coldwater Reach in **March 1942**.
8. Marcus is **47** in October 1943.
9. Marcus was on the Emergency Committee in Aid of Displaced Foreign Scholars rolls **since autumn 1940**.
10. Marcus was interned at **Sherbrooke, Quebec** (a real Canadian internment camp for "enemy aliens," including Jewish refugees from Europe).
11. Marcus's path: **Vienna → Paris → London → Sherbrooke, Quebec → Cambridge, Massachusetts → Coldwater Reach**.
12. D-Day is **June 6, 1944** — within the narrative period. It arrives on the radio.
13. The **Battle of the Bulge** begins December 1944 — also within the narrative period. The town loses two more men.
14. The story ends **before VE Day**, not after it.

## Characters

15. Eira Bowman is **the only woman holding a harbormaster post on the Maine coast** in this period.
16. The Pfeiffer family (Albrecht and Magda) came to Maine from **Bavaria in 1922**. They are **naturalized U.S. citizens**.
17. Albrecht Pfeiffer is from a town **near Nuremberg**, Bavaria.
18. The Pfeiffer farm is **three miles inland from Coldwater Reach**, on Route 9.
19. Four German prisoners are assigned to the Pfeiffer farm. Karl Reinmar is the **youngest**, age 19.
20. Karl Reinmar was captured in **North Africa (Tunisia, spring 1943)**, following a Wehrmacht conscription at age 17.
21. Karl is from a town in **Franconia, approximately fifty kilometers from Albrecht Pfeiffer's birthplace**.
22. The armed escort at the Pfeiffer farm is **Lonzo Reed**, an aging Coastal Patrol private, age 63.
23. Marcus boards at **Henry and Lillian Crow's house on Elm Street**, three blocks from the harbor.
24. Henry Crow's family came north from **Norfolk, Virginia**, in **1924**. Henry is the school's **chief custodian**. He is 51 in 1943.
25. Lillian Crow is **48** in 1943. She is from Waterville, Maine.
26. Marcus teaches **German and Latin** at Coldwater High School. The previous instructor enlisted.
27. Marcus **does not discuss** his internment at Sherbrooke.

## The body on the breakwater

28. The body is male. Not from Coldwater. No papers found on the body.
29. The Coast Guard takes the body and handles the official response.
30. **Eira saw something on the dock the night before the body came in.** This is her load-bearing knowledge. It is not stated outright in any chapter's opening.
31. None of the three main POV characters ever possesses the complete picture of who the man was and what happened.
32. The body's identity is **not fully resolved** before the book ends. The three characters' relationships to it resolve; the underlying fact may remain in shadow.

---

## Setting and infrastructure

33. The Coldwater Reach breakwater is **granite blocks, laid in the 1890s**, approximately 200 feet long, running out from the north shore.
34. The tidal range in the harbor is **eleven feet**.
35. The harbormaster's office is at the **foot of the commercial wharf** — cedar-shingled building, chart table, tide log, field telephone, kerosene stove.
36. Coldwater High School is a **WPA-era brick building**, grades 1–12, approximately 130 students.
37. The German POWs are housed in the **renovated icehouse** on the Pfeiffer property.

---

## Structural rules

38. **The three POV characters never meet as a group of three.** Individual crossing points between pairs are allowed.
39. Eira, Hannah, and Marcus each have approximately **ten chapters** (~30 chapters total, distributed asymmetrically as the story requires).
40. Additional short chapters from **a fourth POV** (Henry Crow, Lonzo Reed, or Karl Reinmar) are permitted but exceptional — they supplement the main three, they do not rotate equally with them.
41. The three POVs have **distinct prose registers**: Eira's is spare and procedural; Hannah's is interior and porous, with German threading in; Marcus's is precise and formally constructed.
42. The ending's resolutions are **character-level, not war-level**. The war ending is background, not foreground.
43. **No spy thriller mechanics**: no coded messages, no submarine landings, no Nazi agents in Maine. The war is present through loss, constraint, and displacement — not through plot-machinery.

---

## Historical accuracy constraints

44. The POW labor program in Maine was real. Camp Houlton in Aroostook County was a real facility. Satellite farm placements were real.
45. The BC-1206 radio set is the correct period wartime radio reference.
46. V-Mail (microfilmed letter system) is the correct period communication reference.
47. By **autumn 1943**, the acute coastal U-boat threat of 1942 had substantially subsided. The body is not a U-boat story.
48. German-born noncitizens were classified as **enemy aliens** under wartime regulations. The Pfeiffers' naturalized citizen status protects them legally but not socially.
49. Race in 1943 coastal Maine is a fact of life rendered through specific behavior and specific silence — not through authorial monologue or lecture.
