# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-the-dock-and-after** — Eira sees a man on the dock at night and stays silent; the next morning his body is on the breakwater, and she reads the gap her silence left in the tide log.
- **02-the-icehouse** — Hannah observes Karl Reinmar across the fence for the first time; he says "Danke" for a tool passed through; Magda speaks one sentence—"Wir hätten früher gehen sollen"—then goes silent again.
- **03-the-second-bedroom** — Marcus teaches the ablative absolute, hears the town's U-boat theory about the body, and finds at the Crows' kitchen table the one warm room in Coldwater where he is not a specimen.
- **04-official-report** — Lt. Parsons takes Eira's formal statement; the case closes as accidental drowning unidentified; Eira reads the Tuesday night log entry three times and closes the book.
- **05-fifty-kilometers** — Karl's Franconian phrase stops Albrecht mid-swing; Hannah learns her father was born in Feuchtwangen and maps the fifty kilometers that locate Karl in relation to him.
- **06-the-half-question** — Hannah asks Marcus what Landsmann means; his precise answer opens the question he'd been refusing since October — what kind of person ends up on a Maine breakwater with no papers.
- **07-portland** — Eira attends the Portland harbormasters' meeting, gets the winter fuel allocation approved, and hears Philip Varnum name what she's been holding without a word: the refugee crossings.
- **08-mutter** — Magda's silence reaches its worst yet; Hannah covers the farm alone, reads to her mother in two languages, and discovers a German phrase she cannot place whose meaning she cannot set down.
- **09-the-breakwater** — Marcus walks the harbor road, pauses at the breakwater, and in a brief exchange with Eira recognizes her compression as the same instrument he has used for years about Sherbrooke and Vienna.
- **10-private-reed** — Lonzo Reed watches a full December work day at the Pfeiffer farm, sees both the Albrecht-Karl fence moment and Hannah's interest in Karl, and writes "no incidents" on his report — because some things are not incidents, and he knows the difference.
- **11-owens-ledger** — During the January equipment inventory, Eira finds Owen's 1934 ledger note about J.T. — a deferred entry, eventually made complete — and holds it beside the silence of her own October log.
- **12-the-school-board** — Marcus defends his position before a three-member school board review and keeps his job; walking home, Henry says "I have too" — the closest Thread 6 comes to naming what neither man names.
- **13-the-gift** — Hannah turns eighteen; Magda makes pancakes without being asked for the first time since November; Hannah finds Karl's carved bird at the fence and keeps it, her first deliberate secret.
- **14-the-unregistered-boat** — A Coast Guard letter names the body for the first time — Jan Václav Horak, Czechoslovak displaced person, last known Halifax 1942 — and the name opens more than it closes.
- **15-franconian** — Hannah brings Marcus a Franconian harvest phrase; he pinpoints Karl's origin near the Czech border and privately assembles the probable network picture connecting Horak to the Pfeiffer farm.
- **16-albrecht-and-karl** — Hannah witnesses Albrecht and Karl speaking in the same Franconian dialect across the fence; a town name stops her father with a slow stillness; Magda watches from the window; nothing is said aloud.
- **17-mr-kleins-room** — Henry completes his morning rounds at the school, reads Marcus's classroom from the outside, and holds what he sees — the grammar on the desk, the loosening — without saying it.
- **18-what-language** — Eira brings Marcus the sounds she heard on the dock; he identifies them as Czech; she gives him Horak's name; they trade partial pictures obliquely and both say exactly as much as they mean to.
- **19-after-eira** — Marcus walks home with the full assembled picture of who Horak was, decides not to act on it, and sits in the Crows' kitchen saying none of it.
- **20-d-day** — D-Day arrives on the field telephone at 3 a.m.; Eira logs the notation, fixes the dock-3 cleat, listens to Eisenhower's voice at Beal's, manages a bent prop shaft, and stands at the breakwater as the boats come in — not knowing if Galen Fuller or Robert Cates are alive.
- **21-karl-speaks** — D-Day news arrives at the Pfeiffer farm through Lonzo's newspaper; Karl gives Hannah his first full English sentence — "I did not think I would live to see it" — and she understands she has a person on the other side of that fence now.
- **22-the-unfinished-letter** — Marcus drafts a letter to Cambridge about Horak, writes four careful paragraphs, cannot send it without implicating Eira and the Pfeiffers, and puts it in the desk drawer.
- **23-august** — Eira learns Galen Fuller died June 17th at Normandy; at the harbor store she finds the only true sentence for Ruth Fuller and goes back to work.
- **24-harvest** — Full harvest on the Pfeiffer farm; Hannah's fence meetings with Karl have become a quiet pattern; Albrecht warns her in German to be careful; she does not yet know if she'll listen.
- **25-what-is-and-isnt-dangerous** — Marcus tells Hannah exactly what the POW regulations prohibit and what they don't, examines the carved bird, and tells her to stop — not because it's wrong, but because neither of them benefits from visibility.
- **26-the-harbor-log** — Eira does the annual log review, reaches October 1943, and writes the late entry in the harbor log — completing the record in her own handwriting, one year on.
- **27-november-weather** — The farm closes in for winter; Hannah keeps her distance from Karl but watches his lamp from the barn door; Magda cycles and returns; the Ardennes news holds Albrecht in two geographies at once.
- **28-the-names-on-the-board** — Two Coldwater men die in the Ardennes; Marcus loses his first student, sits with the loss and Henry in the kitchen, and cannot yet name what he has.
- **29-what-he-left** — Hannah offers Karl the carved bird at the south fence; he refuses it; she keeps it; the prisoners leave Thursday down Route 9; the farm continues.
- **30-the-spring-opening** — Eira opens the spring harbor, hears the war ending on the radio at Beal's, stands at the breakwater one final time with Horak's name and the unidentified second figure still in shadow, returns to her log and keeps writing.
