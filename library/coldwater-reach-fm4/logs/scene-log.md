
## 01-the-dock-and-after
**Summary:** Late October 1943, night into morning. Eira Bowman performs a standard late mooring check after a three-day blow. She knows she should challenge the man as an unauthorized person on the dock. She turns back instead, checks the far smacks, returns to the office, writes nothing about what she saw, and goes home. The next morning, lobsterman Hector Brann reports a body on the breakwater seam. Eira goes out, recognizes the coat, notes the wrong calluses and no papers, and calls the Coast Guard. Lt. Arthur Parsons arrives, asks if anything was unusual the previous night; she says nothing she needs to note. The body is removed. Back at the chart table she reads her own tide log entry and understands exactly what it doesn't say. Thread 1 enters (the gap in the log). Thread 2 enters (Owen's chart table; she reads the record her silence has made).

## 02-the-icehouse

## 04-official-report
