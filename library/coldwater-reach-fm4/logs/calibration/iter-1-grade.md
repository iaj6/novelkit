## Compared sample against brief anchors

**Exemplars anchored against:** Elizabeth Strout's *Olive Kitteridge* (multi-POV coastal Maine, weight of what is not said) and Ron Hansen's *The Assassination of Jesse James* (procedural calm, world rendered through work and weather). The brief asks for literary adult fiction — restrained, precise, third-person limited past, three distinct POV voices within a shared register of restraint.

**POV in sample:** Eira Bowman (harbormaster). The brief specifies: spare, procedural, observant, short declaratives, professional vocabulary, interiority arriving through what she notices and how long she looks — not through introspection.

## What the sample does well

- **Sentence-length distribution is precise.** Most sentences run 5–18 words. The longest two ("Beside him, or rather turning away, a second figure was already moving toward the street end of the dock — walking fast, back to Eira's light, already gone." ~28 words; "He held himself very still — not the stillness of cold or exhaustion but the stillness of a man listening to a place with his whole body." ~28 words) land exactly at the Eira ceiling. Fragments land correctly: "She stopped." / "She had a clipboard." / "She had, technically, a whistle." / "She tightened it." — all in the 2–5 word range at points of extremity, per spec.

- **Chapter-opening convention honored.** "Eira noted the barometer at 2300 — climbing since suppertime, the blow over or near enough — and closed the tide log." opens on professional observation (tide log, barometer reading). She is already doing something. Weather arrives in paragraph four, noticed by Eira in her professional capacity, not as scene-setting.

- **Interiority arrives through observation, not introspection.** The man on the dock is rendered entirely through what Eira can see and measure from thirty feet in lantern-edge-light. "Her mind ran its check. Unauthorized persons on the commercial wharf after dark — a matter for the harbormaster." is free indirect thought in her professional vocabulary — exactly the guidance's prescribed mode.

- **The mystery is carried, not investigated.** Eira sees the man, does not approach, finishes checking the last line on the *Callie*, and goes home. She does not write it down. She does not follow up. This is exactly "character in their world encountering their piece of what happened," not a genre investigation beat.
