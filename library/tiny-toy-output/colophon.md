# colophon

*The Bottle* is the smallest book on this shelf, and it is here to prove the smallest possible claim: that a one-page brief goes in and a finished book comes out. three chapters, about three thousand words, readable end to end in roughly thirteen minutes — a lighthouse keeper, a routine, one interruption. every stage of the press ran on it just as it does on the long books: canon, outline, draft, edit, cover, audiobook.

the brief asked for restraint — a keeper whose interior life shows in weather, light, and ritual rather than introspection — and the draft mostly held to that. its one persistent habit was the sentence after the sentence: explaining what an image had already carried. an editing pass went through and cut those, a few words at a time — the gloss on a simile, the caption on a closing image — and left the images to do the work.
