# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-the-specimen** — Reyes runs her nightly session at the containment yard — sensor check, fifty minutes of passive observation, one veil displacement whose cause she cannot determine — and the shape of eleven months' failure is established.
- **02-first-light** — Brandt's ordinary training day before the Contingency exists — PT, chow, drill, a card game, and gear cleaning in a converted warehouse, Thread 2 entering.
- **03-what-she-tried** — Reyes's modified cognitive bias protocol yields the first non-random behavioral datum in eleven months — two of three metrics, single trial, not enough — and she does not close the formal record.
- **04-voluntary** — The Contingency list is posted with Brandt's name and the president's son's name; the unit absorbs the reality through banter and forms and the first accelerated drill, and Brandt deflects the question of why with the one answer he can say out loud.
- **05-the-report** — Reyes writes and sends the formal report — "proceed without behavioral intelligence" — then stands at the evening yard with the certainty she could not put in it.
- **06-dry-runs** — Eight runs on a full training day shape the unit's ease and fatigue; Tafoya asks the question Kendrick asked, and Brandt gives the same answer with more acknowledged behind it.
- **07-eleven-months** — Reyes runs the mental inventory of eleven months of failed study at the late-night yard; a low-register sound behind the veil cannot be verified; the chapter refuses to land.
- **08-processing** — Brandt moves through the administrative machinery of shipping out — manifest check, processing stations, the emergency contact designation form — and the chapter cuts mid-task in the barracks, boots in hand.
- **09-the-outer-barrier** — The day before the dinner: Reyes comes to the yard without protocol, stands at the outer barrier, and speaks to the creature in plain language for the first time; the chapter cuts before she knows what she has done.
- **10-the-night-before** — The night before the send-off: the last drill done, chow better than usual, the barracks going quiet earlier than any night in eight weeks.
- **11-the-hall** — Reyes arrives at the send-off dinner, observes the diminished hall and the president across the room, and slips out through the service door before the roll call begins — pulled by a frequency she cannot verify.
- **12-staging-area** — Brandt's unit assembles in dress configuration, moves through the compound in the last outside air, forms up in the staging corridor, and stands in the line while the roll call works toward him.
- **13-the-yard** — Reyes at the containment yard during the roll call: she finds a new angle on the veil, sees the creature most fully yet, and witnesses a vocalization aimed not at her but at the direction of the platform.
- **14-the-platform** — Brandt watches Haverford and then the president's son go through; hears the name-before-recognition beat through the wall; steps to the platform; the flash ends his thread.
- **15-dead-morning** — Reyes walks the empty platform in the dead hours, returns to the yard, runs through the two metrics she holds from the night, and stands at the outer barrier as the chapter ends without resolution.
- **16-the-next-list** — Full morning: Reyes moves through the cleared hall, past the next list already forming, and settles at the outer barrier with a field log she cannot write in and a choice she has not made.
