## What's Working

**Register distinction.** The opening sentence of Ch01 vs. the opening line of Ch02 ("0357. Brandt was already awake") establish two unmistakable voices. This distinction holds for all six chapters without drift. The Ishiguro-register Reyes and the Haldeman-register Brandt are doing exactly what the brief asks.

**CPL FARROW, R. — Ch04.** The reveal of the president's son's name is handled with precision: "One kind of familiar — roster-slot familiar, name-on-a-form familiar. He had to wait a half-second for the rank and the unit notation to resolve into the other kind." The flat cadence of the recognition, followed immediately by "Huh." from Tafoya, is the right register for what the information costs the unit. Thread 3 enters cleanly.

**Reyes's methodology as emotional container — Ch03.** "What She Tried" is the act's best chapter. The sedation trial retrospective earns its length because each phase is methodologically distinct and each failure is distinct. The cognitive bias test — improvised endpoint frequencies from the arousal response of month three, a "not a clean implementation" that is nevertheless "the implementation the data and the timeline would support" — is excellent. The formal assessment language that follows ("single trial; standard significance criteria not met; cannot rule out environmental interference; not replicable on this timeline") holds the creature unresolved while rendering the shape of Reyes's certainty.

**Brandt's belief in what he does.** The six-months-prior list submission, mentioned in passing in Ch02, is the right way to establish conviction. He doesn't say why; the fact that he submitted it precedes everything else. The brief asked for conviction present in action rather than speech, and the act delivers this consistently. The hip at position six is a physical correlate of the same quality: he gives it what it owes, and it accepts without ceremony.

**Decline through texture.** Sustained and precise across all six chapters. No infodump anywhere. The world's state is assembled from what can and cannot be obtained at this installation, from the grain product and the real coffee and the field notebooks and the form that has "permanent" in the same font as everything else.

## Concerns

**Over-articulation in Ch05 — medium.** The passage where Reyes considers the sentence "Proceed without behavioral intelligence" has one passage too many:

> "The report required what the methodology had produced. What she was certain of was not what the methodology had produced, and a formal assessment sent to the president's office was not the place for certainty that could not survive review. *If it could not survive review, it was not evidence. She had reviewed the distinction enough times in eleven months that she no longer expected reviewing it to change anything.*"

The first two sentences do the work. The final two sentences name the epistemological gap the chapter has already rendered through her action (not putting the certainty in the report). This is the "sentence-after-the-sentence" failure mode from style.md — the image lands, and then the prose turns to explain it. The last two sentences should come out.

**"Costello was asleep before 2210" — low, watch in subsequent acts.** The Costello closing beat appears at the end of Ch02, Ch04, and Ch06. Three of three Brandt chapters close with this construction (Ch02 uses a variant: the bed-drop observation, then "He was where he had put himself. That was fine with him"; Ch04 adds "Same as always"; Ch06 strips it to "Costello was asleep before 2210." alone, which is probably the most deliberately stripped). The stripping in Ch06 suggests the drafter was aware of the pattern and tried to vary it — and the stripped version is the best of the three. But this is a recurring construction logged in the craft record, and if it persists into subsequent Brandt chapters it becomes a stylistic tic that narrows the act's texture.

**Ch06 final line — low.** "Running it eight times a day didn't close it faster." follows "The gap between what repetition built and what the actual thing would ask of them was not something five runs a day closed." The second sentence adds nothing not already in the first. Should be cut.

**Ch06 structural repetition — low, by design.** "Dry Runs" is the third consecutive Brandt chapter in the same structural key: drill, banter, kit-cleaning, "gate's the only door." The thread 2 advancement is real — "That's what I can say out loud" is stronger than Ch04's deflection, and the beat between Tafoya's read and his "Okay" is earned. But the chapter is working on the thinnest margin of its three. This is inherent to the day-before frame and the brief's structural logic; it is not a flaw so much as a pacing note for whoever reads Acts 2 and 3. The repetition is the point, up to a point.

## Pacing

Act 1 functions as the "day-before" the brief specifies, and it earns that pace. The interleaved Reyes-methodology / Brandt-drill structure creates slow-building tension without announcing it. Neither thread sprawls; the chapters are appropriately short for what they are establishing. The slight repetitiveness of the Brandt sequences is correct — the monotony of drilling is the texture — but the act is at the edge of its structural capacity by Ch06.

## Summary

Act 1 is largely working. Both registers are established cleanly and maintained. The threads enter on schedule. The decline-through-texture discipline is the act's most consistent strength. The creature's unresolvability and the son's opacity are handled correctly. Primary concerns: a single over-articulation passage in Ch05 (easily cut), a recurring closing construction in the Brandt chapters that needs to be broken in Acts 2 and 3, and a final-line redundancy in Ch06. No structural failures. No register collapses. No stakes announced. The book is doing what the brief asks.
