- `had not attempted a forty-third`

- **Reyes:** Spare, precise, Ishiguro-register established. Interiority arrives entirely as procedure — what the protocol requires when interpretation is not available. **Craft notes:** The chapter earns its restraint by never naming what the data is for: the 'occupied weight' of the enclosure's air is described and immediately dropped because she stopped trying to describe it.
