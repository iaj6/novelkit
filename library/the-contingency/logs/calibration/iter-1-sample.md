# Calibration Sample — Iteration 1
## Chapter 01: The Specimen (Opening, ~400 words)

---

The thermal trace was within the existing range. Reyes copied the number into the field log without looking up from the page — she had memorized the range in the first month, and it was useful only when it moved outside it, which in eleven weeks it had not. She wrote it anyway. The log had to be complete.

The acoustic monitor registered 0.04 Hz baseline. She wrote that too.

She worked the outer perimeter in order — thermal trace, respiration indicator, acoustic — checking each sensor housing as she went. The acoustic monitor casing had a crack at the lower left corner that she had photographed in September and would photograph again tonight. It had not propagated in six weeks. She had stopped deciding whether that was reassuring. She completed the circuit and returned to the survey mark.

The cloth veil hung flat against the inner barrier. Wind from the east at three knots would not have moved it.

She set the folding stool, opened to today's page, and adjusted the earpiece to her right ear out of habit — the left-side receiver produced the same signal but arrived at a half-second lag she had not been able to calibrate out. It was probably not relevant. She had documented it in August and moved on.

In eleven months she had learned to sit here without the posture of waiting. Waiting produced a quality of attention that contaminated the record: an observer looking for the event she expected to see. What the log needed was observation — duration, stimulus, response, conditions. She was past the stimulus phase. The acoustic-stimulus trial had run its course in October. The chemical-marker study had run its course before that. She came because watching was not the same as doing nothing, and because eleven months of watching a veiled enclosure at dusk was preferable to eleven months of staying away from it, and because she did not fully trust herself to stay away.

The veil moved.

She noted the time. Displacement: inward, 4.2 seconds, then resettled. She checked the external-wind sensor. Three knots from the east, constant. She looked at the August notation in the margin — ventilation outlet, documented variable. She wrote: consistent with wind. Cannot rule out interior-initiated movement.

That was the language the record required.

She turned to the next line and waited for nothing to happen again.
