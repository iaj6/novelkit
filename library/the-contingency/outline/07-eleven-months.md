# Chapter 07 — Eleven Months

**POV:** Dr. Imani Reyes  
**Tense:** third-person limited past  
**Section:** Approach  
**Target words:** ~2,400  
**Chapter shape:** interiority-heavy (anchored physically at the yard throughout)  
**Ending mode:** elliptical  
**Opening texture:** observation

---

## One-Line Summary

Reyes at the yard at night, running her mental catalog of eleven months of failed study while the compound prepares for the ceremony around her; the creature shifts in its enclosure; the chapter refuses to land.

---

## Setting / Time

The containment yard, late at night — a time Reyes doesn't usually come. The compound is lit differently than usual: visible preparations for the send-off are underway. Tables being moved in the hall's service entrance; a delivery through the back gate. The dinner is now days away, not weeks.

---

## Goal

Reyes has no formal goal for this visit. She came to the yard tonight because she came to the yard. The chapter's internal goal is the inventory: she runs through what she knows and what she cannot know, and the chapter ends when the inventory runs into something it cannot resolve.

---

## Obstacle

The obstacle is epistemic rather than physical: she has all the information she is ever going to have, and it is not enough. The creature has not cooperated with study; it will not now. What she is left with is certainty without evidence — the specifically intolerable condition for a scientist.

---

## Action Beats

- Reyes arrives at the yard later than she usually comes. The outer perimeter, her folding stool, the minimal field kit she brings out of habit even when she has no protocol in mind. The compound behind her is unusual tonight: lights on in the service wing, the sounds of furniture or equipment being moved. She notes this without pursuing it.
- She begins the inventory — not formally, not in her log, but in the register of a mind that has organized itself around this problem for eleven months. What she tried first: the sedation battery. The different agents, the different delivery methods, the different approaches. The creature did not go down. Nobody had seen that before. She tried everything.
- Physical examination. The attempts to get close enough for a sample, for a reading, for any direct data. The equipment she lost. The outer barrier as the permanent limit. She has been inside the inner barrier twice; she will not go again; the cloth veil has been in place since the second time.
- The vocalization. She does not run away from it in the inventory. Eleven months ago, the second visit inside the inner barrier, the creature aimed a sustained sound directly at her — different from its prior sounds, nothing like them. The days that followed: the pressure first, then the threshold shift, then the hearing in her left ear settling into what it is now. She thinks about it with the methodological vocabulary she applies to everything: what she observed, what she cannot rule out, what she cannot conclude. Attack, or the only word it had. She still cannot decide.
- The yard shifts. Something behind the veil — a sound, low register, below what the acoustic monitor catches without the amplifier. A movement, or the shadow of one. She watches. The compound's preparation sounds are audible behind her. She thinks: does the creature know something is happening in the compound? She cannot answer the question. She cannot rule out that it does. She cannot rule out that it doesn't.
- She stays longer than she should. The compound quiets. The preparation sounds stop. The yard is still. The creature behind the veil is present or isn't — she cannot tell from here. The chapter ends with her at the outer barrier in the dark, holding the inventory she has been holding for eleven months, and the veil between her and what she cannot know.

---

## Outcome / Turn

The chapter refuses to land — this is its function. The elliptical ending is not evasion but the formal enactment of Reyes's epistemic situation: there is no resolution available. The creature may have shifted in response to the compound's activity, or it may have shifted for reasons she cannot access. Both things are possible. Neither can be ruled out. The chapter ends on this. The reader is in exactly the position Reyes is in.

---

## New Question or Pressure

Is the creature aware that something is changing in the compound? Is awareness even a concept that applies? These questions are not asked explicitly — they are the shape of Reyes's interiority throughout the chapter. The pressure they create carries into Ch09.

---

## Continuity Notes

- **The inner barrier and the vocalization** are revisited here in detail for the first time since Ch01. The drafter should use this chapter to give the reader a full account of those two visits — what she saw, what she tried, what happened — without dramatizing them as scenes. They are memory and inventory.
- **The hearing motif** gets its fullest treatment so far. The left ear's quality of silence; the ring that is not constant but is not gone; the way the compound sounds arrive differently on the two sides. This should be rendered physically — not as abstract loss but as the specific sensory experience of a hearing deficit.
- **The compound preparations:** delivery trucks, tables being moved, the lit service wing. Reyes observes these without dwelling on them. She does not think about what they are for; the reader who has read Ch04 knows, but Reyes is not thinking about the marines tonight.
- **The creature's shift:** low register sound, possible movement behind the veil. Logged mentally but not formally — she did not bring the acoustic monitor with full deployment tonight. She has the field kit but not the complete setup. This matters: the datum she observes cannot be formally documented, which is consistent with the chapter's function.

---

## Craft Notes for Drafter

This is the most interiority-heavy of Reyes's chapters so far. The inventory of eleven months is the chapter's spine, and it should feel like the inside of a mind that has organized itself professionally around this problem — not like grief expressed directly, but like method that has run out of objects and is now running through itself. The vocabulary is still technical. The emotions reach the reader through the inventory's architecture, not through any sentence that names them.

The creature's shift at the end must be handled with care. It is neither a breakthrough nor a null result — it is exactly the ambiguous datum that this chapter is about. The drafter should not build toward it as a climax; it should arrive the way most things arrive for Reyes: as a notation, a thing she sees, a thing she cannot conclude from.

The elliptical ending is unusual in this book's distribution and should feel earned — not frustrating but honest. The reader does not get a resolution because Reyes does not have one. The chapter's final image should be Reyes at the outer barrier in the dark, and nothing more.
