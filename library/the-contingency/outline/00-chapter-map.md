# Chapter Map — *The Contingency*

## Overview

- **Total chapters:** 16
- **POV distribution:** Reyes × 9 (Ch01, 03, 05, 07, 09, 11, 13, 15, 16) / Brandt × 7 (Ch02, 04, 06, 08, 10, 12, 14)
- **Alternation:** strict R–B–R–B from Ch01 through Ch14; Brandt's thread ends at the flash in Ch14; Ch15–16 are Reyes only
- **Target word count:** ~40,000 words across 16 chapters
- **Tense:** third-person limited past throughout

---

## Arc Shape

```
PART I: DAY-BEFORE (Ch01–02)
  Setup — each character in ordinary life before the Contingency was named
  Chapters run shorter (~2,000 words); prose observes without foreshadowing
  
PART II: APPROACH (Ch03–10)
  Rising pressure — Reyes working against the clock of the dinner;
  Brandt waiting, drilling, processing the reality of the mission
  Chapters middle-weight (~2,200–2,600 words); events connect across POVs
  
PART III: THE SEND-OFF NIGHT (Ch11–14)
  Spine — the banquet, the roll call, the creature's yard, the platform
  Heaviest chapters (~3,000–3,800 words); chronologically interlocked
  Brandt's final chapter ends on the flash; his thread does not resume
  
PART IV: DAY-AFTER (Ch15–16)
  Reyes only — the dead morning; the empty platform; what she carries out
  Chapters return to middle weight (~2,200 words); the book closes on a literary fade
```

## Continuity Checkpoints

- **The camera never crosses the gate.** No scene is set on the far side.
- **The two POVs never meet.** No shared scene, no visual recognition, no contact.
- **The creature's nature is never resolved.** Prose neither confirms nor denies intelligence.
- **The president's son's reasons are never explained.** Seen only from below (Brandt's formation; Reyes's distance across the hall).
- **Brandt's thread ends at the flash.** Ch14 is his last chapter; no POV from or beyond the gate.
- **The day-after section is Reyes only.** Ch15–16; at minimum one chapter contains the empty platform and the next list forming.
- **The book closes on a literary fade.** Ch16 ends on a sensory image, nothing declared, at least one thread deliberately unresolved.
