# Style — *The Contingency*

## POV and Tense

Third-person limited, past tense. Bound tightly to whichever POV the chapter belongs to — the camera does not lift above the character's head. Two co-leads in strict alternating sequence until the alternation severs at the gate. The severance is structural and permanent: Brandt's final chapter ends on the flash of the platform and his thread does not resume. No character other than Reyes and Brandt serves as a POV.

## Narrative Distance

Close — nearly flush with the character's perception. What the POV character cannot know, the reader cannot know. What the POV character misses, the reader misses. The narrative does not editorialize. It does not insert ironic distance between the reader and the characters' beliefs, hope, or grief. Brandt's conviction is reported straight. Reyes's uncertainty is reported straight. The reader feels the weight; the prose does not point at it.

## Two Registers within One Literary Mode

Both registers are literary adult prose. Within that, they are distinctly different — and the difference must be felt within the first paragraph of any chapter.

**Reyes — spare, precise, scientific.** Near Ishiguro's register in *Never Let Me Go*: the horror assembled slowly beneath euphemism and method; interiority that arrives as procedure rather than feeling. Her sentences are measured, often formally balanced, averaging 18–25 words. Longer periodic constructions are acceptable when her analysis requires them; no sentence should exceed 45 words. Her professional vocabulary — *containment*, *assay*, *protected contact*, *the knock-down threshold*, *hot animal*, *specimen*, *wild-caught individual* — enters without explanation, without italics, without gloss. It is the air she breathes. Her emotion is not named: it is the shape of what she observes and what she cannot resolve.

**Brandt — faster, coarser, enlisted.** In the tradition of Haldeman's *The Forever War* and Shaara's *The Killer Angels*: the world through a trained enlisted man who notices logistics, unit status, fatigue, the chain of command, and the specific absurdity of the situation. USMC vocabulary — *soup sandwich*, *FNG*, *Gunny*, *boot*, *zero-dark-thirty*, *hurry up and wait* — enters in idiom and cadence, not in phonetic spelling or dialect exaggeration. Profanity as punctuation and compression, not shock. Average sentence length 10–15 words. Short declaratives cluster naturally; the rhythm is three or four short beats, then a longer sentence when his interiority surfaces — but interiority stays brief, because the character clips it.

## Dialogue

**Reyes's dialogue** is sparse. She is often alone or in brief, careful exchange. Her dialogue deflects before it discloses. **Brandt's dialogue** carries most of his chapter's weight — banter, complaint, the running in-group language of men in close quarters. Both registers use spare tags; neither glosses or interprets what dialogue means. The reader hears the banter; the prose does not explain it.

## The Creature

Never given interiority, translated thought, or language. Referred to as *the specimen* in Reyes's register. Physical descriptions are permitted — what it looks like through the outer barrier, what its behaviors suggest — but these are filtered through Reyes's observational frame and hedged explicitly: *consistent with*, *what appeared to be*, *she could not rule out*. The vocalization is described for its physical effects on Reyes (the pressure, the threshold shift, the left ear going dark by degrees), not for its meaning. The prose neither confirms nor denies intelligence.

## The Gate and What Lies Beyond

The camera never crosses. Nothing on the far side is described, narrated, or inferred by the prose. Characters may speculate; the speculation is never resolved. The mechanism of the gate's one-way quality is never explained by any character, any narration, or any prose voice.

## The Hearing Motif

Reyes's partial deafness (left ear, acquired from the creature's vocalization) is available as texture and motif in her chapters: the muffled hall, voices arriving as if through water, the ring that is not quite constant but is not gone. Use it where the chapter reaches for it organically. Do not stamp it onto every Reyes chapter. The restraint is the motif.

## Tone

Earnest throughout. No satire. No ironic distance from the characters' hope, belief, or grief. The diminished grandeur is rendered as fact and felt as loss. The scope of the stakes — the species, the planet — lives in the catering and the roll call and the empty platform, never in declaration.

## Failure Modes

The AI-author patterns most tempting in this story's register. The drafter should actively avoid these; the compression pass should cut them.

- **Thesis restatement** — the stakes are humanity's survival; the prose must never announce this. Any sentence that abstracts to "the fate of the species," "what this night meant for what remained of the country," or comparable declaration has failed. The significance lives in the catering and the name on the lectern, not in statement.
- **Post-scene subtext naming** — after a strong scene (the creature, the roll call, the hearing, the flash), the prose must not turn and name what the scene was "about." The scene does the work. Cut the gloss.
- **Meta-commentary on cognition** — Reyes in particular will tempt the drafter toward "she noted," "she registered," "she was aware that," "she found herself thinking." If the reader sees it through her eyes, she has already noted it; the label is redundant. Same for Brandt.
- **Sentence-after-the-sentence** — an image lands; the next sentence explains or underscores it. In both registers, this flattens the prose. Land the image and move; do not gloss it.
- **Dialogue glosses** — Brandt's gallows humor and unit banter must never be annotated for the reader. No "as if to say," no interpretive tags on what the joke is doing. The banter is trusted to be heard.
- **Redundant transition / re-summary** — when the POV switches, the chapter does not re-ground the reader with a recap of where we are. Each chapter opens cold in its register. The alternation is the reader's orientation; no throat-clearing.

## Lean-Into Patterns

Patterns the drafter and editors should NOT treat as failures, because this register depends on them.

- **Scientific vocabulary in stride (Reyes)** — terms like *assay*, *protected contact*, *knock-down threshold*, *specimen* should appear without italics or explanation. This is her world; she does not translate it for anyone.
- **Enlisted vocabulary in stride (Brandt)** — *soup sandwich*, *Gunny*, *boot*, *FNG*, *zero-dark-thirty* should appear without apology, gloss, or editorial distancing. The reader is in his register.
- **Earned brief interiority (both)** — short dips into genuine interiority are permitted and welcomed when the chapter has earned them. A single sentence of Brandt's conviction, or a single sentence of Reyes's unresolvable certainty, is not overreach. The error is unearned interiority, or interiority that runs more than three or four sentences without a sensory or action anchor.
