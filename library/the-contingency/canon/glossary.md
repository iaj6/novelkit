# Glossary — *The Contingency*

Alphabetical. Terms are from the research dossier and from the canon. Later phases add to this file as new proper nouns, place names, and in-world terms are coined.

---

**APR doors** — Air pressure-resistant interlocking doors, standard vocabulary in BSL-4 containment. Appears in Reyes's register in the context of improvised high-containment; the creature's enclosure does not formally meet BSL-4 protocol but borrows the spatial logic.

**Assay** — A laboratory test, analysis, or procedure. Standard scientific vocabulary in Reyes's register; enters without explanation.

**Blue Falcon** — USMC slang for a soldier who turns on his unit or disregards a teammate's welfare. Also called "Buddy-fucker." Brandt's register.

**Boot** — A newly enlisted Marine, or any Marine new to a role. Can be applied dismissively even to promoted Marines. Brandt's register.

**Brass** — Officers collectively. Derived from metal rank insignia. Brandt's register.

**Cognitive bias test** — A methodology for measuring emotional states in animals by examining how they interpret ambiguous stimuli. One of the formal tools Reyes has attempted with the specimen.

**The Contingency** — (1) The mission: a one-way transit through the gate by a small trained unit. (2) The unit itself: fourteen marines selected and prepared for the transit. (3) The send-off ceremony at which the roll call is conducted.

**Containment** — In Reyes's register: the physical and procedural arrangements that separate the specimen from unprotected personnel. In the creature's case, improvised rather than protocol-standard.

**Critical Skills Operator (CSO)** — MOS 0372, the Marine Corps special operations enlisted rating. Brandt's MOS and that of his unit.

**FNG** — "F***ing New Guy." Applied to any Marine new to a unit or role.

**Free Contact** — Wildlife research term: staff work with an animal with no barrier in place. Reyes has not used free contact with the specimen.

**The gate** — The working teleportation platform. Sends matter to one destination; nothing has ever returned through it. The platform where the roll call concludes; where Brandt steps at the end of his final chapter. Not formally named in the world — characters call it "the gate" or "the platform."

**Geedunk** — USMC slang for the snack bar; also small comforts and treats available to enlisted personnel.

**Gunny** — Shorthand for Gunnery Sergeant (E-7). Used as a term of respect for senior NCOs. In the Contingency unit, the two squad element leaders are Gunnery Sergeants.

**Hot Animal** — Zoo/wildlife research internal designation for a specimen with high potential for human injury during routine staff activity. The creature is, by any category, the highest-tier hot animal Reyes has encountered.

**Hurry up and wait** — USMC/military phrase for the paradoxical cycle of rushing into staging position, then waiting at length for orders or action to begin. Defines the temporal texture of Brandt's chapters in the Approach section.

**Knock down** — To sedate a specimen via injection or dart for examination. The creature cannot be knocked down; no sedative has achieved the threshold. This is one of the central failures of Reyes's study.

**MARSOC / Marine Raider Regiment** — Marine Corps Forces Special Operations Command. The Contingency unit is not formally designated MARSOC, but draws on the same culture, MOS structure, and small-team philosophy.

**MSOT (Marine Special Operations Team)** — The fourteen-person foundational element of MARSOC. Two five-person tactical elements, each led by a Gunnery Sergeant, plus a Captain (team commander) and Master Sergeant (team chief). The Contingency unit maps to this structure.

**Oorah** — All-purpose USMC affirmation, enthusiasm, greeting. Can be sincere or sarcastic depending on context and delivery.

**Protected Contact** — Wildlife research term: staff work with an animal only with a barrier in place. Standard operating procedure for the creature throughout Reyes's study.

**Semper Fi** — "Semper Fidelis," always faithful. USMC motto. Used as greeting, farewell, or sincere affirmation; context determines weight.

**Slack-man** — MARSOC/Recon-era term for the junior CSO in a small-team tactical element.

**Soup sandwich** — USMC slang for something or someone in complete disarray. Brandt's register.

**Specimen** — Reyes's professional term for the creature. Not a dismissal; the vocabulary of formal study applied to a subject that has resisted all formal study.

**Terminal lance** — A Lance Corporal with no promotion prospects; also used generically for any career E-3.

**Wild-caught individual** — Formal wildlife and conservation biology term for a specimen captured from its natural environment, as contrasted with a captive-born animal. Used in Reyes's register; the creature is wild-caught in the most literal available sense.

**Zero-dark-thirty** — Very early pre-dawn hours; military usage for any hour before the standard workday begins. Brandt's register.

**Containment yard** — The improvised high-containment enclosure behind the compound's service wing where the specimen is held. Consists of an outer chain-link perimeter and an inner concrete-and-chain-link barrier. Not formally BSL-4. Reyes conducts her observation sessions here nightly from a stool at the outer perimeter.

**Outer perimeter** — The first line of enclosure in the containment yard: chain-link fence and unlocked gate. Reyes conducts all observations from within this boundary, 3 feet back from the gate.

**Inner barrier** — The second line of enclosure in the containment yard: concrete on three sides, chain-link on the fourth, 8 feet inside the outer chain-link. The heavy canvas veil hangs across its chain-link face. Reyes has not crossed this line in four months.

**The cloth veil / canvas veil** — A heavy canvas panel running from the top rail of the inner barrier's chain-link face to the ground, tied at 5 intervals along each vertical seam. In place since day 21 of study; second removal was the last. Occludes the inner enclosure from all angles.

**Field log** — Reyes's small gray spiral-bound notebook used for real-time annotation during observation sessions. On her sixth volume at the time of Chapter 1.

**Formal record** — Reyes's larger standard-issue notebook where field log entries are transcribed and archived each evening after observation sessions. On her fourth volume at the time of Chapter 1, started 42 days before the Day-before chapter.

**Passive acoustic sample** — Reyes's current observation methodology after the stimulus phase: she listens through the acoustic monitor to whatever the yard produces, while the monitor records the full frequency range including what she cannot perceive on her left side.

**Stimulus phase** — Six weeks of systematic acoustic assay sessions (42 sessions total) conducted in the fourth through ninth weeks of Reyes's study. The specimen did not respond to any introduced stimulus in any pattern distinguishable from baseline. The phase was terminated; it was not repeated.

**Service corridor** — An approximately 80-meter concrete passage running from the containment yard's outer perimeter gate to the building's rear entrance. Low fluorescent strips on the right wall, utility conduit on the ceiling. The kitchen passage and main hall connection are off this corridor.

**Residential wing** — The wing of the compound where Reyes's quarters are located, accessed by the back stair from the service corridor. Reyes's room is the third door on the right.

**Tafoya** — Unit member in Brandt's compound; runs heavy on his left heel; primary banter dynamic with Brandt throughout training. Present in morning PT, chow, and the afternoon card game.

**Strickland** — Unit member; runs morning PT with Brandt and Tafoya; observes Brandt's form in drill and delivers corrections informally.

**Kendrick** — Unit member; present at chow (the orange/yellow protein-packet dispute) and the afternoon card game.

**Osei** — Unit member; shoulder injury requiring icing during the Chapter 2 afternoon; displaced from the card game by the injury.

**Wick** — The FNG (F***ing New Guy) in Brandt's unit; ten days on the compound at the time of Chapter 2. Asked a direct question about the list at the card table and was met with silence; did not pursue it.

**Costello** — Brandt's bunkmate; sleeps in the top rack above Brandt; can achieve unconsciousness in approximately four minutes on any surface.

**Gunny Haverford** — The Gunnery Sergeant (E-7) who runs the unit's morning drill sequence. Corrections are specific, brief, and non-repeating. Referred to as 'the Gunny' in the text after first introduction.

**Day Twelve Ruling** — Internal procedural precedent in the unit's evolved card-game variant (spades). Introduced in Chapter 2 through use only, without explanation. Wick does not yet have it.

**Administrative annex** — Building on the marine compound containing the chow hall (four rows of folding tables, serving line on the left wall) and the daily schedule board. Schedule is posted by 0600.

**Bay two** — One of the two warehouse buildings on the marine compound converted to barracks. The covered staging area for drill is behind bay two.

**Cognitive bias test** — An animal cognition methodology that measures emotional state by presenting an ambiguous stimulus between a trained positive endpoint and a trained negative endpoint; the subject's response direction indicates whether its baseline affect is optimistic (positive judgment bias) or pessimistic (negative judgment bias). Reyes adapts the methodology in Ch03 using proxy endpoints derived from the specimen's month-three arousal response, given that standard conditioning of the specimen is impossible.

**Negative judgment bias response** — The behavioral outcome in a cognitive bias test in which an ambiguous stimulus produces responses more consistent with the negative endpoint than the positive endpoint, suggesting anxious or pessimistic baseline affect. Reyes's Ch03 assessment describes the specimen's Tone C responses as 'consistent with a negative judgment bias response' — not proof of cognition, but not nothing.

**Acute arousal response** — How Reyes documented the fourth sedation compound's effect on the specimen in study month three: eight minutes of elevated acoustic output, two-degree thermal increase, sustained non-wind-correlated veil movement, then full recovery. The entry explicitly notes it is not sedation. The onset and recovery frequencies from this response become the proxy endpoints for the Ch03 cognitive bias test.

**Tone A / Tone B / Tone C** — The three tones used in Reyes's Ch03 cognitive bias protocol. Tone A: 440 Hz (negative anchor, derived from arousal onset frequency). Tone B: 1440 Hz (positive anchor, derived from arousal recovery frequency). Tone C: 940 Hz (ambiguous midpoint, frequency never previously presented to the specimen). The creature responds to Tone C but not Tone A or Tone B.

**Signal generator** — Portable device used in Reyes's cognitive bias test setup; connects via cable to the portable speaker placed against the inner barrier's chain-link face. Used to output the three target frequencies (440 Hz, 940 Hz, 1440 Hz) at controlled intervals.

**Movement indicator** — A photoreceptor array Reyes added to the outer face of the inner barrier's chain-link that logs any displacement of the veil at the lower hem. One of the three metrics tracked during the Ch03 cognitive bias protocol. Produced no non-random responses in the session.

**Farrow (family name)** — The president's surname. His son, CPL Farrow, R., is on the Contingency list — second from the end of the fourteen-name column. The president's first name and the son's first name are not given in Chapter 4. The president is referred to only as 'the president' in the prose.

**Contingency list** — The official fourteen-name roster of marines assigned to the Contingency mission. Posted in a clear plastic sleeve on the board outside Gunny Haverford's office in the administrative annex corridor. Brandt is eighth on the list. CPL Farrow, R. is second from the end.

**Personal effects form (Contingency)** — A form packet issued to Contingency marines after the list is posted. Includes liability acknowledgment, emergency contact designation, and personal effects declaration. The personal effects section contains two non-standard lines: institutional donation option and a 'permanent archive' personal statement field. Introduced in Chapter 4.

**Chen** — Presidential aide who liaises between Reyes and the president's office on logistical matters. Confirms receipt of the formal report in Ch05. Carries a clipboard. Minimal presence; procedural function only.

**Formal report (the comprehensive assessment)** — The six-phase formal assessment sent by Reyes to the president's office during Ch05. Structure: methodology employed, results obtained, conclusions supportable, conclusions unsupportable, recommendation. Recommendation: 'Proceed without behavioral intelligence.' Unsupportable conclusion: 'This study cannot confirm or deny the specimen's capacity for higher cognition.'

**Acoustic cognitive bias protocol (2,200–8,400 Hz configuration)** — New acoustic stimulus protocol attempted by Reyes on the morning of Ch05. Frequencies ran from 2,200 to 8,400 Hz in overlapping bands of 400 Hz. No prior session had used this configuration. Produced no response above ambient variance.

**geedunk** — USMC enlisted term for snack food and the place or tray where it is served. In the Contingency compound, the break geedunk is crackers in sealed packets, nutrition bars, and a cooler with water and sports drink. Used without gloss in Brandt's chapters.

**the cleared space behind bay two** — The outdoor poured-concrete area behind warehouse bay two on the marine compound, lit by two work lights on poles, used as the primary drill space for the Contingency unit's mission-sequence runs. Established in Ch06.

**alpha-2 agonist** — First compound tested in the sedation battery. Administered by remote delivery through the feeding port. Produced no behavioral change in the specimen.

**acute arousal response (sedation battery)** — Documented response produced by the fourth sedation compound (barbiturate, study week 7): 8 minutes of elevated acoustic output at previously uncatalogued frequencies, 2-degree thermal increase, sustained veil movement uncorrelated with ambient wind, full recovery within the session period. Filed under Phase 3 Appendix A. Not sedation.

**maintenance access (inner barrier)** — A sealed entry point in the inner barrier used in study month 5 to introduce the cognitive task apparatus remotely. The maintenance access was welded shut when the month-5 session closed and has not been re-opened.

**cognitive task apparatus** — Equipment introduced through the maintenance access in study month 5 as Phase 4 of Reyes's study. The session lasted 6 hours. No behavioral data was recovered. The apparatus, secondary speaker array, and Reyes's portable thermal probe were left inside the inner space when the maintenance access was welded.

**feeding port** — Remote delivery port through which the nine sedation compounds were administered during the Phase 3 battery. Location on the inner barrier; not otherwise described.

**secondary speaker array** — Equipment introduced through the maintenance access in the month-5 cognitive task session; left inside the inner space when the maintenance access was welded.

**deployment record copy** — The formal version of the Contingency form packet documents (liability acknowledgment, emergency contact designation, personal effects declaration) submitted at Ch08 processing, distinct from the initial Contingency packet copies filled out in Ch04. Different header, paper stock, and date — same fields. Goes into the official deployment record.

**sector coordinator** — The person in the staging area responsible for final tallying and sign-off on each unit member's staged gear. Signs off at the end of Ch08 staging at 1540.

**secondary pallet** — A supplementary palletized load adjacent to Brandt's main staging position, carrying mission-specific equipment. Covered with its own tarp and tracked on a fifth manifest page separate from the main four.

**behavioral index (reduced)** — Reyes's working set of behavioral indicators for the specimen, reduced from seventeen positions in study month one to three by month four: veil movement, acoustic output, and thermal differential. The reduction was never formally documented in the study record.

**plain-language speaking session (Ch09)** — The first and only time Reyes speaks to the specimen without a methodological framework: she says her name, accounts for eleven months of failed study in ordinary language, states her unprovable certainty of the specimen's intelligence, and notes that the marines go through the gate the following night. Not logged in the formal record or the field notebook.

**night-before card game** — The card game session on the evening before the send-off ceremony. Runs shorter than any prior session; ends before 2100; the Day Twelve Ruling is not invoked. The reduced altitude of the game is the chapter's primary marker of the unit's awareness of the following day.

**personal-effects pocket** — The front top-zipper pocket on unit members' field bags with no tactical purpose. In Ch10, Osei uses it to store an unspecified small personal item the night before the ceremony; Brandt uses it to store his watch.

**Platform operating crew** — The technical personnel responsible for operating the gate's sending surface during the roll call. At least one crew member takes position beside the gray slab when the president moves to the lectern. Not characterized beyond their functional role; not named in the prose.

**Staging door** — The heavy unmarked door in the far wall of the banquet hall, to the right of the platform, that connects to the secondary corridor where the Contingency formation is held before the roll call. It is briefly opened just before the roll call begins, revealing the waiting formation.

**Sub-threshold perturbation** — Reyes's working description for the low-frequency left-side signal she perceives in the banquet hall during Ch11: below the ring frequency, below any formal documentation threshold, arriving with a direction toward the yard. Whether it is a phantom signal from her damaged ear or an external source is never determined.

**Secondary staging corridor** — The narrow interior corridor adjacent to the banquet hall, accessed through a heavy door from the service-corridor side, where the Contingency unit assembles in formation and holds before the roll call. It has a double-panel door at the hall end with a seam through which the hall's light is visible. Longer than it appeared in the briefing diagram; two abreast maximum. Also referred to in the briefing materials as 'the formation hold.'

**Platform sound** — The low-frequency sound the sending platform produces when it activates. Audible from the staging corridor through two concrete walls: below speech and string-quartet range, lasting approximately two to three seconds, less attenuated through concrete than normal sound in that range. Followed by a specific silence. First established in Ch12 from Brandt's position in the staging corridor.

**Delgado** — Named unit member; occupies one of the two positions between Farrow and Brandt in the staging corridor formation. First named in Ch12.

**Veil gap (left seam)** — An 8-inch gap between the lower hem of the containment veil and the concrete floor, located at the leftmost vertical seam of the inner barrier's canvas. Caused by the canvas bowing away from the seam tie. Visible only from a crouching position approximately 4 feet from the left corner of the outer barrier. First used as an observation angle by Reyes in Ch13.

**Second vocalization** — A vocalization produced by the specimen in Ch13, during the send-off night roll call. Shorter than the attack vocalization (less than 2 seconds vs. approximately 4 seconds), directed toward the far wall and the direction of the hall and platform, producing mild physical effects on Reyes (sternum/lower-rib pressure only; momentary tinnitus drop, full recovery). Distinct from the attack vocalization of study month two in duration, direction, and physical effects.

**Corporal Brandt, E.** — Brandt's formal roll-call address at the Contingency send-off ceremony. 13th name on the roll call list, second-to-last to go through the platform.

**Roll call order (Contingency)** — The sequence in which the 14 Contingency marines are called to the platform: positions 1–8 go through in Ch12's timeline (off-page for Brandt, heard through the wall); then Haverford (9), Farrow/president's son (10), Delgado (11), Osei (12), Brandt (13), Tafoya (14).

**Production axis** — Reyes's working vocabulary for the directional vector of a vocalization — the angle from which a sound originates, inferred from physical effect distribution on the observer's body (jaw/orbital bones for a direct-aimed call vs. sternum/lower ribs for a call aimed forward of and lateral to the observer). Used in the Ch15 retrospective analysis of the second vocalization's directionality.
