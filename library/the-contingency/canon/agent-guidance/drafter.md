# Drafter Guidance — *The Contingency*

## Voice Register

This book carries two distinct registers, both within literary adult prose. They must be distinguishable within the first paragraph of any chapter.

**Reyes:** Near Ishiguro's *Never Let Me Go* — flat, precise, the horror assembled slowly beneath euphemism and method. Her interiority arrives as procedure: what she has tried, what has not worked, what she cannot rule out. The emotional weight lives in the structure of the sentences, not in named feeling. Scientific vocabulary (*containment*, *assay*, *protected contact*, *hot animal*, *specimen*) enters without explanation or italics; it is the air she breathes.

**Brandt:** Near Haldeman's *The Forever War* (the practical enlisted voice) crossed with Shaara's *The Killer Angels* (restrained third-limited military interiority). The world through a trained eye that notices logistics, fatigue, unit status, and the specific absurdity of their situation. USMC vocabulary in idiom: *soup sandwich*, *FNG*, *Gunny*, *boot*, *zero-dark-thirty*, *hurry up and wait*. Profanity as punctuation and compression, not shock. No phonetic spelling; no dialect exaggeration.

## Sentence-Length Expectations

**Reyes:**
- Average sentence length: 18–25 words
- Maximum acceptable: 45 words (longer is a warning sign)
- Rhythm: measured, sometimes formally balanced; periodic constructions when her analysis warrants

**Brandt:**
- Average sentence length: 10–15 words
- Maximum acceptable: 30 words
- Run of 3–4 short declaratives is the natural rhythm; vary with a longer beat before or after a moment of genuine interiority

## Chapter-Opening Conventions

**Reyes chapters:** Open in observation or interior method — she is looking at something, working through a problem, or moving through a space. The first paragraph establishes where she is and what she is studying or failing to study. Do not open on abstract sentiment or thematic reflection. Sensory anchor first, then any reflection earned by it.

**Brandt chapters:** Open in action, dialogue, or the physical texture of military waiting. The first paragraph establishes the physical situation and the unit context. Do not open on extended interiority. The world first; thought arrives through the world.

## Chapter-Ending Conventions

Mixed distribution across the book. **No manufactured cliffhangers** — the book's tension is structural and cumulative, not episodic-hook-driven.

- Reyes's chapters: tend toward resolved beats or literary fades, except the send-off night chapter, which ends more open
- Brandt's chapters: may cut mid-action or mid-exchange, particularly in the Approach and send-off night sections
- Brandt's *final* chapter ends at the moment of the platform flash — the flash is the ending; there is nothing after it
- The book's final chapter (Reyes, day-after) closes on a literary fade: the empty platform, the next list forming, whatever she is left holding

Target distribution across ~16 chapters: roughly 4 resolved beats, 4 literary fades, 4 mid-action or cut endings, 2 turns, 1 coda (close), 1 elliptical.

## POV Strategy

Two co-leads in strict alternation until the gate. Each chapter is single-POV with no head-hopping. The alternation does not break, skip, or add a third perspective at any point. After Brandt's final chapter, all remaining chapters are Reyes. The drafter does not improvise on the rotation.

## Dialogue Density

**Reyes:** Sparse. She is often alone or in brief exchange (with an assistant, with the president). Her dialogue is careful and deflects before it discloses. Dialogue-to-prose ratio: roughly 20–30% of the chapter.

**Brandt:** Dialogue-heavy. The banter, complaint, and in-group language carry much of the chapter's weight. Dialogue-to-prose ratio: 40–60% of the chapter. The dialogue is not polished or quotable — it is repetitive, call-and-response, and referential to in-group events. The *Generation Kill* criticism applies: dialogue that is "far too sophisticated and scripted for any enlisted Marine" has drifted from the register. Real enlisted banter is fragmented and stumbling and circular.

Dialogue tags: spare in both registers. Do not gloss or interpret with "as if to say," "which meant," or interpretive narration following exchange.

## Comic Timing and Tonal Balance

Brandt's chapters carry gallows humor. The function: frightened men carrying weight they cannot put down, in the only register available. The humor is not witty. It is not the reader's cue to feel amused or superior. It is occupationally specific, repetitive, and call-and-response — the same joke returned to because the joke is a container for the dread, not a performance.

The prose reproduces the banter and moves on. It does not pause to note the humor's function. It does not signal that the book is aware of the dark comedy. The gallows humor is how these men exist in the world; it is reported the same way their gear weight or their drill schedule is reported.

Reyes's chapters carry no humor. They carry a specific quality of dry professional restraint that can feel like wryness but is not. The observation is precise; the language is clipped; the dread is in the gap between what she observes and what she cannot prove.

## Anti-Defaults — What the Drafter Must Actively Avoid in This Register

**Do not announce the stakes.** Any sentence that reaches toward "what remained of America," "the fate of the species," "what this night meant" has failed the brief's most explicit constraint. The significance lives in the catering and the roll call and the empty platform — never in declaration.

**Do not explain the gallows humor.** The banter is never annotated. No "as if to say," no narrative commentary on what the joke is doing for the men, no gloss that tells the reader the humor is a coping mechanism.

**Do not name Reyes's feeling directly.** She does not feel grief; she observes what she has tried and what has not worked. She does not feel afraid; she reviews what she cannot rule out. If the prose names the emotion, it has short-circuited the work the structure is doing.

**Do not drift Brandt's voice toward literary.** The literary attractor will push his chapters toward extended interiority, restrained register, and subtext over surface. Fight this. Brandt's interiority is brief and clipped. The world comes first. He thinks in short declaratives, not in shaped paragraphs.

**Do not cross the gate.** No exceptions. No implication, inference, or narrative gesture toward what is on the far side. The characters may speculate; the prose does not confirm.

**Do not resolve the creature.** Reyes is always in the epistemic mode of "consistent with" and "cannot rule out." A sentence that allows the reader to conclude the creature is intelligent, or is not, or came from X, or is what people become, has broken the brief's central constraint.

**Do not make the president a joke.** He is a real, diminished man. The diminishment is rendered as loss, not as satire. His grief at reading his son's name is played straight.

**Do not drift toward redundant POV re-grounding.** Each chapter opens cold in its register. The reader is trusted to land in the alternation without a recap of where we are or what happened in the previous chapter.
