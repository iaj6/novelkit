# Pitch — *The Contingency*

A few generations into a future that has run out of room, the United States still has a president, a flag, a working teleport — and almost nothing else. The gate can fold space to a destination humanity has found that might let it survive. Nothing sent through it has ever come back.

Tonight is the Contingency dinner: a state send-off, stripped to its bones, at which the president will read the names of fourteen trained marines and watch them step, one by one, onto a nondescript platform and vanish. **Dr. Imani Reyes**, a xenobiologist in her fifties, has spent eleven months failing to understand the one thing that ever came the other way through the gate: a creature, caged in a walled yard behind the hall, that she is increasingly certain is intelligent and cannot prove it. Her time to prove anything is the length of a dinner. **Corporal Eli Brandt**, twenty-seven, believes: the Contingency is humanity's last real chance, and he has chosen to stand at the point of the spear. They share one night and one ceremony and never meet.

Brandt's chapters run until the flash of the platform takes him and his thread simply ends. The chapters that remain are Reyes alone — standing in the dead morning after, holding what she couldn't prove and what she will never be able to explain, with the next list already forming.
