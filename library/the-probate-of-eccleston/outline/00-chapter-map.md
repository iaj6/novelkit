## Compounding Mechanism

**Two ladders under every chapter:**
1. Every chapter closes or materially narrows one named innocent explanation (a "door"). A door once closed is never re-walked.
2. The hearing date counts down monotonically. The one exception: Ch 14 (the refused commission), where the calendar moves **against** Prescott.

**Nine doors (from `canon/continuity.md` item 26):**
- **(a) the machine** — second codicil typed on mill-office machine, not Hall or any solicitor's
- **(b) Josiah's presence** — his last mill visit: May 1926; after that he did not leave the Hall unaccompanied
- **(c) the witnesses** — carter dead; gardener untraceable in Canada; commission refused
- **(d) the profession** — no solicitor anywhere took instructions for a revocation
- **(e) the firm** — no request for the codicil's text, or its date, reached Garside & Prescott before R5
- **(f) the habit** — every instrument drawn by the firm; diary and correspondence silent on any change of mind
- **(g) the despatch-box** — inventoried with no codicil; six days at the mill in Walter's keeping
- **(h) the September reading** — what the register records: instrument read entire, date included, who was present
- **(i) the date asymmetry** — Walter had the date from R5; he never had the text from anywhere

## Fair-Play Architecture

- **R2 in full:** Chapter 1 — before any Walter misquotation reaches the page.
- **R3 in full:** Chapter 2 — reader has seen "or her issue" and now sees "and her issue" in the recital; narration never flags the variance.
- **Misquotation 1 on page:** Chapter 2 — Walter to Prescott at the caveat attendance, mid-sentence, unremarked.
- **Misquotation 2 on page:** Chapter 11 — R4 as financial exhibit; the "and her issue" phrase on the page, unremarked by narration.
- **Misquotation 3 on page:** Chapter 13 — Nora reports Walter's words to the children (period-true channel: witness testimony in the room).
- **Spacing:** Misquotations land in Chs 2, 11, and 13 — never in consecutive chapters (Chs 11 and 13 are separated by Ch 12).
- **Three-quarter mark:** End of Chapter 15. All nine doors closed. Nothing material enters after this point.
- **The discovery:** Chapter 17 — re-reads what already exists; nothing new.
