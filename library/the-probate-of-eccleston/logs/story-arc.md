# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-the-strong-room** — Prescott and Garside open the Eccleston estate file: the will and first codicil are read in full, the September 1926 strong-room register entry surfaces, and Prescott's own attendance note confirms no second codicil was in the despatch-box.
- **02-the-second-codicil** — Thurston & Marsh produce the second codicil; R3 and R5 are each read in full; Walter calls with his cover story and his first spoken misquotation; the caveat is entered.
- **03-the-habit** — Prescott reviews forty years of Josiah's instruments — all through this firm, none private — and Garside's 1924 attendance note establishes that "or her issue" was the deliberate settled form; door (f) closed.
- **04-bank-parade** — Prescott visits Bank Parade, meets Edgar, Nora, and George Fairhurst, takes instructions, explains the administrator pendente lite, and receives Josiah's March 1924 letter to Margaret as Exhibit A.
- **05-the-hinge** — Garside dies at his desk; Prescott inherits the firm and the file; the strong-room register is read entire, establishing the September reading in full; the hearing is fixed at 290 days.
- **06-the-letter-book** — Prescott works through the firm's letter-book and confirms that no request for the first codicil's text was ever received; R5 gave only the date; door (e) closed.
- **07-the-file** — Prescott surveys Josiah's 1926 diary, correspondence, and the instrument register: every channel is silent on any change of mind, and door (f) — the habit — is closed.
- **08-the-gazette** — Prescott places the Gazette advertisement and canvasses all forty-six Lancashire solicitors; every reply is negative; door (d) — the profession — is locked.
- **09-vale-mill** — Prescott visits Vale Mill: identifies the mill-office Underwood Standard typewriter, fixes Josiah's last confirmed visit at 14 May 1926 from the foreman's daybook, and watches Walter—competent, liked—cross the yard.
- **10-the-machine** — Croft's eleven-page report arrives: the second codicil was typed on the mill-office Underwood Standard; four individual machine defects confirm it; no other machine matches; door (a) closed; 200 days.
- **11-the-sale-contract** — Walter's mortgage, sale contract, and banking correspondence are assembled as financial exhibits; R4 enters the file with its "and her issue" wording unremarked; 185 days remain.
- **12-the-witnesses** — Thomas Birch confirmed dead by death certificate; Frederick Hallett traced to Canada without an address; Croft finds both attestation signatures consistent but unbreakable; commission application submitted, 172 days remain.
- **12-the-sale-contract** — Walter's mortgage, conditional sale contract, and banking correspondence documented as financial exhibits; R4 enters the file with its "and her issue" wording unremarked; 185 days to the hearing.
- **12-the-witnesses** — Both attesting witnesses confirmed beyond reach: Birch dead (28 September 1926), Hallett in Canada without address; Croft finds both attestations consistent but unbreakable; commission application filed, 172 days remain.
- **13-the-fairhursts** — Prescott visits Bank Parade in spring; Nora reports Walter's kind visit and his spoken misquotation ("and her issue"); the human stake is renewed at the case's midpoint.
- **14-the-commission** — The commission to examine Hallett in Canada is refused for want of a traceable address; the witness door closes permanently and the calendar moves against Prescott, leaving 140 days and the text to carry the entire case.
- **15-the-hall** — Hall depositions and Dr. Hartley's medical notes establish Josiah could not have visited Vale Mill after May 14, 1926; door (b) closed; 125 days remaining.
- **16-the-date** — Assembling the hearing brief, Prescott formally establishes the date asymmetry: R5 gave Walter the codicil's date in writing; no source gave him the operative text; the recital proves it.
- **17-the-ninth-word** — Doing a final citation check before dispatching the brief, Prescott reads the second codicil's recital against the executed first codicil and finds the ninth word wrong: "and" where the original says "or"; R4 confirms it.
- **18-counsels-brief** — Prescott assembles and dispatches the complete brief to London counsel — variance as spine, nine doors as elimination, handwriting split stated plainly — sixty days to the hearing.
- **19-solemn-form** — At the hearing, Walter reads the first codicil for the first time in his life, finds "or" where he has always said "and," and his counsel withdraws the claim; the action is abandoned, costs to Garside & Prescott.
- **20-the-estate** — Every account settled: estate granted to Edgar as guardian, mill sold at a loss, Walter charged, firm solvent; Prescott closes the file in the strong-room and reads the September entry in Garside's hand.
