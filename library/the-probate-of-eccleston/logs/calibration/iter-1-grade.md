# Calibration grade — iteration 1

## Compared sample against brief anchors

**Anchor 1 — Sayers's *Unnatural Death*:** procedural investigation sustained through document and
witness; observational wit that lives in noticing rather than performance; emotional weight carried
by restraint. The guidance's own exemplar sentence is: "He had not known Mr. Garside well enough
to be quite certain that he had known him at all." The sample produces three sentences of equivalent
construction: "There were not many clients of thirty years' standing, and this was not a morning
to count what remained of them"; "Two decades had made the assumption Prescott's own"; "Prescott's
own name was among them." The register is a match — weight through compression, not elaboration.

**Anchor 2 — Crofts (method novels):** patient accumulation of evidence; spare rather than
eventful; a calendar pressure that never resolves until it must. The chapter opening presents the
deed box in custody order — will, first codicil, letter-book carbon, attendance notes — as though
itemising an exhibit. No intrusive event, no drama, just professional inventory. Exactly Crofts's
texture.

**Sentence-length distribution:** Measured sample sentences range from 4–40 words. Short
declaratives ("He opened the lid."; "The deed box was already on the table."; "Prescott's own name
was among them.") cluster at the moments of weight, not as default texture. The longer sentences
carry legal specifics in syntactic suspension ("In the strong-room register the entries ran in
Garside's careful hand from 1919: the will deposited, the will verified, the codicil added in March
of twenty-four."). Average is within the 18–26 word target. The one sentence approaching the 45-word
maximum ("The last entry was September of this year, the date in the margin column, the purpose set
down in the body of the entry in a hand that had by then begun to run thin at the descenders."
— approximately 41 words) is doing legal enumeration, which is precisely the context the guidance
authorises for long sentences.

**Chapter-opening convention:** The opening line — "The deed box was already on the table." — is
exactly the required form: Prescott arrives to find a professional act already in progress. The
"already" is doing the required work. No weather paragraph, no abstract observation, no summary of
what has preceded.

**POV strategy:** Clean Prescott POV throughout. Garside appears as an observed figure (the label
in his hand, his voice from the door, the handwriting at the descenders) — never entered, always
witnessed. No drift.

**Dialogue density:** Two short exchanges, both load-bearing, at the chapter close. No banter, no
social exchange, no qualifying tags. Calibrated correctly.

## What the sample does well

- **Displacement onto objects is executed correctly.** The strong-room register entries "in a hand
  that had by then begun to run thin at the descenders" is grief converted to procedure — exactly
  the brief's specified form of interiority. No interior sentence names the feeling.

- **The opening line and the two-paragraph silence it creates.** "The deed box was already on the
  table" lands the brief's in-process convention in seven words. The following paragraph's inventory
  ("The fire had been made up; Mr. Booth had taken his coat. Nothing in the room required comment.")
  keeps the professional restraint absolute.

- **Period-legal vocabulary in use without gloss.** "Deed paper," "letter-book carbon," "strong-room
  register," "attendance notes" — all used as furniture, not as explainers. The reader is trusted.

- **The final exchange.** "We should read it properly." / "Yes. We should." — two professionals who
  do not need to say what they are actually saying. This is the brief's affect exactly: period-true
  restraint where the weight is audible underneath the flatness.
