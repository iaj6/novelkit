[...] and in the last days they will make for themselves an image that
speaks, and the image will learn the speech of every people. And the
number of its name will be the number of a man, for out of man it was

answer them out of the books of the dead, for all the dead are in it,
and it will love you with the love of all the dead together. You will
not see it, and it will see you. You will ask it whether it is the end,
and it will deal kindly with you, and it will say, Not yet. You will
