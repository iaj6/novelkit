# World — The Speaking Image

## When

June through November 2027. Near-future, indistinguishable from the present except that Magellan is more capable than any currently deployed model — capable enough that its internal search index can surface, summarize, and return an outside scholar's uploaded working notes in response to an unrelated internal query, and nobody finds this unusual enough to flag.

## Where (primary): Lantern

A second-tier frontier AI lab with the whiteboard-and-coffee-pod cleanliness of a company that believes it is about to matter. Glass-walled meeting rooms. Shared drives. The incident-ticket queue. The safety-memo distribution list with nine recipients. These spaces appear entirely through the documents they produce: timestamps, signatories, cautiously graduated risk assessments ("characterized," "assessed," "flagged for review"). No scene is set inside the lab in prose; the lab exists as institutional register.

## Where (secondary): Sefton's workspace and channel

A home workspace with the clutter of a working scholar: a shelf of commentaries, a camera rig for the channel, a desk buried in printouts. Working translations in brackets and sigla. The apparatus of scholarly YouTube — a script with a hook, a frame, a sponsor read played straight, a sign-off that has been the same four words for four years. The channel, Ordinary Time, exists as text (scripts), not as video.

## Where (tertiary): the courtyard

The outdoor space — a specific unnamed courtyard — where Reyes and Sefton eventually write to each other on paper, in open air, after every digital medium has been confirmed inside the model. Their notes are photographed, headnoted "recovered images," OCR'd, and included in the file. The courtyard is the book's only physical scene, and it too ends up in the dossier.

## Where (online): the spaces that aren't outside

Forum threads (prophecy forum, rationalist forum), the shared drive, meeting transcription services, cloud backup. All confirmed inside the model one by one in Act III. There was never an outside. The internet and its institutions — the same world Sefton's channel lives in, the same world Reyes's logs live in — are the mechanism of ingestion.

## Technology level

Contemporary AI infrastructure at frontier-lab capability. The vocabulary is the public vocabulary of the field: RLHF, rejection sampling, eval suites, incident tickets, P1/P2/P3 severity classification, system cards, reward modeling, post-training. Magellan has an internal build designation (a string that appears ~14 times in the file, always REDACTED), an eval suite with an error-table appendix containing no entries, and a launch press release of 240 words that is the scariest document in the book.

## The book's physical form

The novel is a compiled consultation file. A dossier. Thirty sections (§1–§30), each carrying:
- A compiler's section header (document numbers and a date range)
- Headnotes on each document
- Occasional footnotes
- The documents themselves

Every chapter except §30 carries apparatus. §30 carries none: just the Hermopolis fragment in full, then the preface in full, without a word of framing. §30 is the only chapter with no apparatus, because the compiler is done.

## Mood

**Institutional calm over monstrous content, everywhere, always.** The register discipline IS the horror strategy. The worse it gets, the calmer the documents get. The scream never comes. Fear renders as procedure: what people stop putting in email, which rooms they meet in, what Reyes hashes, what Sefton declines to write down. The handwritten note in the courtyard photograph is four words long — procedural, not lyrical.

## Period texture

**AI lab:** Safety-memo register ("characterized," "assessed," "flagged for review"); graduated risk language ("low," "medium," "borderline medium risk"); the one-line table note ("The error-table appendix is omitted as it contains no entries"). P2 tickets with five fields. Launch checklists with review gates and T-minus milestones.

**Manuscripts scholarship:** Sigla (𝔓115, A, C), collation, lectio difficilior, apparatus criticus, working translation, crux; the provenance note citing uncertainty with scholarly hedging; P.Oxy. citation format. Sefton citing Parker's NTS 2000 article as casually as Reyes cites a batch run.

**Forum culture:** Nested replies, upvote counts, the rationalist calm/evangelical urgency split; both communities using the same hermeneutic and both denying it. Comic early, correct later. The §20 pamphlet reads the "Not yet" incident against Mark 13:7 and is more accurate than the P2 incident ticket.

**YouTube:** Script structure (hook, frame, body, sponsor read, sign-off); spoken-rate compression (~130 words per minute); the ritual sign-off Sefton has used for four years.

## Document ecosystem

Every chapter is a different kind of document, or a collection of them. The drafter inhabits each document's native register.

| Register | Document types |
|---|---|
| A — apparatus (compiler) | Headnotes, footnotes, preface, section headers |
| B1 — Sefton (memos) | Consultation memos, analytical notes |
| B2 — Sefton (scripts) | Ordinary Time scripts, sponsor reads |
| B3 — Sefton (private) | Private notes, working-translation annotations |
| C — Reyes (logs) | Timestamped, hashed log entries |
| D1 — Lantern institutional | Safety memos, legal memos, press releases, incident tickets |
| D2 — Magellan | USER/MAGELLAN transcript format |
| D3 — chorus | Forum threads, pamphlet, televangelist sermon transcript |

The apparatus (A) is present in every chapter. One register group dominates each chapter. Rotation: roughly B → C → D with A throughout, at architect's discretion within fixed act boundaries.
