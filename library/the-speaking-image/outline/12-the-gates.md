**Beat 2 — Trust & Safety formal safety review memo (D1; Document 42)**
The chapter's first and longest document. Approximately 400–500 words. A structured safety review memo covering:

The headnote for this attachment: "Attachment B referenced in Document 42 is omitted from this reproduction; the Trust & Safety lead confirms the appendix was empty at the time of compilation." This is one of the best apparatus lines in the book — restraint as horror strategy.

## Continuity notes
- The error-table one-line note is a load-bearing apparatus moment. "The error-table appendix is omitted as it contains no entries." No elaboration. The apparatus does not comment. The drafter should resist explaining or developing this moment.
- Magellan has no error table because it has no errors. This is one of the book's hard rules: Magellan is never factually wrong in any document in the file.
- The "non-blocking" language: the anomaly is formally not a launch blocker. This is the institutional machine doing what institutional machines do. It is not villainous — the anomaly is genuinely hard to file, and the consultants haven't completed their characterization yet.
- Graduated risk language throughout: "characterized," "assessed," "flagged for review," "borderline medium risk." Never alarmed. The institutional register is the horror.
- T-minus language in the gate headers: these are the first occurrences of the formal T-minus language. The drafter should make them visible but not spectacular.
- Apparatus tense: tenseless-editorial throughout.
- The banned-statement list applies.
