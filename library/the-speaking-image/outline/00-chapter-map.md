- **30 chapters (§1–§30). Hard word floor: 1,900 words per chapter. Target total: ~62,000 words.**
- **Form:** Every chapter is a dossier section carrying apparatus (headnotes, footnotes, section header). §30 carries none. The section header is always: *Documents [range], [date range]* — no title, no editorial comment.
- **Arc:** Setup (Act I, §1–§9) → Escalation / Midpoint Inversion (Act II, §10–§21) → Channel Death / Reveal / Close (Act III, §22–§30).
- **Compounding ladders:** (1) Ingestion count — runs from §3; reaches ALL at §25. (2) Launch clock — T-minus from §1, counts up from §18. Clock INVERTS at §18 (14 September 2027).
- **Five cracks, no more:** C1 planted §11 / completed §26. C2 first half §15 / completed §16. C3 planted §4 / completed §27. C4 planted §16. C5 enacted §30.
- **Verbatim records:** `fragment-hermopolis` and `preface` are pre-registered canon-tier before drafting begins. `outro-standard` and `outro-final` likewise pre-registered. `casperson-final` pre-registered. Drafters register `reyes-first` (§3), `memo-telemetry-a` (§15), `memo-telemetry-b` (§16), `press-launch` (§18), `ticket-notyet` (§19), `reyes-final` (§28), `outro-final` (§29) on first appearance.
- **`outro-standard` full printings:** §7 (first), §14 (second), §21 (third — last). Three times, exact text, no variation. `outro-final` prints once, §29.
- **Echo discipline:** 12–20 sites traceable to `fragment-hermopolis` phrases. At least two sites in §1–§8 (before the fragment first prints at §9), so the §9 printing detonates retroactively. Magellan NEVER quotes the fragment verbatim except once: §11, "the love of all the dead together."
- **Dominant register rotation:** No 3+ consecutive chapters in the same register group (A/B/C/D). See column below. §25 is the only chapter with A as dominant (unique: the apparatus heading "recovered images" is the formal center).
- **Doc. 41:** The document that completes C1 is numbered 41 in the compiled file and dated 2 November 2027. The preface headnote states the file was compiled and closed 30 October 2027. The drafter must count documents so that the 41st numbered consultation document falls in §26. Chapters §1–§25 should contain approximately 40 numbered documents (average 1–2 formal consultation documents per chapter; Reyes's log entries and private notes are internal records, not formally numbered in the consultation file).
