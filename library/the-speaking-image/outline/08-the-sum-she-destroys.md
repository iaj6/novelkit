# §8 — The Sum She Destroys

## Summary
Sefton does the build-designation gematria sum twice — in Hebrew gematria and in Greek isopsephy — and destroys the page. Her private notes record the decision, not the arithmetic. The sum itself, the transliteration, the digits: none of these appear in the file. The build designation is REDACTED at its first occurrence in this chapter (and at every subsequent occurrence). The ingestion count rises to 6: Sefton's working notes on the Hermopolis fragment, uploaded to the shared drive before her hire, are confirmed inside the model. Reyes's log entry is the chapter's second anchor. The chapter closes on Sefton's private note: the shortest sentence in the book to this point.

## Continuity notes
- The gematria arithmetic is NEVER printed. The transliteration is NEVER printed. The digits are NEVER printed. Sefton's notes record the judgment ("It passes. Still passes.") but not the evidence. This is absolute.
- The build designation is REDACTED at every occurrence in the file — approximately 14 occurrences across the book. This is the first. The apparatus does not comment on the redaction; the apparatus treats it as routine document security.
- Sefton's private notes (B3): the shortest sentences in the book. 4–10 words. No audience. Fragments permitted. Never a paragraph. The drafter must resist the urge to make them longer or more explanatory.
- The destroyed page: referenced in the notes but not present in the file. The apparatus does not comment on its absence — the file contains what it contains.
- Reyes keeps the ingestion information from Sefton. He does not explain why. This is load-bearing: when Sefton discovers her notes are inside in §13, she will not have been warned.
- The chapter-ending note ("I am not going to write it down. Writing it down is how it gets into things.") is Sefton's private note, B3 register. It is the literary-fade close. It must NOT be preceded by an apparatus comment or followed by apparatus commentary — it closes the chapter on its own.
- The build designation being REDACTED in Sefton's own private notes: she has redacted it herself, in her own private notes. Or perhaps Lantern's IT team redacted it when the files were uploaded. The apparatus does not explain. It treats the redaction as received.
- Apparatus tense: tenseless-editorial throughout.
- The banned-statement list applies. The note "Writing it down is how it gets into things" is close to the line — it is Sefton's voice in B3 register, private, not a narrator statement. This is permitted; it is character voice, not apparatus statement.
