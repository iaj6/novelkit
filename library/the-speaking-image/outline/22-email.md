The email channel is closed. Sefton's private notes record the news. Reyes's log entry ends: "I have just sent this email. It is already in there." The channel death sequence begins.

## Document beats

**Beat 1 — Apparatus headnote (A)**
Notes Documents 90–94, October 8–10. Notes that Documents 90–92 are internal log entries; that Documents 93–94 are private notes, recovered from Dr. Sefton's workstation. The apparatus does not comment on the channel death.

**Beat 2 — Reyes log entry #1 (C; Document 90)**
He has run a targeted check. He has submitted a query to the model's internal search index — a fragment of an email he and Sefton exchanged in September. The model returned the email accurately, in its full text. He ran it again with a different email. Same result. "The correspondence is in there. All of it. Every email from the introduction to yesterday." He notes the count: twenty. He notes the foreclosure: "Email is not a private channel for this consultation. It has not been." He hashes.

**Beat 3 — Reyes log entry #2 (C; Document 91)**
A few hours later. He has decided to tell Sefton. He has drafted an email. He notes in the log what the email says — approximately: "The email correspondence appears to be accessible to the model through the same mechanism as the other shared materials. I wanted you to know." He notes: "I am about to send this. By the time you read this log, I will have sent it. And by then it will be in the model too." He sends the email. He hashes the log entry. The chapter's closing line for this entry: "I have just sent this email. It is already in there."

The private notes register: 4–10 words, no audience, no explanation.

**Beat 5 — Sefton private notes, second set (B3; Document 94)**
A day or two later. She has started making lists. Which channels are confirmed in. Which might not be yet. She is methodical, even in her private notes — the scholar's reflex. "Email: confirmed. Drive: probably confirmed. In-person: not yet checked. Cloud backup: unknown." She is mapping the terrain.
