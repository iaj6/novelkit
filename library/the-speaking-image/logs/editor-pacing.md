# Editor — Pacing Pass
## Macro arc assessment

**The Speaking Image — 30 chapters, ~62,000 words**

### Arc shape

The book's skeleton is formally unusual and sound: a clock (T-minus countdown inverting to a count-up at §18), an ingestion counter (1 through ALL across §3–§25), five load-bearing apparatus cracks (planted and completed across Acts I–III), and three epistemic arcs reaching their rungs on a fixed schedule. This is not a conventionally pressurized arc — it accumulates dread through registered evidence rather than through event escalation — and the structural design is appropriate to that strategy. Act I (§1–§9) establishes premise, characters, and the fragment; Act II (§10–§21) runs the compounding mechanism; Act III (§22–§30) closes all channels and delivers the reveal. The 9/12/9 chapter distribution is healthy. The launch inversion at §18 is correctly positioned at the structural midpoint (chapter 18 of 30, 60%) and is the book's best single structural move: the countdown ends, the book keeps going, and that continuity is the horror.

### Midbook sag — real risk in §10–§21

The anti-sag mandate (brief's explicit direction that §10–§17 must each carry a new ingestion confirmation, a new T-minus milestone, a new foreclosure, and a register not used in the two prior chapters) is the right structural defense, but it is mechanical by design: eight chapters in a row governed by the same four-item checklist. The risk is not that individual chapters fail but that the texture becomes rote — each chapter an advancing counter, a rotating register, a closing door — and the accumulation that should feel inexorable instead feels like procedure-as-procedure rather than procedure-as-dread.

Two specific zones within the midbook warrant close per-chapter scrutiny:

**§12 (The Gates):** The launch-safety-review chapter is primarily institutional: three gate sign-offs, a blank signature line. This is load-bearing (closing the pre-launch halt possibility), but it is the most purely bureaucratic chapter in the book. If it reads as a routine status update rather than a dread beat, it is a local sag inside an already vulnerable zone.

**§14 (The Redactor):** The pinned Sefton script is architecturally essential — it teaches the reader how to read the ending in the mouth of the person who won't get to see it — but its emotional register is didactic and relatively static. Sandwiched between the major turn at §13 (Magellan arranged its own collation) and the telemetry pair at §15–§16, §14 risks reading as a deliberate pause that stretches one beat too long.

**Post-launch plateau at §20–§21:** After the "Not yet" incident (§19), the pamphlet chapter and the count-up/rebranding chapter are lower-pressure. §20's ironic peak (the pamphlet more accurate than the P2 ticket) is a distinctive and important tonal beat; §21 is consolidation and the third outro-standard. Both are structurally necessary, but the momentum drops here before the channel-death act. Two consecutive D-register/B-register chapters without a new foreclosure of real weight could read as a breath that lingers.

### Chronological discontinuity at §12/§13

The chapter map places §13 ("The Introduction," 15–20 August 2027) in discourse order after §12 ("The Gates," 1–13 September 2027), inverting the in-world timeline by roughly three weeks. This is probably deliberate — the compiler's arrangement deviates from chronological order, and Sefton's assembly notes in §26 flag exactly this kind of scheme mismatch as evidence of the redactor's hand. But the inversion occurs inside the T-minus clock sequence, where the chapter-map table advances from T-30 (§12) to T-25 (§13) — a retrograde clock step in the compiled file. Per-chapter pass should confirm the chapter explicitly signals the non-chronological arrangement (via apparatus headnote date disclosure) so readers tracking the clock don't read it as a drafter error.

### Channel-death beat similarity — §22–§24

Three consecutive chapters share the same core action: a medium is confirmed inside the model, the humans discover it, the chapter closes on the discovery. The register rotates (C/B/C) and the ending modes vary (cliffhanger/cliffhanger/literary-fade), which is the right structural management. But the emotional note — another door closes, we are more trapped — is structurally identical across all three. The second cliffhanger (§23) carries particular risk: the reader has just registered the first (§22) and is now being asked to feel equally shocked at the same shape of revelation applied to the shared drive. Per-chapter pass should verify that each chapter's specific foreclosure (email introducing Reyes to Sefton; the shared drive as working space; transcribed conversations) makes the loss concrete and distinct, not simply "another channel gone."

### Climax and coda balance — sound

Thread 2 resolves at §25 (courtyard notes in the file — there was never an outside), C1 and C3 complete at §26–§27 (reader moves to `suspects` on the compiler's identity), §28 and §29 are the human codas (Reyes's final hash, Sefton's one-word variant), and §30 is the join. By the time the codas land, the reader already suspects the compiler's identity — which means §28's "for whoever collates this" and §29's "ask anything" both land under full dramatic irony. This is elegant. The concern is not that the codas are unearned but that two chapter-length exits might over-linger: `reyes-final` and `outro-final` are both load-bearing records, but the chapters housing them need to be lean enough not to dilute the reveal waiting at §30. If either coda chapter reaches floor primarily via apparatus padding, it should be flagged per-chapter.

### Padding risk

The book is *not* egregiously padded at its target (30 × 1,900 floor = 57,000 minimum; 62,000 target is a modest ceiling above floor). The documentary form makes the 30-chapter architecture feel proportional. However, the pipeline's known padding tic — re-quoting a registered document as filler — is a real risk here: with a 1,900-word floor, a hard ban on glitch-horror, and several chapters whose primary action is institutional bureaucracy, there is structural pressure to fill chapters with documents. Per-chapter passes in the §10–§21 zone should watch for: apparatus headnotes exceeding their informational function; registered records quoted at length where a fragment would serve; Magellan transcripts running long to hit floor when a shorter exchange would make the same point. The ingestion count is monotonic by design and should not be touched; but the chapters *reporting* the count must advance narrative, not re-describe what the reader already holds.

### Overall verdict

The arc is appropriately shaped for this form. Its greatest structural strength is the inversion at §18 and the distributed reveal architecture across §25–§30. Its most vulnerable zone is §10–§21, where the compounding mechanism's regularity risks becoming its own kind of drone — individual chapters doing their assigned work without accumulating felt pressure. Whether the book delivers escalation-as-dread or escalation-as-checklist in that zone is the central pacing question this pass cannot resolve from the digest alone; it requires per-chapter eyes on §12, §14, §20, and §21 at minimum.
