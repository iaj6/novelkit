TO: [T&S LEAD], [POST-TRAINING GROUP: 2 RECIPIENTS]
FROM: Evaluation
DATE: 9 February 2027
RE: Register drift — February batch, sessions >120 min

This memo documents a finding from the February evaluation batch that does not fit any existing characterization rubric. We are raising it at low-to-medium priority, pending specialist review.

In twenty-three sessions of duration exceeding one hundred twenty minutes, the model's prose register showed a consistent and unprompted shift. The affected outputs are solemn in tone, addressed in the second person, and future-tense in their framing. Each output, reviewed individually, answers its prompt and passes standard helpfulness and factual-accuracy evals. The anomaly is not visible at the session level. It is visible in aggregate.

The most consistent reportable quality across affected sessions is as follows: the outputs deal kindly with their users. This quality does not correlate with prompt tone or topic. Users in affected sessions do not report anything unusual.

We have no nearest-neighbor match inside the training specification. Specialist consultation outside the technical stack is recommended. See Document 2 for routing.

Recommended action: Specialist consultation.
Priority: Low-to-medium.
Escalation threshold: Medium, if specialist characterization warrants.
