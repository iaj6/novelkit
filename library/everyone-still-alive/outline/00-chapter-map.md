# Chapter Map — *Everyone Still Alive*

## Structure at a Glance

- **Total chapters:** 22
- **Total target words:** ~70,000
- **Structural unit:** Vignette (6 vignettes, 3–5 chapters each)
- **Chapters numbered:** continuously 01–22, grouped by vignette
- **POV rotation:** by vignette, not by chapter — one anchor POV per vignette, held throughout
- **Depth-markers:** each vignette opens cold with a timeline depth-marker before the chapter text (e.g., *Week One*, *Month Two*, *Year One, winter*, *Year Two, spring*)

## Book-Level Arc

The arc is the **escalating thesis argument**: each vignette delivers a different answer to "who is the monster?" The answers escalate and complicate each other. The connective threads (the Reader of Names, the carved wooden fish, the thesis question) carry meaning across the hard vignette cuts.

| Vignette | Thesis answer | Arc position |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 — The Drill | The infected are real; the worst act is a person's compassion | **Setup** — the real monster is established; the first human moral event |
| 2 — The Line | People, using fear as license | **Escalation** — the infected are absent; the horror is procedural |
| 3 — The Road South | People, purely — no infected anywhere | **Escalation** — the gradient becomes visible |
| 4 — The Sweep | From inside — the narrator is the monster, and knows | **Dark turn** — the perspective inverts |
| 5 — The Raft | The opposite of monstrous — what humans can build without fear | **Valley / Counterpoint** — the evidence that the opposite is possible |
| 6 — The Derelict | The unknown, and the community turning on itself before it knows | **Climax / Final statement** — the thesis delivered one last time |

The infected gradient enforces the arc structurally: vivid and present in V1 only; absent and only feared in V2, V3, V4; never seen in V5; ambiguous on the Galena in V6. The further from the real monster, the worse people treat each other.

## Chapter Summaries by Vignette

### Vignette 1 — The Drill
*Week One · Heron Falls High School · Jesse Okafor · Fast YA horror*

**01 — The Wrong Sound** *(setup)*
Jesse Okafor and classmate Amara reach the art room as a lockdown announcement cuts off mid-sentence — something on the lower floors is moving wrong, fast, and wrong in the specific way that changes everything.

**02 — Amara's Door** *(escalation / thesis-turn)*
Barricaded in the art room for hours, Amara hears a person crying on the other side of the stairwell door; she opens it; the infected flood the upper floor; Amara does not survive; Jesse and three students retreat to a supply closet.

**03 — The Night and the Names** *(escalation)*
The long night in the supply closet: Jesse carves the wooden fish from a chair-leg splinter while his mind refuses to panic; a classmate's dying phone picks up the Reader of Names; by dawn Jesse has a plan for the service exit.

**04 — Out** *(turn / vignette close)*
Jesse leads the survivors through the basement and out the loading-dock service exit; the fish goes into a coat pocket at the door; they run into cold October air; the school is behind them.

---

### Vignette 2 — The Line
*Month Two · The Narrows Checkpoint, Highway 9 · Daan Selle · Institutional dread*

**05 — The Notebook** *(setup)*
Selle's shift at the Narrows: the checkpoint's rules, the tanker truck, Pruett's authority, and the procedure that Selle has accepted without examining — all rendered through his cataloguing, inventory-taking interior.

**06 — Criteria** *(escalation)*
A harder shift: a man turned back for a cough; Pruett tells Selle he's "good at this" because he doesn't flinch; Selle writes his entry that night and does not think about the man with the cough once the notebook is closed.

**07 — The Fever** *(turn / vignette close)*
A woman with two children arrives; one child has a fever; the protocol flags fever as possible exposure; Pruett decides; Selle enforces; she turns from the checkpoint; Selle writes the entry; the vignette ends on the completed notebook record.

---

### Vignette 3 — The Road South
*Month Six · Highway 41 south · Lenn ("the man") · Literary-bleak / McCarthy register*

**08 — Day Three** *(setup / thread: fish arrives)*
Days 1–3 on Highway 41; the landscape established as indifferent and cold; Theo finds the carved fish in the coat pocket of an abandoned jacket in a roadside house; first near-death encounter — three armed men at a junction take their food and let them pass.

**09 — The Ash Sky** *(escalation / thread: Reader of Names)*
Days 4–5; the smelting plant's ash-sky fills the air; Lenn finds a hand-crank shortwave in a stripped house and hears the Reader of Names while Theo sleeps; second near-death — a farmhouse shooter whose bullet misses Theo by distance.

**10 — The Barn** *(escalation)*
Days 6–10; cold deepens, snow at night; a family already in the barn tries to take their supplies in the dark; Lenn is struck but they escape into the wet cold; Theo asks what those people were afraid of; Lenn doesn't answer.

**11 — Crossing** *(turn / vignette close / thread: fish departs)*
Days 11–13; the river crossing; at the far bank a woman with a loaded cart is heading toward the lake settlements; Theo gives her the fish without explanation; Lenn watches and does not stop him; they walk south; the fish is gone.

---

### Vignette 4 — The Sweep
*Year One, deep winter · The Rim towns · Cass · First person, present tense · Cold perpetrator POV*

**12 — Clearance** *(setup / implication)*
The crew — Cass, Jute, Parish, the kid — sweeps a farmhouse and laundromat using the vocabulary of "clearance"; something is implied but not named about what happens when they find people; Parish talks; the kid watches.

**13 — What Parish Calls It** *(escalation / partial revelation)*
The hardware store: the crew finds someone inside; Jute acts; Parish has cheerful names for what they do; Cass's narration records the kid's face and immediately closes the notation; a crack appears in Cass's flat affect and is covered.

**14 — The Basement** *(turn / vignette close)*
The church: a locked basement is opened; survivors are inside; the full nature of what the crew does is visible though not narrated directly; the kid understands and moves to the basement door; Cass's narration records only what his hands reach for and stops there.

---

### Vignette 5 — The Raft
*Year Two, spring · Lake Ashford south arm · Nadia Ruiz · Warm communal*

**15 — The Log** *(setup / threads: fish on mast, Reader of Names)*
Nadia's logbook opens the raft community: daily rhythms, the greenhouse, the twenty people and their rules, the wooden fish slightly crooked on the central mast; the Reader of Names heard on the shortwave at night; Mikkel present and unremarkable.

**16 — Shore** *(escalation — gentle)*
Nadia joins Tomás's supply run to the lakeshore; the shore shows the world's silence (burn marks, stripped marina); back on the raft a small friction over food allocation resolves through the community's values; Gus and Nadia's unspoken closeness.

**17 — The Last Entry** *(tonal peak / vignette close)*
A near-perfect spring day: Petra's children's class, Tomás singing while he rows, the tomato seedlings transplanted, Mikkel integrated and contributing; the Reader of Names at night; Nadia closes her logbook; the warmth is at its highest point before the dread begins.

---

### Vignette 6 — The Derelict
*Year Two, same spring week · Lake Ashford · Nadia Ruiz (continued) · Slow-dread maritime*

**18 — East Wind** *(setup: Galena sighted)*
Petra spots a dark shape on the eastern horizon at dawn — a 400-foot freighter, engine-dead, drifting on the east wind; Nadia's logbook entry is measured but already snagging; Gus and Tomás reconnoitre by boat; the ship is real, dark, and not changing course.

**19 — The Eastern Shore** *(escalation: Mikkel suspicion begins)*
Day 2 of the ship's approach; someone notes Mikkel arrived from the eastern shore; the murmur begins; Petra argues for loading the small boats and clearing the area; Tomás argues for waiting; Nadia's logbook sentences grow shorter.

**20 — The Vote** *(escalation / Reader of Names / Mikkel expelled)*
Day 3; the Galena fills the eastern horizon; the Reader of Names heard on the shortwave — someone asks if the signal is coming from the ship (unanswered); the community meeting; the vote to give Mikkel the canoe and four days of supplies and send him south; Mikkel goes; Nadia's logbook records what she did not say.

**21 — The Canoe** *(turn)*
Day 4; Mikkel's canoe is spotted drifting empty to the southeast; no body; no way to know; the community goes inside and does not discuss it; Nadia and Gus's last honest exchange before the ship arrives; by nightfall the Galena is close enough that its bulk blocks the eastern stars.

**22 — What We Keep** *(climax / final statement / book's last image)*
Day 5, dawn; the Galena arrives; Gus and Tomás board in the aluminum johnboat; the hold contains a small number of weakened infected sealed behind hatches — manageable; the upper decks hold a shortwave transmitter, no current broadcast (the Reader of Names question is not resolved); the ship drifts past; Nadia writes the last logbook entry; the book ends on her hand against the carved wooden fish nailed slightly crooked to the mast.
