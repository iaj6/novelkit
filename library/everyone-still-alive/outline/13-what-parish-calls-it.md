# Chapter 13 — What Parish Calls It
*Vignette 4: The Sweep · Year One, deep winter · The Rim towns*

## One-Line Summary
The crew reaches the hardware store; they find someone inside; Jute acts; Parish has cheerful names for what the crew does; Cass records the kid's face and closes the notation; a crack appears in Cass's flat affect and is sealed immediately.

## POV and Tense
Cass — first person, present tense.

**Register:** Cold perpetrator POV. The partial revelation: by the end of this chapter, the reader knows what the crew does. Cass's narration does not say it directly — ever — but the chapter makes it visible through the gap between what Cass observes and what Cass chooses to describe. The crack in Cass's affect is one short sentence, then closed.

**Depth-marker:** *Year One. Winter.* (A day or two after Ch 12.)

## Setting / Time
The Rim towns, continuing the circuit. The hardware store in the second town's main commercial block.

## Chapter Shape
Single scene — the hardware store sweep.

## Ending Mode
**Elliptical** — the chapter ends on something Cass notes (the kid's face, or a specific physical detail that implies what has happened without stating it), and the narration moves on or cuts off before completing the thought. The reader holds what Cass refuses to hold.

---

## Beats

1. **Opening:** The second town. The crew on foot, Jute leading. Parish naming the streets — he has named every street in the Rim towns, cheerfully, after things they have found in them. Cass notes the names and notes that this is how Parish makes the circuit legible to himself. The kid walks beside Cass.

2. **The hardware store:** A mid-size hardware and farm-supply on the main block. The door is locked from inside — not just stuck, locked. Jute does not note this as unusual. He assesses it the way he assesses everything: for what it costs to open. He opens it.

3. **What's inside the hardware store:** The front of the store is stripped and dark. The back of the store has supplies the previous searchers did not reach or did not know to look for: hardware (nuts, bolts, wire, chain), seed packets in a metal cabinet, a propane regulator. These are useful. The kid is assigned the back shelves while Jute and Parish go to the storage room.

4. **The storage room:** Parish opens the storage room door. Someone is inside. Cass hears this from the back shelves, where they are cataloguing seed packets — they hear it as a sound (a voice, words, Parish's response). Cass puts the seed packets down.

5. **What Cass sees:** They enter the storage room in time to see the end of what Jute does. This is rendered in one sentence, obliquely — a detail of the room, a detail of what is present that was not present before. The sentence is short. Cass moves on.

6. **Parish:** He is already talking about something else. He has a name for this. He says the name cheerfully, the way he names the streets, as if naming things makes them smaller. Cass notes the name. Does not repeat it. Notes it.

7. **What Cass thinks:** One short sentence that comes close — something that would, in another register, be the full moral acknowledgment. In Cass's voice, it arrives and Cass covers it in the next sentence. The cover is immediate. The crack was there.

8. **The kid:** He is at the back shelves where Cass left him. He had the storage room door in his sightline — not Cass's angle, but his own. His face is a fact that Cass records. The kid does not say anything. He finishes cataloguing the seeds. He puts them in the pack.

9. **Cass's notation on the kid's face:** They note it. They note that the kid is noting something. They close the notation. This is in the text, explicitly — Cass's voice, present tense, brief. *I note his face. I close this.*

10. **The rest of the hardware store:** The crew completes the sweep. The propane regulator, the wire, the chain, the seed packets. Jute inventories. Parish tells a story about his cousin who worked in a hardware store before. The story goes on for three minutes. The story is very cheerful.

11. **Chapter close:** They leave the hardware store. The door closes behind them. Cass notes what it is they have taken. The notation is accurate. The door is closed. The chapter ends on one of these — the notation, or the door, or the kid's face a second time without any additional language around it.

---

## Outcome / Turn
The partial revelation: the reader now understands what "clearance" means. The turn: Cass has a crack in their affect and closes it. The kid has seen something. Parish's cheerful vocabulary is the most disturbing element — he has made the moral horror into a kind of humor, and the humor is the most precise register of how far from any reckoning the crew has traveled.

## New Question / Pressure
What will the kid do with what he understands? How long before the crack in Cass's affect opens and does not close?

## Continuity Notes
- **Partial revelation established:** By the end of this chapter, the reader understands that the crew kills people they find in structures during their sweeps. This is shown through the oblique rendering of the storage room event, Parish's cheerful naming, and Cass's one short sentence of near-acknowledgment.
- **Establishes:** Parish's naming as the crew's psychological mechanism — he names what they do to make it livable.
- **Establishes:** The crack in Cass's affect — one short sentence that almost names it. Immediately covered. This sets up Ch 14's fuller break.
- **Thread: Thesis** — Year One, no infected; the crew has become the thing they were afraid of, and the chapter's formal horror is that Cass is narrating this in present tense without flinching.
- **Note to drafter:** The storage room event must not be rendered directly. It is present as a gap in Cass's narration — a detail before (entering the storage room) and a detail after (leaving it) that the reader assembles. The word for what Jute did is not used. Parish's cheerful name for it is heard but not repeated in the narration. The reader knows.
