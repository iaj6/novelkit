# Chapter 01 — The Wrong Sound
*Vignette 1: The Drill · Week One · Day 1, ~8 a.m.*

## One-Line Summary
Jesse Okafor hears a lockdown alarm that isn't a drill, gets himself and Amara to the third-floor art room with nine people, and barricades the door — something on the lower floors is moving wrong, and by chapter's end it is trying the art room door handle.

## POV and Tense
Jesse Okafor, 15 — close third person, past tense.

**Register:** Fast YA horror. Short declaratives. Sensory and immediate — what Jesse's body knows before his brain catches up. Thoughts arrive as physical impulses: *his legs moved before he decided to move.* Paragraphs close a half-step nearer to danger. The infected are introduced by motion and sound, not description.

## Setting / Time
Heron Falls High School. Day 1, approximately 8 a.m. Jesse's geometry classroom (second floor); the second-floor corridor; the stairwell to the third floor; the art room (third floor, wide windows, ledge outside). Early October — leaves mostly down, pavement cold through sneakers. The hospital three blocks away is audible but not responding.

## Chapter Shape
Single scene — Jesse moves through the school during an unplanned lockdown and arrives at a barricaded room.

**Genre convention:** The infected must be physically present (sound and wrong motion) within the first 300 words. Do not delay. The opening sentence should land in a moment already wrong.

## Ending Mode
**Cliffhanger** — something is trying the art room door handle from outside. The chapter ends before Jesse knows whether it is a person in distress or not.

---

## Beats

1. **Opening (≤300 words):** Jesse is in geometry class, second period. The fire alarm sounds — two short pulses. Not the drill pattern. The PA system opens: Principal Warburg's voice begins an announcement and cuts off mid-word. Jesse looks across the room at Amara. She is already standing.

2. At the end of the second-floor corridor: something that is not moving like a person. Sound first — feet wrong, speed wrong. A silhouette at corridor's end moving fast and at an angle that a person does not move. Jesse's body has already turned before his mind names what he is seeing. The corridor smells of copper and heat.

3. Up, not down. Jesse's instinct is the art room — third floor, reinforced door, chair-rail ledges, high windows, a view of the parking lot and the water tower. He and Amara take the north stairwell. Seven other students follow without being asked. A substitute teacher — Ms. Kwan — is last, dragging the door shut behind her.

4. The art room: nine people inside (Jesse, Amara, seven students, Ms. Kwan). The substitute stacks stools against the door. Her hands shake. Jesse helps because his hands need to be doing something.

5. From the art room's high windows: the school parking lot, ordinary. The water tower of Heron Falls. A car with its driver's door standing open in the lot.

6. From below: sounds that are not people in distress — faster than distress, and rhythmic in the wrong way. Then silence. Then a sound from the floor below that is closer.

7. Coach Briggs's voice, two floors down, giving orders. Crisp, authoritative. What he is ordering is not clear from here; it matters in hindsight.

8. Ms. Kwan tries the classroom's wall phone (hardwired lines are the administrative office, first floor — this is an intercom only). No response. She tries again. Jesse is watching the door.

9. Something — one thing, moving quickly — arrives at the stairwell on the third floor. Heard through the walls: fast, wrong.

10. **Chapter close:** The art room door handle moves. Slowly. Then faster. Stops. Jesse does not move. Amara has her hand on the barricade.

---

## Outcome / Turn
Nine people are barricaded in the art room, alive. The infected have reached the third floor. The thing at the door has not gotten through. The chapter ends before Jesse knows whether it is infected or a student in panic — a distinction that matters enormously and cannot yet be answered.

## New Question / Pressure
Is the thing at the door a person who needs help? Can they afford to find out?

## Continuity Notes
- **Establishes:** Heron Falls High School geography — four stories, second-floor classrooms, third-floor art room (windows overlook parking lot and water tower), north stairwell.
- **Establishes:** Week One depth-marker; the collapse begins in Heron Falls as early as Day 1.
- **Establishes:** Infected move fast and wrong — introduced by silhouette and sound, not description; they are not slow.
- **Cast introduced:** Jesse Okafor (POV), Amara Soyinka, Ms. Kwan (substitute teacher, unnamed in text), seven unnamed students.
- **Coach Briggs:** heard but not seen; his orders from the lower floors will matter in retrospect.
- **World invariant:** "Zombie" is not used in the narration. The infected are referred to obliquely — by movement, by sound, by what they are not.
- **Thesis thread:** The infected are real and present; no human moral failure has occurred yet. The horror is physical and immediate.
