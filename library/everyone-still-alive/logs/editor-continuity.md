## 03-the-night-and-the-names

- **Issue 1 — Fish carving location contradicts canon**
  - **Conflict:** `canon/continuity.md` Part B (Vignette 1) states *"Jesse whittles the fish during the art-room wait."* The draft is unambiguous: in `02-amaras-door.md` Jesse only stares at the splinter during the art-room wait; at the chapter's end he drags the stool into the supply closet. In `03-the-night-and-the-names.md` he carves the fish entirely in the supply closet over the course of the night. The `logs/story-arc.md` entry for ch03 also reads *"Jesse carves the wooden fish through the long night in the supply closet,"* confirming that the draft's intent is the closet, not the art room.
  - **Suggested resolution:** Update `canon/continuity.md` Part B Vignette 1 to read *"Jesse whittles the fish during the overnight vigil in the supply closet"* (or equivalent). The chapter text is correct and internally consistent; the canon phrase predates the full draft.

- **Issue 2 — "Chair-leg splinter" vs. stool (minor, related)**
  - **Conflict:** `canon/continuity.md` and `logs/continuity.md` both describe the fish as made from *"a chair-leg splinter."* The draft in both `02-amaras-door.md` and `03-the-night-and-the-names.md` calls the object a *stool* — specifically a "broken stool" whose cracked leg yields the splinter. A stool and a chair are different furniture items; only a chair has a back. The distinction matters for the future chain-of-custody passages where downstream characters might handle or describe the fish.
  - **Suggested resolution:** Update the canon/log references from *"chair-leg splinter"* to *"stool-leg splinter"* to match the draft's consistent usage. Low stakes for downstream chapters unless V3 or V5 explicitly name the source object.

## 04-out

- **Issue:** Cafeteria placed on the first floor, contradicting canon's "basement cafeteria"
- **Conflict:** `canon/continuity.md` Part B (Vignette 1) states *"basement cafeteria (two exits including a loading-dock door)."* `draft/03-the-night-and-the-names.md` reinforces this when Jesse explicitly maps the route: *"The basement had the cafeteria. Near the cafeteria was the service exit."* In `draft/04-out.md`, however, Jesse's descent stops three times: (1) the second-floor landing (infected sounds, kept going); (2) the first floor, where *"the door opened onto the corridor that ran toward the cafeteria"* and Jesse sees *"the double doors at the far end of the cafeteria hallway"* before deciding not to enter; (3) a separate set of *"basement stairs"* leading down to the loading-dock corridor. This structure puts the cafeteria doors at ground-floor level, with the loading dock below it — the inverse of the canon layout. The offending passage begins *"At the first floor he stopped again"* and ends *"He turned away from them. He didn't go toward them. / The basement stairs."*
- **Suggested resolution:** Restructure the descent so the cafeteria encounter happens at the basement level, not the first-floor landing. The simplest approach: the first-floor stop becomes a brief, uneventful check (nothing detected; Jesse keeps going). Jesse then descends the remaining flight, and the cafeteria-door moment — Briggs in his mind, the choice not to enter — plays out in the basement corridor before Jesse reaches the loading dock. This also resolves a minor tension in the phrase *"Last night Coach Briggs had been down here"* (spoken while Jesse is on the first floor, which reads awkward; it reads naturally once Jesse himself is in the basement). The overall scene rhythm and voice do not need to change; only the floor label and the placement of two paragraphs shift.

The chain-of-custody meaning is unchanged; only the hand that pulls it from the pocket differs.
