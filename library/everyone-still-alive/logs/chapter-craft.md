
## 01-the-wrong-sound
**Ending mode:** cliffhanger
**Opening texture:** set-piece
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Short declarative clusters as base rhythm — three to five short sentences in sequence, often terminal: 'He did not move. Amara did not move. In the room behind them, nine people held still and said nothing.'
- Body-knowledge before cognition throughout: Jesse's legs move, his body turns, before he names what he sees — interiority arrives as physical impulse, not language
- Hard-cut section breaks (---) marking spatial thresholds rather than time gaps — each break is a new room entered, not a scene change
- Infected introduced exclusively through motion data and atmospheric sense: wrong-angled gait, too-fast speed, copper/heat smell — no clinical description, no explanatory annotation after any signal
- Refusal of explanation: every infected detail lands and is left standing — no sentence follows to gloss what the reader just received
**Recurring constructions:**
- `going on and on without anyone answering`
- `He did not move`
- `Nothing went through`
**POV register:**
- **Jesse Okafor:** Sensory-first, action-driven — fear managed through physical work (counting, barricading, watching). Interior life shows in what he does, not what he says about what he feels. No self-commentary. Thoughts arrive as physical events: body turns, legs move, hands reach for the table. Short declarative access to his interiority: 'He held onto that for a moment. Then he let it go.'
**Craft notes:** Register held firmly to fast YA horror throughout — sentence average near 12 words, no subordinate sprawl, section breaks pace spatial transitions.
