# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-the-wrong-sound** — Jesse Okafor hears a wrong alarm, gets himself and eight others to the third-floor art room, barricades the door, and waits as something tries the handle from outside.
- **02-amaras-door** — Amara opens the stairwell door because someone is crying — the most human possible choice — and does not survive it; Jesse retreats to the supply closet with three others.
- **03-the-night-and-the-names** — Jesse carves the wooden fish through the long night in the supply closet; the Reader of Names is heard once on a dying phone and cuts off mid-name; by predawn Jesse has a plan and something is in the hall.
- **04-out** — Jesse leads the three survivors down the south stairwell and out through the service exit; he tucks the carved fish into a grey wool peacoat pocket near the loading dock and runs into the cold October morning without looking back.
- **05-the-notebook** — The Narrows checkpoint is established: Private Selle runs a cold procedural shift, approves two crossings, turns back a young man who will not show his hands, and closes the notebook having noted nothing wrong.
- **06-criteria** — Selle runs a harder shift — three crossings, one turned back for a cough plus borderline temperature — and accepts Pruett's praise for not flinching without examining what it costs.
- **07-the-fever** — A woman with two children is turned back because her three-year-old has a fever; Selle enforces, the woman says "you know that," and the vignette closes on the completed notebook entry.
- **08-day-three** — Lenn and Theo walk Highway 41 south on Day Three; Theo finds the carved wooden fish in an abandoned coat pocket; three armed men take their food at a junction; Theo asks "But not us" and Lenn has no answer.
- **09-the-ash-sky** — Lenn hears the Reader of Names alone at midnight on a found shortwave; a farmhouse shooter nearly kills Theo on Day 5; they shelter at nightfall with the ash-sky thinning south.
- **10-the-barn** — Lenn and Theo shelter in a barn; a hostile son tries to take their pack in the dark; they escape into wet snow; Theo asks what those people were afraid of and the man does not answer.
- **11-crossing** — Theo gives the carved wooden fish to a stranger heading toward Lake Ashford's settlements; the man watches and does not stop him; they walk south and the fish is gone.
- **12-clearance** — Cass, Jute, Parish, and the kid sweep a farmhouse and a laundromat in the Rim towns; the vocabulary of "clearance" acquires its full meaning without being stated.
- **13-what-parish-calls-it** — The crew sweeps the second town's hardware store; someone is found inside; Jute acts; Parish has a cheerful word for it; Cass has a crack in one sentence and seals it; the kid sees everything.
- **14-the-basement** — The crew opens a church basement and finds living survivors, including children; the kid locks the crew out with their own tool; Cass's narration stops at Jute's hands.
- **15-the-log** — Nadia's logbook opens the raft's twenty-person community through one spring day: the morning count, the greenhouse, shared work, a small friction resolved at dinner, and the Reader of Names heard at night.
- **16-shore** — Nadia joins Tomás's supply run to the lakeshore, finds burn marks she cannot explain, returns to watch the community resolve a spinach dispute through its own rule, and sits with a blank logbook page at evening.
- **17-the-last-entry** — Nadia's logbook captures a full spring day at peak warmth — the killdeer named, Mikkel integrated, the array at capacity — and closes with the fish on the mast, the community written as evidence, and Vignette 5's arc complete.
- **18-east-wind** — Petra spots the Galena on the eastern horizon at pre-dawn; Gus calculates three to five days before it reaches the raft; the community reconnoitres and agrees to watch — and feels in control.
- **19-the-eastern-shore** — The Galena closes to two days out; Petra and Tomás stake opposing positions; a community member states plainly that Mikkel came from the eastern shore, and the fact stays in the air.
- **20-the-vote** — Day 3: the Reader of Names is heard at dawn and the Galena-connection question goes unanswered; the community votes Mikkel south in the canoe; Nadia's logbook records what she did not say.
- **21-the-canoe** — Mikkel's canoe drifts back empty; the community stays by default; Nadia loses her language; by nightfall the Galena fills the eastern sky.
- **22-what-we-keep** — The Galena arrives and is boarded; its sealed holds hold slow movement, its radio room holds a transmitter on the Reader of Names frequency; the ship drifts west; Nadia writes the last logbook entry and puts her hand on the fish.
