## 04-out.md

**Borderline register drift — lyrical construction in a pause beat (writer's judgment needed)**

Near the end of the parking-lot section, the sky description reads:

> "the sky above the treeline was beginning to pale at the edges — not light yet, but the suggestion of it, something returning that had seemed like it might not."

The closing clause — *"something returning that had seemed like it might not"* — is a complex subordinate construction with embedded negation and a slightly abstract quality ("something returning"). It leans toward Vignette 3's literary-bleak rhythm rather than Vignette 1's short declarative register. It also does real thematic work (the light as survival image, echoing the fish), which is why this wasn't fixed in-text. A writer's call: if the thematic freight justifies the register stretch at this pause beat, leave it. If not, something like "not light yet, but coming" holds the register tighter.

### Fix 2 — Single word: `surrender` → `exposure` (register: inventory vs. meditation)

Changed to `exposure`. One word.

## 08-day-three.md

**Analytical framing in the smell passage — possible register drift**

The paragraph introducing the smelting plant leans on a cognitive-analytical construction that sits at the edge of the McCarthy register:

> "The man was assembling a partial memory of this smell — not the exact smell but its general family, its range of possible sources — and when the smelting plant appeared on the right side of the road at around two in the afternoon the assembly was complete and he understood."

Three elements read as clinical/interior rather than action-and-silence: "assembling a partial memory," "its range of possible sources" (categorisation language), and "the assembly was complete." This trio frames Lenn's olfactory cognition as a diagnostic process — consistent with the chemistry teacher Lenn is, but more explicitly interior than the register permits. The style guide says feeling (and here, perception) lives in what the man does, not in the narrated mechanics of how he thinks. McCarthy would more likely arrive at the plant and have the man understand without narrating the arriving.

This cannot be fixed at the word or clause level without rewriting the sentence; it needs a judgment call about how much of Lenn's analytical register is meant to bleed through the narrator's voice vs. where the McCarthy contract requires silence. If the bleed is intentional and this is meant to be the one place it shows, a small label — perhaps cutting "not the exact smell but its general family, its range of possible sources" to reduce the inventory quality — might be sufficient. But that's a compression-adjacent cut and should be the writer's choice.

*(Two voice fixes were applied directly to the file: the self-glossing clause on the rifles sentence was removed, and "at a different frequency than the sun had sent it" was replaced with "wrong.")*

## 11-crossing.md

**Vignette 3 — The Road South**

Three word/clause fixes applied directly:

1. `"the way you carry things that may not hold up to examination"` → `"the way a man carries things that may not hold up to examination"` — casual second-person "you" is off-register for the McCarthy cadence; the vignette's register consistently uses "the man" as the default subject, and generic "you" imports a contemporary literary-fiction texture.

2. `"looked at what it actually was"` → `"looked at what it was"` — "actually" as a colloquial intensifier is slightly anachronistic for the register; McCarthy doesn't reach for it.

3. `"He did not know how to go further with this distinction and he did not try"` → `"He did not know how to go further with it and he did not try"` — "this distinction" names and categories the conceptual move the man just made, which is the kind of analytical self-awareness the register prohibits. "With it" preserves the sense without the meta-label.

**A. Attribution tags at the crossing dialogue**

The style guide specifies indent-only, unattributed dialogue for Vignette 3. The chapter uses `"The woman with the cart said:"` (an explicit prose tag) and `"And then:"` (a theatrical connective between two speech acts from the same speaker). Both drift from the register's unattributed pattern. With four strangers at the crossing, some attribution may be practically necessary, but the current tags are more pronounced than the McCarthy register warrants — the register more typically handles speaker clarity through context and line separation, not prose tags. Writer judgment on whether to pull the tags or accept them as a necessary concession to the multi-speaker scene.

Offending lines:

**B. Interior meta-commentary on emotional categorization**

The prohibition for Vignette 3 is explicit: "No interior explanation of feeling — feeling lives in what the man does with his hands, what he eats, how fast or slowly he walks." The clause `"because what he could not name this time was not the feeling he was accustomed to not naming"` has the man silently categorizing his emotional states in comparative terms — identifying this unnamed feeling as a *different kind* of unnamed feeling from his usual unnamed feeling. That is interior emotional taxonomy, not action-as-feeling. The compression pass left the full paragraph intact; this clause is the voice-level residue. Cannot be fixed at the word/clause level without removing the sentence — writer's call on whether to collapse the clause or let it stand.

## 16-shore.md

**Apparent internal contradiction in the closing beat — author's judgment needed.**

At the chapter's close, Nadia sits with the open logbook and the text reads:

> *She had the notes: burn marks on the shore, what the marina gave them, the spring birds in the hardware store, Mikkel's rack, the spinach question and its resolution, the particular sound the community had made when Marta said she liked spinach. She had not written any of it.*

But the chapter previously shows she wrote most of these things:
- Burn marks → written in small notebook on shore, then explicitly transcribed to the logbook ("She had barely finished transcribing the notebook into the logbook when the spinach question arrived.")
- Marina inventory → same small notebook, same transcription pass
- Mikkel's rack → written in the logbook verbatim: "*Mikkel: drying rack in greenhouse upper air, excess heat. Gus to build. Spinach preservable for weeks. This is what it looks like when a skill finds its moment — no ceremony, just the offer and the yes.*"
- Spinach question + Marta → written in the logbook verbatim: "*Spinach peak. Commons question. Rule applied. Both parties; resolved. Marta on record.*"

The only item in the list she *didn't* write down is the spring birds in the hardware store (she put the small notebook away and did not reopen it there).

"She had not written any of it" as a flat statement contradicts the chapter's earlier actions. This could be:

(a) **A continuity error** — she demonstrably did write these things.

(b) **Intentional ambiguity** — meaning she hasn't written the *lived resonance* of the day, only its factual record; "any of it" gestures at the gap between documentary record and genuine witness, which is Nadia's central tension. If this reading is intended, a small clarification at the word level would close the contradiction: e.g., *"She had not written any of it right"* or *"She had not written the felt part of it"* or even *"She had not written what she actually meant."*

This touches directly on Nadia's core voice — her documenting impulse and its limits are her defining register — so the ambiguity matters. Compression has already cleared the chapter; this is the one item that needs authorial intent to resolve. Not fixable from outside.
