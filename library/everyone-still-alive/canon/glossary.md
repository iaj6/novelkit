# Glossary

*Alphabetical. Vignette scope noted in brackets where relevant.*

---

**Amara Soyinka** [V1] — Jesse Okafor's closest friend; age 16; opens a locked stairwell door for a crying person on the other side, letting the infected into the upper floors. She does not survive. Not a villain — the book's first proof of its thesis.

**Ashford River** [V2] — The river spanned by the Narrows checkpoint on Highway 9. Runs fast in November; carries ice. Twenty meters below the bridge deck.

**Briggs, Coach** [V1] — PE teacher at Heron Falls High School; age 44. Locks students in the cafeteria and refuses window signals during the lockdown. Survives.

**Cass** [V4] — First-person narrator of *The Sweep* (Vignette 4); scavenger crew member; age approximately 30; gender unspecified throughout. Name appears once. Joined the crew seven months before the vignette.

**Crane, Lenn** [V3] — Father in *The Road South* (Vignette 3); age 37; former chemistry teacher; close third POV, often referred to as "the man." Walking south with his son Theo.

**Galena, the** [V6] — A 400-foot lake freighter; engine-dead; drifting on the east wind toward the raft. Appears on the horizon in Vignette 6. What it carries is the book's final revelation.

**Gus Pereira** [V5/V6] — Age 54; raft engineer and systems maintainer; Nadia's unspoken companion. Under pressure in Vignette 6, proposes solutions the community did not think it would need.

**Halse, Petra** [V5/V6] — Age 27; teaches the raft's four children; arrived from a refugee column alone. First to spot the Galena. Becomes the argument for leaving.

**Heron Falls** [V1] — Mid-size lake-country town; site of Vignette 1. Home of Heron Falls High School, a water tower, a main street.

**Heron Falls High School** [V1] — Four-story cinderblock school; basement cafeteria (two exits); third-floor art room (window ledge); first-floor administrative office (hardwired phone). Setting for Vignette 1.

**Highway 9** [V2] — The road carrying the Narrows checkpoint. Runs east-west; the bridge over the Ashford River is the control point.

**Highway 41** [V3] — The southbound corridor walked by Lenn and Theo in Vignette 3. Passes through emptied towns and the ash-sky of a burned smelting plant.

**Jesse Okafor** [V1] — Age 15, sophomore, POV for Vignette 1. Carves the wooden fish during the school lockdown. Gets survivors out through the service exit. Leaves the fish in a coat pocket.

**Jute** [V4] — De facto leader of the scavenger crew in Vignette 4. Compact, efficient, has stopped apologizing.

**The Kid** [V4] — Unnamed; age 17–18; newest member of the Vignette 4 crew. Has not fully understood what the crew does. Understands by the end. What he does with that understanding is the vignette's final beat.

**Lake Ashford** [V5/V6] — Large inland lake; the setting for Vignettes 5 and 6. The raft is anchored on its south arm. Wide enough that the far shore is not always visible. Freighter-scale.

**Mikkel** [V6] — Most recent arrival at the raft community; three weeks before the Galena appears; former marine engine mechanic; came from the eastern shore. No connection to the Galena. Becomes the focal point of community suspicion.

**The Narrows** [V2] — The bridge checkpoint on Highway 9 at the Ashford River; Vignette 2 setting. Unofficial name for REGIONAL TRANSIT CONTROL, HIGHWAY 9.

**Nadia Ruiz** [V5/V6] — Age 62; former high school librarian; POV for Vignettes 5 and 6. Keeps the raft's logbook. Defines herself by insisting that beauty and care remain possible. The book's one returning consciousness.

**Parish** [V4] — Scavenger crew member, Vignette 4. Talks constantly; the chatter is the most disturbing thing in the vignette.

**Pruett, Sergeant Cutter** [V2] — Age 39; former county sheriff's deputy; commands the Narrows checkpoint. Not cruel by temperament — fear has become policy.

**The Raft** — The floating community platform on Lake Ashford's south arm. Assembled from dock sections and a houseboat hull over two winters. Carries twenty people in Vignettes 5 and 6.

**The Reader of Names** — A pirate radio voice, low and measured, of indeterminate gender, that broadcasts each night and reads aloud the names of the confirmed dead. Heard in Vignettes 1, 3, 5, and 6. Identity and purpose are never resolved.

**The Rim Towns** [V4] — A ring of emptied small lake-country towns swept by the Vignette 4 scavenger crew. They blur together; no single one is named.

**Selle, Private Daan** [V2] — Age 21; former ecology student; POV for Vignette 2. Enforces the Narrows checkpoint rules. Trying to remain a reasonable person. Losing.

**Theo** [V3] — Age 9; Lenn's son. Finds the carved wooden fish in a roadside house coat pocket (Day 3, Vignette 3). Gives it to a stranger heading toward the lake settlements at a river crossing near the vignette's end.

**Tomás** [V5/V6] — Age 35; Nadia's logbook assistant and confidant; rows supply runs. First to say aloud that the community must decide what to do before the Galena arrives.

**Wooden fish, the carved** — A small fish, palm-sized, whittled by Jesse Okafor from a chair-leg splinter during the Vignette 1 lockdown. Chain of custody: school service-exit coat pocket → roadside house coat pocket (found by Lenn/Theo, V3, Day 3) → Theo gives it to a south-bound stranger at a river crossing → arrives at the raft by unnarrated hands → nailed to the raft's central mast (V5/V6). The book's physical proof of shared humanity across time.

**Warburg, Principal** — [V1] Principal of Heron Falls High School. His PA announcement is cut off mid-sentence on Day 1 of Week One. He is heard, not seen, in Chapter 1.

**Haas, Mr.** — [V1] Geometry teacher at Heron Falls High School; Jesse's second-period teacher on Day 1. Keeps chalking when the alarm sounds.

**Ms. Kwan (the substitute)** — [V1] Substitute teacher assigned to room 211 at Heron Falls High School on Day 1. Organized under pressure; moves stools before anyone asks, tries the wall phone methodically. Named in outline as Ms. Kwan but referred to only as 'the substitute' in the prose. She is the adult in the art room with the nine survivors.

**Heron Falls General Hospital** — [V1] Hospital three blocks east of Heron Falls High School. Its fixed electronic siren ran continuously and unanswered starting Day 1 of Week One, audible from the school's third-floor art room windows.

**Supply closet (art room)** — The interior supply closet off the Heron Falls High School art room (third floor). Approximately 4–5 feet wide. Metal shelves with paint cans; canvas rolls against the back wall. Smells of turpentine and old clay. Jesse and three unnamed students take shelter here at the end of Ch 02. The broken stool — source of the fish-splinter — is initially propped against this door.

**The broken stool** — A cracked art room stool in the corner of the third-floor art room, leaned against the supply closet door. One leg is cracked with a pale fibrous grain-split; a 2-inch pale wood splinter juts from the break. Jesse observes it throughout Ch 02 without touching it. In Ch 03, he uses the splinter to carve the wooden fish during the supply closet wait.

**Jesse's father's folding knife** — A folding knife Jesse Okafor has carried in his pocket for three years, inherited or borrowed from his father. Used to carve the wooden fish in the supply closet on Day 1 night / Day 2 predawn. Not a weapon — a utility tool. First named in Ch03.

**Reader of Names (first V1 appearance)** — The pirate radio broadcast heard for the first time in V1, Ch03, on a dying phone in the supply closet. Voice: low, measured, indeterminate gender. Reads names of the confirmed dead one at a time without commentary. First six names heard: Michael Voss, Renata Poole, Carla Tran, Antoine Reyes, Simone Hendricks, Diane Var— (cut off mid-name at 0% battery). Subsequent appearances: Vignettes 3, 5, 6 on a hand-crank shortwave and the raft's working set.

**South stairwell (Heron Falls High School)** — The stairwell at the south end of the third-floor corridor, running from the third floor to the basement. Jesse's planned escape route in Ch03: south stairwell → basement → service exit (loading dock, south side of building). Condition at Ch03 close: unknown — something is in the south corridor hall.

**Michael Voss** — Name read by the Reader of Names in its first V1 appearance (Ch03). One of the confirmed dead. First name on the broadcast heard by Jesse and the three students in the supply closet.

**Renata Poole** — Name read by the Reader of Names in its first V1 appearance (Ch03). One of the confirmed dead.

**Carla Tran** — Name read by the Reader of Names in its first V1 appearance (Ch03). One of the confirmed dead.

**Antoine Reyes** — Name read by the Reader of Names in its first V1 appearance (Ch03). One of the confirmed dead.

**Simone Hendricks** — Name read by the Reader of Names in its first V1 appearance (Ch03). One of the confirmed dead.

**Diane Var—** — Partial name read by the Reader of Names in its first V1 appearance (Ch03) — cut off mid-surname when the phone battery dies at 0%. The incomplete name is the broadcast's final audible word.

**Service exit (Heron Falls High School)** — A heavy steel door with a push bar at the south end of the school basement / loading dock area. The escape route used by Jesse and the three survivors in Ch04. Near the door: a metal coat rack with winter coats (including the grey wool peacoat that holds the carved fish) and a plank shelf with a soup can, first-aid kit, and dead flashlight.

**Grey wool peacoat (the fish's first resting place)** — A heavy grey wool peacoat hanging on the coat rack near the school's service exit in the basement. Jesse tucks the carved wooden fish into its pocket during the escape in Ch04. It is the fish's first resting place in its chain of custody across the book. Owner unknown. Jesse does not look at it after placing the fish.

**Private Daan Selle** — POV character, Vignette 2 (The Line). Age 21, former second-year ecology student. Volunteered at the Narrows checkpoint in the first weeks; still there two months later with a rifle and a spiral notebook of rules. His interiority is bureaucratic — he notes, catalogs, and defers conclusions. He is losing the project of remaining a reasonable person one small compliance at a time.

**Sergeant Cutter Pruett** — Commands the Narrows checkpoint, Vignette 2. Age 39, former county sheriff's deputy. Not cruel by temperament — genuinely frightened, and fear has become policy. Wrote the five rules governing the checkpoint. Trusts Selle because Selle does not challenge the rules, which Pruett reads as agreement. He is wrong about what that trust means.

**The green spiral notebook** — Vignette 2's central object. Found on the Narrows bridge in Week One of the collapse. Front two-thirds: Pruett's operational notes and the five checkpoint rules. Back third: Selle's shift logs. Divided by red electrical tape. The chapter 'The Notebook' is named for it.

**Hendricks** — The overnight watch at the Narrows checkpoint (Vignette 2). Posted at the western end of the bridge with a thermal imager. Reports to Pruett. Observed the two overnight river-crossing attempts on the night before Chapter 05's shift.

**Copley** — A small town east of the Narrows checkpoint (Vignette 2 geography). Stated origin of the farmer who crosses west in Chapter 05.

**Larkin** — A city to the west of the Narrows checkpoint (Vignette 2 geography). Destination of the farmer who crosses in Chapter 05 (his sister's place). Equivalent to a mid-size lake-country city.

**Minna** — A town approximately 18 km west of the Narrows checkpoint (Vignette 2 geography). Destination of the bicycle woman who crosses in Chapter 05.

**Dawes Road** — A rural road or area southeast of the Narrows checkpoint (Vignette 2 geography). Where the bicycle woman's mother's house is located; her stated origin in Chapter 05.

**Devlin** — Checkpoint personnel at the Narrows (Vignette 2). Runs the overnight kiosk log during night shifts while Hendricks holds the thermal-imager watch position on the bridge. One of two additional personnel beyond Pruett and Selle. Introduced in Ch06.

**Caraway** — A village or town west of the Narrows checkpoint on Highway 9. A family of three crossed heading there in Ch06 (V2). Added to the map of named western destinations.

**Brennan** — A small town east of the Narrows checkpoint. Stated origin of the man with the cough turned back in Ch06 (V2).

**Caltrough** — An area east of the Narrows checkpoint. Stated origin of the woman traveler approved in Ch06 (V2).

**Wenden settlement** — A settlement approximately 30 km west of the Narrows checkpoint. Not previously known to Selle; stated destination of the woman traveler approved in Ch06 (V2). Named in the crossing log without further information.

**secondary-review protocol** — A checkpoint procedure called for in the Narrows operational guidance when a traveler presents respiratory symptoms combined with any elevated temperature. Requires an observation period before a crossing decision. No functional protocol exists for this at the Narrows checkpoint as of Month Two — there is no facility, no waiting area, no procedure. The gap between the guidance and the available protocol is load-bearing in Ch06-07 (V2).

**don't flinch** — Pruett's stated criterion for effective checkpoint performance, articulated to Selle in Ch06 after a day of hard calls. The unofficial virtue standard of the Narrows checkpoint. 'You don't flinch. Most people... flinch. Doesn't change the outcome; the rule is the rule. But there's a cost to the flinch.'

**Marta Solak** — Woman turned back at the Narrows checkpoint in Ch07 (Vignette 2). Traveling west with two small children from the dissolved Camp Rydell toward her sister's house west of Minna. Her younger child (F, approx. 3) had a fever of 38.7°C from a reported respiratory illness, exceeding the checkpoint's 38.5°C threshold. Turned back by Pruett per the applicable protocol. Her name appears in the notebook entry but not in the narrative prose. Her fate after she walks east is not shown.

**Camp Rydell** — An evacuation camp approximately 12 km east of the Narrows checkpoint, in the watershed area near Copley. Officially dissolved approximately two weeks before Ch07's events (mid-November, Month Two). Its dissolution is the source of the increased traveler traffic the checkpoint has been processing. Physicians were present at the camp before its dissolution.

**Lenn** — POV character of Vignette 3 (The Road South). Age 37, former high school chemistry teacher (not stated in prose). Walking south with his son Theo on Highway 41, Month Six. Referred to as 'the man' in narration; his name appears sparingly. Inner life surfaces through action and silence, not interior monologue.

**Theo** — Lenn's son, age 9. POV-adjacent character in Vignette 3. Walks behind and to the right of his father. Collects things from the road: a broken watch (stopped at 11:04), a blue bird book, plastic bags. Finds the carved wooden fish on Day 3 in a roadside house coat closet. Carries it forward.

**Highway 41** — The southbound corridor Lenn and Theo walk in Vignette 3 (Month Six). Two lanes, faded centerline, intermittently salted. Passes through emptied towns, a gas station with a hole in the roof, a collapsed smelting plant (right/west side, Day 3), and a T-junction where the first near-death encounter occurs.

**The smelting plant (V3)** — A burned industrial site on the right (west) side of Highway 41, encountered on Day 3 of the walk. Processing shed mostly standing; east wall collapsed. Burned in Month Four of the collapse. Source of the ash-sky: fine residual particulate that thickens the air for two or more days of walking distance south.

**The ash-sky (V3)** — A brownish thickening of the air from the smelting plant's residual particulate. Begins Day 3 of the walk south. Makes the sky gray-brown to copper-orange at evening; thins sunlight. Persists through at least Days 4-5 per outline.

**The junction (V3)** — A T-junction where a county road crosses Highway 41 from the east, Day 3 afternoon. Site of the first near-death encounter: three men with a pickup truck (cracked windshield) and two rifles take Lenn's pack (four days' food) and let them pass.

**Theo's bird book** — A small blue-covered field guide to birds, pages swelled from rain, illustrations in pen and gray wash (vireo, nuthatch, cedar waxwing, ovenbird above its domed nest). Found on the road by Theo in Days 1-2 of the walk. Carried throughout Vignette 3.

**Theo's stopped watch** — A wristwatch with a broken crystal, hands stopped at eleven minutes past four. Worn on Theo's wrist throughout Vignette 3. He wears it knowing the time is wrong.

**The unnamed tributary (Highway 41 bridge crossing)** — A fast-moving tributary of the larger river system crossed by Lenn and Theo on Day 13 of Month Six, via a concrete bridge span on Highway 41. Not the Ashford River. The site where Theo gives the carved wooden fish to the woman with the cart heading toward Lake Ashford settlements. The bridge is functional, approximately 100 meters long, with ice in the current.

**The woman with the cart (unnamed, V3/V5 connective figure)** — An unnamed woman encountered at the tributary bridge crossing in Ch11. Traveling northeast with a companion toward Lake Ashford lake settlements, pulling a cart loaded with tools, jars, a tarp, and a sleeping bag. She receives the carved wooden fish from Theo at the crossing and puts it in her coat pocket. Her path to the raft is not narrated; she is the unnarrated link between V3 and V5 (Thread T2). Must not be named or given backstory in any subsequent chapter.

**the Rim towns** — A circuit of emptied lake-country small towns worked systematically by Cass's crew in Vignette 4 (Year One, deep winter). No single proper name; they blur deliberately. Features include farmhouses on the outskirts and a main-street commercial block (hardware store, pharmacy, diner, laundromat). Three miles south of the first Rim town is the crew's camp farmstead.

**Dry Cleaner's, the** — Parish's crew designation for the laundromat on the main street of the first Rim town (Vignette 4). Folded laundry still on machines; two deep-winter coats taken by Jute. The nickname appears in Cass's Day 84 log entry.

**Jute** — De facto crew leader in Vignette 4 (The Sweep). Compact, efficient. Works without commentary or apology. Manages pack weight, inventory, and take-counting. Described Cass's new kid member as having 'aptitude' on recruitment.

**Parish** — Crew member in Vignette 4 (The Sweep). Talks constantly in a cheerful, case-study register. Gives locations crew nicknames. His cheer is the most disturbing quality in the vignette because it is entirely ordinary.

**the kid (V4)** — Unnamed crew member in Vignette 4, approximately 17-18 years old. Joined the crew one month before Chapter 12. Assigned attic sweeps. Still in the process of understanding what the crew's work fully entails; his reckoning is the vignette's final beat (Ch14). Has aptitude per Jute's assessment.

**Cass** — First-person narrator and POV of Vignette 4 (The Sweep). Age approximately 30. Gender unspecified; prose avoids pronouns and does not invite the question. Precise in physical description, evasive in moral description. Keeps the crew log. Name appears in the log header ('CASS. Day [number].') and once in Parish's dialogue per chapter.

**clearance (crew vocabulary)** — In Vignette 4, 'clear a structure' / 'cleared' is crew vocabulary that has acquired meanings beyond its original sense (searching and securing). In Cass's log, 'cleared' appears in the same syntactic slot as 'no take' and 'prior entry,' absorbing its full implications into the log's flat register.

**Receipt Street** — [V4] Parish's crew-designation for the main commercial block of the second Rim town. Named because Parish found a grocery receipt (items illegible, total torn) on the sidewalk during the first sweep of the town. Now called 'Receipt' for short.

**Hammer Street** — [V4] Parish's crew-designation for the third side street off the main commercial block in the second Rim town. Named for a ball-peen hammer (rubber grip, good condition) found in the gutter outside the abandoned barbershop during a prior sweep.

**Propane Street** — [V4] Parish's crew-designation for the first side street off the main commercial block in the second Rim town. Named for an empty but serviceable propane tank found behind a house there.

**Jam Street** — [V4] Parish's crew-designation for the second side street off the main commercial block in the second Rim town. Named for three jars of preserves found in a kitchen pantry during a prior sweep — Parish calls it a very good day.

**the Driveway** — [V4] Parish's crew-designation for the fourth side street off the main commercial block in the second Rim town. Has no notable find; does not earn a better name.

**Meridel** — A town mentioned by Parish in Chapter 13 as the location where his cousin worked in a hardware store before the collapse. Referenced once in Parish's story about his cousin's fastener knowledge; not otherwise established in the world.

**Church (Rim towns)** — The final structure on the Vignette 4 crew's circuit: a white clapboard church with a community hall extension to the right, a cemetery visible through the gap between the two buildings (stone markers, one metal cross), and a rear hall with a padlocked door concealing a basement where survivors have been living for weeks. Location of the vignette's closing scene.

**Marta** — Age 7; one of the raft's four children, enrolled in Petra Halse's morning class. Her family's history is not specified in the logbook. First appears Ch15.

**Luka** — Age 9; one of the raft's four children; brother of Dani. Both brothers arrived on the raft together. First appears Ch15.

**Dani** — Age 6; one of the raft's four children; younger brother of Luka. First appears Ch15.

**Feodora** — Age 11; one of the raft's four children; oldest of the group and technically too old for Petra's fourth-grade class, but sits with the younger children because they need her. First appears Ch15.

**Raúl** — Community member on the raft; stationed at the cooking station on the port side alongside Simone. No other identifying information given. First appears Ch15.

**Simone** — Community member on the raft; at the cooking station with Raúl; developing herb oil in small batches on the camp stove. First appears Ch15.

**GHP** — Nadia's logbook shorthand for Gus Pereira (Gustavo Heitor Pereira, or simply her notation convention). First appears Ch15.

**duplicate-receipt book** — The supply-list format Tomás uses for johnboat run planning — a real duplicate-receipt book with the columns adapted into a grocery list. First appears Ch15.

**north township** — Vaguely named community origin of three young men who arrived on the raft together. Not further specified. Used in Ch15 only as a place of origin.

**Recreation marina (Lake Ashford south arm)** — The raft community's regular supply-run landing point, approximately a quarter mile south of the main town on the lakeshore. Dock half-collapsed and listing east; boat storage empty; marina shop gutted. A hardware store is one block inland. Introduced Ch16.

**Small notebook (Nadia's field notebook)** — A separate, smaller notebook Nadia carries on supply runs to the shore when the main logbook stays on the raft. More abbreviated in style; notes transcribed into the logbook on return. Distinct from the main logbook. Introduced Ch16.

**Dry-preservation rack (greenhouse)** — A drying rack proposed by Mikkel and accepted by the community in Ch16, to be built by Gus Pereira in the greenhouse's upper air using excess heat near the roof panels. Intended to preserve spinach for weeks rather than days. Under construction as of Ch16.

**Killdeer (Charadrius vociferus)** — A shorebird common to the northern lake country, brown above and white below with two distinctive black breast bands and a piercing 'kill-dee' call. Returns early to the region in spring. First identified on the raft by Dani (V5, Ch17); named and entered in Nadia's logbook on Day 415.

**Ilario** — One of the three north-township young men on the raft; the least talkative of the three. Named in Ch17 (Day 415). Present in Vignette 5 and continuing into Vignette 6 as a background community member.

**Yolanda Ferreira** — A woman from a settlement approximately two hundred miles south of Lake Ashford, connected to a community member on the raft. Her name was noted in Nadia's logbook (autumn entry) after a community member mentioned her. Read by the Reader of Names on the night of Day 415 (Ch17); the connected community member was asleep and did not hear it. Nadia recorded the name.

**Day One (Galena approach sub-count)** — [V6] Nadia Ruiz's secondary day-counter, begun in the logbook on Day 416 to track the Galena's approach. Parallel to the main logbook count (which continues from Day 415). The reader follows Nadia's Galena-approach count (Day One through the vignette's end) as the primary marker of elapsed time in Vignette 6.

**Benedetto** — [V5/V6] One of the three north-township young men on the raft. One of the first people to come out to the eastern railing on Day One after the Galena sighting. Less prominent than Ilario; speaks once in the Day One discussion to ask about repositioning the raft.

**GALENA (name on hull)** — [V6] The name painted at the bow of the derelict ship in white lettering on dark hull. Flaking at the edges but legible. Read by Nadia Ruiz through binoculars from the raft's mast during the Day One recon; confirmed by Gus Pereira.

**Benedetto** — A background member of the raft community. Present from Vignette 5 onward. In Ch19 (Day Two), he is the community member who states, while looking through binoculars at the Galena, that Mikkel came from the eastern shore. The statement is made without accusation, as a fact observed aloud.

**second johnboat (motor)** — The raft community's second aluminum johnboat, equipped with a small motor. The motor has been running roughly since before Mikkel's arrival. In Ch19, Mikkel diagnoses a cracked carburetor float as the likely cause and reports that a fix from the spare-parts bin may be possible by the following morning.

**Mikkel Iversen** — Full name of the raft's most recent arrival in Vignette 6. A former marine engine mechanic who came from the eastern shore of Lake Ashford three weeks before the Galena appeared. Admitted under the community's standing rules. Voted out on Day Three by a 15-4 community vote; given the canoe and four days of supplies and told to travel south. His last name is first used in Nadia's formal logbook entry recording the vote. He stated once: 'I have no connection to that ship.'

**What I did not say** — A section in Nadia's running logbook account on Day Three, after Mikkel's departure. A short list recording Nadia's silence during the community meeting: the evidence she did not cite, the argument she did not make, the vote she did not record. Written in logbook voice. Ends: 'I am writing this because if I do not write it no one will.'

**Jacob's ladder (Galena)** — A rope or metal-rung ladder on the Galena's port midship side, hanging to within a few feet of the water. Used by Gus Pereira and Tomás to board the ship on Day Five. The ladder angle became unsafe as the ship's drift continued during the boarding.

**Galena radio room** — A dry room in the Galena's superstructure containing a shortwave transmitter more powerful than the raft's. Ship's power dead; transmitter intact. Frequency dial set to the Reader of Names broadcast frequency. Whether the Galena was the Reader of Names' transmission source is deliberately unresolved.

**Galena lower-deck hatches** — Hatches accessing the Galena's lower holds. Dogs latched from the corridor side (outside), not from below. Someone sealed them from outside before the ship went dark. Slow, irregular sounds audible behind them on Day Five; three or four sources, alive but diminished by cold. Gus and Tomás did not open them.
