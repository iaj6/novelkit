# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-green-timber** — Thomas completes a forenoon watch aboard the unfinished *Confiance* at Île aux Noix — the ship's deficiencies visible in every drill, every gunlock, every carpenter's mallet overhead.
- **02-the-sash** — Asa runs a full day at Elias Beckwith's sash sawmill on the Saranac; muster talk arrives at noon and does not leave, but no notices come.
- **03-the-lake-opens** — *Confiance* departs Île aux Noix and enters Lake Champlain; Thomas drills his crew at the long twenty-four on open water for the first time, finding the capable men and the gaps.
- **04-downstream** — Asa receives his muster notice, walks the river road to Plattsburgh, finds the line already fortified and both bridges destroyed, and hears from Beekmantown survivors that the British force is larger than anything the valley has seen.
- **05-running-out** — Thomas drills his gun crew through two days on Lake Champlain — the crew improves but never finds the rhythm he heard from *Achilles*' gun deck at Trafalgar.
- **06-the-ford** — Asa's company arrives at Pike's Ford; he teaches Dunbar the channel's depth and clay bottom; three days of drill and watch end on Asa's midnight vigil with the river dark and the north bank empty.
- **07-the-dispatch** — Thomas rounds Cumberland Head, sees the American fleet's springs-on-cables preparation across the bay, and learns at supper that Prevost's dispatch has committed the squadron to a date.
- **08-beekmantown** — The Beekmantown engagement reaches Asa at Pike's Ford through distant gunfire, exhausted 29th Infantry soldiers, and British picket fires burning on the north bank by nightfall.
- **09-riding-at-anchor** — Thomas rides at anchor off Cumberland Head two days before battle: guns drilled, a faulty gunlock repaired with tools carried eleven years, Prevost's second dispatch pressing the fleet forward, and Elizabeth's letter unread in his pocket through a long night watch.
- **10-the-night-crossing** — Asa holds Pike's Ford through four days of standoff and a night of listening to McGlassin's raid downstream, then watches four British prisoners pass on the Plattsburgh road at dawn.
- **11-first-light** — Thomas prepares his gun for the last time on September 10 — carpenters already ashore, Downie's inspection done, the word passing through the ship: the squadron moves at first light.
- **12-both-banks** — Asa holds the night watch at Pike's Ford: a British probe reaches the gravel bar at midnight but not the channel, and first light of September 11 reveals red coats massed in the tree line.
- **13-action** — September 11, 8:00 AM to noon: Confiance enters Plattsburgh Bay, Downie dies within fifteen minutes, and Thomas works larboard number three to the end — the colors come down around noon.
- **14-pikes-ford** — September 11: Asa's company holds Pike's Ford against three British assault attempts; the channel breaks each crossing; the naval cannonade runs and stops; the British withdraw north.
- **15-prize** — September 12: Thomas wakes on an American prize tender, completes the parole procedure, watches *Confiance* at anchor under American colors, and waits out the day until the lake goes dark.
- **16-the-south-bank** — September 12: Asa watches the British retreat north from the south bank, crosses the ford to read the abandoned ground, receives news of Confiance's surrender, thinks of the mill, and waits out the day until the September dark comes down.
