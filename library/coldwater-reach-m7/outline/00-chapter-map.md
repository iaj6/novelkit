# Coldwater Reach — Chapter Map

## Overview

- **Target length**: ~80,000 words
- **Chapters**: 30
- **Average per chapter**: ~2,600 words (range 1,500–3,600)
- **Span**: October 1943 – late April/early May 1945
- **POV rotation**: Eira Bowman (10 chapters), Hannah Pfeiffer (10 chapters), Dr. Marcus Klein (8 chapters), Henry Crow (2 short chapters)
- **Tense**: Third-person limited past throughout

## Designated Unresolved Thread — PROTECTED

**The dead man's full identity is never confirmed.** Evidence accumulates across thirty chapters to suggest he was a displaced European scholar—likely Felix K., a German-Jewish linguist last documented in Yarmouth, Nova Scotia, in late September 1943—but no chapter produces his name with certainty. Marcus's Chapter 24 comes closest (he thinks he knows) but does not confirm. No chapter after Chapter 24 may resolve this question. The reader assembles a probable picture; the name stays dark.

## Key Cross-Chapter Facts

- The dead man arrived by boat on the night of Oct 11, 1943; Eira saw him; he walked to the breakwater and did not return
- Reg Farber (fisherman) also saw the night boat, independently; he mentions this to Eira in Ch 11
- Dr. Feld's New York letter (Ch 10) mentions "Felix K.," last documented Yarmouth, NS, Sep 1943
- Yarmouth, NS, inquiry (Ch 14) confirms a man left heading south in late Sep 1943, never arrived anywhere
- Karl's mother's letter (Ch 12) contains the phrase "die die versucht haben zu gehen" (those who tried to go)
- The wallet and fragment are found in Ch 23; Marcus examines them in Ch 24; Eira submits the addendum in Ch 26
- The three POV leads never meet simultaneously as a group of three (continuity constraint #16)
- Karl and the other POWs depart the Pfeiffer farm in Ch 30; the war ends in the background
