**Ending mode:** turn
**Opening texture:** routine-procedure
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Short declarative triplets to establish procedure before any event arrives (tide clock, kettle, log entry)

- The tide clock as structural frame: four minutes fast at open (before reset), four minutes fast at close (never reset) — the unperformed routine as chapter's closing image
**Recurring constructions:**
- `not from here`
- `she had not reset it`
- `she wrote what she had seen and what had been done`
**POV register:**
- **eira-bowman:** Pure procedural. No interior monologue; no named feelings. Sentences average 14-16 words; short declaratives (2-4 in sequence) dominate action beats. Emotional content arrives only through precision: specific numbers, log notation, the clock, the gap in the ledger. The peacoat established without sentiment. Ennis's investigation rendered through her observation of his looking, not her interpretation of it.
**Craft notes:** The chapter deliberately withholds Eira's 11 pm observation at every level — no interior flash, no physical tell, no hesitation the reader can name. The reader registers the gap from the negative space: she writes the facts the record required, she does not write what she saw. The tide clock (never reset) is the chapter's only allowed affect: one routine that didn't happen, structurally symmetric across open and close. Later chapters must not fill the gap too early; Ch 07 is the first full glimpse. The body's city clothes (double-breasted overcoat, leather city shoes) are placed here as forensic observation without implication — Eira notes them; she does not theorize from them.
