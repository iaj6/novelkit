Cut: *"the practice of extracting meaning from fragmentary texts was not incidental to his training but central to it, and the habit persisted across genres."* (semicolon changed to period after "twenty-three years")
Reason: The reader does not need an explanation of what a philologist does. The reconstruction that immediately follows ("The body on the breakwater: no papers, no name, not from here.") demonstrates the skill; the label annotates what the demonstration already shows.

**[4] Lillian's glance — confirmatory gloss**

Reason: "Not a look of doubt" is sufficient. The second clause explains what the first already rendered.

Reason: The preceding analysis of Henry's practiced discipline already shows why it's unusual. The sentence labels the conclusion; the paragraph earns it. "He was still in the process of learning it" (retained) carries more than the explicit assessment.

**[6] Shortwave acquisition — reasoning explained**
Cut: *"The reasoning had been straightforward enough: there were things happening in Europe that he required to know about, and the distance between him and Europe was now such that knowing about them required a particular kind of machinery and persistence."*
Reason: Whole sentence. The reader understands from context — a German academic, exiled, in wartime — why a man would spend money he needed on a shortwave. The explanation annotates what the reader already infers from "money he had needed for other things and had spent on this instead."

**[7] Tuning knob — "which was patience"**
Cut: *", which was patience"* (from "something he had too much time to develop, which was patience")
Reason: Names what "something he had too much time to develop" leaves productively oblique. The naming reduces the weight.

**[8] BBC motif — significance qualified**
Cut: *"he would not have been able to reduce to mere preference or habit"* (from "a significance he would not have been able to reduce to mere preference or habit")
Reason: "A significance" is the landing point; the qualifier describes the significance without adding to it. The word standing alone is stronger.

**[9] Listening to the broadcast — annotation of method**
Cut: *"— noting what was said and what was not said with equal precision, the gaps in a broadcast being, in his professional experience, as informative as the content, sometimes more."*
Reason: "The full attention he gave to news from Europe" is the image. The long appositive explains what that attention consists of — which is what the previous philologist observation already established. The explanation does the philologist's work twice.

**[10] Self-knowledge sentence**
Cut: *"He was aware of not knowing."* (standalone sentence following "He did not know why the man on the breakwater interested him with this particular quality of attention.")
Reason: Pure meta-commentary on cognition. "He did not know" is already the fact. "He was aware of not knowing" labels his relationship to the fact — the definition of the failure mode.

**[11] Pursuit — "which was a thing he had also learned"**
Cut: *", which was a thing he had also learned"* (from "because the pursuit would not help, which was a thing he had also learned")
Reason: The clause names the lesson after "because the pursuit would not help" already states it. The lesson is in the "because"; the label adds nothing.
