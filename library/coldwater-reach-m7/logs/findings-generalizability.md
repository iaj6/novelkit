# Generalizability Brief: Do Coldwater Findings Abstract to a Generic Pipeline?

*Panel of 6 archetype editors (thriller, comic, maximalist, YA/romance, fantasy/SF, autofiction) + adversarial skeptic. Abstraction score = #universal + 0.5·#conditional, out of 6.*

## 1. Verdict

Yes — but unevenly, and the two cleanest wins are the ones that have nothing to do with this book. The findings split into a hard core that abstracts fully and a soft shell that abstracts only as a *mechanism* once you strip off the magnitude and the register. The two **method** findings (F8 measurement noise-floor, F9 cross-path convergence) are unanimous (6/6 universal each) — they are pipeline epistemics, not Coldwater literary findings, and they are the safest things to build on. One structural craft finding (F7, template reuse) is near-universal (5/6). The remaining craft findings (F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6) abstract as a *mechanism* but not as a *prescription*: every one carries a "knob" that a different archetype would set differently, and three of them flip into a *feature* for at least one archetype. The honest read: the **direction** of each craft finding generalizes; the **shape and severity of the fix** is register-conditional. The single most important caveat from the skeptic is that the most seductive finding (F4, "restraint is the risk axis") is partly *falsified by Coldwater's own data* (Eira is restrained AND the most reliable register) and is the highest overfit risk in the set.

## 3. The Reversal Cases

These are the sharpest evidence of what is Coldwater-specific, because an archetype rated the finding **not-applicable or reversed it into a feature**:

- **F2 reversed by comic (NA):** Coldwater treats the boundary coda as over-resolution to be cut; for comic voice the section-end is *exactly* where the button/punchline lives. The same model behavior is a defect in one register and the comedic payoff in another.
- **F6 reversed by thriller (NA):** Coldwater frames sagging momentum as a tradeoff *inherited from* an interiority-heavy brief. The thriller brief selects the opposite register, so the causal story inverts — pace is the native strength, not the casualty. You cannot learn "the pipeline sacrifices momentum" from a book that *commissioned* momentum sacrificed.
- **F5 not-applicable to autofiction (NA):** Multi-voice distinctness is a multi-POV virtue; single-POV autofiction wants one sustained consciousness and neither exercises nor benefits from it.
- **F4's internal reversal (skeptic, not panel):** Coldwater's *own data* falsifies the clean claim — Eira's register is ALSO "withhold, do not name" (grief only through log entries) yet is the book's MOST reliable register (7.95). Restraint is therefore *not* the risk axis; the real variable is whether the withholding has a concrete displacement object to carry the unsaid.
- **F3 near-reversal:** thriller/comic/maximalist all note that narrated cognition is *commissioned core technique* in their register — penalizing it edges toward scoring the model down for obeying the brief, "just less artfully."

## 4. The Load-Bearing Generalization

**Measure your own disagreement before you trust any score, and trust a pattern only when two paths with uncorrelated failure modes both flag it.** (F8 + F9, the only 6/6-unanimous findings, both register-independent by construction.)

Stated for a generic pipeline: derive a per-fleet noise floor from inter-rater mean-absolute-disagreement, treat any score-gap smaller than the floor as noise rather than signal, grep-verify every cited quote to eliminate fabricated evidence, and escalate a craft pattern to "real" only when an independent deterministic/positional audit and an independent semantic craft critic — paths that fail in *different* ways — converge on it. This is the one principle that owes nothing to register, genre, or this book; every other finding is a craft observation whose fix must be parameterized.

## 5. What NOT To Carry Over (Skeptic's Highest Overfit Risks)

- **F4 — HIGH (the most seductive, the most wrong).** Do NOT adopt "restraint-defined registers are the variance/risk axis." It is induced from n=1 restraint mode (Hannah) and *contradicted within the same book* by Eira (restrained AND most reliable). The actionable truth is narrower: the model fails where withholding lacks a **concrete displacement object**, not where the brief says "withhold." Do not steer briefs away from restraint.
- **F6 — HIGH (a specified outcome mistaken for a discovered tradeoff).** Do NOT conclude "interiority briefs are pace-risky, steer toward plot." Low momentum was the *commissioned* outcome (~80k words of quiet interior literary fiction, "the war ends in the background"). The only kernel that might survive is **non-compensation**: the model doesn't deploy craft tricks (compression, scene-cutting, white space) to defend pace within a low-velocity constraint.
- **F2 — MEDIUM.** Do NOT bake a blanket "cut the last paragraph" rule. The *direction* (models resolve at boundaries) generalizes; whether it's a defect is brief-conditional — a hook-driven genre wants the closing beat.
- **F3 — MEDIUM.** Do NOT generalize the *breadth*. This is largely a Marcus/Hannah-register artifact ("a man who continues to translate his thinking" was *commissioned*, then penalized). Defensible claim is narrow: models default to *labeling* interior states rather than externalizing them.
- **F5 — MEDIUM (positive-finding trap).** Do NOT declare "voice is solved." Distinctness here is *easy-mode*: registers separated by gross lexical/topical handles (nautical vocab, untranslated German, academic syntax) that a blind judge tags partly by *topic*. The hard case — voices differing only in cadence/stance while sharing diction — is untested, and the three registers share the same failure modes, suggesting distinctness is a surface layer over a common default.
- **F1 — LOW (mechanism safe, magnitude not).** Stake the roadmap on "add a subtractive pass," NOT on the tell-count (~90/30ch), which is brief- and register-specific and partly measures "this brief defined glosses as defects."
- **F7 — LOW.** Mechanism robust; only the *visibility* is amplified by this brief's leitmotif-heavy design.
- **F8/F9 — LOW.** Re-derive the floor per fleet (don't import 0.43); watch for rater non-independence (shared model/rubric inflates agreement); confirm the two paths weren't both aimed by the same failure-mode list before calling their agreement "independent."

## 6. Pipeline Implications (Generic, Parameterized — not Coldwater-only fixes)

1. **A subtractive pass with a register-keyed scalpel (F1 + F3, generalized).** Add a distinct pass whose *default verb is delete*, explicitly licensed to remove text — because "improve/polish" prompts have an additive prior and will not cut. Parameterize the target: it deletes **flat interpretive/gloss clauses** (image-then-tell-the-reader-how-to-feel; flat un-elaborated cognition labels) while a `protect` list shields register-legitimate elaboration (comic digression, maximalist accretion, YA name-the-feeling, performed/recursive cognition). Default verb fixed; target set per-brief.

2. **A recurring-entity render tracker (F7, generalized).** Maintain explicit state of the *first full render* of every recurring location/object/character; downgrade later mentions to fragments rather than re-instantiating from the same latent scaffold. Parameterize the *granularity*: place/object scaffolds for location-heavy briefs, sentence-level tic vocabulary for location-light/autofiction briefs (per the autofiction dissent on F7).

3. **A register-conditional boundary-trim, not a blanket coda-cut (F2, generalized).** Flag the over-resolving beat one past the natural end at every section/chapter boundary, but gate the *action* on a brief parameter: `boundary_register ∈ {withhold | hook | button}`. Withhold-briefs cut the coda; hook-briefs (YA/thriller) *preserve* it as a cliffhanger; button-briefs (comic) preserve it as the payoff. The detector is universal; the verdict is parameterized.

4. **A "concrete-vehicle-for-the-unsaid" check on restrained registers (F4 re-derived, replacing the overfit version).** For any register the brief defines by withholding/restraint, require a concrete displacement object (a log, a clock, a pump — something the model can point a camera at) to carry the unsaid; warn when a restrained register is *abstract* (sensory accumulation with no procedural object), since that — not restraint per se — is where the model reaches for the abstract label. This is the actionable, non-falsified form of F4. (Pair with a `compensation` check from F6's kernel: when a brief selects a low-velocity register, require evidence of craft-level pace management rather than letting chapters run under word-target with slack middles.)
