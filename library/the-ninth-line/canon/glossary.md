# Glossary — The Ninth Line

Terms, names, places, and objects. Alphabetical. This file will grow as drafting proceeds; later phases append entries here.

---

**Adidași** — smoked pig feet; nicknamed "adidas" because of the stripes on the rind. A common protein substitute in 1984 rationing conditions.

**AFE (Intreprinderea de Fibre Artificiale)** — the Artificial Fiber Enterprise at Dej. The dominant employer; approximately 1,500 workers. Produces celofiber (cellulose-based synthetic fiber used in downstream textile manufacturing). Two shifts. The administrative building houses the bookkeeper's office, the personnel section, and the factory physician.

**Ana Petrescu** — 19 years old at death; died 4 November 1984 at the Spitalul Orășenesc Dej. Never a POV character.

**Bloc** — standard communist-era apartment block. Also a surveillance unit: building superintendent (administrator de bloc), bloc committee, stairwell lights on a timer.

**Buletin (buletin de identitate)** — communist-era identity booklet. Eight pages; photograph, occupation, Miliția registration stamps. Required to be carried at all times. Issued and renewed by the Miliția.

**Cartelă** — ration card. Issued monthly or quarterly; non-transferable. In 1984: 300 g bread/day, 500 g meat/month, 8–12 eggs/month, 100 g butter, 1 litre oil, 1 kg sugar, 500 g cheese.

**Celofiber** — regenerated cellulose fiber (similar to rayon) produced at the AFE. Hot-wet production process; the enterprise has a distinctive chemical smell, especially in cold weather when steam sits in the valley fog.

**Contabil-șef** — chief accountant at a state enterprise. Responsible for all ledgers, required to produce records on demand for state auditors. Ilinca Roșca's title.

**copy-n4** — Ilinca's cellar copy; second generation; made 3 November 1983. Lines 1–8 identical to N1; bracket line transcribed as numbered line 9. Registered record. The poisoned ancestor.

**copy-n11** — the regularised copy; fourth generation; from N4. Line 9 reads: `9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.` Registered record. The fatal mutation.

**copy-n14** — Doina's copy; fourth generation; from N3. Still has the bracket. Clean. Registered record.

**copy-n17** — Ana's copy; fifth generation; from N11. Identical to copy-n11. Registered record.

**Dej** — municipality in Cluj County, Transylvania. ~36,000 residents in 1984. Located at the confluence of the Someșul Mic and Someșul Mare, 60 km north of Cluj-Napoca. Home of the AFE and associated textile production. Site of the Spitalul Orășenesc.

**Decree 770** — Ceaușescu's decree of October 1966 banning abortion and contraception. In force throughout the book's 1984 setting. Enforced through mandatory monthly gynecological examinations, physician reporting obligations, Securitate hospital surveillance, and a network of approximately 450,000 informants.

**Doina Marcu** — 19, night-shift mill worker. POV character, first person past. Holds copy N14 (clean). 

**Dosar (penal)** — (criminal) file; the unit of a Miliția investigation. The basic administrative object of Munteanu's work.

**Ilinca Roșca** — 41, contabil-șef at the textile mill. POV character, third person present. Wrote the master (N1) in October 1983.

**Inspectoratul Județean de Miliție** — the county-level Miliția office, Cluj-Napoca. Munteanu's case escalates here if unresolved within 31 days of Ana's death.

**Locotenent** — Lieutenant; Munteanu's rank. Lower-middle of the Miliția officer hierarchy.

**master-n1** — the canonical master document. Registered record. Eight numbered lines plus a bracketed ninth in the margin. Written by Ilinca, October 1983. The document the book is about.

**Miliția** — Romania's civilian police force, under the Interior Ministry. Territorial, Railroad, and Prison branches. Each district office has a Population Section, Investigations Section, and Information Office.

**Munteanu** — see *Vasile Munteanu*

**Nechezol** — the coffee substitute: chicory, chickpeas, and barley. Nobody likes it.

**Procuror** — state prosecutor; involved from the outset in classifying suspicious deaths; oversees the dosar penal in parallel with the Miliția's Investigations Section.

**Securitate (DSS — Departamentul Securității Statului)** — the state security apparatus. Distinct from the Miliția; handles political intelligence. ~11,000–13,000 full agents; ~450,000 informants nationwide by the mid-1980s. In Cluj County: 54 doctors recruited as informants. Agents stationed in hospitals. Co-opts Miliția investigations when they acquire political significance.

**Someș** — the river at Dej; the Someșul Mic and Someșul Mare join 4 km upstream at the village of Mica. The combined river runs through the town at 285 m elevation. Fog collects in the valley in November.

**Spitalul Orășenesc Dej** — the district hospital at Dej. Where Ana Petrescu dies on 4 November 1984.

**Tovarăș / Tovarășă** — Comrade (m./f.); official form of address in institutional and party contexts.

**Vasile Munteanu** — 34, Miliția lieutenant, Investigations Section, Dej district. POV character, third person past. Assigned Ana's death as an administrative matter; turns it into a real investigation. His collation is arithmetically correct. He never understands what the ninth line means.

**Ilinca Roșca** — Chief bookkeeper (contabil-șef) at the Dej textile mill; 41 years old; lived alone on the third floor of a bloc on Strada Avântului; wrote N1 in October 1983; POV 1 (close third, present tense).

**Mill administrative building** — The administrative wing of the Dej textile mill, separate from the production floor; shares a heating pipe with the weaving hall; contains Ilinca’s office and the ledger shelf where N1 is hidden.

**Ledger (1983)** — Standard Ministry of Finance four-hundred-page form-book; holds Ilinca’s mill accounts for 1983; N1 is hidden folded in the spine; shelved on the second shelf between the quarterly labour plan compliance records and the annual balance submission; binding retaped February 1984.

**Strada Avântului** — Street in Dej where Ilinca’s apartment building (bloc) stands; her flat is on the third floor, approximately fifteen minutes' walk from the mill gate.

**Strada Crișan** — Street in Dej; a coal cellar off a building on this street is where Ilinca made N4, the third copy, on November 3, 1983.

**Strada Someșului** — The river road in Dej leading down to the Someș bridge; Ilinca walks it on her route home from the mill.

**Domnu Boteanu** — Accountant on the second floor of the mill administrative building; minor character; consistently the last to leave before Ilinca.

**Doina Marcu** — 19-year-old night-shift worker at the AFE mill in Dej; POV 2 (first person past). Holds N14 (clean, bracket intact) in her factory locker inside a geography book. Received the paper from an unnamed woman on the bus from Gherla approximately eleven months before November 1984. Never used the paper, never showed it to anyone. Lives in the AFE workers' hostel, second floor.

**geography book (the)** — A 1978 geography of northern Transylvania, purchased by Doina's mother. Kept in Doina's factory locker since she arrived at the mill. N14 is stored between pages 34 and 35 (Northern Carpathians watershed drainage diagram). Never opened by Doina until November 5, 1984.

**woman on the bus (the)** — Never named, never found, never appears. Gave Doina N14 on the bus from Gherla to Dej approximately eleven months before November 1984. Doina's silence about her identity is the source of Doina's danger throughout the book.

**AFE workers' hostel** — Workers' residential hostel for AFE mill employees in Dej. Doina Marcu lives on the second floor. Door latches can be lifted from outside.

**Georgeta Marin** — Production supervisor for Hall B at the AFE mill, Dej. Holds N2 — the copy Ilinca made the third week of November 1983. Known to Ilinca for eleven years. Carries her keys on a lanyard tucked into her left palm.

**Livia** — Georgeta Marin's neighbor on her street; holds a G3 copy descended from N2. A private woman who has not passed it further. Surname unknown to Ilinca. Seen by Ilinca approximately three times in eleven years.

**Elena Florescu** — Third-shift worker at the AFE mill, Dej; holds N3, one of Ilinca's direct copies. Known to Ilinca for eleven years. They cross paths at the bakery on Strada Lungă (Elena's route to the morning handover) — 14 exchanges in 3 years counted by Ilinca.

**bakery on Strada Lungă** — Bakery in Dej on Strada Lungă; on Elena Florescu's route to the morning shift handover (05:30) and Ilinca's route home on certain days. A recurring contact point between the two women.

**stat de plată** — Monthly payroll document; the nominal payroll form each enterprise submits, listing name, labour category, days worked, deductions, and net for every worker on the administrative and technical rolls.

**Felix 5010** — Adding machine model in use at the AFE mill administrative office; original equipment from 1972; adds and subtracts only, keeps running total on paper strip.

**Vasile Munteanu** — Miliția lieutenant, Investigations Section, Dej district. Age 34; seven years in the district. Assigned the Petrescu death as an administrative matter on November 6 and pursues it as a real investigation. POV character: third-person past. His desk is second from the window in the investigations room.

**Rodica Petrescu** — Ana Petrescu's sister-in-law; wife of Ana's eldest brother. Living at Strada Gării 41 since spring 1984. Stayed with Ana through the night of November 3–4; did not take her to the hospital until the evening of the 4th because she did not know what to say on arrival. Gave N17 to Munteanu.

**Vera (neighbor)** — Neighbor of Rodica Petrescu; lives in the next bloc on Strada Gării, approximately 100 meters from number 41. Approximately 45. Source of N17 (gave it to Rodica); holds N19 (her own copy, stored in second kitchen drawer from the left). Named in the dosar from Day 4 onward.

**Sandu** — Investigator in Munteanu's office at the Dej district Miliția station. Sits at the far desk. Minor presence.

**N19 (copy-n19)** — G5 copy on the fatal branch, descended from N11. Held by Vera; collected by Munteanu on November 7. Line 9: 'If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.' Line 3: '...the paper beside the bed.' (differs from N17's 'by the bed').

**Petrescu dosar** — The criminal file on Ana Petrescu's death, opened by Munteanu. Assigned November 6 as an administrative determination; Munteanu treats it as a real investigation from the first field day.

**Strada Gării** — Street in Dej where the Petrescu house (number 41) is located. Also the address of Vera's bloc (next bloc, approximately 100 meters away). Two blocks from the Miliția district station.

**dosar penal** — Criminal file (Romanian). The official case folder held by the Investigations Section of the Miliția. Opening a dosar penal places the named individuals under formal investigation.

**Marta (Ilinca's cousin)** — Day-shift worker in hall A at the AFE mill; lives on Bulevardul Victoriei, second floor; Ilinca's cousin since childhood; no connection to the document network. Used as an unknowing intermediary to reach Elena Florescu in Ch05. Has an eleven-year-old daughter who attends afternoon school shift.

**Bulevardul Victoriei** — Street in Dej where Marta (Ilinca's cousin) lives; Elena Florescu lives in the adjacent bloc on the same street.

**Luiza** — Doina Marcu's former hostel roommate. Transferred to the Cluj plant in September 1984, leaving Doina alone in room 14 of the AFE workers' hostel.

**locksmith's shop (Doina's street)** — The locksmith's shop on Doina's street, outside which Munteanu (the man in the good coat) is seen questioning two production-floor women on November 9 (Day 6).

**Pântelimon** — Factory committee secretary at the AFE mill administrative building, Dej. A minor figure who sits at a desk near the entrance and notices things; mentions them in open rooms in a way that invites reciprocity. In the Romanian context, entirely ordinary. First appears Ch07.

**supplies corridor** — A corridor in the mill administrative building where Georgeta Marin conducts her monthly inventory and where Ilinca meets her for the materials-discrepancy reconciliation. A legitimate working space that provides cover for brief private exchange.

**Lucia Gal** — Day-shift worker, second weaving hall, AFE textile mill, Dej. Holder of N11 — the G4 regularised copy, written in blue pencil on the back of a grey AFE mill production form. She received her copy from a production-floor colleague (the N9 holder) and gave N11 to Munteanu on 11 November 1984. She denies further transmission. She worked at the mill since 1972.

**Captain Niculescu** — Munteanu's superior officer at the Dej Miliția district station. Wants the Petrescu dosar 'writable' — closed and sent to the county archive. On November 7 he asked Munteanu when to expect a closure; Munteanu said end of week.

**N11 (copy-n11)** — The regularised G4 copy on the poisoned branch, from N9. Written in blue pencil on the back of a grey AFE mill production form, by Lucia Gal. Line 9 reads: '9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.' — this is the fatal regularisation: the bracketed override 'Do not wait for the morning' has been absorbed into the sequence as an instruction to wait until morning. N11 is the source of N17 and N19.

**Mill workers' market** — Market near the AFE mill district in Dej. Lucia Gal's bloc on Alecsandri street is approximately 300 metres east of it.

**Alecsandri street bloc** — Three-story yellow-plaster bloc on Alecsandri street, Dej, near the mill workers' market. Lucia Gal lives on the third floor.

**Stela Ionescu** — Personnel-section worker in the AFE mill administrative building, six doors down the corridor from Ilinca's office. A quiet woman with flat shoes; known to Ilinca by proximity over eleven years, nothing more. On Day 9 she brings a maintenance-request countersignature and, sideways, delivers the exact wording of the mutant line nine — relayed from her bloc-committee friend who was questioned by the investigator that morning.

**N1 (first full printing)** — The master document, first printed in full in chapter 09-the-bracket. All subsequent chapters may quote only fragments of N1, except ch28 (the climax, fourth and final full printing alongside N4). Canonical text with eight numbered lines and the bracketed ninth.

**Marta (bloc/section worker)** — Night-shift worker at the AFE mill, Dej; works the loom adjacent to Doina Marcu's station; lives on the fourth floor of Doina's workers' hostel bloc; has a son in the fourth form and a husband who drives for the transport depot. Distinct from Ilinca's cousin Marta (day-shift, Hall A, Bulevardul Victoriei). Introduced Ch10; delivers the information about Floarea and Geta to Doina.

**Floarea** — Finishing-section worker, AFE mill, Dej. Taken in for questioning on Monday 12 November 1984 (Day 9) along with Geta. Known to Doina only by sight from the morning bus (six months; blue coat on cold days). Minor character — exists to show the investigation's outer ring reaching the factory floor social space. First appears Ch10.

**Geta** — Finishing-section worker, AFE mill, Dej. Taken in for questioning on Monday 12 November 1984 (Day 9) along with Floarea. Minor character; name only in Ch10. Do not develop further unless the outline requires it.

**Cellar woman (unnamed)** — The woman for whom Ilinca made N4 on 3 November 1983 in a coal cellar. Lives near Dorobanților street, Dej. Not a mill worker. Never named in the narrative. Has a sister who works the laundry on Strada Mihai.

**Cellar woman’s sister (unnamed)** — Recently moved to Dej from the village; works the communal laundry on Strada Mihai, Tuesdays through Saturdays. Likely holder of N10, G3 copy on the N4 branch. Never named in chapter 11.

**Laundry, Strada Mihai** — Municipal communal laundry on Strada Mihai, Dej. The cellar woman’s sister works there Tuesdays through Saturdays. First referenced in chapter 11 as a location Ilinca needs to reach.

**Pharmacy, Eminescu (unnamed acquaintance)** — Pharmacy near second bloc, Eminescu street, Dej. Narrow single-story building between corner post office and a shoe repair. Counter-woman is an unnamed acquaintance of Ilinca’s; she relays information about the cellar woman’s sister in chapter 11 via a note in the clinic delivery.

**Dorobanților street** — Street just behind the second bloc on Eminescu, Dej. Where the cellar woman lives. First referenced in chapter 11 (pharmacy acquaintance’s description).

**Viorica Damian** — Worker at the AFE enterprise laundry facility on Strada Mihai, Dej (transferred from internal production services, February 1982; born 1949, Turda district). Holds N10 (G3, poisoned branch), written in pencil on the back of a factory production form. Sister of Rodica Moga. Interviewed by Munteanu on Day 12 (15 November 1984); named in the dosar penal.

**Rodica Moga** — The cellar woman — the woman Ilinca made the emergency copy (N4) for on 3 November 1983. Holds N4 (G2, poisoned branch). Lives on Dorobanților, bloc 3, apartment 14, behind the second bloc on Eminescu, Dej. Sister of Viorica Damian. Name and address obtained by Munteanu from Viorica Damian on Day 12; he is heading to her address at chapter's end. Never identified by name in Ilinca's chapters until Act III.

**SERVICIU SPĂLĂTORIE** — The enterprise laundry facility designation, stenciled on the door of the laundry at Strada Mihai, Dej. Ground floor of bloc 1 in the courtyard at the end of Strada Mihai.

**Dosar penal** — The formal criminal file (Munteanu's). Kept for the procuror; must contain only what can be demonstrated. Distinct from Munteanu's working notebook, which carries his candid textual observations. Evidence entries in the dosar are a single formal line per collected document.

**Bread delivery man (second cousin's husband)** — Unnamed minor character; Ilinca's second cousin's husband; delivers bread daily from the bakery on Strada Lungă to the laundry on Strada Mihai. Used as a one-time relay in ch13 to carry an inquiry to Viorica Damian (N10 holder). Not a contact; a fact in Ilinca's inventory of available routes.

**Bloc neighbor (N18 holder)** — Unnamed; lives on Viorica Damian's bloc, Strada Mihai, Dej; received copy N18 from Viorica Damian in spring 1984; has a small child; her name is not held by Ilinca and is never given to the bread delivery relay. She is the G4 node on the N4/poisoned branch.

**N18** — G4 copy on the N4/poisoned branch; descended from N10 (Viorica Damian); held by the unnamed bloc neighbor with a small child on Strada Mihai; received spring 1984. Physical details unknown; inherits the numbered line 9 and 'nothing at all' from its ancestor N10.

**Hostel communal bathroom** — The shared bathroom at the end of Doina's hostel corridor. Has a window opening over the yard and a tin ashtray (bent rim) on the windowsill with matches beside it. Used by Doina in ch14 to burn the N14 test paper.

**N14 memorization arc** — Doina begins memorizing N14 in ch6 (November 1-9, reciting in her head on the night shift). Completes full memorization test in ch14 (17 November). The bracket and 'each hour' are the two elements she works hardest to fix. Closed in ch14; the knowledge persists even after the paper is taken in ch30.

**G4 on N3 branch (unnamed)** — Unnamed woman in Elena Florescu's bloc, second stairwell, third floor, Dej. Holds a copy received from a G3 intermediate on the N3 branch (not directly from Elena). Has been asking around about Miliția questions as of Day 15. Never named in narrative.

**presence-check vs. category-check** — Ilinca's retrospective analysis of her cellar-copy check (night of 3 November 1983): she verified each element was present in sequence, but not that each element was in the correct category (bracket vs. numbered line). This distinction is the book's hinge; established as explicit in Ch15.

**December 5, 1984 (administrative deadline)** — The date by which Munteanu must file a determination with the procuror's office in the Petrescu case. Stated aloud by his captain in ch16 (Day 17). Fifteen days from November 20.

**N10 (Viorica Damian's copy)** — G3 node on the poisoned/N4 branch. Written on the back of a pale-green laundry work-order form in pencil. Collected by Munteanu on Day 17. Line 9: numbered, wording '7 or 8 — take her in... Do not wait for the morning.'

**N8 (clean-branch mill copy)** — G3 node on the clean/N3 branch; bracket below line 8 intact. Collected by Munteanu from a mill worker during general canvassing approximately Days 13-16. Source unknown to Munteanu. First appears alongside other copies in ch16 for the two-branch comparison.

**Niculina Hora** — Bread-queue acquaintance of Ilinca's; lives at the corner of Strada Avântului and Strada Livezilor, Dej. Husband formerly at the AFE plant. Daughter lives near the Strada Mihai laundry and attends the same church as the bloc neighbor with the child (N12 holder). Used by Ilinca in ch17 as a relay contact to learn of the G5 church-copy extension on the N4 branch.

**N20 (copy-n20)** — G5 copy on the N4 (poisoned) branch, from N12 (the bloc neighbor with the child). Given by the bloc neighbor to an unnamed woman from her church near the market in October 1984. Poisoned branch: inherits numbered line 9 (no 'in the morning'). Known to Ilinca as node 9 in her mental map from ch17 onward; holder unnamed.

**Miliție questioning room (Dej station, Strada Republicii)** — Room in the Dej district Miliție station used for questioning: table and two chairs, lamp on the table, frosted-glass window. First entered in ch18 (Doina Marcu's arrest and questioning).

**Daniela Lungu** — Factory committee secretary at the AFE textile mill in Dej. Handles meeting minutes, internal correspondence, and directives from the enterprise administration. Introduced in ch19 as the person who tells Ilinca about Doina Marcu's arrest, naming her as 'the one from the hostel on Strada Vasile.'

**Strada Vasile** — Street in Dej where Doina Marcu's workers' hostel is located. First named by Daniela Lungu in ch19.

**N18** — G4 copy of the document, descended from N10 (which descended from N4, Ilinca's cellar copy). Collected from a woman at 18/A/4 Strada Mihai (Viorica Damëan's bloc) on 24 November 1984 by Constable Mureșan. Physical form: AFE mill stationery written on the blank reverse, folded lengthwise. Distinguishing features: 'Burn nothing at all' in line 6 (inherited from N10); numbered line 9, long form. Poisoned branch, but carries 'do not wait for the morning' — only the short-form copies (N11, N17) carry the fatal 'in the morning' phrase.

**Mureșan** — Constable working under Munteanu in the Investigations Section, Dej district Miliția. Conducts production-floor bloc canvassing from Day 19 onward; collects N18 from the Strada Mihai bloc on 24 November 1984.

**Vasilica Drăgan** — G5 terminal node on the N4 branch of the document network; church acquaintance near the church on Piața Libertății in Dej; grey coat; received a copy of the paper from the bloc neighbor (G4) and passed it to no one; confirmed dead end for the N4 branch in ch21.

**Gabi (records office)** — Records-office worker two doors from Ilinca's office in the AFE mill administrative building; Ilinca's established indirect channel for intelligence about the N4 branch; normally uses internal post; comes in person when she has something to say that cannot be written down.

**Piața Libertății** — A square in Dej near which Vasilica Drăgan lives; location of a church attended by women in the N4 branch sub-network.

**Cellar woman's building (Strada Crișan)** — Four-story postwar bloc on Strada Crișan, Dej. Entry code 264. Sand-colored render on lower floors, unpainted concrete above. The cellar woman lives on the third floor, left-side door; name-card holder with no card. The coal cellar in the basement is where Ilinca made N4 on 3 November 1983.

**Q4 materials budget supplement** — Standardized county finance form (two-part) that Ilinca obtains from the county finance office across the river; serves as her official reason for crossing the Someș bridge on 27 November 1984.

**N12** — G4 copy on the N4 poisoned branch; descended from N9; collected by Munteanu from an unnamed woman at Bloc 17, Strada Cuza Vodă on Day 25 (28 November 1984). Physical: household accounts ledger page, ruled reverse. Distinctive variants: line 1 'lying still' (not 'lying down'); line 5 'influenza' without article (inherited from N9); line 9 long-form numbered.

**Strada Cuza Vodă** — Street in Dej, six minutes on foot from the Miliță station. Bloc 17 is the source location of N12, collected by Munteanu in Chapter 23.

**cuza-voda-woman** — Unnamed woman, about 35, Bloc 17 second floor, Strada Cuza Vodă, Dej; N12 holder; mill-adjacent; received her copy by hand in a public place the previous year; refused to name the source; had not passed the copy on.

**Strada 22 Decembrie (Dej)** — Street in Dej where the district Miliția investigations station is located. Munteanu's workplace. Ilinca plans to go there to hand over N1 and N4.

**Column procedure (Ilinca's idiom)** — The habit of writing sequential numerals flush at the left margin — the bookkeeper's reflex that produced a '9' in the coal cellar instead of reproducing N1's bracket. Never named as such in the prose; referred to in the world store as a characterising fact about the N4 copying error.

**Population Section (Secția de Populație)** — The section of the Dej district Miliție station that maintains residential registers. Located at the far end of the investigations corridor; green linoleum floor; one registrar; handwritten ledgers organized by street; smells of carbon paper. Munteanu uses it on Day 28 to confirm the cellar woman’s name, flat number, and the documented sister connection to Viorica Damian.

**Petrescu dosar inside cover** — The inside front cover of the Petrescu Miliție file (dosar penal). On 1 December 1984 (Day 28), Munteanu writes '5 dec' in pencil in the blank lower margin — the first time the December 5 administrative deadline appears in written form within the file.

**distribution sheet (payroll)** — The sign-off form used in the mill administrative wing to log the distribution of payroll envelopes. Ilinca does not sign it on 4 December 1984.

**Miliția form (blank)** — Standard white form with the Miliția heading, used for processing detained individuals. Munteanu fills one out for Ilinca at the close of ch28.

**Petrescu dosar** — The formal case file (dosar penal) for the Ana Petrescu death investigation, maintained by Munteanu. Open on his desk at the time of the handover.

**Dosar penal** — Criminal file. The formal case file in the Petrescu matter, opened by Munteanu on 5 November 1984 and closed with a signed determination on 5 December 1984. Dispatched to the Inspectoratul Județean, Cluj-Napoca.

**Formal determination (determinare)** — The six-page document Munteanu writes on 5 December 1984 closing the Petrescu matter. Five sections: nature of death, the document, origin, chain of transmission, responsible party. Names Ilinca Roșca; clears Doina Marcu.

**Evidence room** — Ground-floor room at the Dej Miliția district station. The eleven evidence envelopes in the Petrescu matter are transferred here at 16:30 on 5 December 1984.
