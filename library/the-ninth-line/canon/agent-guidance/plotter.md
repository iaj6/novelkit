# Plotter guidance — The Ninth Line

## POV count and rotation

**Three POVs. One POV per chapter. No exceptions.**

- **A = Ilinca Roșca** — third person close, present tense
- **B = Doina Marcu** — first person past
- **C = Lieutenant Vasile Munteanu** — third person close, past tense

**Acts I–II rotation: A–B–A–C** (repeating unit). This means Ilinca appears twice as often as Doina or Munteanu in the first two-thirds of the book, because she is the primary collation engine and the reader's anchor on the document tree.

**Act III rotation: A–C–A–C** (tightening the braid as the two collations converge). Doina's chapters drop in Act III because she is in the cell — she can observe but not act, and the compression of her voice into silence is appropriate. She gets the final chapter.

**Final chapter: B (Doina).** Non-negotiable per the brief.

## Chapter-shape preferences

Each chapter must advance exactly one rung on one of the two compounding ladders:

1. **Nodes held** — one of the two collectors (Ilinca or Munteanu) acquires a new copy, learns a new link in the chain, or closes a new branch. Name, in the chapter-map row, which specific node is acquired or confirmed.
2. **Days elapsed** — the running count of days since Ana died (November 4, 1984). The 31-day deadline is November 5, 1984 + 31 = **December 5, 1984**. Track this across the map; the Act III chapters should be approaching or past that date.

**No chapter re-measures a count already established.** If the reader already knows Ilinca holds 7 nodes, a chapter that shows her going through her 7 nodes again is a failed chapter. Each chapter must move something.

**Each chapter's advancement must name a specific foreclosure** — something that is now impossible that was possible before: a woman now known to the Miliția, a branch now closed, a name now on a list. Write this foreclosure into the chapter-map row in the outline.

## Episode-vs-arc structure

This is **not episodic**. Chapters are stations in two simultaneous collations that are running toward convergence. No chapter is self-contained; each chapter changes the state of at least one of the two ladders and leaves the reader tracking where both collectors now stand.

The three-POV braid should feel like a race, without any character calling it a race. At any point in Act II, a reader should be able to say: "Ilinca has N nodes; Munteanu has M nodes; there are D days left." The plotter must structure the chapter map so this is always true — not as explicit statement, but as something inferrable from what the chapters contain.

## Ending-mode distribution across 28–30 chapters

Target (not a rigid prescription — use craft judgment, but approximate):
- **Cliffhanger or mid-action-cut:** 10–12 chapters (concentrated in Act II, where both collations are accelerating)
- **Literary fade (cold image, physical sensation, no resolution):** 8–10 chapters
- **Resolved beat:** 4–5 chapters
- **Turn (reveal, recontextualization):** 2–3 chapters, including the Act I close (reader sees N1 and N17 adjacent) and the Act III climax chapter
- **Coda or declarative close:** 2–3 chapters
- **Elliptical:** 1–2 chapters

**Do not cluster the same ending mode in runs of more than two.** After two literary fades, the next chapter must cut hard or resolve. The brief specifies "mixed by design."

## Chapter target words

**Floor: 1,900 words per chapter. Target: 2,000–2,200 words.** Total target: ~62,000 words across 28–30 chapters.

The pipeline has historically delivered under plan on this kind of brief. The plotter should structure chapters so they contain *enough material* to fill 2,000 words with procedural content, document quotation, and scene — not summaries. A chapter that can be written in 1,200 words has been underloaded in the outline.

**Do not write "short transition chapters."** In this book there are no connective tissue chapters. Every chapter is load-bearing: it acquires a node, forecloses a possibility, or changes an epistemic state in a way the reader can track.

## Genre-specific structural conventions

### Act I (chapters 1–9): The death, the paper, the irony established

- Chapter 1: Ana's death — introduced through one of the other POVs, not Ana's. The fact of the death, the paper, the hospital record. The reader learns this from outside.
- Chapters 2–8: Establish all three POVs; introduce N1 (the master); begin both collations.
- **Act I close (chapter 9 or the chapter immediately after):** The chapter that places N1 and N17 adjacent on the page for the reader, establishing the irony `ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step`. This is the book's engine. The reader must have this before Act II begins. Ilinca must be pinned as `unaware` of the mutation as of this same chapter.

### Act II (chapters 10–21): Both collations build; Doina is arrested

- Both ladders advance every chapter; the reader can track node counts and days.
- The proposition `doina-copy-is-clean` is established for the reader in Doina's first chapter (she has the bracket); Munteanu's wrong belief develops across Act II as he works toward her.
- Ilinca's arc: `unaware` → `suspects` (around chapter 14–16) → building toward `knows` (not yet).
- **Chapter 19 (approx.):** Doina is arrested. This is the crisis point; the reader knows her copy is clean; Munteanu does not. This irony must be live from her arrest to her release.
- Munteanu's collation closes on Doina but cannot climb further because she will not give him the woman on the bus.

### Act III (chapters 22–30): The cellar, the choice, the handover, the release

- **Chapter 22–24 (approx.):** Ilinca's cellar recollection — the chapter where she understands what N4 is. This is where she moves to `knows` on both load-bearing propositions. The chapter must show her remembering the coal cellar, the hurry, the light from the door — and understanding what she did.
- **Chapters 25–28:** The choice. She has the master and the cellar copy. She knows that handing them over exonerates Doina and ends herself.
- **Climax chapter (chapter 27 or 28):** Ilinca produces both documents to Munteanu, in scene, on the page. This is the book's spine and it must not be summarised, elided, or placed offstage.
- **Final chapter (chapter 29 or 30):** Doina, released. First person past. She carries nothing. She has memorised the ninth line — bracket included. The paper is what killed Ana; the memorised line is what survives.

## Epistemic bookkeeping — seed each rung under the correct chapter

**Use these exact proposition slugs:**
- `ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step`
- `ana-copy-descends-from-ilinca-cellar-copy`
- `doina-copy-is-clean`

**`ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step`:**
- `@reader`: `knows` — as of the Act I close chapter (N1 and N17 adjacent)
- `ilinca`: `unaware` — pin this at the Act I close chapter (not just at her early chapters)
- `ilinca`: `suspects` — around chapter 14–16
- `ilinca`: `knows` — at the cellar recollection chapter in Act III
- `munteanu`: `unaware` throughout; he never holds this proposition

**`ana-copy-descends-from-ilinca-cellar-copy`:**
- `ilinca`: `unaware` → `suspects` → `knows` (stairstepped across Acts II–III)
- `munteanu`: `unaware` throughout → `knows` only when Ilinca hands him the documents

**`doina-copy-is-clean`:**
- `@reader`: `knows` — from Doina's first chapter
- `doina`: `knows` but `concealing` (she will not name the woman on the bus)
- `munteanu`: `wrong_believes` — from the arrest onward

**Stamp each rung under the chapter where it lands, not under a canon pseudo-chapter.** Two stances for the same knower on the same proposition under the same chapter collapse; use distinct chapter ids.

## The compounding mechanism in the outline

**Every chapter-map row must specify:**
1. Which ladder advances (Ilinca's node count / Munteanu's node count / days elapsed)
2. What the new count is after this chapter
3. What specific foreclosure occurs (what is now impossible that was possible before)
4. Which prior chapter's state it advances (not re-measures)

The plotter's output must make it possible for a drafter to write 28–30 chapters and, at the end of each one, for the reader to know both node counts and the day count without being told them explicitly.
