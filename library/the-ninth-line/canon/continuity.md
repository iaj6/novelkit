# Continuity — The Ninth Line

Hard facts that must not break in later drafting. Every numbered item is binding. **No editor, repair, or compression pass may contradict any item in this list.**

---

## The document — N1, the master

1. **N1 (the master)** was written by Ilinca Roșca in October 1983. It has exactly eight numbered lines plus a ninth line written across the bottom margin inside a hand-drawn bracket. The bracket line is NOT a numbered item. RecordId: `master-n1`.

2. **The text of N1 is canonical and fixed.** It is reproduced verbatim under `master-n1`. It must be reproduced exactly, including bracket and indentation, wherever quoted in full. The full text is printed at most four times in the book.

3. **N1's ninth element reads:** `[ 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why. Do not wait for the morning. ]` — it is a bracketed marginal override, not a sequential step.

---

## The poisoned branch

4. **N4 (Ilinca's cellar copy)** was made by Ilinca herself on 3 November 1983 in a coal cellar, by the light of the door, in a hurry. Lines 1–8 are identical to N1. The ONLY difference: the bracketed ninth element has been transcribed as a numbered line 9. Every word is correct; the formatting is different. RecordId: `copy-n4`. This is a second-generation copy (G2).

5. **N11 (the regularised copy)** is two generations from N4 (G4). Lines 1–8 are identical to N1. Line 9 reads: `9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.` — **this is correct as written and must not be changed by any pass.** RecordId: `copy-n11`. The phrase *in the morning* is the fatal mutation; it is a loss of urgency, not a wrong quantity.

6. **N17 (Ana's copy)** is a fifth-generation copy (G5), descended from N11. It is identical to N11 in every line. RecordId: `copy-n17`. It is a separate physical object with separate provenance. The full text of N17 is printed once in the book, beside N1, at the Act I close that establishes the dramatic irony.

7. **The fatal mutation is a loss of urgency.** The warning "Do not wait for the morning" became a step "take her in in the morning" through a chain of two formatting transcriptions made in good faith. No one in the chain was careless. No quantity, substance, dose, or procedure was ever wrong.

---

## The clean branch

8. **N14 (Doina's copy)** is a fourth-generation copy (G4), descended from N3 (a G2 branch that is NOT the poisoned N4 branch). N14 still has the bracket. It is clean. Doina did not use it. Doina received it from a woman on the bus whose name she does not know. RecordId: `copy-n14`.

9. **Doina's copy is clean; Munteanu does not know this.** Munteanu's collation reaches Doina and is arithmetically correct — her copy points upward in the tree. He arrests her because he can reach her, not because his arithmetic is wrong. His wrong belief (`doina-copy-is-clean` = false in his stance) is the book's second dramatic irony.

---

## The tree structure

10. **Twenty-two nodes, five generations.** G1: N1. G2: N2, N3, N4 (N4 is the poisoned ancestor). G3: six copies. G4: seven copies including N11 (from N4) and N14 (from N3). G5: five copies including N17 (from N11). Every node is a distinct `record` with its own recordId; no two nodes share a recordId. **Intentional cross-node differences are not continuity errors.**

11. **All 22 nodes differ from each other by design.** Differences between nodes are inherited through the copy chain. No editor, repair pass, or continuity agent may reconcile, harmonise, or "correct" any node toward any other. Nodes off the poisoned branch may differ from N1 only in incidental ways (spelling, dropped comma, line-break, numbering style) that never touch lines 7, 8, or 9.

12. **Only the poisoned branch (N4 → N11 → N17 lineage) touches line 9**, and only in the two ways described in items 4–6 above. Every other node carries line 9 as the bracketed marginal element.

---

## The compounding mechanism

13. **Two monotonic ladders, moving in opposite directions, under every chapter:**
    - *Nodes held*: Ilinca's collation count and Munteanu's collation count each increase. Neither ever loses a node. The reader tracks both.
    - *Days since Ana died*: against the fixed 31-day administrative deadline. When the deadline passes, the file escalates from Munteanu to the county inspectorate — from a lieutenant who wants the truth to an office that wants a name.

14. **Each chapter advances exactly one of these ladders by one rung, and names a specific foreclosure** — a woman is now known, a branch is now closed, a name is now on a list. No chapter re-measures a count already taken or re-walks a copy the reader already holds. This is monotonic: the situation only gets worse.

---

## The ending

15. **The ending is fixed and must be delivered in full, on the page:**
    - Ilinca produces the master (N1) and the cellar copy (N4) to Munteanu. This is the climax and it happens in scene.
    - Munteanu's collation is completed by this handover — he now has the top of the tree.
    - Doina is released.
    - Ilinca is taken.
    - The last chapter is Doina, carrying nothing, having memorised the ninth line — the bracket included.

16. **Do not soften this ending into hope, and do not withhold it.** The paper is what killed Ana; the memorised line is what survives. The ending is not ambiguous.

---

## Safety constraints (absolute — override all other direction)

17. **The circulated document is aftercare and when-to-seek-help ONLY.** It contains no method, no substance, no dose, no procedure, no route of administration, at any node, in any chapter, in any variant. Ana's pregnancy and its ending are never described. These constraints hold at every generation of the copy chain, in every node registered as a record, in every in-prose fragment, in every variant.

18. **The fatal mutation is a loss of urgency** — a warning that stops reading as a warning — not a wrong quantity of anything. If any phase finds itself specifying medical technique, it has misread this brief.

---

## Characters and events

19. **Ana Petrescu** dies on 4 November 1984 at the district hospital in Dej (Spitalul Orășenesc Dej). She is 19. She is never a POV character and never appears in a flashback scene.

20. **Ilinca Roșca** is 41. She is the mill's chief bookkeeper (contabil-șef). She wrote N1 in October 1983.

21. **Doina Marcu** is 19. She works the night shift at the mill. Her copy (N14) came from a woman on the bus. She is innocent and cannot prove it because she cannot name the woman on the bus without identifying her.

22. **Lieutenant Vasile Munteanu** is 34. Miliția lieutenant, Investigations Section, Dej district. He never holds the proposition `ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step` at any point in the book. His collation is arithmetically correct and morally beside the point.

23. **The woman on the bus is never identified, never found, and never appears.** Doina's silence about her holds.

---

## What no character ever does

24. No character draws the document tree, explains the tree, diagrams it, or uses the word "tree." The collation happens in kitchens, on a bus, in a mill office, in a militia room with copies laid on a desk — in objects and hands, never on a whiteboard.

25. No character delivers a speech about the regime or the decree. Nobody explains the political situation to anyone else, because everyone already lives there.

26. No torture, no interrogation set-piece, no Securitate melodrama. Munteanu's power is paperwork and knocking on a door in the afternoon.
