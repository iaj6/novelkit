# Chapter 11 — The Sister

**One-line summary:** Ilinca traces one generation further on the N4 branch and discovers it goes deeper than she knew — the cellar woman gave a copy to her sister, who lives in Dej.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 14 November 1984 (Day 11)  
**Setting:** The mill administrative building; the street outside the mill; Ilinca's office  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Mid-action-cut  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Trace the N4 branch (the cellar copy) at least one generation forward, to understand what the cellar woman did with her copy after receiving it.

**Obstacle:** The cellar woman is not a mill worker. Ilinca cannot approach her through the mill's informal network. She needs a second-degree connection — someone who knows the cellar woman's circle.

**Beats:**

1. **Her desk, morning.** She is thinking about the N4 branch. N1 → N4 (the cellar copy, November 3, 1983). She knows the cellar woman personally. She has not spoken to her since that night, a year ago. They are not close; the connection was a specific emergency and she doesn't know what the cellar woman has done since. Has she kept the copy? Passed it on? To whom?

2. **She thinks about how to find out.** Direct contact with the cellar woman would require going to her part of the city, which is visible and unusual for Ilinca. She needs a route. She has one: an acquaintance who works at a pharmacy near the cellar woman's bloc — not a close friend, someone she knows from the market queue. Ilinca can go to the pharmacy on a legitimate errand.

3. **The pharmacy, mid-morning.** She needs aspirin; the aspirin is real. She gets it. While she is there, she asks the acquaintance, in the course of a normal conversation, whether she knows the Ionescu family on the next street. She does not know their name; she describes them instead. The acquaintance thinks. She says: there's a woman on that street who just moved her sister in from the village. The sister works at the laundry on Strada Mihai. Is that who she means? Ilinca says she's not sure; she was thinking of someone whose husband worked at the chemical plant. The acquaintance says she'll find out, if Ilinca wants to know.

4. **Back at her desk.** She has a thread: the cellar woman may have a sister who recently moved to Dej. This is new information. The cellar woman had a sister. The sister is in the city. The sister might have received a copy.

5. **The information arrives.** The acquaintance sends a note that afternoon, via a delivery from the pharmacy to the mill clinic. (The mill clinic is adjacent to the administrative building; this is a plausible route.) The note says: the woman on that street has a sister who works the laundry. That's all. No name in the note. The note is about aspirin supplies and nothing else; Ilinca reads what's between the words.

6. **Ilinca puts it together.** N1 → N4 (cellar woman) → one G3 copy given to the cellar woman's sister, who now works the laundry on Strada Mihai. That G3 copy exists. She didn't know about it until now. The N4 branch goes deeper than she thought.

7. **Noise in the corridor.** The end of the shift, people leaving the administrative building. Ilinca is still at her desk. She has a new node in her map that she didn't plan for. The N4 branch is longer than she believed.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with the shift bell and Ilinca at her desk in the emptying building, the new node in her mental map — a G3 copy she didn't know about, held by a woman she hasn't been able to reach. She needs to find out more. The mid-action-cut is the noise of the corridor at shift end, interrupting her accounting.

If the cellar woman gave one copy to her sister — who is in Dej, at the laundry — did the sister make any further copies? How deep does the N4 branch go?

## Continuity notes

- The cellar woman: never named. Ilinca does not say her name in her head. The drafter should maintain this. She is referred to only as "the woman from that night" or "the woman from the cellar."
- The sister: a new character, tentatively placed as working at a laundry on Strada Mihai. This is the G3 node on the N4 branch. The architect may have assigned a name; the drafter should use whatever the architect provided, or assign a plausible Romanian name (e.g., Lucia, Vera — not a name already used in the book).
- The pharmacy acquaintance: an extremely minor character. No name needed. Exists to show how information travels through informal networks without the document ever being named.
- The information-delivery route (pharmacy note to mill clinic) is an example of how careful women in this period moved information: on the surface of a legitimate transaction, beneath which the real content rides. The note says nothing that could be incriminating.
- This chapter is where the N4 branch's extension becomes concrete. The drafter should resist the urge to have Ilinca feel alarmed — she is efficient, not panicking. She logs the new node the way she logs any discrepancy: with attention, without fuss.
