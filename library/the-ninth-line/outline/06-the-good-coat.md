# Chapter 6 — The Good Coat

**One-line summary:** Doina on her day off reads N14 three more times, starts to know it by heart, goes out to buy bread, and sees a man in a good coat writing something down near two women from the mill floor.

**POV:** Doina Marcu — first person past  
**Date:** 9 November 1984 (Day 6)  
**Setting:** Doina's hostel room; the market street; back to the hostel  
**Chapter shape:** Bottle (the hostel room, with one excursion)  
**Ending mode:** Cliffhanger  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Doina's goal this chapter:** Decide what to do about the paper.

**Obstacle:** There is no safe decision. Destroying it is an action. Keeping it is an action. Showing it to anyone is an action. Not showing it is also an action. Every option is an action and she is nineteen and alone in a hostel room.

**Beats:**

1. **Hostel room, morning.** Doina does not have a roommate this season — her last one was transferred to the Cluj plant in September. She has the room to herself, which she mentions not because it's comfortable but because it's the reason she could take the paper out without worrying about who was watching. She describes the room: two cots, a shared wardrobe, a window that faces the factory yard. The district heating came on for forty minutes this morning and then stopped. She is in her coat in the room.

2. **She takes out the geography book.** She has been thinking about it since she woke up. She opens it, takes the paper out, reads it. She reads it three times, which is two more times than she needs to read it to understand it. She is not reading it for information. She is reading it the way you look at a thing you're not sure what to do with.

3. **The question of burning it.** There is nowhere to burn anything in the hostel room. The ashtray on the communal table in the hallway is for cigarettes, not for papers. She could burn it in the factory yard, but in November the yard is watched. She could burn it in the toilet — but then she's burning it in a hostel toilet and that is a story someone would tell for years. She does not burn it. She puts it back between pages 34 and 35.

4. **Memory.** She realizes, putting it back, that she already knows line 3 by heart. And line 7. And the bracket. She didn't try to memorize it; she's just read it enough times that it's there. She tests herself on line 4 and gets it wrong by one word, and then gets it right. This is not a decision. This is how her memory works.

5. **The bread queue, afternoon.** She goes out because the bread allocation needs to be used before the end of the week and she keeps putting it off. The queue on her street is shorter than usual — someone said the delivery came late and the loaves are small. She stands in line. She sees, across the street, two women from the production floor she knows. They are talking to a man. The man has a notepad; she sees him write something. He is wearing a coat she hasn't seen before — not a factory coat, not a bloc-committee coat. A different coat. The kind of coat that belongs to a job where you go to other people's places.

6. **She does not stop.** She buys her bread. She goes home. She does not cross the street. She does not say hello to the two women. When she gets back to the hostel she goes directly to her room and does not take off her coat immediately and thinks: the geography book is at the factory. It is in the locker. The locker is locked.

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Doina sitting on the cot in her coat, holding her bread, thinking about the locker and whether the locker is locked. She is fairly certain the locker is locked. She is not entirely certain.

## Continuity notes

- **The man in the good coat is Munteanu.** Doina does not know his name, rank, or purpose yet. She should not be told; she only sees the coat and the notepad. The reader will understand. Doina should not understand more than she can plausibly see from across a street.
- **Geography book location:** IMPORTANT continuity — the geography book is in Doina's FACTORY LOCKER, not at the hostel. This is what saves her when Munteanu comes to the hostel in ch 18. The drafter must establish this clearly here.
- Doina is beginning to memorize N14 without deciding to. This is foreshadowing for ch 14 (she memorizes it fully) and ch 30 (she recites it from memory after everything is gone). The memorization should feel involuntary — her mind is good and the document is short.
- Doina's voice: she is wry even about fear. The question of burning the paper ("then she's burning it in a hostel toilet and that is a story someone would tell for years") is the kind of observation that is funny and also true. This is the register that distinguishes her chapters.
- The two women Munteanu is talking to are not identified; Doina doesn't know their full connection. They are from the production floor. They are probably being informally questioned.
