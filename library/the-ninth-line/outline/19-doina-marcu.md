# Chapter 19 — Doina Marcu

**One-line summary:** Ilinca learns that Doina Marcu has been arrested; her private suspicion of the N4 branch hardens to near-certainty; she sits at the ledger and does not open it.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 23 November 1984 (Day 20)  
**Setting:** The mill administrative building; Ilinca's office  
**Chapter shape:** Interiority-heavy  
**Ending mode:** Cliffhanger  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Understand what Doina Marcu's arrest means for the investigation and for Ilinca's own exposure.

**Obstacle:** She does not know Doina by anything more than the night-shift roster. She does not know whether Doina has a copy. She does not know whether Doina's copy is on the N4 branch or a different branch. She cannot find out without asking, and asking is too dangerous now.

**Beats:**

1. **Her desk, morning.** The factory committee secretary mentions, in a way that is careful and loaded, that a girl from the night shift was taken in yesterday afternoon — Marcu, the one from the hostel on Strada Vasile. She had a document, apparently. Ilinca says: I see. She keeps her pen moving.

2. **She thinks about what she knows.** Doina Marcu. She knows the name from the payroll register: night shift, production floor, monthly salary. She has never had a reason to speak with Doina. She doesn't know if Doina has a copy or what generation it is. She doesn't know what branch.

3. **What Munteanu's interest in Doina tells her.** If Munteanu has gone to Doina Marcu, he has either traced the N4 branch far enough to reach her (making Doina a G4 or G5 node on the N4 branch) or he has found her through a different path — a mill-wide canvass, a referral from someone on the N3 branch or another branch entirely. Doina is four generations from the master. She could be on any branch.

4. **The reasoning she cannot escape.** She has traced the N2 branch: closed and clean, no contact. She has traced the N3 branch: Elena confirmed clean. That leaves the N4 branch as the only branch Munteanu could be working on that would produce names from the mill floor. And if Munteanu has Doina, and if Doina is on the N4 branch, then his path from Doina goes backward — through G3, through G2 (N4), and then: toward the person who made N4. Which is Ilinca.

5. **She forces herself to consider the other possibility.** Doina could be on the N3 branch. N3 went to Elena, then to one G3 node, then — possibly — to Doina as a G4 node. If that's true, Doina's copy is clean (it has the bracket; it doesn't have the mutation). And Munteanu, tracing from Doina, would reach Elena and then hit the N3 branch — which leads, eventually, to N1 and to Ilinca. Either way, Munteanu is working toward her. She is on both paths.

6. **The question of Doina.** Doina is in a cell. If Doina has a clean copy and Munteanu can't prove it, Doina stays in the cell. If Ilinca presents herself — produces N1 and N4 — she exonerates Doina. She also ends herself.

7. **She opens the ledger.** She does not take N1 out. She looks at the spine where it's folded. She closes the ledger.

8. **The cliffhanger.** She is at her desk with the closed ledger and the knowledge that there is a nineteen-year-old in a cell because of something Ilinca did in a coal cellar in November of last year. She does not know this as a certainty. She is afraid it is a certainty.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Ilinca at her desk, the closed ledger in front of her, the problem that has been approaching all month now close enough to see. She has not decided anything. She has not gone to the cellar woman. She has not confirmed N4's text. She only knows what she suspects, and what she suspects is specific and terrible.

---

## New question raised

Doina is in custody. Ilinca suspects she is the cause. Can she live with suspecting without confirming — or does she have to know?

## Continuity notes

- Ilinca does not know what branch Doina is on. She does not know Doina's copy is clean. The reader knows (from ch 2 and 14). The dramatic irony here: the reader knows that Doina's arrest is unjust; Ilinca is only beginning to understand that she may be responsible for an innocent person being in custody.
- The reasoning in beats 3–5 is Ilinca's private accounting — columns and implications. It should read the way she thinks: not emotional, procedural. "If Munteanu has Doina, and if Doina is on the N4 branch, then..." The procedure of elimination.
- She does NOT go to see the cellar woman yet. That is ch 22. This chapter is the last moment before she acts.
- The cliffhanger: the closed ledger and the unspoken thought. The drafter should end on the physical detail — the ledger, the lamp, Ilinca not moving — not on a statement of the thought.
