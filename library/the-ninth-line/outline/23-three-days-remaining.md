# Chapter 23 — Three Days Remaining

**One-line summary:** Munteanu, under pressure from the approaching deadline, gains one more node from the N4 branch; he has the cellar woman's address and plans his visit; seven days remain.

**POV:** Vasile Munteanu — third person close, past tense  
**Date:** 28 November 1984 (Day 25)  
**Setting:** The Miliția station; an address in the mill district; Munteanu's desk  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Cliffhanger  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Munteanu's goal this chapter:** Gain another node on the N4 branch and prepare for the visit to the cellar woman (N4's holder), which will put him in G2 territory.

**Obstacle:** He has the cellar woman's name and address but has not visited. He's been working other angles. This chapter forces him to commit: he will visit the cellar woman within the next forty-eight hours.

**Beats:**

1. **His desk, morning.** He counts. November 28. December 5 deadline. Seven days. He has six copies (N14 plus five from the N4 branch) and a shape: two branches from a common G1. He needs to close the G1. The G1 is above both N2 (Ilinca is N4's maker, but Munteanu doesn't know this) and N3 (Elena, who is N3's holder). He needs to find the person who made both N2 and N3 and N4.

   Wait — he doesn't know N2 and N3 as concepts. He knows he has two branches, one of which carries the mutation and one of which doesn't (Doina's N14). He is working backward on the mutation branch. He hasn't yet traced the N3 branch above Doina.

2. **He goes to another address.** In his canvassing he has found a woman who got her copy from someone adjacent to the cellar woman's network — a G4 node on the N4 branch he hasn't formally processed yet. He goes to her apartment. She has a copy. He takes it.

3. **He now has seven copies on the N4 branch.** Back at the station, he adds the new copy to his desk array. He lays them out — weighted with the stapler and shoe polish tins, which have become a habit. He reads the line 9 on each. *In the morning* across all seven.

4. **He reviews what he knows about the cellar woman.** Her name is from the G3 node (the sister) in ch 16. The sister said she got the copy from her sibling, who lives on Strada Furnicilor (or similar). He has the address. He should have visited by now. He knows he should have visited by now.

5. **He decides: tomorrow.** He will go to the cellar woman's address tomorrow morning. If she has a copy, she is the G2 node — one step below the origin. From her, he should be able to get the name of whoever gave her the copy in the first place. That person is the author of the document and the source of the mutation.

6. **He reads his notes.** His private notebook: *G1 figure: female, mill or mill-adjacent, access to quality document materials, some education (lines are grammatically precise, spacing intentional). Not in population register under any name associated with the copies so far.* He is a good detective and his profile is accurate and it fits Ilinca Roșca perfectly. He does not yet know her name.

7. **The cliffhanger.** He closes the notebook. He picks up the cellar woman's address from his file. He puts it in his coat pocket. Tomorrow morning, Strada Furnicilor. He has seven days.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Munteanu going home with the cellar woman's address in his coat pocket and a seven-day clock on the case. The cliffhanger: he is one step from N4's holder and one step from asking who made N4. He doesn't know that Ilinca was at the cellar woman's address yesterday.

## Continuity notes

- Munteanu does not know that Ilinca visited the cellar woman yesterday (ch 22). He doesn't know Ilinca exists. He doesn't know anyone has recently been to the cellar woman's address. When he arrives in ch 27, he will find the copy is gone — and learn from the cellar woman why.
- His detective profile is accurate: female, mill-adjacent, educated, not in the copy network under her own name. This is good police work. The reader, knowing it describes Ilinca, can feel the closing distance.
- The seven copies on N4 branch: he has been collecting steadily. The reader can track his count: seven copies on the poisoned branch, one on the clean branch (N14). He is close to having every node on the N4 branch that Ilinca doesn't physically hold.
- The note about "quality document materials": this is Munteanu's observation about the paper stock of the original document, inferred from the copies' aging and weight. Mill-grade paper. This is an accurate inference.
- The deadline pressure: seven days left. This is now a concrete countdown. The drafter should make the number present without being dramatic about it.
