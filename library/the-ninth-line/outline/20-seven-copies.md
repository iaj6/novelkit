# Chapter 20 — Seven Copies

**One-line summary:** Munteanu questions Doina a second time; she holds her silence; he gains one more copy from a parallel track; ten days remain.

**POV:** Vasile Munteanu — third person close, past tense  
**Date:** 24–25 November 1984 (Days 21–22)  
**Setting:** The Miliția station questioning room; Munteanu's desk  
**Chapter shape:** Bottle  
**Ending mode:** Resolved beat  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Munteanu's goal this chapter:** Get Doina to name who gave her the copy. She is the highest accessible node he has on the N3 branch. Above her is the woman on the bus, and above the woman on the bus is the chain leading to the origin.

**Obstacle:** Doina will not say. He is precise and she is quiet and neither of them is going to change.

**Beats:**

1. **The questioning room, morning.** He has N14 on the desk between them. He has had four days to look at it. He has noticed: N14 has the bracket in line 9, not the morning instruction. This is different from every copy he has from the N4 branch. Two different line 9 constructions. He is interested in this difference, but he is not sure what to do with it yet.

2. **He lays it out for her.** He shows her N14. He shows her one of the N4-branch copies (N11 or a G4 copy). He reads them both — the bracket and the morning instruction — and asks her: these say two different things in the same place. Where did you get the one with the bracket? She looks at both. She says: I don't know how that's possible. He says: it's possible because they come from two parts of the same chain, and the chain went one way in one part and a different way in another. She says: that's interesting. He says it is. He says: so where did you get yours? She says: I found it on the bus.

3. **He does not believe her.** He has heard this story. He has no counter-evidence. He asks her again, twice more, at intervals. The answer is the same. He writes in his notes: *Marcu, D., maintains "found on bus" account. Inconsistent with pattern of chain transmission. Probable link to woman who distributed on route 4 (morning). Cannot confirm.*

4. **He thinks about her copy.** N14 has the bracket. All the N4-branch copies have the morning instruction. If N14 and the N4-branch copies are from the same tree, they diverged above her. Doina's copy is from a branch that did not receive the mutation. This is interesting. It does not exonerate her — she still had the document, she still is concealing her source — but it means the mutation's entry point is above her. He needs to get above her to find it.

5. **He gains another copy from parallel work.** While Doina has been held, one of his junior officers has been canvassing other addresses on the production-floor bloc. A woman contacted through this canvass has a G4 copy on the N4 branch — not Doina's copy, not from her branch, from the poisoned branch's G4 tier. Munteanu receives this copy late in the afternoon. He now has six copies on the poisoned branch, one on the clean branch (Doina's N14).

6. **He writes in his working notes.** He has been careful about what goes in the formal file. In his private notebook: *Source: female. Writing October–November 1983 (inferred from paper stock and wear pattern). Mill district or adjacent. Possibly factory-affiliated. Has not yet entered the investigation voluntarily. Above G2 on the poisoned branch — the G2 node (cellar woman's sister's copy source) points up.* He also notes: *Bracket branch (N14): separate G2 ancestor. Both branches converge at G1.*

7. **The resolved beat.** He has reached a conclusion that is smaller than the case: he knows there are two G2 nodes and a G1 above them both. He doesn't have the G1 yet. But he has the shape. He closes the notebook. He is going to visit the cellar woman's sister and work upward.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends on the small settled note of a man who has concluded what he can conclude from what he has, and who now knows the next step. He has ten days. The next step is the cellar woman (N4's holder), whose name he got from the sister in ch 16 and whose address he hasn't yet visited.

---

## New question raised

Doina's copy is from a different branch than the fatal copies — this is clear to Munteanu. But she will not name her source. So she sits in the cell while Munteanu goes looking for the person above her.

## Continuity notes

- This is a bottle chapter: one location (the questioning room) + brief desk work. The physical space should be confined — the table, the two chairs, the evidence envelopes.
- Munteanu notices N14's bracket but has not yet concluded that the bracket exonerates Doina. His reasoning: she could have received a bracket copy (clean branch) and still be a link in the chain. Her refusal to name her source is what keeps her there.
- The resolved beat is small: Munteanu has the shape of the problem and the next step. It is NOT a big resolution — the case is not closed. The resolution is epistemic (he knows the shape) not situational (Doina is still there).
- He has ten days (November 25 → December 5). This is the countdown that begins being tracked explicitly here.
- The G1 node observation: he does not write the word "origin" in his notes — too dramatic. He writes something procedural: "both branches converge at a single prior document." This is his articulation of what the reader already knows.
- Doina is not on the page in this chapter in any full scene; she is at the center of Munteanu's analysis but the chapter is his perspective.
