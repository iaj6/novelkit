# Chapter 18 — The Hostel Door

**One-line summary:** Munteanu knocks on Doina's hostel door; she leaves the geography book in her factory locker; she says she found the copy; she is held; Munteanu has N14.

**POV:** Doina Marcu — first person past  
**Date:** 22–23 November 1984 (Days 19–20)  
**Setting:** The workers' hostel; the Miliția station  
**Chapter shape:** Single scene  
**Ending mode:** Mid-action-cut  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Doina's goal this chapter:** Keep her mouth shut about the woman on the bus.

**Obstacle:** She is nineteen and in a Miliția holding room and the man across the desk is patient and not unkind and she is still going to keep her mouth shut.

**Beats:**

1. **The hostel, afternoon of the 22nd.** She is in her room on her afternoon break before the night shift. There is a knock. She thinks: nobody knocks on hostel doors in the afternoon unless they have a reason. She does not look through the gap first. She opens the door.

2. **Munteanu on the doorstep.** He is younger than she expected. He has the kind of face that is not going to be remembered for anything in particular. He says: Tovarășă Marcu? He says his name and rank. He says he would like to speak with her about a matter. He says it the way men in his position say it — in a way that means she will come. She thinks: the geography book is at the factory. It is in the locker. It is behind a locked metal door.

3. **The choice she makes in thirty seconds.** She does not go back for the book. She does not reach for her coat and say *let me just get something*. She takes her coat off the hook and puts it on, in the room, and she goes with him. The geography book stays in the locker. This is the decision she will remember longest.

4. **The Miliția station, the questioning room.** The room has a table and two chairs and a window with frosted glass. He sits across from her. On the table: several pieces of paper in evidence envelopes. She can see they are paper documents, the size and shape of what she has. She cannot read them from where she sits.

5. **He asks her about the paper.** He does not name it; he doesn't have to. He says: we have reason to believe you may be in possession of a document that has been circulating in the factory district. He asks if she can tell him anything about it. She says: I don't know what you mean. He gives her the opening. She takes it.

6. **She says she found it.** He asks where. She says she found it on the bus, between seats. He asks when. She says last spring. He asks which bus. She says the number 4, the morning route. This is the truth. This is also a lie by omission of the largest magnitude: she found it because the woman on the bus put it in her hand. She does not say this. She will never say this.

7. **He shows her N14.** He asks her to identify it. She says: yes, that's mine. He reads her the bracket line. He asks if that's how she read it. She says yes. He asks if she knows what it's for. She says: I assumed it was medical advice of some kind. She does not ask where he got it — he has it from the locker, which means the locker was opened. She knew the locker could be opened.

8. **She is held.** He does not charge her. He says she is being held for further questioning and that she may be released once certain things are clarified. She says: what things? He says: where you got the document. She says: I told you. He says: I know what you told me. She sits back.

9. **The mid-action-cut.** The chapter ends with Doina in the chair, the table between them, the geography book in the locker at the factory where she can no longer reach it.

---

## Outcome / turn

Doina is in custody. N14 is in Munteanu's possession. The geography book — the container for the original — is in a locked locker at the factory, evidence in an envelope, no longer in her hands. The thing that keeps her in the cell is the thing she's not saying: the woman on the bus.

---

## New question raised

Munteanu has N14 now. It has the bracket. This means Doina's copy is demonstrably different from the fatal copies on the poisoned branch. Munteanu can see the difference. Does he understand what it means?

## Continuity notes

- **CRITICAL CONTINUITY:** The geography book is in the FACTORY LOCKER, not in the hostel room. When Munteanu's men search Doina's room they will not find it there. They retrieve it from the locker. This detail was established in ch 6 and 10 and must hold here.
- Doina's voice: first person past, but this is a high-stakes chapter and her voice should be direct and spare. The wryness is present but quieter. She is nineteen and she knows what she's doing. "I want that on the record: I knew what I was not saying." The retrospective narrator is calm about it.
- Munteanu's conduct: he is not cruel. He is not a villain. He is a policeman doing his job, which is to find out where the paper came from. He is also the instrument of a state that withholds treatment from dying women until they name names — but he is not the one doing that, and the chapter should not editorialize about it. The irony is structural, not stated.
- Doina should not waver. She does not almost name the woman on the bus. The decision was made before she opened the door.
- N14 on the table: Munteanu has it in an evidence envelope. He reads her the bracket line. This is a quotation site: he reads a fragment of N14 aloud.
