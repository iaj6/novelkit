# Chapter 1 — The Ledger Spine

**One-line summary:** Ilinca learns Ana Petrescu is dead; she retrieves the master from the ledger spine and reads it line by line; her private count begins.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 4 November 1984 (Day 1)  
**Setting:** The mill's administrative building; Ilinca's office; her apartment afterward  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Literary fade  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Verify that N1 (the master) is correct and begin privately accounting for every copy she knows she made.

**Obstacle:** She does not yet know which copy reached Ana's household. She cannot ask directly. She cannot make a list.

**Beats:**

1. **End of workday, the corridor outside the personnel office.** Ilinca overhears two women talking in lowered voices. The name she catches: *Petrescu*. She knows the Petrescus — the family on Strada Gării. She knows which Petrescu is nineteen years old. She goes back to her desk. She does the numbers she is paid to do. Her adding machine is mechanical; she counts on it until the office empties.

2. **The administrative building, after hours.** She is alone. She opens the ledger — not the most recent one, the one from 1983 that she has kept because it was not yet fully audited and she has a legal reason to retain it. She opens it to the spine. The folded paper is there. She takes it out with two fingers, careful of the fold.

3. **She reads N1 at her desk.** Line by line. Eight numbered lines. The bracket in the margin. She has read it so many times she could recite it, but she reads it now because reading is checking. Everything is correct. The bracket is correct. The instruction inside the bracket is correct. She folds it back along the same creases.

4. **She counts, privately, what she has done.** Three copies she made herself from N1, in the fourteen months since she wrote it. The first two, to women she trusts by name. The third — November 3 of last year, in a coal cellar, by the light that came through the cracked door, in a hurry because the woman could not wait. She does not write these names down. She never writes them down. She holds them in the same column she holds everything she cannot afford to lose.

5. **The walk home.** Cold, fog already on the river. She thinks: the copies she made were three. The women she personally handed them to — she knows each by name and face. But she does not know who those women handed their copies to.

6. **Her apartment.** One lamp, the 40-watt that everyone has. She does not take the coat off immediately. She stands in the dark hallway and counts. The question she does not write down is: which of those three copies is Ana Petrescu's sister-in-law's.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Ilinca sitting at her kitchen table in her coat, in the lamp-dark, holding the shape of the question. She has not yet concluded anything is wrong. She believes her copies are correct. She begins her private collation.

## New question raised

Which of the three copies she made from N1 reached Ana's household — and through how many hands?
