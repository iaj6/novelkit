# Chapter 9 — The Bracket

**One-line summary:** ACT I CLOSE — Ilinca reads N1 in full; the full text of N17 (Ana's copy) is placed beside it on the page for the reader; the reader now understands the fatal difference that Ilinca cannot yet see.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 12 November 1984 (Day 9)  
**Setting:** Ilinca's office, late afternoon and evening; the mill corridor  
**Chapter shape:** Single scene (Ilinca alone with the master, then receiving news)  
**Ending mode:** Turn  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Understand what the mutant copy says. She has heard secondhand that a copy circulating in the mill district says something different in line 9. She needs to know the exact wording.

**Obstacle:** She cannot ask the person who told her to show her the copy — that would be an admission that she knows what document they're talking about. She must work with what has come to her indirectly.

**Beats:**

1. **The office, late afternoon.** A woman Ilinca knows — not by the document, just a corridor acquaintance — comes in with a maintenance request needing the chief bookkeeper's signature. While Ilinca signs the form, the woman mentions, nearly as a separate conversation, that her friend from the bloc committee was called in to speak to someone about the Petrescu matter. She says: they asked about a piece of paper that supposedly says what to do if someone is ill. The copy her friend had said something about waiting until morning. The exact line, as her friend told her: *9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.*

2. **Ilinca's hands do not stop.** She gives the signed form back. She thanks the woman. The woman leaves. Ilinca picks up her pen and sets it down.

3. **She takes N1 from the ledger spine.** She opens it flat on the desk and reads it — every line, every word, including the bracket. She counts: eight numbered lines. Then the bracket.

4. **N1 PRINTED IN FULL — FIRST FULL PRINTING.**

   ```
   1. Keep her warm and lying down. Someone stays with her the whole night.
   2. Small sips of water, often. Nothing else by mouth for six hours.
   3. Count the cloths. Write the number and the hour on the paper by the bed.
   4. If she sleeps, wake her every hour and speak to her until she answers you properly.
   5. No visitors. No telephone. If anyone asks, she has the influenza.
   6. Burn nothing until she is well. Wash and keep.
   7. Two cloths in one hour is too many.
   8. A fever that will not come down by morning is too long.
      [ 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why.
        Do not wait for the morning. ]
   ```

5. **She reads the bracket three times.** *Do not wait for the morning.* She drew that bracket in October of last year specifically because she knew the ninth instruction was different in kind from the other eight — not a step in a sequence, but an override. If 7 or 8, stop managing it at home. Go now. The bracket marks the difference between a step and an override. The bracket cannot be turned into a numbered line without destroying that difference.

6. **She thinks about what she was told.** *In the morning.* A copy that says *waiting until morning* in line 9. She holds the two texts in her mind, the one she is looking at and the one she was given.

7. **N17 PRINTED IN FULL — SECOND FULL PRINTING.** (Drafter's device: the chapter shifts briefly into Ilinca's internal reconstruction — she has been given the exact wording of line 9, and she knows lines 1–8 are invariant across all copies, so she can reconstruct the full text of the copy. The drafter renders this reconstruction as a document block, identical to N1 except for line 9. This is the brief's required "Ana's copy beside it at the reveal-to-reader" — both texts appear in this chapter, on the page, adjacent or near-adjacent, so the reader can see every word of both.) The reconstructed text:

   ```
   1. Keep her warm and lying down. Someone stays with her the whole night.
   2. Small sips of water, often. Nothing else by mouth for six hours.
   3. Count the cloths. Write the number and the hour on the paper by the bed.
   4. If she sleeps, wake her every hour and speak to her until she answers you properly.
   5. No visitors. No telephone. If anyone asks, she has the influenza.
   6. Burn nothing until she is well. Wash and keep.
   7. Two cloths in one hour is too many.
   8. A fever that will not come down by morning is too long.
   9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.
   ```

8. **The difference the reader now holds.** The bracket says: *the ninth instruction is not a next step, it is an override — go now.* The numbered line 9 says: go in the morning. Lines 1–8 are identical. Line 9 is the only point of divergence. The reader can see it because both texts are now on the page. Ilinca can see it too — she understands that a numbering error has destroyed the urgency. But she believes the error was made *downstream* by someone she doesn't know.

9. **She folds N1 back into the ledger.** The fold has gone slightly furry at the crease — the paper has been folded and unfolded many times in fourteen months. She puts it back. She closes the ledger.

10. **What she does not see.** Ilinca's conclusion is logical and wrong. The error was not made downstream of all her copies — it was made by her, in N4, which then propagated into every copy on that branch. She cannot know this yet. The reader, who will eventually understand the document tree, is ahead of Ilinca from this chapter forward.

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Ilinca at her desk, the ledger closed, believing the mutation was made by someone she doesn't know, downstream of everything she touched. The turn is for the reader: both texts are now on the page, the difference is visible, and the reader is now ahead of Ilinca on the question of what the ninth line means. This irony will hold until chapter 22.

## New question raised

Where did the error enter the chain? Ilinca believes it was in a generation she didn't make. The reader suspects (but doesn't yet know) the direction of the answer. The irony is now live.

## Continuity notes

- **CRITICAL — FOUR PRINTINGS CEILING:** This chapter contains BOTH the first and second full printings (N1 and N17). After this chapter, two printings remain: Doina's copy (ch 14) and the master + cellar copy together (ch 28). No other chapter may quote either document in full.
- **CRITICAL:** N1 must be reproduced verbatim with the bracket notation exactly as shown in beat 4. Any drift creates a continuity contradiction.
- **CRITICAL:** N17 (reproduced in beat 7) is identical to N1 in every line except line 9. Line 9 of N17 reads exactly: `9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.` No other word differs.
- **CRITICAL:** The fatal mutation is a formatting change (bracket → numbered line) that converts an override into a sequential step. This is never described as a wrong quantity, a wrong substance, a wrong dose, or a wrong procedure. SAFETY: the document contains only aftercare and when-to-seek-help instructions. Nothing in the drafter's text may add medical content beyond what the canonical document text above shows.
- The drafter's device for printing N17 in full is flexible: Ilinca's mental reconstruction is one option; a typographic insert (the document appears without attribution, as readers of literary fiction understand paratextual evidence blocks) is another. The requirement is that both texts appear in full in this chapter, close to each other.
- Ilinca does not say anything that approaches the correct explanation. She does not suspect herself. Her conclusion (downstream copyist error) is wrong in direction, not in mechanism — someone did make a copying error; she just doesn't know it was her.
- The ledger detail (fold gone furry from repeated folding) signals that this paper has been lived with, not stored away. Physical life of the document.
- **MUNTEANU REFERENCE:** Munteanu in ch 4 received N17 from his sister-in-law and noted it without full-quoting. The full text of N17 appears here in ch 9 (Ilinca's chapter) rather than in ch 4. The drafter of ch 4 must NOT reproduce N17 in full — that is reserved for this chapter.
