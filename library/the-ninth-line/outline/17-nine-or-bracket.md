# Chapter 17 — Nine or Bracket

**One-line summary:** Ilinca traces a second G4 node on the N4 branch and moves firmly to `suspects` that the mutation entered through her own cellar copy; she holds N1 near the lamp and counts the bracket.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 21 November 1984 (Day 18)  
**Setting:** The mill administrative building; Ilinca's apartment, evening  
**Chapter shape:** Interiority-heavy  
**Ending mode:** Literary fade  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Trace one more G4 node on the N4 branch, and make a definitive accounting of what she knows and doesn't know about N4's text.

**Obstacle:** The G4 nodes on the N4 branch are in Munteanu's territory now. She needs to trace them without approaching people he has already interviewed.

**Beats:**

1. **Her desk, morning.** She has been thinking about the N4 branch since yesterday — about whether the cellar copy has the bracket or a numbered line. She does not know. She needs to know. She also needs to trace the other G4 node she identified two days ago: the sister's neighbor with the child.

2. **Tracing the G4 node.** Through a third-degree connection (a woman who knows the laundry district, someone Ilinca has exchanged bread-queue pleasantries with for years, who has a daughter on the laundry street), she gets word about the G4 node. This woman — the bloc neighbor with a child — gave one copy to someone at her church. That means a G5 node exists on the N4 branch. The N4 branch extends even deeper than Ilinca mapped.

3. **She adds the G5 inference.** N4 branch: N4 → G3 (sister) → G4 (sister's neighbor with child) → G5 (church contact). This is consistent with the mutation propagating down the N4 branch. She does not know, at any of these nodes below N4, whether they have the bracket or the numbered line. She only knows they exist and that they're on the N4 branch.

4. **She thinks about what she knows.** She has traced the N2 and N3 branches: clean. She has traced the N4 branch: four nodes below N4, all unconfirmed as to line 9 content, all on the same branch. The mutant copies Munteanu has been collecting are on the N4 branch (she knows this from the mill gossip). Every piece of information she has points to the same place. But she still cannot say it because she still hasn't looked at N4.

5. **Evening, the apartment.** She takes N1 out. She doesn't put it on the desk. She holds it near the lamp — the 40-watt on the kitchen table — and reads the bracket. The bracket is correct. She drew it herself, in October 1983, with a ruled line in ink. She drew the bracket because she knew the ninth element was different in kind from the other eight: not a next step, but an override.

6. **She thinks about November 3, 1983.** The cellar. The cracked door. The woman waiting. The lamp she didn't have. She was reading N1 by the light from the door, which was a streetlamp somewhere outside. In that light — angled, insufficient — a bracket on the left margin might look like a capital 9.

7. **She puts N1 back.** She folds it along the furred crease. She holds the shape of the thought she just had: a bracket, in poor light, might look like a 9. She does not yet go to the cellar woman. She does not yet call it confirmed. But she knows she needs to see N4.

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Ilinca putting N1 back in the ledger spine and sitting in the dim kitchen. The lamp is on. She knows she needs to see N4. She has not yet gone to see it. The literary fade is the lamp on the table and the ledger closed and Ilinca not moving.

She was copying in insufficient light and in a hurry. She needs to physically see N4 to know. She has resisted going to the cellar woman. She cannot resist much longer.

- The central beat of this chapter is Ilinca holding N1 near the lamp and having the specific thought about the bracket / poor light / capital-9. This is the thought that will become knowledge in ch 22. Here it is a hypothesis she cannot confirm without seeing N4.
- The physical detail of the bracket: Ilinca drew it herself in ink, in October 1983. She knows what it was meant to be. The question is whether the hastily-made copy preserved the bracket's character (a marginal override indicator) or converted it into a numbered list item.
- The lamp: the 40-watt kitchen lamp. This is the same poor light that she was writing in during the cellar (the cracked door = streetlamp light). The echo is intentional.
- The literary fade should feel like suspension: she is one action away from knowing (she needs to see N4) and is not yet taking it.
