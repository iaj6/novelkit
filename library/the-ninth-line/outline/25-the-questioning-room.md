# Chapter 25 — The Questioning Room

**One-line summary:** Munteanu questions Doina a final time; she holds; through the population registers he locates the cellar woman's exact address; four days to deadline.

**POV:** Vasile Munteanu — third person close, past tense  
**Date:** 1 December 1984 (Day 28)  
**Setting:** The Miliția station questioning room; the Population Section register room; his desk  
**Chapter shape:** Bottle (the questioning room dominates; brief bureaucratic interlude)  
**Ending mode:** Mid-action-cut  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Munteanu's goal this chapter:** One final attempt to get Doina to name her source; then, regardless of outcome, commit to visiting the cellar woman the next day.

**Obstacle:** Doina is not going to name her source. He knows this. He asks anyway because procedure requires it and because on rare occasions, after a week in custody, people decide that the name is a smaller risk than the continued hold.

**Beats:**

1. **The questioning room, morning.** She looks better than he expected for someone who has been held for nine days. She eats what is given to her; she sleeps; she does not make the mistake of performing distress. She sits down without being asked.

2. **He lays N14 on the table.** He does not say anything immediately. He lets the paper sit there between them. She looks at it. She does not pick it up. He asks: is there anything you'd like to add to your previous statements? She says: no. He asks: you found this on the bus? She says: yes. He asks: in the spring? She says: yes. He asks: do you know a woman who distributed this document on bus route 4 during the spring of 1984? She says: no.

3. **He shows her one more document.** He shows her a copy from the N4 branch — the poisoned line 9, "in the morning." He asks: your copy says something different in the last line. Different from this one. He reads both. He asks: do you know why yours is different? She says: I don't know. He says: it's different because your copy comes from a part of the chain that didn't have the error. She says nothing. He says: which means wherever you got yours from, it was from a branch that predates the error. She says nothing. He says: if you could tell me where you got yours from, I could close this faster. She says: I found it on the bus.

4. **He understands.** She is not going to tell him. He has known this since the third day of her custody. He is asking for procedure. He writes in the file: *Marcu, D., maintains original statement. No new information.* He closes the folder.

5. **He releases her to the holding room.** She goes back. She has not broken. He is not surprised.

6. **The Population Section register room.** He goes down the hall to the Population Section, which maintains the residential registers for every bloc in the district. He asks for the street record for Strada Furnicilor (or the cellar woman's street). He finds the cellar woman's full name and flat number in the register. He writes it on a fresh page.

7. **He also notices something.** The cellar woman's registration record shows an amendment from two years ago: she was registered in 1982 as living alone; in late 1983 her sister joined the household temporarily. The sister has since moved to Strada Mihai (the laundry — which he already has). The connection between the cellar woman and the laundry woman is now confirmed via the population register. He already knew the sister connection from ch 16's interview; this is administrative confirmation.

8. **Four days.** He goes back to his desk. He has the address confirmed. He plans to visit tomorrow. He does the arithmetic again: four days. He writes the date on the inside of the folder: December 5. He will have a determination by then.

## Outcome / turn

The chapter cuts at the moment of Munteanu leaving his desk — coat on, address in hand, the folder on the desk behind him. The mid-action-cut is the beginning of the visit that will happen in ch 27.

## Continuity notes

- The questioning room: a physical description of the space (table, chairs, frosted window) was established in ch 18. The drafter can use the same room; it should feel the same confined space nine days later.
- Munteanu's tone with Doina: consistent with ch 18 and 20. Precise, not unkind, not emotional. He is doing his job. He is also, in some part of himself, not comfortable with what he is doing — but that part is not running this chapter. Procedure is running this chapter.
- The Population Section interaction: show the bureaucratic mechanics. The register is a physical book — in 1984 Romania, these are handwritten residential registers maintained at each district Miliția station. He finds the address in a book that smells of carbon paper.
- The sister connection confirmation: Munteanu now formally links the cellar woman to the G3 node (the laundry sister). This confirms his line of backward tracing. He already knew it from ch 16; the population register makes it documented.
- Four days: December 5 is now four days away. Munteanu has committed the date to paper for the first time in the formal folder.
- IMPORTANTLY: Munteanu does NOT visit the cellar woman in this chapter. The visit is ch 27. This chapter is the preparation.
