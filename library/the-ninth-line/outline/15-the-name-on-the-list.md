# Chapter 15 — The Name on the List

**One-line summary:** Ilinca learns through the mill network that Munteanu has contacted the G4 node on the N4 branch; she traces one G4 node on the N3 branch and moves to `suspects` that the N4 branch is the source of the mutation.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 18–19 November 1984 (Days 15–16)  
**Setting:** The mill; Ilinca's apartment  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Cliffhanger  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Trace one more node on the N3 branch and understand whether Munteanu has yet reached anyone she personally knows.

**Obstacle:** The news reaching her from the mill is indirect — no one comes to her office and says "Munteanu has been to Strada Mihai." She has to read the information from what people say around the edges of it.

**Beats:**

1. **The mill, Tuesday morning.** Someone on the production floor — not a name Ilinca has marked in her private map, just a woman from the adjacent section who eats lunch at the administrative building canteen — mentions to another woman, within Ilinca's earshot, that a friend of hers from the laundry was asked to come in for a talk. Laundry. Strada Mihai. Ilinca counts to herself: G4 node on N4 branch. Munteanu has been to the sister's neighbor. He is in G4.

2. **She processes this.** If Munteanu is in G4 on the N4 branch, he is one step from the G3 (the cellar woman's sister), and two steps from N4 itself (the cellar woman). He is tracing backward toward the cellar copy. He has not yet reached N4; he doesn't have the cellar woman's name. But he is close.

3. **N3 branch check.** She needs to know that N3's branch is still safe. Elena (N3's holder) — she hasn't had a message from Elena since the initial contact in ch 5. She sends another oblique message through the cousin's daughter: just checking on the influenza. The response comes back the same day: Elena is fine. Her copy has been passed to nobody new. There is one G3 node on the N3 branch (the woman Elena gave it to), and that woman has been quiet.

4. **But Elena also said something else.** The message from Elena, relayed through the cousin's daughter: there's a woman in Elena's bloc who has a copy — not from Elena, from someone else. A G4 node on some branch of N3 that Ilinca hadn't mapped. This woman has been asking around quietly. Asking about the Miliția questions.

5. **She adds the node.** N3 branch has a G4 node she didn't know about — someone in Elena's bloc who got her copy from an intermediate source (the G3 node on N3 branch). This is a new node. She maps it. The N3 branch is cleaner than N4's branch in terms of Miliția contact, but it's longer than she thought.

6. **The connection she is making.** Both branches — N3 and N4 — are longer than she traced in her first accounting. The branches are propagating. And Munteanu is in G4 on the N4 branch, working toward the cellar copy. Ilinca looks at N1 in the ledger spine without taking it out. She thinks about the cellar. November 3, 1983. Was she right that she copied it correctly? She thought so. She is no longer entirely sure.

7. **Evening, her apartment.** She is not sleeping well. She sits at the kitchen table. She thinks: the mutation entered the chain somewhere on the N4 branch. It must have. Both the N2 and N3 branches are clean — Elena confirmed it, and the N2 branch was closed and clean. Which means whatever was wrong entered through the cellar. But she copied the cellar copy correctly. She is fairly certain. She was in a hurry. She was in the dark.

---

## Outcome / turn

The cliffhanger: Ilinca at the table, in the dark, the thought arriving that she cannot fully dismiss — that she was in a hurry, and that the coal cellar was dark, and that she didn't read the copy back after making it. She has never read N4 since the night she made it. She does not know, with the exactness she needs, that N4 is correct.

## New question raised

## Continuity notes

- The G4 node on N3 branch (woman in Elena's bloc): a new character in Ilinca's mapping. Not named. Not seen. Exists as a data point in Ilinca's private accounting.
- The news about Munteanu reaching Strada Mihai comes through overheard conversation, not a direct report to Ilinca. This is how information flows in this world: indirectly, through the margins of legitimate social interaction.
- The moment at the end — Ilinca alone, thinking she was in a hurry, she didn't read it back — is the chapter's real movement. This is the beginning of her path to knowledge. She is not there yet; she is at the edge of the question.
- The cliffhanger should leave the reader with Ilinca at the table, suspended between belief and doubt. The doubt is new. It matters.
- Do not have Ilinca decide anything in this chapter. She sits in the dark and she does not know.
