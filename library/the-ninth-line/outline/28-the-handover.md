# Chapter 28 — The Handover

**One-line summary:** CLIMAX — Ilinca walks into the Miliția station; puts N1 and N4 on Munteanu's desk; waits while he reads them; he releases Doina; he calls his captain; Ilinca sits.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 4 December 1984 (Day 31)  
**Setting:** The mill, briefly (she takes N1 from the ledger); the Miliția district station; Munteanu's office  
**Chapter shape:** Single scene  
**Ending mode:** Turn  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Produce N1 and N4 to Munteanu. Exonerate Doina. End herself.

**Obstacle:** There is no obstacle. The obstacle was the previous twenty-seven chapters. This chapter is the execution of a decision that was made in the dark of a kitchen on the 30th of November.

**Beats:**

1. **The mill, morning.** She arrives before the shift. She does the payroll run she prepared last night. She goes to the administrative building, sits at her desk, and opens the legitimate ledger. She lifts N1 out of the spine. She folds it into her breast pocket. She buttons her coat.

2. **She walks to the Miliția station.** Fifteen minutes. She does not hurry. It is December 4 and clear, the kind of cold that makes sound carry. She walks past the bread queue on the corner. She walks past the bloc where three women she knows by name live. She does not think about this. She has spent the last four days not thinking about things she cannot afford to spend time on.

3. **The Miliția station.** She goes in through the public entrance. She tells the officer at the reception desk that she needs to speak with Lieutenant Munteanu regarding the Petrescu matter. She gives her name.

4. **She is shown into Munteanu's office.** He is at his desk. His case materials are on the desk — copies in envelopes, his notebooks, the formal dosar penal, a coffee cup empty. He looks at her and she can see that he knows this is the person he has been looking for. He says: Tovarășă Roșca? She says yes. He asks her to sit down.

5. **She puts both documents on his desk.** She takes N1 from her breast pocket and N4 from her coat lining (she transferred it there this morning). She lays them flat on the desk, side by side. She does not say anything. He looks at them. He picks up N1 and reads it. He reads it line by line. He picks up N4 and reads it. He sets them side by side.

6. **N1 AND N4 PRINTED IN FULL — FOURTH AND FIFTH FULL PRINTINGS (the paired climax printing, the book's final quotation occasion).**

   N1 (the master):
   ```
   1. Keep her warm and lying down. Someone stays with her the whole night.
   2. Small sips of water, often. Nothing else by mouth for six hours.
   3. Count the cloths. Write the number and the hour on the paper by the bed.
   4. If she sleeps, wake her every hour and speak to her until she answers you properly.
   5. No visitors. No telephone. If anyone asks, she has the influenza.
   6. Burn nothing until she is well. Wash and keep.
   7. Two cloths in one hour is too many.
   8. A fever that will not come down by morning is too long.
      [ 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why.
        Do not wait for the morning. ]
   ```

   N4 (the cellar copy):
   ```
   1. Keep her warm and lying down. Someone stays with her the whole night.
   2. Small sips of water, often. Nothing else by mouth for six hours.
   3. Count the cloths. Write the number and the hour on the paper by the bed.
   4. If she sleeps, wake her every hour and speak to her until she answers you properly.
   5. No visitors. No telephone. If anyone asks, she has the influenza.
   6. Burn nothing until she is well. Wash and keep.
   7. Two cloths in one hour is too many.
   8. A fever that will not come down by morning is too long.
   9. 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why.
      Do not wait for the morning.
   ```

7. **He looks at both.** He looks at line 9 on N1 — the bracket. He looks at line 9 on N4 — the number. He understands what he is looking at. He has been looking for the mutation's entry point. He is looking at it.

8. **He asks her if she made these.** She says: yes. He asks: which one first? She says: the one with the bracket. She does not explain. He does not need the explanation. He is a good detective.

9. **He picks up the telephone.** He calls the holding section. He asks for Doina Marcu to be released. He says: immediately. He puts the phone down. He picks it up again and calls his captain. He says: I have resolved the Petrescu matter. He says the name of who he has in his office.

10. **Ilinca sits.** She does not speak while he is on the telephone. When he puts the phone down she is still sitting across from him. Both documents are on his desk. She does not ask what happens now. She already knows. He already knows she knows.

11. **The turn.** The chapter ends on this: the two documents on the desk between them, one with a bracket and one with a nine, and Ilinca sitting across from the man she has just given everything to.
