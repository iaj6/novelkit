# Chapter 7 — Livia

**One-line summary:** Ilinca learns the Miliția has been asking around the mill; she confirms through Georgeta that Livia's copy went nowhere further, closing the N2 branch completely; a small release, a ledger opened.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 10 November 1984 (Day 7)  
**Setting:** The mill administrative building; the personnel-section corridor; Ilinca's office  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Resolved beat  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Confirm that the N2 branch terminates at Livia — that Livia gave no further copies to anyone — and close that branch from her mental map.

**Obstacle:** She can't ask Livia directly. She needs another pass through Georgeta (the supervisor), and that's a second contact with the same person in a week, which is one more than is comfortable.

**Beats:**

1. **The mill, morning.** The factory committee secretary — a man whose job is to sit at a desk near the entrance and know things — mentions to Ilinca, while she is picking up the November payroll forms, that there has been some unpleasantness. A Miliția officer has been speaking with people about the matter of the Petrescu girl. He says this in a way that suggests he would like Ilinca to say something in response. Ilinca says: yes, a tragic business. She takes the forms and goes back to her office.

2. **Ilinca thinks through what she knows.** Munteanu (she does not know his name yet; she thinks: the officer) has been asking around the mill for at least several days. He hasn't come to the administrative building. If he had been asking about her, she would know by now — the factory committee secretary would have told her differently. She is not yet a name on a list. She has time.

3. **The N2 branch.** She needs the confirmation on Livia. She finds Georgeta on a legitimate errand — they need to reconcile a materials-usage discrepancy from October, which is real work and gives her ten minutes of legitimate proximity. She works through the discrepancy with Georgeta at a standing table in the supplies corridor. In the last two minutes of this, she asks — sideways, briefly — whether Livia has been bothered by anything lately. Georgeta says: she hasn't been to Livia's in a week, but her daughter says Livia is fine, kept herself quiet, gave the copy to nobody.

4. **That is the confirmation.** N2 branch: N1 (Ilinca) → N2 (Georgeta) → G3 (Livia). Dead end. No further transmission. No Miliția contact. Closed. Ilinca thanks Georgeta for the materials reconciliation, which is real, and goes back to her office.

5. **At her desk, a small accounting.** She runs a column in her head. N2 branch: closed. N3 branch: closed (Elena's message through the cousin). The cellar branch: she believes it's clean. If she's right, then whatever reached Ana's household came from somewhere she didn't touch — from a later generation of the chain, passed along by people she doesn't know, through a mutation she didn't introduce. This is what she believes, and it is wrong, and she does not know it is wrong.

6. **She opens the legitimate ledger.** She does the numbers. She opens the correct columns, enters the correct figures. For an hour she is exactly what she appears to be: a bookkeeper doing her job. At the end of the hour something in her shoulders releases.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends on the small resolution: Ilinca has confirmed the N2 branch is clean, and for this afternoon, this is what she has. She opens the ledger. The numbers go in. The lamp is on. She does not know what she doesn't know.

---

## New question raised

If both the N2 and N3 branches are closed and clean, then the mutation — whatever reached Ana — must have come from somewhere else. But Ilinca cannot conceive of another source; she accounts for every copy she personally made. She has not yet asked herself whether accounting for the cellar copy requires looking at it, not just remembering it.

## Continuity notes

- The factory committee secretary is a minor character who exists to show the surveillance apparatus operating at a low level. He is NOT a Securitate officer or an informer in a dramatic sense — he is just a man who notices things and occasionally mentions them. He is, in the Romanian context, entirely ordinary.
- The chapter is the "false relief" chapter: Ilinca closes two branches and feels, briefly, a release. This release is structurally important because it is wrong. The drafter should not undermine it from inside the chapter — Ilinca's relief is earned by her logic, and her logic is sound for the data she has.
- The resolved beat ending is specifically a small resolution: not the problem solved, just one branch closed. It should feel quiet and real, not triumphant.
- Do NOT have Ilinca look at N1 in this chapter. She doesn't need to; she's thinking about branches, not the document itself.
- Georgeta's name is a placeholder — use whatever name the architect has assigned for N2's holder.
