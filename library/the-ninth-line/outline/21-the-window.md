# Chapter 21 — The Window

**One-line summary:** ACT II CLOSE — Ilinca traces one last N4-branch node; with Doina in custody for three days, she reaches the logical conclusion she has been refusing: the mutation is hers, and the answer requires N4.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 25–26 November 1984 (Days 22–23)  
**Setting:** Ilinca's office; the window overlooking the factory yard; her apartment  
**Chapter shape:** Interiority-heavy  
**Ending mode:** Turn  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Close her mental map of the N4 branch and force herself to the conclusion she has been circling.

**Obstacle:** The conclusion requires admitting she may have made an error that killed a girl. Ilinca's entire working life is built around not making errors. This is not a logical obstacle; it is the kind of obstacle that has no solution except to look at it directly.

**Beats:**

1. **Her office, early morning.** Doina Marcu has been in custody for three days. Ilinca has done her work during this time — the payroll, the materials reconciliation, the November audit preparation — and her work has been correct. This is what it looks like when Ilinca is working correctly while also not working on the thing she should be working on.

2. **She traces one final node on the N4 branch.** Through a route she's used before, she gets word about the G5 node on N4 branch (the church contact) — whether the church contact passed the copy to anyone. She did not. Dead end. The N4 branch, as Ilinca can map it, extends: N4 → G3 (sister) → G4 (bloc neighbor) → G5 (church contact) → no further. Four nodes below N4.

3. **She looks at her private map.** N2 branch: clean. N3 branch: clean. N4 branch: four nodes mapped, all descended from the cellar copy. The mutation is in the N4 branch's copies — she knows this from Munteanu's movements through the mill and from the mutant line she heard about in ch 9. The mutation is NOT in the N2 or N3 branches. The mutation is in the N4 branch.

4. **The conclusion.** If the mutation is in the N4 branch, it entered at or before N4. The nodes below N4 just propagated what they received. The node that introduced the mutation was either the cellar woman (who received N4 and then copied it herself — but that doesn't make sense, because N4 is what Ilinca gave her) or Ilinca herself, in the act of making N4. The mutation is Ilinca's.

5. **She stands at the window.** The factory yard below. November fog still at the river, two hundred meters away. She is present tense: she is standing at the window looking at the fog. She does not say the conclusion aloud. She is not the kind of person who says things aloud that she has already concluded internally. But the conclusion is there, in the column where she holds things she cannot afford to lose.

6. **What she does not yet know — and now must know.** She needs to see N4. She still hasn't seen it. She formed the hypothesis in ch 15 (the bracket might look like a 9 in poor light) and she needs to confirm it. She has been to the cellar woman yet?

7. **Evening, her apartment.** She does not take N1 out. She sits at the table. She is going to have to go to the cellar woman and ask to see the copy. If N4 has the bracket, the mutation entered somewhere below N4 (in the G3 or G4 nodes) and Ilinca is not the source. If N4 has a numbered nine, Ilinca is the source, and everything follows from that.

8. **The turn.** She stands up. She goes to the window. The turn is the resolution of the question she has been refusing: she is going to find out. She will go to the cellar woman tomorrow. The chapter ends not on the decision to act (which would be too neat) but on the impossibility of not acting.

---

## Outcome / turn

The turn is Ilinca resolving to look at N4 — not because she has courage, but because the alternative (knowing without confirming, which is the same as not knowing while believing) is incompatible with being the kind of person she is. She is a bookkeeper. She checks her columns.

---

## New question raised

If Ilinca made the error — if N4 has a numbered nine — then she is the origin of the mutation that killed Ana. What does she do with that?

## Continuity notes

- This is the last chapter of Act II. The Act II/III junction is chapters 21 and 22 being consecutive Ilinca chapters — a double Ilinca beat as the understanding arrives.
- The turn ending here is NOT the same as the cellar recollection turn in ch 24. Ch 21's turn is about the decision to confirm; ch 24's turn is about the full understanding of what happened. Ch 22 is the physical confirmation (she sees N4).
- The window: the factory yard, the fog on the Someș two hundred meters away. This is a recurring physical orientation — Ilinca's office overlooks the yard. Establish the distance between the administrative building and the river, which appears in the atmospheric descriptions.
- She does NOT make the decision to go to Munteanu in this chapter. That decision comes much later (ch 26). In ch 21 she only decides to see N4.
- Doina is in custody but Ilinca cannot act on this yet. The awareness that a girl is in a cell because of something Ilinca did — if her hypothesis is correct — is present but not stated.
