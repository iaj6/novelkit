# Chapter 2 — The Geography Book

**One-line summary:** Doina reads her copy of the paper on her break and sees the bracket is still there; she puts it back without telling anyone.

**POV:** Doina Marcu — first person past  
**Date:** 5 November 1984 (Day 2)  
**Setting:** The mill — production floor, women's break room; Doina's hostel room afterward  
**Chapter shape:** Bottle (the factory floor and break room; the hostel at the end)  
**Ending mode:** Elliptical  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Doina's goal this chapter:** Figure out what, if anything, she should do about the paper in the geography book.

**Obstacle:** She has no one to ask without naming the paper. Asking is worse than not asking.

**Beats:**

1. **Night shift, walking to the factory before dawn.** Doina's voice, first person past, establishes itself immediately: the cold on the bridge, the smell of the mill's wet-heat processes mixing with the river fog, the women ahead of her she knows by their coats before she can see their faces. She heard about Ana Petrescu — the way you hear about a fire two streets away. You don't see it, you smell what's burning. She heard this before the shift started, at the hostel, from a woman who said it carefully to the wall rather than to anyone in the room.

2. **On the production floor, the shift begins.** The loom noise makes private talk impossible. This is its own kind of relief. Doina watches women she has known for two years and thinks about what any of them might know about the paper in her geography book. She decides quickly: none of this. She does not think about it again until the break.

3. **The break room, twenty minutes.** She takes the geography book from her locker — she keeps it there as a habit; she brought it to work the day she arrived and never brought it home. She takes it to the bathroom. Nobody has ever once opened her geography book, including her. Eleven months it sat there.

4. **She reads N14 in the bathroom stall.** She opens the book, takes the folded paper out from between pages 34 and 35 of the geography of northern Transylvania, and reads it. She reads it straight through. Lines 1 through 8. Then the bracket. **FULL TEXT OF N14 IS NOT PRINTED HERE — only the bracket line is quoted.** She notices: the bracket is there. She can see it is there. She has never been able to tell anyone what line 9 says — but she can see it is bracketed. She could not have told you why that mattered, before today. Today it matters.

5. **She puts it back.** Between pages 34 and 35. She tells herself: this is still a thing I own, like the coat. What it is not is evidence of anything I did. She wants that on the record, she says to her imagined listener. She never used it. She never showed anyone. She could not recite line 9 from memory.

6. **After the shift, the hostel room.** She goes straight to her room. The geography book is in her locker at the factory. She thinks: good. She lies on her bed with her coat on and stares at the ceiling. She does not know what to do and therefore does nothing. This will not be the last time she makes this decision.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends with Doina on her bed, coat on, staring at the ceiling: the geography book is exactly where she left it, and she has no idea whether that is the right decision.

---

## New question raised

The paper exists. It has been read. Doina has seen the bracket and does not know yet why the bracket matters. She will, eventually. But not today.
