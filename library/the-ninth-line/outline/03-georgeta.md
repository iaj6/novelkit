# Chapter 3 — Georgeta

**One-line summary:** Ilinca makes oblique contact with N2's holder (the mill supervisor) and traces the N2 branch one generation further; a boy from personnel delivers an unasked-for form as the shift bell rings.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 6 November 1984 (Day 3)  
**Setting:** The mill administrative building; the women's bathroom on the production-floor corridor; Ilinca's office  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Mid-action-cut  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Confirm that N2's holder (the mill supervisor she gave the second copy to) has not been contacted and that the N2 branch is safe.

**Obstacle:** She cannot ask directly. The conversation has to look like something else. The mill supervisor (N2's holder) may not understand why Ilinca is asking and may say more than she should.

**Beats:**

1. **Ilinca's office, morning.** She enters and does the legitimate work first: the November payroll run, the signed stat de plată she needs from the floor manager. The numbers are real and she does them. This is how she manages what she cannot manage. The work is also the cover: a bookkeeper who visibly keeps working is a bookkeeper with nothing to hide.

2. **The production-floor corridor, mid-morning.** Ilinca has a reason to be on the floor — she needs a signature on a materials consumption form from the floor section chief. The mill supervisor (N2's holder) works this section. The women's bathroom is at the end of this corridor.

3. **Three minutes before a shift change in the bathroom.** Ilinca goes in when the supervisor goes in. They are alone for two minutes. The conversation: Ilinca mentions she's been thinking about whether the influenza is going around — one of the women on the floor has been ill. The supervisor understands. She says: I gave mine to my neighbor Livia. Livia on her street. Nobody else. She says it without saying what *mine* means; neither of them names the paper aloud. Ilinca says: and Livia? The supervisor says: Livia hasn't shown it to anyone. She's a private woman.

4. **Back at the desk.** Ilinca has two pieces of information: N2 is with the supervisor, the supervisor gave one copy (a G3 node on N2's branch) to Livia her neighbor. Two nodes confirmed. Both known by name. Both on a branch that does not, so far as she can trace it, connect to Ana Petrescu.

5. **She thinks about N3.** The production-floor woman she gave N3 to — Elena — works a different shift. She can't reach her today. She writes nothing. But she begins to plan the contact.

6. **Late afternoon.** A boy from the personnel office knocks and comes in with a form Ilinca did not request — a supplementary materials-usage calculation for a quarter that was already closed. She has never seen this form before. She takes it. She signs for it. When the boy is gone she looks at it again. Standard form, Ministry of Finance series, nothing unusual. She puts it in the in-tray. She does not know whether someone sent it to see if she would react.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends at the shift bell, the boy's footsteps already gone down the corridor, Ilinca holding the form — present tense, not moving, not yet. The form is probably nothing. She has been in this work fifteen years and she knows what a test feels like and she is not sure.

---

## New question raised

Is anyone watching Ilinca directly, or is this paranoia? She doesn't know. She may never know. This is the texture of the work.
