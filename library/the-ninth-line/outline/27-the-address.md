# Chapter 27 — The Address

**One-line summary:** Munteanu visits the cellar woman; her copy is gone — taken by the woman who originally gave it to her; she doesn't name her, but the direction is clear; two days remain.

**POV:** Vasile Munteanu — third person close, past tense  
**Date:** 3 December 1984 (Day 30)  
**Setting:** The cellar woman's apartment on Strada Furnicilor; the street; Munteanu's desk  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Declarative close  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Munteanu's goal this chapter:** Interview the cellar woman (N4's holder), obtain her copy, trace the chain above her to the next person.

**Obstacle:** N4 is gone. The cellar woman gave it back to "the woman who originally gave it to her" — pointing at Ilinca, but not naming Ilinca. Munteanu knows someone is one step ahead of him.

**Beats:**

1. **Strada Furnicilor, morning.** He drives to the address he confirmed through the Population Section yesterday. The building is a four-story bloc similar to all the others. He finds the flat number. He knocks.

2. **The cellar woman opens the door.** She is not surprised. She does not have the face of someone who was not expecting this. She has the face of someone who has been waiting to be asked a question she cannot fully answer.

3. **He introduces himself.** She invites him in. The apartment is single room, a kitchen alcove, a single window. Modest. She sits. He sits. He explains the matter: the Petrescu investigation. He asks if she has a document that has been circulating in the community.

4. **She tells him it's gone.** She says: I had something like that, yes. He asks: do you still have it? She says: no. He asks: what happened to it? She says: the woman who gave it to me came and took it back. She says this as though she were answering a simple question about the location of a cup. He writes this down.

5. **He asks when.** She says: two days ago. He writes: two days ago. That is the 1st of December. He visited the Population Section on December 1 and confirmed this address. On the same day, someone took the copy back. This is not a coincidence.

6. **He asks who the woman is.** The cellar woman says: I don't know her family name. He asks: what do you know? She says: she works at the mill. She's older than me. She gave me the copy in the autumn of last year, in November. He asks: where did she give it to you? The cellar woman hesitates. She says: I'd rather not say. He says: I understand. He writes it down anyway: *refused to specify location of original transfer. Consistent with clandestine transmission.*

7. **Mill. Female. Older.** He has this profile from his own working notes in ch 23. He now has the same profile confirmed by a witness. He is not at the G1 node yet — the cellar woman won't name Ilinca — but he knows the G1 figure is a mill-connected woman who is now aware of the investigation (she took the copy back when she heard the investigation was closing in, or she had some other reason). He does not know her name.

8. **On the street.** He stands outside the building. Two days. The woman who made the document took N4 back, two days before his visit. She is somewhere in the mill. She has the master (N1) somewhere, and now she has N4. She knows the investigation is reaching her. She is either going to run, or go to ground, or come to him.

9. **His desk.** He sits down. He writes, in his working notes: *G1 figure: mill-connected. Female. Has recovered G2 document. Aware of investigation. Two days to deadline.* He stares at the page. He has two days. He picks up the phone to call his captain and then puts it back down.

---

## Outcome / turn

The declarative close: he writes *Two days.* in his working notes and closes the notebook. He does not call his captain. He waits. He knows — with a flatness that surprises him — that either she will come to him or he will have to send the file upstairs with Doina Marcu's name on it. He does not want to send it up. He waits.

---

## New question raised

The G1 figure has N4 and N1. She is aware of the investigation. She took the copy back forty-eight hours before Munteanu reached it. She is going to do something. What?

**Ladder:** Munteanu's node count.  
**Munteanu gains:** 1 node — the cellar woman's testimony is the node gain for this chapter; it confirms the G1 figure exists, is mill-connected, and is ahead of the investigation.  
**Ilinca's count:** 12 (unchanged).  
**Munteanu's count after this chapter:** 9.  
**Day:** 30.  
**Foreclosure:** Munteanu now knows the G1 figure is aware of the investigation and has recovered N4. He has two days. If she doesn't come to him voluntarily, he will charge Doina Marcu with the document on file and send the case up. Doina will be held. The G1 figure will disappear into the mill. The case will close wrong.

## Continuity notes

- The cellar woman does NOT name Ilinca. She says "the woman who gave it to me" and "she works at the mill" and "older than me." She refuses to be more specific. She is protecting herself and, to some degree, protecting Ilinca. Or she simply does not know Ilinca's surname.
- Munteanu's flatness: the brief's exemplar passage for Munteanu ends on "a flatness that surprised him." Use this register. He has concluded something uncomfortable and he is doing it anyway.
- The declarative close should be short and physical: a notebook closed, a phone replaced on its cradle without making the call, a specific number written. Not dramatic. Flat.
- The dramatic irony: the reader knows Ilinca is going to come to him tomorrow. Munteanu does not. He is waiting for something to break without knowing it's already in motion.
- The cellar woman is slightly protected by this chapter: she has given Munteanu enough (the mill connection, the approximate profile) but not the name. Her partial cooperation puts her in a middle ground.

## Quotation sites (this chapter)

This is a quotation from his own notes, not from any copy. It represents the document appearing through its negative: the shape of the person who made it, as inferred from the evidence.
