# Chapter 22 — Nine Numbered

**One-line summary:** Ilinca goes to the cellar woman; asks to see the copy; holds N4; reads the numbered line 9 instead of the bracket; gives it back; walks home through the fog knowing what she did.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 27 November 1984 (Day 24)  
**Setting:** The cellar woman's neighborhood (other side of the river from the mill); the street home  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Literary fade  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** See N4. Confirm or deny whether it has the bracket.

**Obstacle:** Going to the cellar woman directly is a visible action. The cellar woman knows Ilinca only as the person who gave her the copy; if Munteanu finds out they spoke, the connection becomes legible. But Ilinca has no other way.

**Beats:**

1. **The walk across the river.** She crosses the bridge in the morning. Fog on the water below. The Someș in late November: brown, wide, cold. She has taken a legitimate errand as her reason to be in this part of the city — she needs a form from the county finance office on the other side. The form is real. She will get the form.

2. **She finds the cellar woman's building.** She knows where it is; she has known for fourteen months. She has never gone back. She goes up the stairwell — unlit, cold concrete — and knocks on the door she remembers.

3. **The conversation.** The cellar woman opens the door. She is not exactly surprised and not exactly unsurprised. She recognizes Ilinca; Ilinca can see this. They speak in the corridor, not inside. Ilinca says: I need to see the copy I made for you. Just to check something. The cellar woman doesn't ask what she needs to check. She goes inside and comes back with a folded piece of paper.

4. **Ilinca reads N4.** She unfolds it. Lines 1 through 8: correct, as far as she can see quickly. She reaches line 9.

   N4's line 9 reads:  
   `9. 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why. Do not wait for the morning.`

   It has a number. Not a bracket — a number. The text is correct, every word. The formatting is wrong. The ninth item is a step in the list, not an override of the list. She made it a step.

5. **She reads it twice.** She does not say anything. She folds N4 back along its crease and hands it to the cellar woman. She says: thank you. She does not explain. The cellar woman does not ask her to.

6. **The walk back.** She walks back across the bridge. The Someș below. She is not crying. She does not do that in a public place. She is counting.

7. **What she is counting.** Line nine. The number in front of it. The bracket she did not draw. The word *morning* that does not appear on N4 but that appears, two generations downstream, because the woman who made N11 read nine numbered lines in a list and made a tidy list of nine numbered steps, and step nine said take her in, and step eight said *a fever that will not come down by morning*, and the woman made the logical inference and wrote *in the morning*. That woman was not careless. She was tidy. She was reading nine steps and turning them into a better nine steps.

8. **At her door.** She stops before going up to her apartment. She is present tense: she stands at the door. She knows. She knows what she did in the cellar and she knows what it became.

## Outcome / turn

The literary fade: Ilinca walking, crossing the bridge, reaching her door. She knows. The fog is on the river. The chapter does not state what she knows; it renders her walking home with the knowledge.

## Continuity notes

- **N4's text is given in the brief.** The drafter should quote N4's line 9 exactly as written in the brief and canon: `9. 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why. Do not wait for the morning.` This is the first time N4's line 9 appears in the book (it will appear again in ch 28). It is NOT a full printing of N4 here — just the key line. The full text of N4 appears only in ch 28.
- The drafter should make the difference between N1 and N4 legible in this scene: N1 has a bracket on the margin (`[ 7 or 8 — ... ]`); N4 has a number in front (`9. 7 or 8 — ...`). Every word after the bracket/number is identical. The change is formatting only, and it is fatal.
- Ilinca does NOT take N4. She gives it back. She will return for it in ch 26. This moment is about seeing, not possessing.
- The cellar woman: never named. Ilinca's interior monologue does not use her name even now. She is "the woman from the cellar" or "she" or, most precisely, not named at all.
- The walk-home beat: Ilinca does not speak to anyone, does not stop, does not show anything. The knowledge is entirely interior. The prose renders it through physical detail (the bridge, the river, the door), not through statement.
- The drafter should NOT state the book's argument in Ilinca's interior. She understands what she did; she does not articulate what it means in terms of state culpability, the conditions of the work, etc. The argument is carried by the tree. Ilinca carries only the fact.
