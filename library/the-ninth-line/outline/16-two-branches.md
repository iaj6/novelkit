# Chapter 16 — Two Branches

**One-line summary:** Munteanu traces back to the G3 node on the N4 branch (the laundry on Strada Mihai); with five copies he can now see the two-branch structure of the document tree; his captain calls about the deadline.

**POV:** Vasile Munteanu — third person close, past tense  
**Date:** 20 November 1984 (Day 17)  
**Setting:** The laundry on Strada Mihai; the Miliția station; Munteanu's desk  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Declarative close  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Munteanu's goal this chapter:** Interview the G3 woman (the cellar woman's sister) at the laundry, collect her copy, and understand whether it carries the same line 9 mutation.

**Obstacle:** This woman is a G3 node. Her copy predates N11 by one generation. If her copy also says "in the morning," then the mutation entered at or before G3. If her copy says something different, the mutation entered below her — between her and N11.

**Beats:**

1. **The laundry on Strada Mihai, morning.** Munteanu arrives before the morning delivery rush. The laundry is a long room with a condensation problem; in November this means a smell of damp and industrial soap. He asks for the woman by name. She comes out from behind the wash line.

2. **The interview.** She is not as frightened as the others have been; she is something else — wary in the way of someone who has been through official questions before, not recently. She sits with him at a folding table near the window. He explains the matter: the Petrescu case. She says she heard. He asks if she has a piece of paper, a document concerning health, that was passed through the community. She says yes. He asks her for it.

3. **She gives him her copy.** A G3 node on the N4 branch — let the architect assign the specific record. He puts it in the evidence envelope without reading it in front of her.

4. **He asks where she got it.** She says: from her sister. He writes the name of the sister. The sister lives on Strada Furnicilor; he notes the address.

5. **At the station, he reads the G3 copy.** He now has four copies on the poisoned/N4 branch: N17 (G5), the G4 copies, N11 (G4), and now the G3. He lays them out. He reads line 9 on each. The G3 copy also says *in the morning*. This means the mutation entered at or before the G3 node.

6. **The two-branch recognition.** He has also recently (through his general mill canvassing) collected another copy from a different part of the network — a copy with the bracket intact, from someone on the N3 branch who gave it up under questioning. He lays this copy beside the others. Line 9 on the bracket copy is different: it says *do not wait for the morning*. Two versions of line 9. Copies from two different lineages. He has both. He can now see, without drawing a diagram, that the tree has at least two branches from a common ancestor. One branch carries the morning mutation; the other does not. The mutation entered somewhere on the morning branch, before the oldest copy he has from that branch.

7. **The captain's call.** His captain, from the Inspectoratul Județean in Cluj-Napoca, calls in the late afternoon. How is the Petrescu matter going. Munteanu says it is progressing. He has identified the document chain and is tracing it toward its origin. The captain says: good. He also says: the administrative deadline for filing a determination is the fifth of December. Munteanu says he is aware. The captain says: yes, but I am saying it because sometimes awareness and action are different things. Munteanu says he understands. He hangs up the telephone.

8. **He counts.** November 20. December 5. Fifteen days.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends on the counted days: fifteen. He writes it in his working notes, not the formal dosar penal — just the number. He closes the notebook. The chapter ends there, on that flat fact.

---

## New question raised

With the two-branch structure now visible, Munteanu needs to trace above the G3 node. The G3's sister is the next link. But his captain has just reminded him that the administrative clock is running.

## Continuity notes

- Munteanu has also picked up a clean-branch copy (from the N3 branch, from his general mill canvassing) during this period. He has it but hasn't focused on it. This chapter is when it becomes significant — it's the other branch, with the bracket. He doesn't yet know which is the correct one or why they differ.
- The G3 copy on N4 branch also says "in the morning" — confirming the mutation predates G3 on the N4 branch. This means it entered at G2 (N4 itself) or was a property of N1. Munteanu doesn't know which yet.
- The cellar woman's address (Strada Furnicilor or similar): Munteanu has it written. He plans to visit. He will not visit until ch 27, because he's still working other leads (including toward Doina on the N3 branch).
- The deadline: December 5 is now spoken aloud in the text for the first time, through the captain's call. Fifteen days. The reader and Munteanu now both know the number.
- The declarative close: "fifteen days" or just the counting. Not a dramatic line. The flatness is the character.
