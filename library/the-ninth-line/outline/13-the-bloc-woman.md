# Chapter 13 — The Bloc Woman

**One-line summary:** Ilinca traces a G4 node on the N4 branch (the sister's neighbor); her map of the N4 branch is extending into territory Munteanu is already working; she closes the ledger and does not open it again that evening.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 16 November 1984 (Day 13)  
**Setting:** Ilinca's office; the laundry on Strada Mihai (exterior only); the walk home  
**Chapter shape:** Interiority-heavy  
**Ending mode:** Literary fade  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Trace the G3 node on N4 (the cellar woman's sister at the laundry) one level further — did the sister make any copies of her own?

**Obstacle:** She cannot approach the laundry directly. The laundry is in the part of town where Munteanu has been working. She needs to be careful about being seen anywhere near it.

**Beats:**

1. **Her desk, morning.** She has the pharmacy acquaintance's note from yesterday: the cellar woman's sister works the laundry on Strada Mihai. She needs to know what the sister did with her copy. Did she keep it? Pass it on?

2. **Thinking about the route.** She can't use the pharmacy acquaintance again so quickly — two contacts in two days on the same subject is too visible. She thinks about who else she knows in that part of the city. She has a distant relation — a second cousin's husband — who delivers bread to the laundry in the mornings. This is a real person; she has seen him twice in three years at family occasions. She can reach him.

3. **Contact via the bread delivery man.** She does not call him — telephones are a risk. She writes a note on a bread-delivery chit (she has mill paper that looks administrative) and gives it to a boy at the mill gate who makes deliveries to the market district. The note asks the bread man to mention to the laundry woman that her cousin's wife says hello. The response, if any, will come back the same way.

4. **Waiting.** She works through the afternoon. The November payroll is due at the county finance office by Friday. She does the payroll. The adding machine counts. The figures go in the correct columns.

5. **The response.** By late afternoon: the bread man's response, passed back through the boy: the laundry woman said she gave hers to a neighbor on her bloc, a woman with a child, last spring. That's all. So: the cellar woman's sister (G3 on N4 branch) gave one copy to a bloc neighbor (G4 on N4 branch). That G4 copy exists. The G4 node is a woman with a child in the laundry sister's bloc.

6. **Ilinca accounts for this.** N1 → N4 (cellar copy) → sister's copy (G3) → bloc neighbor (G4). She now has four nodes on the N4 branch. She does not yet know what happened below the G4 node. She doesn't know how many more copies were made from the G4 node or whether they carry the bracket or not. She is tracing forward; she cannot see ahead of herself.

7. **Walking home past the laundry street.** She does not go down Strada Mihai. She walks the long way around. She passes the end of the street and glances. The laundry's light is on. She walks on.

8. **At home, the lamp.** She sits in the kitchen. She does not take N1 out. She holds the shape of the N4 branch in her mind: four nodes. She does not know if the mutation is on this branch. She does not know which of the four nodes might have introduced it. She closes her eyes. She does not open the second lamp.

---

## Outcome / turn

The chapter ends quietly — Ilinca with her eyes closed in the kitchen, the N4 branch extending four nodes in her mental map, and the question of where the mutation entered still unanswered. The literary fade is the closing of the ledger she didn't open and the lamp she didn't light.

---

## New question raised

The N4 branch now has four nodes Ilinca can trace. At which point did the formatting change — the bracket becoming a numbered line? She still doesn't know. She doesn't even know the bracket became a numbered line anywhere on *her* branch. She is thinking about the wrong problem (downstream corruption) when the error was upstream (her own hand).

## Continuity notes

- The G4 node on N4 branch (the bloc neighbor with a child): this is a new character who appears only in Ilinca's accounting. She is never seen on the page; she is a node in the map.
- The bread delivery man: a real detail, not a plot device. Ilinca uses what is available — the informal channels that ordinary life in a Romanian mill town provides. He is not an ally; he just delivered a message.
- The literary fade: Ilinca sitting in the dark kitchen, the ledger unopened, the lamp unlighted. This should have the quality of a woman holding something she doesn't want to put down but can't act on. Not despair; not calm. A suspended state.
- Strada Mihai: establish this as the street with the laundry where the cellar woman's sister works. This address becomes important in ch 25 (Munteanu locates it) and ch 27 (he visits it).
- The bread chit / delivery note: a lovely illustration of how communication happens in this world — through the surface of legitimate transactions.
- Note the parallel: Munteanu in ch 12 wrote down the laundry on Strada Mihai as his next address. Ilinca in this chapter walks past the laundry. They are converging on the same G3 node without knowing it.
