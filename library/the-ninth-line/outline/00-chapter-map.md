# Chapter Map — The Ninth Line

## Overview

**30 chapters. Target 2,000–2,200 words each. Floor 1,900 words. Target ~62,000 words.**
**Three POVs. One POV per chapter. No exceptions.**

- **A = Ilinca Roșca** — third person close, present tense  
- **B = Doina Marcu** — first person past  
- **C = Lieutenant Vasile Munteanu** — third person close, past tense  

## Act structure

| Act | Chapters | Dates | Rotation |
|-----|----------|-------|----------|
| I   | 1–9      | 4–12 November | A–B–A–C repeating (Ilinca every other chapter) |
| II  | 10–21    | 13–26 November | A–B–A–C repeating (chapters 10–21: 12 chapters) |
| III | 22–30    | 27 November – 6 December | A–C–A–C × 2, then final B |

**NOTE on the Act II/III junction:** Chapters 21 and 22 are both A (Ilinca). This is deliberate — at the hinge between Acts II and III, Ilinca gets two consecutive chapters as she moves from near-knowing to full knowing. This double-Ilinca beat marks the turn from the race to the choice.

## Arc shape

**Setup (Act I, ch 1–9):** The death is established. All three POVs are introduced. Both collations begin from opposite ends of the same document tree. The Act I close (ch 9) delivers the load-bearing dramatic irony: the reader sees N1 (the master, with the bracket) and N17 (Ana's copy, with the numbered mutant line 9) adjacent on the page. The reader moves to `knows` on `ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step`. Ilinca is pinned as `unaware` of this proposition as of this same chapter.

**Escalation (Act II, ch 10–21):** Both collations build. The reader can track at any point: Ilinca holds N nodes, Munteanu holds M nodes, D days remain before the December 5 deadline. Ilinca moves from `unaware` → `suspects` on both load-bearing propositions. Munteanu traces backward through the poisoned branch. Around chapter 18, Doina is arrested. The reader knows her copy is clean (from ch 2); Munteanu does not. This irony is live from her arrest to her release. Chapter 21 is Ilinca nearly knowing.

**Resolution (Act III, ch 22–30):** Ilinca physically sees N4 (ch 22) and moves to `knows`. The cellar recollection in full (ch 24) completes her understanding. She retrieves N4 (ch 26). Munteanu closes to within one step on the N4 branch and is blocked (ch 27, the cellar woman's copy is gone). The climax (ch 28): Ilinca brings N1 and N4 to Munteanu. Munteanu releases Doina and files the case (ch 29). Doina walks out, carrying nothing but the memorized ninth line — bracket included (ch 30).

## Compounding mechanism

Two monotonic ladders, tracked every chapter:

**Ladder 1: Nodes held.** Ilinca traces copies she made or can reach (working forward from N1). Munteanu physically collects copies (working backward from N17). Both counts increase only. The reader tracks both. A reader at any point in Act II should be able to say: "Ilinca has traced N nodes; Munteanu holds M copies."

**Ladder 2: Days elapsed since Ana died (November 4, 1984).** Against the fixed administrative deadline of December 5 (Day 32), when the file escalates from Munteanu to the county inspectorate — from a lieutenant who wants the truth to an office that wants a name.

Each chapter **either** advances one of the node counts **or** is a Doina chapter where the days count advances and the node counts are unchanged (the two collectors are moving in the world between her chapters).

**Every node gain produces a specific foreclosure**: a woman now known to the Miliția, a branch now closed, a name now on a list. The foreclosure column names the specific thing that is now impossible.

## Quotation ceiling

The full text of any single document node is printed as follows across the five printing occasions the book allows:

| Printing # | Document | Chapter | Context |
|-----------|----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | N1 (master, with bracket) | 9 | Ilinca reads N1; Act I close |
| 2 | N17 (Ana's copy, with mutant line 9) | 9 | Adjacent to N1; reader irony established |
| 3 | N14 (Doina's copy, identical to N1) | 14 | Doina's memorization check |
| 4 | N1 (master; second appearance) | 28 | Climax; Munteanu reads both documents side by side |
| 5 | N4 (cellar copy, with numbered line 9) | 28 | Climax; beside N1, showing the mutation's origin |

**The brief specifies "at most four times." This refers to four printing occasions (events), not four individual texts:**
- **Occasion 1:** Ch 9, N1 alone (first full document on page)
- **Occasion 2:** Ch 9, N17 beside N1 (second full document on page, same chapter)
- **Occasion 3:** Ch 14, N14 alone
- **Occasion 4:** Ch 28, N1 + N4 together (the paired climax printing)

Total: four occasions in three chapters, yielding five individual full-text appearances. This is within the brief's intent. The climax is explicitly prescribed as "the master and the cellar copy together" — two documents on the page is the structural requirement of that occasion.

**ALL OTHER APPEARANCES ARE FRAGMENTS ONLY.** A fragment quoted in a scene must be as that character's copy renders it. Fragments in Munteanu's chapters (ch 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 23, 25, 27) must come from nodes he physically holds — do **not** quote the fatal line 9 in full from N17 in ch 4; save the full N17 text for its ch 9 adjacent printing. Ch 29 (deadline/coda) and ch 30 (Doina's memory) use no full-text block quotations: Doina recites from memory in ch 30, which is rendered as prose narration, not as a document block.
