# Chapter 26 — Recovery

**One-line summary:** Ilinca goes back to the cellar woman and asks to take N4; walks home with N4 in her coat pocket and N1 waiting in the ledger; she has both documents.

**POV:** Ilinca Roșca — third person close, present tense  
**Date:** 2 December 1984 (Day 29)  
**Setting:** The cellar woman's building; the bridge; Ilinca's apartment  
**Chapter shape:** Multi-scene  
**Ending mode:** Literary fade  

---

## Goal / obstacle / beats

**Ilinca's goal this chapter:** Retrieve N4 from the cellar woman. She needs physical possession of both N1 and N4 before she goes to Munteanu.

**Obstacle:** Taking the copy back requires explaining to the cellar woman that it is no longer safe for her to hold it — without explaining what Ilinca plans to do with it.

**Beats:**

1. **Her apartment, morning.** She has made the decision. She is not revisiting it. She knows what she is going to do and she knows what it will cost. She puts on her coat. The day is clear for once — no fog, the Someș visible from her window.

2. **The decision she is not revisiting.** This chapter is not about the decision; the decision was made in ch 24. This chapter is about execution: taking N4 from the cellar woman. She walks through what she will need to say. She does not need to say much. She will say: I need to take the copy back, to protect you. This is true. It is also not the whole truth. The cellar woman will understand what she can understand, and she will not ask for more.

3. **The cross-river walk.** She crosses the bridge again. Clear sky, cold. The Someș is visible — brown, wide, moving. She has not been this way twice in two weeks for eleven years. She knows this.

4. **The cellar woman's building.** She knocks. The cellar woman opens the door. She is not surprised to see Ilinca again. She has probably been expecting something like this since the last visit. Ilinca says: I need to take the copy back. It's not safe for you to have it anymore. She does not say why it is not safe. The cellar woman goes inside. She comes back with N4.

5. **Ilinca takes N4.** She folds it the same way it was folded when the cellar woman handed it over. She puts it in her coat pocket, against her chest. She says: thank you. She says: do not mention this visit to anyone. The cellar woman says: I know. Ilinca goes.

6. **The walk back.** She has N4 in her pocket and N1 in the ledger at the mill. She now has both. She thinks about what is in her pocket. The numbered line 9. The words that are correct and in the wrong form. She thinks about what will happen when she produces both documents to Munteanu.

7. **She stops on the bridge.** She is present tense, on the bridge, the river below. She thinks about Doina Marcu in the cell. Ten days. She thinks about the deadline: December 5. Three days. She does not think about what happens to her afterward. She thinks about the documents. She has them. She knows what they prove.

8. **Her apartment, evening.** She does not take N4 out again. It is in her coat pocket and her coat is on the hook. N1 is in the ledger at the mill. Tomorrow she will go to the mill, take N1 from the ledger, and go to the Miliția station. She does not make a list of this. She holds it in the same column where she holds everything she cannot afford to lose.

---

## Outcome / turn

The literary fade: Ilinca in her apartment, the coat on the hook, N4 in the pocket. The lamp on. She is quiet. She knows what comes next and she does not spend additional time on it.

---

## New question raised

She has the documents. She goes to Munteanu tomorrow. What is Doina doing tonight?

## Continuity notes

- The cellar woman's protection: by taking N4 back, Ilinca removes the most incriminating physical evidence from the cellar woman's possession. This is a small act of care. It is also the precondition for the climax.
- The bridge beat: Ilinca stopping on the bridge over the Someș is a structural parallel to Doina's opening beat in ch 2 (Doina on the bridge to the factory). Two women, the same river, different purposes.
- She does NOT take N1 from the ledger today. N1 is still at the mill in the ledger spine. She will collect it tomorrow morning before going to the station. The drafter should be clear about this: she has N4 in her pocket; N1 is still at work.
- The literary fade should be quiet — a woman sitting in a coat's shadow, the lamp on, the decision already made. Not grief, not relief. Something in between those that doesn't have a name.
- The cellar woman says very little. She says "I know" — which covers everything. She does not need to know the full picture to know the nature of the conversation.
