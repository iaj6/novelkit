# Calibration sample — iter 1
## Chapter 1 opening, Ilinca POV

---

The numbers come up short by twenty-three lei. Third time. The error is hers.

She reruns the column. The adding machine is mechanical; its handle returns with a snap. The discrepancy holds.

In the corridor outside, two women are talking in the low voice people use after four o'clock. Ilinca does not look up. The name she catches is *Petrescu*.

She reruns the column.

Petrescu is not an unusual name. There is a Petrescu on Strada Gării with a daughter-in-law who is nineteen. She knows this from the payroll. Not from interest.

She keeps her hand on the handle. The hallway goes quiet.

The error is on line eight of the column: a transposition, six where the nine should be. She corrects it in pencil, writes the total in the margin, dates it. The ledger closes the same way it opens — two-finger pressure, no bend to the spine.

She stays at her desk until the building empties. This is not unusual. She often stays. The night janitor has a key and she has a key, and there is no reason a bookkeeper cannot work late on a Thursday.

At five o'clock the heat goes off. By half past she can see her breath.

---

The 1983 ledger is on the second shelf of the back cabinet. She pulls it by the spine. She does not look around first. Looking around is the thing that looks wrong.

She opens it to page forty-one. The folded paper is there. It has been there since last October — she pressed it flat with her thumb, returned the ledger to the shelf, and went home. She takes it out with two fingers, careful of the crease.

The lamp is a forty-watt. She sets the paper in its circle and reads from the top. Line one. Line two. She knows it well enough to recite it, but reading is checking and checking takes time. She reads to line seven: *Two cloths in one hour is too many.* Line eight: *A fever that will not come down by morning is too long.* At the bottom of line eight the bracket begins. She reads what is inside it. She folds the paper back along the same creases and holds it in both hands for a moment before she sets it on the desk.

The bracket is correct. The instruction inside the bracket is correct.

She takes a breath and begins to count.
