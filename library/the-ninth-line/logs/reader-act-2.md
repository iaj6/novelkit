# Reader — Act 2 Assessment (Chapters 11–20)

## Brief-adherence checks for this act

1. **POV legibility at first sentence?** Yes, without exception. Ilinca's chapters open in present-tense procedural close-third ("She does the variance three times"); Doina's open in first-person-past direct address ("The district heating had come on for half an hour in the night"); Munteanu's open in deliberate past close-third with investigative texture ("The two copies occupied the right side of his desk"). The three registers are distinguishable before the second sentence in every chapter.

2. **Both node-count ladders visible?** Yes. Ilinca's count advances in each of her chapters: 5→6 (ch. 11), 6→7 (ch. 13), 7→8 (ch. 15), 8→9 (ch. 17). Munteanu's count advances: 2→3 (ch. 12), 3→4+ (ch. 16, including the canvassed bracket copy), 4→6 (ch. 20). A reader can state both counts at any point in the act without being told them explicitly.

3. **Day count legible?** Yes. The individual chapter dates carry the clock, and key markers are embedded: "Day 12" surfaces in ch. 15 via the canteen conversation; "15 days" is stated explicitly in ch. 16 when the captain calls; "November 24-25, 10 days remain" closes ch. 20.

4. **Act I irony properly established?** Confirmed from Act I groundwork: the reader holds `ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step` entering this act, and the irony is live and visible throughout — most sharply in ch. 16 and ch. 20, where Munteanu reads both versions of line 9 side by side and records the difference as a formatting variable. He has the murder weapon in his notes and does not understand what it is. The gap is functioning.

5. **Safety constraint?** Holding without exception. Every quoted fragment is aftercare only. No chapter acquires method, substance, dose, or procedure at any node.

6. **Fear named?** Near-clean. No instance of "she was afraid" or equivalent. Ch. 14 offers the act's best handling of this constraint: "Eight has the word *morning* in it. Since the beginning of November everything with *morning* in it has acquired a quality I do not have a name for." The refusal to name the feeling is itself in voice. One possible near-miss: ch. 15's final line ("she cannot tell — in the account she keeps, in the column she has been maintaining for thirteen months without a single entry she could not defend — whether what she holds under N4 is a bracket or a nine") names an epistemic gap, not an emotion, and is clean.

## What is working

**Voice discipline.** This is the act's primary achievement and it holds across all ten chapters without detectable drift. Ilinca's chapters run entirely in columns and reconciliations; her interiority is expressed as tallies, entries, nodes counted (ch. 13: "Five, and now this: six"; ch. 19: "Munteanu: three. The two counts have been moving in the same direction all month"). Doina's chapters are digressive, honest, retrospectively self-aware in exactly the nineteen-year-old register the brief specified. Munteanu's chapters are deliberate and orderly in the pipeline's default literary voice. The irony that the state's investigator speaks in the most artful register is never pointed out and never needs to be.

**The arrest scene (ch. 18)** is the act's best chapter and one of the book's likely centerpieces. Doina's account of going to the door without looking back, the geography book staying in its locker, the sentence "every sentence of it was true, in the way that a set of true sentences can describe a true thing and leave the whole of the true thing sitting in the middle of them, in the space the sentences make around it, untouched" — this is the exact register the brief demanded: nineteen, honest, retroactively clear-eyed, and not self-exculpating. The pacing of the interrogation scene (measured waiting, specific lies that are technically truths) is controlled throughout.

**The lamp-tilt scene (ch. 17).** Ilinca takes N1 from the ledger, looks at the bracket in forty watts, then tilts the paper until the lower horizontal stroke goes into shadow — physically re-creating the cellar conditions in her kitchen. This is the book's central question rendered as a physical act of testing, not as emotion. It advances the thread (suspicion to near-certainty) without naming the reckoning. It's exactly how this character's mind works.

**Thread development is clean.** Thread 1 moves from false confidence to working suspicion to near-knowledge on schedule. Thread 2's silence is formally enacted in ch. 18, with the crucial moment that Doina does not look back at anything in the room — a physical enactment of the decision before it becomes a decision. Thread 3's deadline goes from implicit to explicit in ch. 16 (captain's call). Thread 4's wrong frame is preserved exactly: Munteanu sees the bracket/numbered distinction in ch. 16 and ch. 20, records it as a formatting variable, asks "which format is the source" (the genealogical question), never asks what the ninth position means for the woman following it. Thread 5 completes the knowledge-to-memory transfer in ch. 14.

**The compounding mechanism has no dead chapters.** Each chapter closes one option: a branch is confirmed clean, a node is now known to the militia, a woman is in state custody, an address is written in a notebook that will not lose it.

## What is not working

**Desk-layout scene repetition (chs. 12, 16, 20).** All three Munteanu chapters in this act involve him laying copies across his desk, weighting corners with what is at hand, reading each copy in turn, and making a two-tier note (working notebook vs. formal file). The choreography is identical across all three. The brief's own exemplar passage describes exactly this scene ("He laid the four sheets side by side on the desk and weighted the corners with whatever was nearest"), and ch. 16 reproduces that framing nearly verbatim. Each instance advances something new — ch. 12 establishes the line-9 divergence, ch. 16 reveals the two-branch structure, ch. 20 distinguishes the three-way categorization. But the procedural container is the same each time, and by ch. 20 the reader has learned to expect it. The scene has become a template rather than a discovery. A single desk-layout scene, or at most two, would carry more weight than three.

**Literary-fade ending dominance.** Of the ten chapters, at least five to six end in atmospheric quiet: lamp going off, fog sitting on the street, heat coming on briefly, eyes closed at the kitchen table. The brief explicitly licenses "quiet endings where the chapter wants them" and the ending-mode mandate is "mixed by design." Ch. 18 (hard cut on Munteanu reaching for his pen) and ch. 15 (elliptical close on the bracket-or-nine question) offer genuine variety. But the pattern in this act tilts substantially toward quiet fade, and a reader who has internalized the book's clock will notice several chapters in a row ending in the same mode of held suspension. Not a structural failure, but an act-level texture that is heavier than the brief's "mixed" instruction intended.

**Potential document-drift to verify (ch. 14, N14, line 6).** Doina's chapter quotes N14 in full: line 6 reads "Burn nothing until she is well. Keep and wash." The canonical N1 text reads "Burn nothing until she is well. Wash and keep." The word order is reversed. This is either an intentional incidental variant (the brief permits these on non-terminal lines) or an in-prose drift from the registered N14 canonical text. The brief is explicit: "Before re-quoting any node in a later chapter, `read_record` it and reproduce it exactly." A reader checking this variant against the registered N14 record will either find it matches (in which case it is an intentional variant, not a problem) or find it does not (in which case it is a continuity-fact error). This needs verification against the registered `copy-n14` record before the manuscript goes further. If the registered text reads "Wash and keep" and the chapter reads "Keep and wash," this is a continuity-fact finding.

## In-act pacing

The act maintains forward motion throughout. The chapters are dense but not congested; the payroll scenes and the bread-relay and the canteen eavesdrop are doing procedural work that also functions as texture and delay — the reader feels the grinding effort of Ilinca's collation alongside its progress. The arrest scene in ch. 18 is the act's hinge, correctly positioned at chapter 18 of 20, and it elevates the stakes for the Ilinca chapters on either side. Ch. 19 is the act's strongest payoff for the Ilinca thread: she names, in accounting language, the arithmetic she has been refusing to finish. The act closes with Munteanu one step from Dorobanților and both collectors' next moves clearly staged. Pacing is sound.
