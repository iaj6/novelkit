# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-the-ledger-spine** — Ilinca hears Ana Petrescu has died at the district hospital and privately counts the three copies she made from the master, including one made in haste in a coal cellar.
- **02-the-geography-book** — Doina reads N14 on her break, sees the bracket is intact, returns the paper to the geography book in her locker, and tells no one.
- **03-georgeta** — Ilinca confirms the N2 branch (Georgeta and neighbor Livia) is clean; plans Friday contact with Elena; receives an unasked-for personnel form that may or may not be a test.
- **04-the-file-opens** — Munteanu opens the Petrescu file, collects N17 from the sister-in-law and a second copy from a neighbor, and sees the two copies differ by one word in line three — inherited, not identical.
- **05-elena** — Ilinca traces N3 through her cousin to Elena Florescu, confirms the branch clean, and lies down almost certain the cellar copy is correct — the false confidence now complete across all three branches she seeded.
- **06-the-good-coat** — Doina spends her day off reciting N14 from memory in a locked hostel room, then spots a man in an official coat taking notes near two mill women; she goes home and sits with the word "fairly."
- **07-livia** — Ilinca confirms the N2 branch terminates at Livia with no further transmission, closes the branch, and spends the afternoon doing correct payroll figures under an off lamp.
- **08-copies-laid-out** — Munteanu collects N11 from Lucia Gal, lays three copies side by side, confirms the morning instruction is upstream of all, and leaves for the next name.
- **09-the-bracket** — Ilinca learns the exact wording of the mutant line nine; she reads N1 in full beside her reconstruction of N17, and the reader now holds both texts — while Ilinca concludes the error is downstream of everything she made.
- **10-the-outer-ring** — Doina works the night shift on Day 10; Marta tells her Floarea and Geta were called in; she walks home along the fog-covered Someș not knowing if not-yet means not-going-to.
- **11-the-sister** — Ilinca traces the N4 branch through a pharmacy acquaintance and learns the cellar woman has a sister working the laundry on Strada Mihai — a sixth node she hadn't accounted for.
- **12-fourth-generation** — Munteanu traces Lucia Gal's referral to Viorica Damian (N10, G3 node, Strada Mihai), documents the line-9 mutation in his working notes, and writes the cellar woman's address on Dorobanților — one step from G2.
- **13-the-bloc-woman** — Ilinca traces the N4 branch one node further via a bread-delivery relay, learns of a bloc neighbor with a child (node 7), and walks past the Strada Mihai laundry's lit window without knowing Munteanu was already there.
- **14-the-ash** — Doina tests herself on N14 from memory, burns the test paper in the hostel bathroom, confirms the whole document including the bracket is in her head.
- **15-the-name-on-the-list** — Ilinca learns Munteanu has reached G4 on the N4 branch; discovers an unmapped G4 on the N3 branch; and sits in her kitchen unable to confirm whether she drew a bracket or a nine in the cellar copy.
- **16-two-branches** — Munteanu collects N10 from Viorica Damian, sees the two-branch structure (numbered-9 vs bracket), and learns from his captain the December 5 deadline — fifteen days.
- **17-nine-or-bracket** — Ilinca learns of a G5 extension on the N4 branch, reaches node-count 9, and shows herself by lamplight what the bracket looked like in the cellar dark.
- **18-the-hostel-door** — Munteanu knocks on Doina's hostel door; she goes without looking back; she says she found the paper on the bus; N14 is in his evidence envelope; she is held.
- **19-doina-marcu** — Ilinca learns of Doina Marcu's arrest, works through the branch logic placing both paths at her own door, and sits at her desk unable to open the ledger or put down the pencil.
- **20-seven-copies** — Munteanu conducts his second questioning of Doina, who holds her silence; he gains a sixth copy from canvassing and concludes both branch lines converge at a single prior document.
- **21-the-window** — Ilinca confirms the G5 dead-end on the N4 branch (ten nodes total), completes the accounting that places the error in N4, and resolves at her apartment window to go to the cellar woman tomorrow.
- **22-nine-numbered** — Ilinca crosses the river, reads N4 in the cellar woman's corridor, finds the nine instead of a bracket, gives the paper back, and walks home knowing.
- **23-three-days-remaining** — Munteanu collects a seventh copy (N12) from Strada Cuza Vodă, reads his unrevisable G1 profile, and puts the Dorobanților address in his coat pocket for tomorrow.
- **24-the-light-from-the-door** — Ilinca alone with N1 reconstructs the coal cellar moment — she wrote a 9, not a bracket — and understands the full entry; she commits to retrieving N4 and going to Munteanu.
- **25-the-questioning-room** — Munteanu questions Doina a final time — she holds — then confirms the cellar woman's name and address via population registers; four days remain.
- **26-recovery** — Ilinca crosses the river, retrieves N4 from the cellar woman, and walks home with both documents now in her possession — N4 in her coat pocket, N1 waiting at the mill.
- **27-the-address** — Munteanu visits the cellar woman (Day 30); N4 is gone — retrieved December 1 — and she confirms a mill-administrator woman of forty took it back; he gains node 9 but not the name, and writes "Two days."
- **28-the-handover** — Ilinca retrieves N1 from the ledger, walks to the Miliția station, and lays both documents on Munteanu's desk; he releases Doina and calls his captain; Ilinca sits.
- **29-the-deadline** — Munteanu writes the formal determination, seals eleven evidence envelopes, closes the Petrescu dosar on the deadline day, and goes home in the fog.
- **30-the-bracket-included** — Doina walks out of the Miliție station, recites all nine elements from memory on the step — bracket included — and walks along the fog-covered Someș with nothing in her hands.
