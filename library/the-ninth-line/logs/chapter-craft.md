
## 01-the-ledger-spine
**Ending mode:** literary-fade
**Opening texture:** routine-procedure
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Short declaratives (8-14 words) clustered tightly during the overhearing and the ledger-retrieval beats, with sentence length as a pressure gauge
- Free indirect interiority filtered entirely through accounting and bookkeeping vocabulary: columns, entries, checking, balance, supporting documents
- Negative enumeration as fear-rendering: what she does not say, does not write, does not turn on — the procedure of withholding
- Physical object as chapter spine: the ledger, the folded paper, the bracket — anchoring every interiority beat in a thing rather than a feeling
- Sensory texture deferred until the walk home — no weather or location at the open; the cold arrives only when she leaves the building
**Recurring constructions:**
- `She does not`
- `checked it once`
- `does not write`
**POV register:**
- **ilinca-rosca:** Close-third present, spare declaratives averaging 10-14 words; interiority runs in accounting categories (column, balance, entry, check); fear never named, rendered as lists of what she does and does not do; no sentence names a feeling; the bracket is the chapter's load-bearing object and she handles it procedurally
**Craft notes:** The bracket is described but never quoted in full (per outline's quotation site constraint — fragment only in ch1, full text saved for ch9). Lines 7 and 8 are the only quoted fragments. The cellar copy receives substantially more physical texture than the other two copies because it is the load-bearing one; later drafters should treat that asymmetry as intentional, not as padding. The literary-fade ending (static lamp / fog / single lit window in the opposite bloc) is deliberately quiet for a chapter that opens a thriller — the dread is in the arithmetic, not the atmosphere.

## 02-the-geography-book
**Ending mode:** elliptical
**Opening texture:** transit
**Heavy stylistic moves:**
- Implicit-listener address as tonal signature: 'I want that on the record' and 'I am not asking for anything. I just want it noted' — Doina speaks from a future remove to an assumed listener throughout, establishing that she survived to tell this
- Retrospective hedging against hindsight: 'I didn't know why. I know why now.' — each beat tagged with what she knew then vs. what she knows as narrator, without making the gap explicit
- Physical-object catalogue as innocence-declaration: the coat, the locker key, the geography book, the fold creases — Doina enumerates what she owned and did not do as evidentiary procedure
- Fragment quotation of the bracket line (*7 or 8 — take her in.*) without the full N14 text, saving the complete N14 print for Ch 14; the bracket is described structurally (a drawn rectangle) before it is quoted at all
- Wry understatement as survival register: 'I find this restful' (ceiling stain), 'I am very grateful for the noise, which is not something I say lightly because the looms are genuinely very loud' — the comedy lives in her situation, never in the system
**Recurring constructions:**
- `I want that on the record`
- `I did not know`
- `I had not`
**POV register:**
- **doina-marcu:** First-person past, average 20-28 words per sentence with latitude for longer digressions; wry and self-aware, addressing an implicit future listener from throughout; humor through understatement and careful enumeration; fear rendered as procedure (what she kept, what she could not recite, which locker she chose); no sentence names a feeling directly; retrospective framing explicit ('as I found out later') without becoming didactic
**Craft notes:** Doina's voice is established this chapter as self-defending without being self-pitying — the 'I want that on the record' refrain does double duty as wry comedy and genuine legal testimony. The bracket is described as a structural feature (a drawn rectangle setting an instruction apart) before it is quoted in fragment, so the reader understands the form-vs-content distinction before the content lands; this seeds the Ch 09 irony without naming it. The ending deliberately avoids the literary-fade closing mode of Ch 01 (fog/lamp): the final movement is interior, not atmospheric, landing on the decision-that-is-not-a-decision ('doing nothing, which is itself a kind of decision, as I found out later') — the ceiling stain is a glance, not a sustained image. Later drafters: the hostel room's second-floor location and the locker's shelf position are load-bearing for Ch 18.
