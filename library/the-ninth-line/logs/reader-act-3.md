# Reader — Act 3 Assessment (Chapters 21–30)

## Overall character of the act

Act 3 is the book's earned payoff, and it largely earns it. The convergence of both collations is felt in the prose itself — sentences in Ilinca's chapters compress further, Munteanu's chapters strip to their most methodical, and Doina's single chapter arrives after nine chapters of silence and lands exactly as it should. The structural contract (climax on page, final chapter Doina, threads resolving as specified) is honored without exception. Three of the ten chapters are very good; two are genuinely exceptional; one is the weakest in the act for a specific mechanical reason.

---

## What works

**Chapter 22 ("Nine Numbered")** is the strongest chapter in the book. The irony gap — open since Ch 9, sustained for thirteen chapters — closes in a corridor on Strada Crișan, and the technique is exactly right. Ilinca reads N4, line by line, tracking the numerals. She reaches the ninth position. The copy gives her the nine. The chapter renders her recognition through method, not feeling: "She reads it once. She reads it again." The coda — "She is not crying. She does not do that where anyone can see" — is the book's best sentence. It carries everything the register requires and names nothing.

**Chapter 24 ("The Light from the Door")** handles the most technically demanding scene in the act: the coal cellar reconstruction. Ilinca has to understand not just *that* she wrote a nine but *how*, and the chapter achieves this entirely through physical specifics of light angle, column procedure, and what the hand does on autopilot under pressure. The accounting metaphor holds through the key passage: "The figure was right. The column was wrong. She did not check the column." The chapter also navigates the woman-who-regularised-the-ninth-step without exculpating her — "a careful woman reading nine numbered lines... she is not careless" — which is exactly the argument the book is carrying in the tree, rendered here as inference, not thesis.

**Chapter 28 ("The Handover")** delivers the climax on the page with appropriate brevity. Both documents printed in full side by side — the only place the book does this — is a structural necessity, not padding; these are the last two verbatim reproductions (within the four-quotation ceiling) and the juxtaposition of bracket versus numbered nine is the case made visible. Munteanu's comparison of the two is rendered entirely through his eye movements. The phone call releasing Doina is six words of dialogue. "She sits" closes the chapter. None of this is over-managed.

**Chapter 30 ("The Bracket Included")** is exactly the book the brief demanded. Doina is released, the bread roll in the evidence drawer is the right comic-sad opening note, and her recitation of the ninth line on the step — with the bracket rendered as a pause in memory ("there is no way to draw a bracket in memory; what you have instead is the space where the bracket was") — is precise. She recites it correctly. She does not explain it. She walks into the fog. The voice throughout is completely intact: the line-four correction she catches mid-recitation, the pigeon, the honest ambivalence about Ana ("I find I can hold that thought for a certain stretch before it tips into something I can't do anything useful with"). This is Doina at her full register, which is a relief after nine chapters without her.

**Chapter 27 ("The Address")** is Munteanu's best chapter in the act. His reasoning about why Ilinca retrieved N4 the day after the Population Section confirmed the address — "He was not suggesting that anyone in the Population Section had copied the log entry... He was noting that a woman who had been doing careful, deliberate work for more than a year would have developed, over that time, some sense of how information about the investigation was moving" — is the flatness-that-surprises the brief calls for. He is exactly one day behind her and he knows it.

## What doesn't work

**Chapter 21 ("The Window")** re-walks inference the reader already holds. Ilinca's three-branch accounting ("N2: confirmed, closed. N3: confirmed, closed. N4: one line of descent...") was established in Ch 19 and is being reinventoried here. The two possibilities (bracket or nine in N4) were the open question at the end of Ch 19. The chapter advances Thread 1 by formalizing the decision to go to Strada Crișan, and that decision is new — but the chapter lingers in the arithmetic for roughly two-thirds of its length before reaching it. This is partial re-measurement: the reader has the count; what the chapter adds is the commitment. That commitment could have been reached faster. **Gate 2 is not fully passed** — the resolution element is new, but much of the chapter re-takes a reading already on the page.

**Chapter 23 ("Three Days Remaining")** acquires a seventh copy (N12) from Strada Cuza Vodă, but N12 is "consistent with four of the five other numbered copies" and adds nothing to the genealogical picture the reader holds. The new count (seven copies) advances gate 1. But the chapter's middle section re-reads Munteanu's G1 profile from his notebook — "G1 figure: female. Mill or mill-adjacent. Working familiarity with standard documents..." — a profile the reader already holds in full from earlier chapters. This is a direct re-inventory that fails gate 2. The chapter is held together by the day count (seven days remaining) and the decision to go to Dorobanților, but it is the weakest in the act.

**Chapter 25 ("The Questioning Room")** contains borderline tree-explanation. In the interrogation of Doina, Munteanu says: "The difference is inherited — it means your copy comes from a branch that did not go through the version that produced this wording. A branch that is older, or separate. That is what the arithmetic says." The brief's constraint is explicit: no character narrates the structure of the network to another character. Munteanu uses "branch" and "inherited" and explains document genealogy directly to Doina. The scene is dramatically justified — he's using his analysis as an interrogation lever — and the word "tree" does not appear. But this goes further than the brief wants. The scene works; the explanation is an overstep.

**Chapter 29 ("The Deadline")** includes this sentence in the formal determination: "In one branch of transmission, a formatting difference in the source's ninth element propagated a material change in the rendered instruction." The brief's constraint ("that is the argument of the book, and it must be carried entirely by the tree of copies — never stated by a character") applies here. The determination is a written document, not dialogue, so this is not quite a character narrating to another character. But it is Munteanu writing explicit causal language about what the formatting change did — which is close to thesis-restatement. The rest of the determination is careful about staying in institutional register ("a material change in the rendered instruction" avoids saying "and this is what killed her"). This is a medium concern, not a breaking one. The chapter's final non-filing passages — "The file did not contain the reason the document had existed... The file did not contain the reason the cellar had been dark" — are excellent precisely because they show the file's limits without naming them. The determination's one explicit-causal sentence reads differently in that light.

## Thread resolution

All five threads resolve or deliberately decline resolution as specified:

- **Thread 1 (Ilinca's Guilty Knowledge)**: Complete. On-page. Fully earned by the thirteen-chapter irony gap.
- **Thread 2 (Doina's Silence)**: Complete. The woman on the bus is never found. The silence held. Ch 30 closes this without resolving it into a lesson.
- **Thread 3 (The Administrative Deadline)**: Complete. Ilinca arrives on Dec 4 (one day before the Dec 5 deadline). The pressure is legible throughout Act 3: chapter headings are dates and the day-count recurs within each chapter without re-stating the full arithmetic every time.
- **Thread 4 (Munteanu's Wrong Frame)**: Correctly unresolved. He reads both documents and understands the origin; he never holds `ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step`. "He does not understand that a line in a list is a step and a line in a bracket is an override" — this is the irony the book has been running and it closes cleanly without either vindication or punishment for him. "He went home" is the right ending for his final chapter.
- **Thread 5 (The Form of the Knowledge)**: Complete. Ch 30, Doina, on the step, reciting from memory, bracket included, in order. The paper is in an evidence room. This is what survives.

## Voice assessment

**Ilinca (third-present)**: Voice holds throughout Act 3. Ch 22 is the benchmark. Ch 24 extends the interiority across a long reconstruction without losing the column-and-tally idiom. The one slip is in Ch 21, where the reiteration of the three-branch accounting is in exactly Ilinca's idiom but performing work the reader doesn't need. The present tense registers correctly in every chapter.

**Munteanu (third-past)**: Voice holds. Ch 27 is his best chapter in the act. Ch 23 is weaker than his Act 2 chapters, largely because the N12 acquisition adds less than the reader expects. The determination in Ch 29 is well-executed formal prose without tipping into literary excess. He remains neither villain nor sympathiser throughout.

**Doina (first-past)**: One chapter, fully intact. The wry-honest register is present from the first line. No flattening toward the book's default voice. The line-four correction mid-recitation is exactly the kind of specific, slightly embarrassing detail that makes her testimony feel like testimony.

## Brief-adherence checks (Act 3 specific)

1. **POV legible by end of first sentence**: ✓ All ten chapters pass. "She fills the third column..." (Ilinca), "He took the vinyl sleeve..." (Munteanu), "N1 is on the kitchen table." (Ilinca, present tense), "The officer who gave me back my coat..." (Doina).
2. **Climax on page**: ✓ Chapter 28, fully rendered.
3. **Final chapter Doina with ninth line memorised and paper gone**: ✓ Chapter 30.
4. **Day count legible as pressure**: ✓ The remaining-days count appears in Ch 23, 25, 26, 27, 28, and the date headers function as a calendar the reader can feel closing.
5. **Safety**: ✓ No method, substance, dose, procedure, or route of administration in any chapter.
6. **Fear rendered as procedure**: ✓ No named fear. Ilinca's terror runs in column checks; Munteanu's in what will and will not survive being described to his captain.
7. **Doina's voice genuinely funny**: ✓ Ch 30 passes without qualification.
8. **Re-measurement instances**: Ch 21 (partial), Ch 23 (G1 profile re-inventory).
9. **Tree-explanation by character**: Ch 25 (borderline — Munteanu to Doina in questioning, using "branch" and "inherited").
