## canon
- N1, the master (recordId: master-n1), was written by Ilinca Roșca in October 1983. It has eight numbered lines and a ninth line written in the bottom margin inside a hand-drawn bracket. The bracket is not a numbered step; it is an override. This distinction is the book's entire argument.
- N4, Ilinca's cellar copy (recordId: copy-n4), was made on 3 November 1983 in a coal cellar by the light of the open door, in haste. Its only difference from N1: the bracketed ninth element was transcribed as a numbered line 9. Every word is correct. The formatting mutation is the fatal change.
- N11, the regularised copy (recordId: copy-n11), is the fourth-generation copy where line 9 reads: '9. If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.' This text is CORRECT AS WRITTEN and must not be changed by any editorial or repair pass. The phrase 'in the morning' is the fatal mutation — a loss of urgency, not a wrong quantity. The chain from N4 to N11 passes through N9.
- N17, Ana's copy (recordId: copy-n17), is textually identical to N11 in every line. It is a separate physical document with separate provenance. The full text of N17 is printed once in the book, adjacent to N1, at the Act I close. The two texts side by side establish the dramatic irony 'ninth-line-is-a-warning-not-a-step' for the reader before Act II begins.
- N14, Doina's copy (recordId: copy-n14), descends from N3 — a second-generation branch entirely separate from N4. It is clean: the bracket is intact. Lines 7, 8, and the bracket element are identical to N1. Doina is innocent. Munteanu's collation reaches her because her node is four generations down from N1 and points upward in the tree. He is not wrong about the arithmetic; he is wrong about what the arithmetic means.
- All 22 document nodes differ from each other by design. The differences are inherited through the copy chain and are part of the book's subject. No editor, repair pass, continuity agent, or compression pass may reconcile, harmonise, or 'correct' any node toward any other. The audit's findRecordDivergences tool does not fire cross-recordId; every node is its own record and intentional cross-node differences are safe from the audit.
- The fatal mutation is a loss of urgency: a warning that stops reading as a warning, not a wrong quantity of anything. The circulated document is aftercare and when-to-seek-help ONLY. It contains no method, no substance, no dose, no procedure, and no route of administration at any node, in any chapter, in any variant. This constraint is absolute and overrides all other direction.
- Ana Petrescu, age 19, dies on 4 November 1984 at the Spitalul Orășenesc Dej. She arrived approximately nine hours too late. She is never a POV character and never appears in a flashback. Her sister-in-law had copy N17 and followed it.
- The book's two compounding ladders are both monotonic. Nodes held by each collector (Ilinca's and Munteanu's collations) only increase. Days since Ana died only increase. Neither counter ever goes backward. Each chapter must advance exactly one rung on one of these ladders and name a specific foreclosure — something that is now impossible that was possible before.
- The ending is fixed and must be delivered on the page: Ilinca produces the master (N1) and the cellar copy (N4) to Munteanu in scene. Doina is released. Ilinca is taken. The final chapter is Doina, carrying nothing, having memorised the ninth line — bracket included. The paper is what killed Ana; the memorised line is what survives. Do not soften this ending and do not withhold it.
