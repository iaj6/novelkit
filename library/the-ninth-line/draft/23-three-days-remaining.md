# 28 November 1984

He took the vinyl sleeve from the top drawer and counted the copies before he did anything else. Six. He set the sleeve to his left, pulled the desk calendar toward him: November 28. He counted from November 4, which was the day the file had opened. Twenty-five days. Seven until December 5.

The ceiling light in the investigations room was on because the window light at this hour was no light at all — the fog had been sitting over the valley since before dawn, and whatever sun came would come at the wrong angle for the east-facing wall anyway. The radiator was working this morning, which it had not been doing yesterday; the room had accumulated the cold of a space designed for central heating suddenly left without it, and now the radiator was releasing heat in short pulses, with a contraction tick between each one as the pipe settled. On his desk, left: the formal file, closed, nine entries since November 4. Right: the working folder, which was his own document and not for the captain's file. On the floor beside his chair: two empty evidence envelopes, not yet requisitioned for new copies, because the outer ring had thinned. The thinning was expected.

He opened his notebook to the sixth page.

The Dorobanților address was in the second line, written below the Damian entry on the twelfth day — November 15. Below it, added the following week in a different pen: *Not yet visited.* He had been working other addresses. On the fourteenth day one new copy, on the sixteenth another, on the twenty-second day the Strada Mihai bloc copy that Mureșan had brought in from the canvass. Three additional copies in the thirteen days since he had written the Dorobanților address, and none of them the Dorobanților address. That was a serviceable record.

He had another address to go to first. He put the notebook in his inner pocket, picked up the Cuza Vodă sheet from the working folder, and put on his coat.

---

Strada Cuza Vodă was six minutes from the station on a steady walk. The fog had not lifted. It sat close over the rooftops and compressed the distances so that the building faces opposite disappeared above the second-floor windows and the far end of the block was only an outline against gray. He passed a queue outside a cooperative that had not opened yet — fifteen people or so with baskets, a woman with a child's pram, everyone facing the door. He did not look at them. The cold had the river in it; the Someș was two streets north and the valley air held it.

Bloc 17 was the fourth building from the corner, five stories, a thin discoloration running down the stucco from a joint in the guttering. He went in through the street entrance and up the stairs. The stairwell smelled of coal and boiled meal and a faint chemical note — cleaning powder that had dried in the floor and was releasing again into the winter air. Second floor. Third door on the left. He knocked once.

The woman who opened it was perhaps thirty-five, wearing a housedress and a gray wool cardigan with a safety pin at the collar where a button had been. She had been at the window — he had come from the direction that gave her a sightline to the street, and she had had enough time. She left the door open when she stepped back. He came in.

The apartment was one room and a kitchen, the kitchen door standing open. On the kitchen table: a half loaf of bread on a cutting board. On the chair back: a child's jacket, navy, the elbow seam beginning to go. No child. The wall clock read 9:12. On the windowsill, a tin jar with herbs in it gone gray and brittle with the season, set there with some care at some earlier point. From somewhere above them, through two floors of concrete, a radio doing the morning program.

He told her his name and his section and told her he was working a case that involved a handwritten document and its circulation, and that he needed to know whether she had received such a document and, if so, whether he could examine it.

She said: what kind of document.

He said: a handwritten document containing instructions of a domestic nature, passed between women.

She looked at him and then at a point past his left shoulder, and then she went to the cabinet beside the kitchen window. She opened the lower drawer, reached beneath a stack of folded dishcloths, and took out a piece of paper. She put it in his hand.

He unfolded it against the kitchen doorframe. The paper was a page torn from a household accounts ledger — the pocket kind, with printed date and amount columns left entirely blank, the text written on the lined reverse side. The handwriting was different from the Strada Mihai copies: wider letters, more even spacing. He checked line 1.

*Keep her warm and lying still. Someone stays with her the whole night.*

He noted the variant — *lying still* — and read the rest down. Line 5 read *If anyone asks, she has influenza*, without the article the other copies carried. He reached line 9.

*9. 7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why. Do not wait for the morning.*

He noted it. Long form. Numbered nine. Consistent with four of the five other numbered copies.

He asked where she had received the copy. She said from a woman she knew through the mill. She said she did not know the woman's name. He asked her to describe the circumstances. She said it had been given to her by hand, in a public place, the previous year. He asked whether she could give him anything more specific. She said no. She said it would not help either of them.

He looked at her. She had been holding her hands at her sides since he had come in, and she was not gripping the doorframe or the cabinet handle.

He asked whether she had passed the copy to anyone. She said no.

He thanked her and took the copy. She had closed the door before he reached the landing.

---

He was back at the station before eleven. Mureșan was at his desk, working the old Totul typewriter — the one with the misaligned e-key that printed slightly high. He had a stack of forms at his left elbow and a finished page face-down to his right. Munteanu set the Cuza Vodă copy on his desk, took all six copies from the sleeve, and put all seven out on the desk surface.

The desk was not wide enough. The accounts-ledger page from Cuza Vodă, being a wider format than standard mill stationery, overhung the right edge by three centimetres. He weighted the corners with the stapler and the three shoe-polish tins. Mureșan had been leaving a fourth tin on Munteanu's side of the room since the sixteenth day without being asked. Munteanu had not mentioned this. He used the pen from his breast pocket to hold the lower edge of the Cuza Vodă page level.

He read the copies in order from left to right.

Far left: the Marcu copy. He could identify it at a glance now — the text set into the left margin, the two horizontal strokes and the vertical enclosing it, no numeral in the column.

The remaining six: all numbered. He checked line 9 on each in order.

Two short-form copies: *If 7 or 8, take her in in the morning.* The Petrescu chain copy and the Gal copy. The shortening had entered at or above the Gal level; the Cuza Vodă copy gave him nothing to revise.

Four long-form copies: *7 or 8 — take her in. Say she began bleeding on her own and you do not know why. Do not wait for the morning.* The Damian copy, the Strada Mihai bloc copy, the fourteenth-day canvass copy, and now the Cuza Vodă accounts-ledger copy. All four with the numbered nine. All four with the same last sentence.

He looked at the seven for a moment, then put them back in the sleeve.

In the margin of his working sheet he wrote: *CuVodă — long form, consistent. L1: lying still. L5: no article. L9: numbered, long. Source: mill-adjacent, no name, no address.*

---

He opened the notebook to the fourteenth-day page and read the profile entry as he had written it, without adding to it.

*G1 figure: female. Mill or mill-adjacent. Working familiarity with standard documents. Access to AFE-grade paper stock, 1982–83. Grammatical precision — not necessarily advanced education, but trained toward accuracy. Spatial intentionality — the original bracket is a formal distinction between a sequence step and an override instruction. This person knew the difference between a numbered element and a marginal element and used both deliberately. Not in the population register under any name so far encountered in the transmission chain.*

He had not revised this in eleven days. Nothing from the seven copies had given him cause to.

The paper stock of the oldest copies in the Petrescu chain was consistent with AFE administrative issue, circa 1982 to 1983. Not conclusive. He had been thinking about the conditions under which the document had been produced — at speed, in private, with no chance to go back and check — since the fourteenth day, and had not found a better description of them than *trained toward accuracy*. It was the accuracy of someone who did not trust anything, including her own work, and had learned to produce documents that were clean because a clean document left no question open.

He did not have her name. She had not appeared under any name the transmission chain had so far given him.

The Dorobanților address was one step from her.

He closed the notebook.

---

He put on his coat. He checked that the notebook was in the inner pocket. From the active section of the working folder he took the sixth-page notation — the Dorobanților entry, the cross-note from the Cuza Vodă woman about the mill-adjacent source — and folded it once and put it in the right outer pocket of his coat, where he could reach it without opening the buttons.

He stopped at the file rack before leaving. He found the Marcu dosar and opened it to the second interview entry, written on the twenty-second day: *N14 (Marcu) — bracket format. Divergence from numbered format in Petrescu chain predates N14's entry into transmission. N14 probably descends from a different G2-level source than the numbered copies. Stated provenance: found on a public bus, date unspecified. Account consistent across two interviews. Not independently verifiable.* He closed the file and put it back in the rack.

The woman had been in the holding section for six days. She had been consistent throughout — consistent in the particular way where the account gave him nothing that could be independently confirmed and nothing that could be independently disproved. He had interviewed people who were telling the truth and produced that pattern. He had also interviewed people who had prepared a story carefully enough to produce it. He had not resolved which this was, and her copy's structural position — on the bracket branch, divergent from the numbered copies above a G2 node he had not yet identified — did not resolve it. What it told him was that the numbered branch's G2 source was not the source that had produced her copy, which meant there was a second G2-level document, which meant there was a G1-level document above both, which meant there was a person who had made that document. The Dorobanților address pointed at the numbered branch's G2 source. From the numbered branch's G2 source, he expected to be directed one step further up.

Tomorrow morning.

He had seven days.

He went out.
