# Chapter Map — *A Line of Smoke*

## Overview

- **Total chapters:** 16
- **POV rotation:** Opens with **Silas** (ch 1); strict alternation holds throughout — Silas, Asa, Silas, Asa, …
- **Target total words:** ~40,000
- **Average per chapter:** ~2,500 words
- **Narrative span:** ~August 28–September 12, 1814

## Arc Shape

| Phase | Chapters | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup** | 1–2 | Day-before: the ordinary world before the crisis arrives |
| **Approach / compression** | 3–12 | British crossing, Beekmantown, Saranac standoff, eve of battle |
| **Climax** | 13–14 | September 11 — the naval engagement and the ford defense |
| **Coda** | 15–16 | September 12 — what remains |
