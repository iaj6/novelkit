# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-the-garland** — Silas runs his pre-dawn garland check, a full working day at the starboard battery, and an evening on the fo'c'sle watching the dark hills; the guns are right; nothing changes.
- **02-the-head-race** — Asa works a full day at Eli Beckwith's sash sawmill on the Saranac — head race, pine boards, a hidden knot in the afternoon — and walks home at dusk with his father's fever the weight of the road.
- **03-first-reports** — Dispatches report Prevost's column at Champlain; Macdonough orders action-rate drills; Silas notices an unexplained anchor arrangement on the stern and stores it without asking.
- **04-the-levy** — Asa receives the levy call-up, hears his father's lucid instruction, marches two days to Plattsburgh, and camps south of the Saranac — the father's words still in his pocket.
- **05-spring-lines** — Silas hears Beekmantown's distant guns, traces the kedge's full geometry on the main deck, watches British camp smoke rise over the north bank, and lies awake counting night cannon from the river while the lake stays empty of the British fleet.
- **06-beekmantown-road** — The Clinton County militia breaks at Beekmantown; Asa stays with Cole's rearguard through the fighting withdrawal to Halsey's Corners, then is assigned to Pike's Ford — his uncle's home water.
- **07-at-anchor** — Silas holds the standoff routine on the *Saratoga*: drill improves, volunteers go ashore for Macomb's line, and the grape-shot allocation shifts toward close action.
- **08-pikes-ford** — Asa walks Cole through every stone of Pike's Ford, the companies cut their firing lanes, Solomon Veeder is established, and the two-rank drill begins — ending mid-sequence on the eighth.
- **09-the-kedge** — Silas overhears the winding maneuver named, traces its geometry to the port battery, meets Marsh, and stores the completed fact in its right place.
- **10-two-ranks** — At Pike's Ford, the drill becomes automatic; British soldiers scan the ford from the north bank and cannot read its bottom; Asa stands midnight watch, waits out a sound in the brush, and earns Cole's single word of praise.
- **11-cumberland-head** — Pre-dawn September 11: the *Saratoga* goes to quarters before 4 AM, waits through dawn, watches the British fleet round Cumberland Head in column, and opens fire.
- **12-before-light** — Asa wakes before dawn at Pike's Ford on September 11; the company mans its positions in the dark without orders; the naval cannonade arrives from the east as pressure before sound; the north bank stays empty.
- **13-port-battery** — The starboard battery is destroyed gun by gun; Jonas Fry is wounded and carried below; the *Saratoga* winds on the kedge; Silas crosses to the port battery and fires into the battered British line.
- **14-the-ford** — The British assault Pike's Ford in force; the two-rank fire holds; Asa's knowledge of the submerged log breaks the flanking attempt; the naval cannonade stops; the British withdraw in order; the ford holds.
- **15-prize-procedures** — Silas accounts for his dead and his four wrecked guns on the morning after the battle; the prize ships ride in the bay; he closes the port cover and goes to write the report.
- **16-the-retreat** — The British army retreats north; the ford stands empty on September 12; Asa walks the south bank upstream, looks back at the ford whole, thinks briefly of his father, and returns to the sound of the river.
