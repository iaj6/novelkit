# Editor — Continuity Pass

Notes from per-chapter review. Chapters with no entry below had no continuity issues.

## ch04-the-levy

- **Issue:** Duplicate surname "Webb" across alternating POV threads
- **Conflict:** Ch03 (Silas) establishes a USS *Saratoga* gun-crew member referred to only as "Webb" (three uses: "Webb's fall a count behind"; "Webb's full weight behind the fall from the first haul"). Ch04 (Asa) independently introduces "Isaac Webb, about his own age, a blacksmith's striker from the village" as a militia character who shares Asa's mess and is present at the chapter's close. These are two unrelated fictional characters. Because the chapters alternate strictly (Silas/Asa/Silas/Asa…), a reader meets naval Webb in ch03 and militia Isaac Webb in ch04 back-to-back. The shared surname will register as a continuity confusion rather than coincidence.
- **Suggested resolution:** Rename the militia character in ch04 (and any later Asa chapters that carry him forward) to a surname not already in use on the naval side — e.g., "Isaac Morse" or "Isaac Treadwell." Alternatively, give the Saratoga crew member a distinguishing first-name mention in ch03 to make clear they are different people, but that approach requires editing outside this chapter's scope. Because the militia "Isaac Webb" may carry into later Asa chapters (ch06, ch08, ch10, ch12, ch14), the rename should be applied globally across all Asa chapters before finalising.

## ch13-port-battery

- **Issue:** "Nine days before" used three times to reference events from ch09 ("the-kedge"), but ch09 falls within the September 7–10 standoff — placing those events at most four days before the September 11 battle, not nine.
- **Conflict:** Chapter 13 states Silas "worked out the geometry nine days before," "had been to [the port battery] once, nine days before," and "logged [the garland count] nine days before." Chapter 09 is chronologically after ch05 (Beekmantown, September 6), so it cannot be earlier than September 7; September 11 minus nine days = September 2, which predates the standoff entirely.
- **Resolution applied (small fix):** All three instances of "nine days before" replaced with "two days before" (placing ch09 on approximately September 9). Edit made directly in draft/13-port-battery.md via write_file.

## ch16-the-retreat

- **Issue:** "Asa had been fifteen / four years since" — age and elapsed-time figures are mutually inconsistent with Asa's canonical age and the story's September 1814 setting.
- **Conflict:** The chapter contains the passage: *"He had come home in June with a fever that had not left him. Asa had been fifteen. From that June forward he had been the wage earner and the man of the house, and those two things had not stopped being true in the four years since."* Three facts are asserted: (a) Nathan Beckwith returned from Sackett's Harbor "in June [1813]"; (b) Asa was fifteen at that moment; (c) "four years" have elapsed since that June. All three cannot be simultaneously true. September 1814 is only approximately fifteen months after June 1813 — not four years. Furthermore, canon fact #2 establishes Asa as 19 years old in September 1814, which places his birth around 1795; in June 1813 he would have been approximately 17–18, not 15. Canon fact #4 says Asa "has been the wage earner since age fifteen," which is arithmetically consistent with roughly four years before September 1814 (onset ≈ 1810) — but the chapter explicitly anchors both the age-fifteen claim and the "four years since" phrase to the June 1813 Sackett's Harbor return. The "four years" figure is correct as a duration from age 15 to age 19; the June 1813 date is correct as the Sackett's Harbor return; but they cannot be connected as cause and endpoint.
- **Secondary concern:** The chapter describes the Battle of Sackett's Harbor as *"the action that took the fight north across the lake into British-held Ontario."* The Battle of Sackett's Harbor (May 29, 1813) was a British amphibious attack on the American naval base that the Americans successfully repelled; it was not an American offensive into British-held Ontario. (The American operations into Ontario — York, Fort George — were separate actions concurrent with but distinct from the Sackett's Harbor defense.) This clause may mislead a reader about the nature of Nathan Beckwith's service and should be reviewed alongside the age/date fix below.
- **Suggested resolution:** The author must decide whether the Sackett's Harbor return (June 1813) was the trigger for Asa's wage-earning role, or whether that role began earlier (~1810, when Asa was 15) for some other reason (e.g., earlier illness, absence, or injury to the father predating his 1813 service). If Sackett's Harbor is the trigger, then "fifteen" should become approximately "seventeen" and "four years since" should become "a year and more since" — but this conflicts with canon fact #4. If "since age fifteen" (~1810) is the correct onset, the chapter passage should be rewritten to decouple the wage-earning start from the June 1813 return; the father's Sackett's Harbor illness would then be a later, confirming chapter in the father's story rather than the originating event. The erroneous historical description of Sackett's Harbor as an offensive action should be corrected or removed as part of the same revision.
