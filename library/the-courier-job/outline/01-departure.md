# Chapter 01 — Departure

**One-line summary**: Tess is handed the sealed bag and a last cryptic instruction she can't ask about; the party assembles in Drey's square; they're barely off the road before Pip names what Tess already doesn't like.

---

## Vitals

| | |
|---|---|
| **POV** | Tess |
| **Tense** | Third-person limited past |
| **Setting** | Drey — Mara's cottage, the main square, the eastern road out of the village |
| **Time** | Dawn to mid-morning |
| **Target words** | ~3,200 |
| **Chapter shape** | Multi-scene (three beats: the private handover; the assembly; the departure) |
| **Ending mode** | Revelation cut |

---

## Chapter Goal

Establish Tess, Mara, the contract, and all four party members. End before the party has gotten far, with the first sign that something about this job is not what it was billed as.

---

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene A: The Handover (~900 words)
- Opens inside Mara's cottage at dawn — sensory grounding: dried herbs, the smell of the well outside, the cold of the stone floor. Tess has not slept well.
- Mara hands Tess the sealed bag. It is heavier than it looks. The seal is the Weavers' Guild's reformist-faction mark (Tess doesn't know the factions exist; she sees "a guild seal" and nothing more).
- Mara explains the contract as Tess knows it: sealed delivery, named recipient, no questions asked. Contract fee covers Mara's debts.
- Tess asks the obvious question: "What's inside?" Mara says: "Nothing that concerns you yet."
  - Note for drafter: this is not a lie and not a comfort. Mara's voice is practical, not evasive. She answers what she can answer.
- Mara runs through what Tess needs to know about the other three: Brann is reliable in his body, Iri does real magic (Mara says this without inflection; Tess hears it as a judgment), Pip knows the city they're going to.
- Mara then says the last thing — the thing she says when they are alone and the cottage door is shut. She takes Tess's hand (she does not do this habitually), presses it over the bag, and says: *"Deliver it whole. Whatever happens on that road, deliver it whole."*
- She does not explain. Tess does not ask. This is the operative clue for the whole book; Tess files it in the category of "things Mara is right about without explaining why."
- Tess takes the bag. She takes her kit. She leaves.

### Scene B: The Assembly (~800 words)
- The main square of Drey. Cold, early. The village is not up yet.
- Brann arrives first, already loaded: a pack that looks like it weighs what Tess weighs. He says approximately four words to her: "You Tess?" "Right." He sets his pack down and waits. He does not seem annoyed or eager. He simply waits, the way someone waits for rain.
- Iri arrives second. Half-elven ears. A pack that is too small for three weeks (Tess notices and doesn't comment). Iri looks at Tess's bag — specifically at the seal — then looks away.
  - Note for drafter: this is the first retroactive beat. Iri recognizes something about the seal. She doesn't say anything. This should be rendered as a brief pause, not as dramatic notation.
- Pip arrives last, talking before he's in sight. He has opinions about the weather, opinions about the road, opinions about the best route, all delivered at a pace that leaves no gap for anyone to interrupt. He also has, tucked under one arm, something that may be a lute or may be a very small coffin, and when Tess asks what it is, he says: "Insurance."
- Brief party dynamics: Brann watches Pip without expression. Iri looks like she is solving a math problem. Pip, surveying the group, says: "Great. A muscle, a mystery, a hedge-witch, and a courier. Very professional operation." He's not wrong.
- Tess does a silent inventory of what she knows about each of them: almost nothing, almost nothing, almost nothing. This is the party.

### Scene C: The Departure (~500 words + ending)
- They leave east. The road out of Drey is rutted mud; Pip nearly goes over in the first fifty meters. Brann catches his elbow without breaking stride.
- Tess looks back once. Mara is in her doorway. When Tess looks, Mara has already turned back inside and shut the door.
- They walk. The village thins and then it's just road.
- At the edge of what counts as Drey — the last fence post, a field marker — a rider comes toward them going west. He's unremarkable: gray cloak, road-traveled look, horse in reasonable condition.
- He passes them. He glances at the bag on Tess's shoulder. Just a glance, the flick of eyes that moves to an object before the face can school itself. He does not slow.
- Tess is halfway through deciding she imagined it when Pip, at her shoulder, says — quiet, no joke in it: *"That man works for the Guild."*

**ENDING (revelation cut):** The chapter cuts here, before Tess can ask how Pip knows. She doesn't get to ask. They're walking.

---

## Character Beats

- **Tess**: Competent but not comfortable. She's done the preparation (kit packed, contract papers sorted, the bag's seal examined until she knows every line of it). The competence is visible; the discomfort is visible under it.
- **Brann**: Economical. Shows up, does the job of showing up. One physical act (catches Pip's elbow). No explanation of himself. Stranger mode — he is a fact about this road, not yet a person to Tess.
- **Iri**: The pause at the seal. The small pack. The math-problem expression. She is not unfriendly; she is simply already somewhere else in her thinking. Tess finds this mildly annoying and does not say so.
- **Pip**: Immediate presence. Too much talking, all of it precise if you listen carefully. The "Insurance" item (to be named later if the drafter chooses — it's a distraction, not a plot item). His warning about the rider is the first time he's serious, and the chapter ends on it.
- **Mara**: Appears only here. She is fond of Tess in ways she won't say. She is afraid of something. Neither of these surfaces directly in the prose; both should be legible through what she does with her hands and what she doesn't say.

---

## Continuity Notes

- The sealed bag first appears here: leather, sealed with a guild mark, heavy.
- Tess's kit is fully loaded for the first time — chalk, salt, herbs, cord. This is its baseline state.
- Mara's exact words — "deliver it whole" — should be rendered verbatim or near-verbatim; they will echo in ch 11.
- Pip identifies the rider as guild-connected. He does not explain how he knows. This is Pip's Act 1 competence moment.
- The guild seal on the bag is never described in enough detail for the reader to recognize guild factions; it is "a guild seal" to Tess.
- Iri's recognition of the seal is latent — a narrative beat planted here to be returned to by a careful rereader in ch 11.

---

## New Facts This Chapter Introduces

- The contract: sealed delivery, paid in advance, Weavers' Guild, recipient named Sable Orr in Asterhall.
- The party: Tess (courier of record), Brann (carry and protection), Iri (magic), Pip (city knowledge).
- Mara's instruction: "Deliver it whole. Whatever happens."
- A rider at the village edge, identified by Pip as Guild-connected.
- The journey is three weeks of road east.

## Loose Thread Raised

- Who was the rider, and what will the Guild do next?
- What does "deliver it whole" mean — and why did Mara say it like that?
- How does Pip know guild people on sight?
