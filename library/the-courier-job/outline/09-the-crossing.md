# Chapter 09 — The Crossing

**One-line summary**: The road bridge is out; a fast ford goes wrong; Tess goes under with the bag and Brann pulls her out; Iri uses magic she doesn't have and collapses; Pip is stranded on the far bank until Brann rigs the rope; Pip finds a folded note in his coat pocket he didn't put there.

## Chapter Goal

Third and final action set piece: physical emergency, not combat. Different in character from ch 3 (sudden violence) and ch 6 (confrontation-standoff). This one is the environment turning against the party. Everyone is tested, everyone contributes, and the cost is high — Iri is depleted again; Tess's kit is compromised; Pip is alone on the wrong bank. And then there's a note. The chapter ends before anyone knows what it says.

---

## Scene Breakdown

### Section A: The Bridge (~500 words)
- Day 17. The road is wide here — the approach to a regional bridge over a significant tributary river. This is the main road's major crossing; the next ford is a half-day upstream.
- The bridge is out. Not from age — from recent damage: a section of the central span has collapsed, the timbers are in the water below, and the damage looks intentional (cut supports, not rot).
  - Note for drafter: Tess does not reach the conclusion of "intentional" immediately. She notices it later, in retrospect. In the moment, it's just: the bridge is out and they need to get across.
- The ford. Pip knows it — it's marked on his mental map of the approach roads to Asterhall. Knee-deep in dry summer. Waist-deep after rain. It has rained recently.
- Pip's read of the ford: "Doable. Stay together. Don't try to hurry."
- They make a plan: rope rigged between two trees on either bank, tied to Brann (who will go first, anchor the far side), then Tess (bag), then Iri, then Pip last. Pip will hold the near rope until Brann is positioned.

### Section B: The Crossing Goes Wrong (~2,000 words)
- Brann crosses first. The current is strong but he's heavy and stable. He makes the far bank, wraps the rope, signals.
- Tess crosses second. The bag is strapped to her chest — she is not carrying it on her shoulder, she has it bound against her body. She steps in. The cold is immediate and brutal.
- She is halfway across when she slips — a submerged rock, angled, the current hits her hip at the wrong moment. She goes under.
- Under the water: fast, cold, disorienting. The bag is against her chest and she's holding the rope but the rope has gone sideways and she's being pulled downstream.
- Note for drafter: this is action sequence, not slow interiority. Tess is processing in fragments. The relevant facts: cold, rope, bag, surface. She does not think, she does things. She gets a hand on a submerged boulder and pushes herself back toward the line.
- Brann comes in from the far bank. He moves into the current, plants himself, grabs her arm. He drags her to the far bank. The bag is still against her chest. Still sealed. She can feel the seal with her fingers while she's coughing up river water.
- She checks the bag immediately upon gaining the bank. Intact. The outer leather is soaked; the seal is intact; the documents inside are protected by the leather case's waxed lining (Mara's preparation).
- **What Tess loses**: her kit satchel, which was on her back. The satchel made it to shore — she held the strap — but the contents are compromised: water got in. The chalk (what little remained) is destroyed. The herbs: damaged. The salt: dissolved in river water. What survives: the cord, the case itself, one sealed herb packet that was double-wrapped. Tess's magic is now effectively zero.
  - Note for drafter: this is a significant moment. Tess's craft has been degrading across the journey. Now she has almost nothing left. The emotional beat here is not despair — she's too busy surviving — but it's a fact she files.
- **Iri**: She's crossing when Tess went under. Iri sees Brann go in after Tess and makes a choice in the current — she uses magic to stabilize herself and to give Brann a clear approach line (clears some of the river's resistance from his path, for three or four seconds). It's a small working but her reserves are not fully restored from ch 6 and 7. The cost hits immediately: she makes the far bank and her legs don't want to hold her. She sits down in the mud at the river's edge, hands in her lap, shaking.
  - Note for drafter: Iri does not complain. She does not say what she did or what it cost. She just sits. The shaking hands are the information.
  
  **(Iri Act 3 competence moment)**: She used what she had to clear Brann's approach to Tess. She didn't have to. It cost her.

- **Pip**: On the near bank. He was holding the rope for Tess's crossing when she went under; the rope went taut and then sideways and then slack, and he was left with rope in his hand and nobody on it. He calls across. They're on the far bank. The rope is fouled in the current — it went with Tess's weight and wrapped around the boulder that tripped her.
- Pip cannot cross without the rope. The rope is in the river.
- Brann, from the far bank: he goes back in. Without the rope this time — he wades to the rope, frees it from the boulder, pulls it back to its original line. He is very cold now. He rigs it again. Pip crosses, hand over hand, saying nothing. He makes the bank without incident.
  
  **(Brann Act 3 competence moment)**: Two crossings under pressure. He got Tess out and then he went back in to rig the rope for Pip. No speech. Just the next necessary thing.

### Section C: After the Crossing (~1,000 words + ending cut)
- Four wet people on the east bank. Cold, getting dark. Iri cannot walk well. Brann is wringing water out of his shirt with methodical efficiency. Pip's pack is soaked.
- Tess inventories the kit. The results are as listed above: chalk gone, herbs damaged, salt dissolved. She has a kit case and cord. That's it.
- She holds the bag in both hands and checks the seal. Intact. She does this for longer than is necessary.
- Assessment: Iri cannot work magic for at least twelve hours and is physically unsteady. Tess has no usable craft. Brann is fine physically but cold. Pip is wet and now silent.
- They need shelter. There is a shepherd's hut visible through the trees — stone walls, the kind of structure that exists on this road for exactly this kind of emergency.
- They make for it. Inside: dry (barely), a stack of old straw, no one home, a firepit with old ash.
- Brann makes the fire. Nobody told him to; he does it because it's the next thing.
- They are drying their gear in silence when Pip, wringing out his coat, stops.
- He puts his hand in the inner pocket. He takes out a piece of folded paper that is dry (it was in an oilskin-wrapped document sleeve — Pip habitually wraps his documents; this one wasn't his document).
- He reads it. He goes still.
- He looks up at Tess.
- "Someone put this in my pocket at the ford," he says. "While we were crossing."

**ENDING (mid-action cut):** He doesn't say what it says. The chapter cuts here — wet, cold, firelight, Pip's face, the note in his hand.

## Character Beats

- **Tess**: Goes under and comes back up. She holds the bag while she's drowning. The kit loss is the genuine cost — she is now nearly without her craft. Her response to this is not panic; she's too tired for panic. She checks the bag and that's what matters.
- **Brann (Act 3 competence moment)**: Two crossings. He got Tess out, then went back for the rope. This is Brann at full operational effectiveness: he does what needs doing, in sequence, without waiting to be asked. He has become a known quantity to Tess. The trust is now functioning.
- **Iri (Act 3 competence moment)**: Small working, immediately expensive. She cleared Brann's path. Nobody asked her to. She did it because it was available to her and it helped. The cost is visible and real and she doesn't ask for acknowledgment.
- **Pip**: The note. Its appearance is the chapter's mystery. He's been on this road for seventeen days and no one has handed him anything without the party seeing it. At the ford, in the chaos, someone was close enough to put something in his pocket. This means someone was watching the ford specifically, knew they'd be there, and decided to communicate with Pip specifically (not Tess, not Brann — Pip).

---

## Subplot Beats

- **Tess/Brann**: He pulled her out of the river. She was holding the bag while she was drowning. Neither of them has to say anything about it. The physical fact of it is there. "Noticing mode" has reached a new register — she knows now that he will go into the river after her. This is not the peak moment (ch 10) but it is preparation for it.
- **Pip's backstory**: The note addressed to him, delivered at the ford by someone who was waiting there and knew he'd be crossing. This is the thread becoming active: whoever slipped him the note knows who he is and is treating him as a contact. The note will be addressed in ch 10.

## Continuity Notes

- **Tess's kit is now essentially destroyed**: chalk gone (destroyed in ch 3 and Pavers' Country), herbs damaged/depleted, salt dissolved in the crossing. She has cord and the kit case. She cannot work hedge magic in any meaningful way for the rest of the journey. This is permanent for this book; she does not resupply.
- Iri's depletion: her third significant working in the book. She is badly depleted. She will be functional (ambulatory, capable of minor working) by ch 10 but not at full capacity until ch 11.
- Bag: intact, dry internally (waxed leather lining), sealed. This must be stated clearly in the prose.
- The note: dry, in an oilskin sleeve, in Pip's inner pocket. The note was placed at the ford, during the chaos. Whoever placed it knew Pip would be crossing here. They were watching.
- The bridge collapse: whether it was intentional is ambiguous. The drafter should not resolve this — it's a planted question that contributes to the sense of a closing net.
