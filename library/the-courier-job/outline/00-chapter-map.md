## Cliffhanger Distribution (per brief and agent guidance)

All 12 chapters end on cliffhanger or mid-action. No literary fades. Ending types distributed to avoid consecutive repetition:

| Type | Chapters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Revelation cut (information lands before response) | 1, 4, 7, 11 | 4 |
| Recognition cut (threat arrives / danger becomes undeniable) | 2, 5, 8, 10, 12 | 5 |
| Mid-action cut (chapter stops at peak physical tension) | 3, 6, 9 | 3 |

Alternating sequence: R-K-M-R-K-M-R-K-M-K-R-K (no two consecutive same type) ✓

## Subplot Tracking

### Tess / Brann romance (slow burn)
- Ch 1–3: Stranger mode — barely speak beyond necessity
- Ch 4–6: Reluctant-ally mode — operate in coordination; small moments
- Ch 7–8: Noticing mode — Tess notices things she wasn't noticing; Brann says one unnecessary thing (ch 8)
- Ch 10: Peak moment — kiss or explicitly avoided kiss before the city gate
- Ch 12: Unresolved — they don't name it; Brann's last line covers everything he won't say

### Tess / Iri magical legitimacy thread
- Ch 1–2: Tess is intimidated; hides it behind professionalism
- Ch 3–5: Tess observes what Iri's magic costs her; intimidation begins to shift
- Ch 6–7: Iri touches one of Tess's patterns without asking (ch 7); they survive the night differently
- Ch 11: Iri says "Your master is brave" — the most personal thing she's said; alliance confirmed

### Pip's backstory (acknowledged, unresolved)
- Pip doesn't explain why he left Asterhall; referenced in ch 8 (avoids a checkpoint) and ch 12 (tells Tess something true about why he came back)
- Not resolved in this book

### Iri's guild status (acknowledged, unresolved)
- Established as having guild-level training without a current license
- In ch 7, she hints at training-era knowledge of the enforcement wing; never fully explained
- Not resolved in this book

## Retroactive Clues (for re-casting in ch 11)

The bag's contents (seven depositions documenting guild extortion) must be seeded so that ch 11's revelation re-casts earlier chapters:

| Chapter | Planted clue | How it re-casts in ch 11 |
|---|---|---|
| Ch 3 | Attacker shouts "Get the **bag**!" — not "Get the money" | Confirms the ambush was targeted enforcement action, not opportunistic robbery |
| Ch 5 | Innkeeper's ledger shows the same enforcement-wing seal as the badge found in ch 4 | Confirms she was an enforcement asset, pre-positioned on their route |
| Ch 6 | Rival leader says "You're carrying someone's **testimony**" — sounds like bluster | In ch 11: literally true — the bag contains seven sworn testimonies |

## Continuity Anchors

All items from `canon/continuity.md` are observed. Key flags for the drafter:

- Ages fixed: Tess 15, Brann 17, Iri 16, Pip 14 — never drift
- Bag never opens before ch 11; it is physically with Tess in every chapter
- Magic has visible costs in every scene that uses it
- No POV outside Tess; other characters' interiority is always framed as her inference
- Every chapter ends on cliffhanger — no exceptions; no literary fades
- Ch 12 ends open — no resolution of the decision, no next-book exposition dump
