# Calibration Sample — Iteration 1
## Chapter 1: Departure (opening, Scene A)

---

The bag was heavier than it looked.

Tess had known it for three days. Since Mara pulled it from the locked chest and placed it on the table, unhurried, with both hands. Now she had it in her own hands. Heavier than it looked. The seal pressed into red wax in a pattern she'd memorized over breakfast without meaning to — the Weavers' Guild mark. She knew it from the letterhead on Mara's outstanding invoices.

"You're looking at it like it's going to tell you something," Mara said.

"Isn't it?"

"No." Mara was at the hearth, banking what little fire there was. The cottage was cold — stone-floor cold, dawn cold, the kind that sat in your feet and didn't move. "Sealed delivery, named recipient. You open it, the contract voids. The fee is forfeit. You know this."

"I know."

"So stop looking at it."

Tess put it in her pack. Outside, the light was just coming up — the color of dirty water. The rain Mara had been predicting since Tuesday hadn't arrived yet. Three weeks to Asterhall. She had worked the numbers in every direction and they always came back to three weeks.

"The other three," Tess said.

"Are capable."

"Brann I've seen lift a forge anvil, so I'll take your word on that one. Iri I've mostly seen from a distance." She left a gap.

Mara let it sit. "She can do what you've read about," she said finally. "The workings that cost something. The real kind." A pause. "Don't be impressed in ways you say out loud."

"I'm a professional."

"You've been a professional for three months."

Tess let that go.

Mara crossed the room. She was not a woman who took your hand — hadn't been, in three months, in any situation that might have called for it. She took Tess's hand now. The one not on the pack strap. She pressed it flat against the place in the bag where the seal sat, like she was checking something only she could feel.

"Deliver it whole," she said. "Whatever happens on that road. Deliver it whole."

She did not explain. Tess did not ask. These were the terms: Mara said the thing that mattered once, in exactly the words she meant, and that was enough.

Tess took her kit and went.
