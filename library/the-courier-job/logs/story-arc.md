# Story Arc

One tight line per drafted chapter, in chronological order.

- **01-departure** — Tess receives the sealed bag and Mara's cryptic instruction; the party of four assembles and departs Drey; a guild rider's glance at the bag's seal is the first sign something larger is at work.
- **02-the-wagon-road** — Party finds its travel rhythm through banter and small friction; a guild checkpoint logs their seal number non-standardly; a riderless gray horse and a watcher on a hill confirm they are being tracked.
- **03-the-ambush** — Five coordinated enforcers ambush the party at a river-bend cutting; Brann holds the line, Iri's costly working turns the fight, Pip routs the horse-holder with his boot; the bag survives but hoofbeats are already coming from the east.
- **04-the-guild-token** — The hoofbeats resolve to a harmless merchant; Pip identifies an enforcement-wing badge recovered from the attacker's coat; Iri reveals she knows the seal from "places she used to know," then Brann closes the door before Tess can ask what she means.
- **05-the-innkeeper-at-long-crossing** — At Long Crossing, Rossa's inn logs the party and sends a rider east before dawn; Brann discovers the overnight room search; word of their presence is now ahead of them on the road.
- **06-the-rival-party** — A professional rival party tries to buy the bag, then takes it by force; Iri's second major working ends the confrontation but puts her on the ground; Vel's parting word "testimony" is the chapter's planted clue; ridge watchers above mean the chapter cuts before the party knows how surrounded they are.
- **07-the-pavers-country** — On the Oldwork road, Tess lays a full hedge ward through three hours of difficult night work; Iri corrects one chalk line with a fingertip; both the geometry and the distance between them shift; Iri's half-sentence about the case the bag builds goes unfinished when Tess falls asleep.
- **08-two-days-out** — The party exits the Pavers' Country; Pip steers around a junction checkpoint without explaining who he's avoiding; Brann tells Tess her ward-work was "Real"; Pip drops his performing face at the fire and warns Tess to run without asking why if he says so — and can tell she's not sure she means it the way he needs.
- **09-the-crossing** — The road bridge is out; Tess goes under at the ford with the bag, Brann pulls her out twice over; Iri depletes herself helping; Pip finds a dry note in his pocket that someone placed there during the chaos.
- **10-the-walls** — V's note reveals the advocate is watched; the party arrives at Asterhall, Tess and Brann's peak moment happens at the vendor stalls; an enforcement-wing secondary checkpoint inside the gate puts a hand on the sealed bag.
- **11-the-advocates-court** — Pip's hidden letter clears the inner checkpoint; Sable opens the bag; Mara's name is on the sixth deposition; every attack re-cast as one operation; Tess must choose to testify or leave by morning.
- **12-the-decision** — The party's three true things reach Tess in a Low Streets room; she writes Mara a letter she doesn't send; at dawn she stands between the civic hall and the gate and takes one step.
