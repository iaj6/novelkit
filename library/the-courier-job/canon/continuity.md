# Continuity

Hard facts that must not change between drafting passes or chapters. Numbered for reference.

1. **Character ages are fixed**: Tess is 15. Brann is 17. Iri is 16. Pip is 14. Ages matter to how they act and how the world treats them; they must not drift.
2. **Mara Weft is 58** and is Tess's master. She does not travel to Asterhall. She appears only in Drey (opening) and possibly via letter or Tess's memory.
3. **Starting village is Drey. Destination is Asterhall.** The journey takes approximately three weeks. There is no shortcut and no magical fast-travel.
4. **The party is exactly four**: Tess, Brann, Iri, Pip. No fifth member is added. No member permanently leaves the party before Asterhall.
5. **POV is Tess only.** No exceptions. No scenes from Brann, Iri, or Pip's perspective. Other characters' interiority is always Tess's inference, never presented as fact.
6. **The sealed bag is never opened before Asterhall.** It is delivered intact to Sable Orr, advocate-recorder for the Asterhall city council.
7. **Contents of the sealed bag**: seven sworn depositions from hedge-witches documenting extortion by the Weavers' Guild enforcement wing. Mara Weft is one of the seven deponents. Tess learns this in chapter 11 or 12.
8. **The contract is legitimate but contested**: arranged by a reformist faction within the Weavers' Guild. The opposing faction (the extortionists) sent the ambushers (ch 3) and the rival party (ch 6). The discovery of the bag's contents must re-cast these as targeted, not opportunistic.
9. **Magic has costs**: Iri's guild magic costs her visibly (pallor, shaking, need for food and sleep). Tess's hedge magic requires her physical kit (chalk, salt, herbs) and preparation time. Neither can improvise freely. Magic is never a free resource.
10. **The romance subplot (Tess/Brann) peaks in chapter 10 or 11** with a kiss or explicitly avoided kiss. It stays in subplot. No on-page sex. No melodrama. YA-appropriate scale throughout.
11. **Action set pieces in chapters 3, 6, and 9**: ch 3 is a roadside ambush (guild enforcement agents operating as bandits); ch 6 is a confrontation with a rival party also seeking the bag; ch 9 is a near-disaster crossing or environmental emergency. These chapters must deliver physical action, not interiority around action.
12. **Chapter count is 12. Target word count is ~42,000 words** (~3,500 words per chapter). Each chapter is named for one encounter or milestone.
13. **Every chapter ends on a cliffhanger or mid-action beat. No exceptions.** Literary fades (quiet, resolved, mood-based endings) are not permitted in any chapter.
14. **Chapter 12 ends open.** Tess makes a decision. We do not see its consequences. The next-book hook. The reader closes the book not knowing what she'll do.
15. **Pip's backstory (why he left Asterhall) is acknowledged but not resolved** in this book. It is a loose thread that may be picked up in a sequel.
16. **Iri's guild status (why she left or was removed) is not resolved** in this book. It is established that she has guild-level training and is not currently licensed; the reason is not disclosed.
17. **The Tess/Iri thread resolves into friendship or alliance by chapter 11–12.** The thread is about magical legitimacy, not romance. Iri quietly respects hedge work; Tess comes to see that Iri's power costs her.
18. **No omniscient narrator moments.** The narration has no perspective outside Tess's. The world beyond Tess's line of sight does not exist in the prose.
