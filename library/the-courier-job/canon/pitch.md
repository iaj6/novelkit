# Pitch

Fifteen-year-old hedge-witch apprentice Tess has never left the village of Drey. When her master, Mara Weft, takes a sealed-courier contract from the Weavers' Guild to cover her mounting debts — a leather bag delivered to a named recipient in the city of Asterhall, no questions asked — Mara is too old and too ill for the three-week road. Tess goes in her place.

She doesn't go alone. The contract pays for four. Mara picks from the short list of Drey residents who can afford to leave: Brann (seventeen, the blacksmith's son, quiet and oversized, paying off his older sister's debt), Iri (sixteen, half-elven, three years a stranger in town, capable of real guild-level magic that makes Tess feel like a child with chalk and salt), and Pip (fourteen, street kid, the only one of them who has actually been to Asterhall and won't say why he left).

Six encounters on the road. Three weeks. A job that turns out to be much larger than the contract said. By the time Tess reaches Asterhall and learns what is inside the bag, she has companions she didn't expect to trust — and a decision she didn't expect to carry. The discovery re-casts everything that happened on the road. Tess has to decide what to do with what she now knows, and the book ends before we find out.
