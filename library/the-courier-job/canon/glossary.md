# Glossary

Alphabetical. This file grows as the pipeline adds names, terms, and places. Initial entries established by the Architect phase.

---

## People

**Brann** — Seventeen, blacksmith's second son from Drey. The party's muscle and physical protection. Quiet, deadpan, taking the job to pay off his older sister's debt. See *characters.md*.

**Iri** — Sixteen, half-elven. Three years a stranger in Drey. Has guild-level magical training and does not explain herself. See *characters.md*.

**Mara Weft** — Fifty-eight. Hedge-witch of Drey; Tess's master. Broke. One of seven deponents in the sealed bag. Does not travel with the party.

**Pip** — Fourteen. Street kid originally from Asterhall; arrived in Drey two summers ago without explanation. The party's city knowledge and comic engine. See *characters.md*.

**Sable Orr** — Mid-forties. Advocate-recorder for the Asterhall city council. The named recipient of the sealed bag. Building a case against Weavers' Guild extortion.

**Tess** — Fifteen. Hedge-witch apprentice to Mara Weft; the book's sole POV character; courier of record on the guild contract. See *characters.md*.

---

## Places

**Asterhall** — City of approximately 7,000; seat of the Weavers' Guild; destination of the delivery. Three weeks of road from Drey. See *world.md*.

**Drey** — A farming village of roughly 200 people; starting point of the journey. Three weeks west of Asterhall. See *world.md*.

**Long Crossing** — A market town roughly at the midpoint of the road between Drey and Asterhall. Has a proper inn, a weekly market, a road guild office. Site of a named chapter encounter.

**The Pavers' Country** — A stretch of ancient stone-paved road (Oldwork construction) through rough, forested terrain between Long Crossing and Asterhall. Jurisdictionally contested between guild and crown. Where the ambush occurs (ch 3).

---

## Organizations

**The Weavers' Guild** — Primary magical and commercial guild in the region, based in Asterhall. Officially licenses magical practitioners, sets road-toll rates, and maintains enforcement. Contains two factions: a reformist faction (which arranged the delivery contract) and an enforcement faction (which has been extorting unlicensed practitioners and which sent the ambushers in ch 3 and the rival party in ch 6).

---

## Objects

**The sealed bag** — A locked leather courier bag. The object of the Weavers' Guild delivery contract. Contains seven sworn depositions from hedge-witches documenting extortion by the guild's enforcement wing. Must arrive intact and sealed at Sable Orr's office in Asterhall. See *continuity.md* items 6–8.

**Tess's kit** — The leather satchel Tess carries at all times. Contains the materials for hedge magic: chalk, salt, dried herbs, cord, and related supplies. If the kit is lost, damaged, or wet, Tess's magic is severely limited.

## Magic Terms

**Cattle wards** — Hedge-magic protective charms for livestock. One of the standard services a village hedge-witch provides. Must be renewed at the solstices.

**Guild magic** — Formal, trained magic of the kind Iri practices. More immediate and powerful than hedge magic. Costs the practitioner visibly: pallor, shaking hands, physical depletion, need for food and rest.

**Hedge magic** — Small-scale, practical, material-dependent magic of the kind Tess practices. Preparation time measured in minutes to hours. Requires specific materials. Does not improvise without a kit.

**Oldwork** — Term for ancient construction (roads, structures) predating current memory; origin unclear. The Pavers' Country road is Oldwork.

**Well charms** — Hedge-magic charms that keep a water source clean and reliably flowing. Standard village hedge-witch service; one of Tess's duties under Mara.

**Pip's insurance object** — A canvas-wrapped item Pip carries from departure (ch 1), roughly the shape and size of a small lute or a very narrow coffin. He calls it 'insurance' and declines to explain what it is. Its nature is not revealed in ch 1.

**Tess's contract case** — A flat leather folder Tess uses to organize the paperwork for the delivery contract: Mara's authorization, the contract papers, Sable Orr's address, and the second-payment document. She carries it inside her kit satchel.

**The guild rider (ch 1)** — An unnamed rider in a gray traveling cloak encountered heading west on the county road just past Drey's last fence post on the morning of the party's departure. He glanced at the sealed bag's seal before looking away and riding on. Pip identified him as working for the Weavers' Guild. First node of the enforcement network (Thread 5).

**Blue-gray road livery** — The uniform worn by road guild officers at toll checkpoints on the Wagon Road East and other guild-maintained roads. Blue-gray in color, guild-marked. Distinct from the full enforcement blue of the Weavers' Guild enforcement wing. The distinction matters: road guild officers handle tolls and contract logging; enforcement wing officers handle compliance and coercion.

**Wagon Road East** — The merchant road running east from Drey through the river valley toward Long Crossing. Broad, well-traveled, with guild road-toll checkpoints at junctions. The first week of the party's journey follows this road. Cold at night; flat valley terrain; willows along the river bank.

**Wicken Street** — A street in Asterhall's residential quarter near the Cloth Quarter, mentioned in Pip's anecdote about following a merchant in ch 2. A cloth trader visited his grandmother there every afternoon. Background detail establishing Pip's familiarity with Asterhall's street geography.

**The junction checkpoint (Wagon Road East)** — A guild road-toll checkpoint at a road junction on the Wagon Road East, passed by the party on day 3 of the journey. Stone booth, ledger, officer in blue-gray road livery. Notable for logging the sealed bag's seven-character seal number — stated by the officer as standard for high-value sealed contracts, identified by Pip as non-standard. Thread 5 node 2.

**The river-bend cutting (ch 3 ambush site)** — A shallow cutting on the Wagon Road East where the road bends around a stand of willow and alder at a river bend. River ten feet below the left bank through brush; trees crowding the right verge; approximately forty-foot sight lines in both directions. Site of the ch 3 ambush. Day 6 of the party's journey, late afternoon.

**Tess's linen bandage** — A roll of linen bandage Tess packed in the bottom of her kit satchel over Mara's objection that it was unnecessary. First used in ch 3 to bandage Brann's left forearm cut. Part of Tess's kit inventory from departure.

**Pip's short sword** — A short sword dropped by one of the ch 3 ambush attackers. Pip claimed it ('Mine now') and added it to his gear. He had previously carried no edged weapon.

**The ch 3 horse-holder** — An unnamed member of the ambush party stationed at the treeline with a bridled horse ready — the group's designated withdrawal route. Pip startled the horse with his boot; the horse-holder ran after the bolting horse into the scrub and did not return. One of six total attackers (five who engaged plus the horse-holder).

**Advance-contract money** — Pip's term for money paid to hired operatives before a job rather than split from the take afterward. He identified the small purse recovered from the ch 3 attackers' gear as advance-contract money, distinguishing the ambush party from opportunistic road bandits and indicating they were paid by a specific employer with a specific target.

**The enforcement wing badge** — A small (approx. one inch) round badge of dark blue enamel on copper backing. Design: a flat horizontal bar with a solid circle above it, bisected by a single vertical line. Worn under the coat, sewn into a breast lining pocket — not visible from outside. Identifies the bearer as a Weavers' Guild enforcement wing operative (distinct from road guild and trade guild). Recovered from a downed ambush attacker in ch 4 by Tess. Now carried in Tess's kit pocket. The same design appears on the innkeeper's ledger at the Crossed Keys in ch 5.

**Mara's salve** — A wax-sealed tin of medicinal salve, Mara Weft's own formulation. Wintergreen-sharp, with an unidentified secondary scent. Packed in the inner pocket of Tess's kit before departure, over Tess's objection that it was extra weight. First used in ch 4 to clean and dress Brann's left forearm cut.

**The road-guild sub-office ledger stub (ch 4)** — A receipt from a road-guild sub-office west of the river junction, three weeks old at the time of recovery. Found among the ambush attackers' gear. Pip kept it in his coat pocket despite it appearing to have no immediate use. Its significance is unannounced.

**The unnamed merchant (ch 4)** — A middle-aged woman traveling east alone with a loaded gray mule, encountered on the Wagon Road East just after the ch 3 ambush on day 6. She confirmed the road stretch had been trouble for three months and that guild enforcers were out looking for something. She offered to ride with the party as far as the next market cross; Brann declined. She is not named and does not recur.

**Long Crossing** — A market town at the midpoint of the road between Drey and Asterhall. Has a proper market square with a guild seal set in the paving, cobblestones on the main street, three wells, a road guild office, and three inns including the Crossed Keys. The road guild office holds the east road license under a charter contested with the county (county seal slightly larger than the guild seal above the door). An enforcement wing asset location: the innkeeper Rossa logs courier traffic and passes information through a watcher network. The party arrives on day 9 and departs pre-dawn on day 10.

**The Crossed Keys** — A mid-sized inn at Long Crossing. Stone frontage, two-story, carved wooden sign with two painted keys crossed. Two exits: main door to the market square and a side door onto the alley beside the chandler's. Innkeeper: Rossa. The party stayed in one upstairs room (one full bed, one narrow cot, single alley-facing window). An enforcement wing information asset: Rossa logs sealed-goods couriers in her ledger, which bears the enforcement wing inlaid seal on the spine.

**Rossa** — Innkeeper of the Crossed Keys at Long Crossing. A compact woman in her fifties, gray hair pinned back, brown coat. Moves with the efficiency of thirty years of the same circuit. She is a Weavers' Guild enforcement wing asset — her lodger ledger has the enforcement wing seal inlaid in the binding and she records the names and roads of sealed-goods couriers for the enforcement network. She asked the party about 'sealed goods in transit' and invoked a (false) guild manifest requirement. Her watcher (the corner man) relayed information to a rider who went east before dawn.

**The corner man (ch 5)** — An unnamed enforcement wing watcher who sat at a corner table in the Crossed Keys common room on the evening of day 9. He came in just after the party, never ordered, never left during their meal. Pip identified him immediately as waiting rather than staying. Before dawn on day 10, he passed information to a rider on a fresh horse below the inn window; the rider turned east toward Asterhall.

**Brann's placement habit** — Brann deliberately places his possessions in a known state (e.g., leaving a pack buckle loose) so he can detect if they have been disturbed. Established in ch 5 when he found his deliberately-loose buckle latched during his night watch, confirming the room had been professionally searched. A consistent character trait: he knows exactly where his things are.

**Vel** — Leader of the ch 6 rival party. Brown coat, well-fitted. Professional, calm — she made a genuine offer of twice the contract fee before escalating to force. Named herself to Tess during the offer scene. She knows the bag contains 'testimony' — the precise word, from a precise briefing. A confirmed enforcement-faction operative.

**The rival party (ch 6)** — A four-person professional party encountered on day 11 east of Long Crossing. Composition: Vel (leader), two fighters, one ledger-person (also a trained fighter). Well-equipped, on horseback. Pre-briefed on the sealed bag's contents and destination. Sent by the Weavers' Guild enforcement faction to acquire the bag. Withdrew after Iri's major working put both fighters down.

**The ch 6 confrontation clearing** — An oak-shaded midday rest stop east of Long Crossing with a dry stone well at the center. Site of the ch 6 standoff between the party and the rival party. On the road between Long Crossing and the Pavers' Country.

**Ridge watchers (ch 6)** — Four or five unidentified individuals positioned on the north ridge above the ch 6 confrontation clearing, with a full sightline to the clearing. Present for the entire confrontation, including Iri's major working. Spotted by Pip after the rival party withdrew. Third operational layer of the enforcement network on this road. Unresolved at chapter's end.

**Oldwork road** — Ancient stone-paved road through the Pavers' Country, built in a period no living memory reaches. Pale gray stone fitted without mortar, joint to joint with near-invisible seams. Center stones worn lower than edges by centuries of traffic. Originally warded; the wards are now several hundred years past renewal and slipping, producing ambient magical interference. Jurisdictionally ambiguous. The party traverses it on day 13–14.

**The Pavers' Country** — A stretch of rough, forested hill terrain through which the Oldwork road runs, approximately in the final stretch before Asterhall. Trees crowd close on both sides. Ambient wrongness from the Oldwork's slipping wards: sounds arrive from wrong directions, dreams feel more real than reality. The party camps here on night 13–14. No inn within reach; the party must camp on the road.

**milestone marker (Pavers' Country)** — A worn stone post at the ch 7 campsite in the Pavers' Country with a distance chiseled in a script the party cannot read. Party camps here on night 13–14.

**protection-and-clarity ward** — Mara's standard travel ward formula for Tess's hedge-magic practice. Activation layer uses salt along the chalk boundary and herbs crumbled into groove-seams. Described as the most reliable under field conditions, first formula Mara taught Tess. Used in ch 7 at the milestone marker campsite.

**The Four Counties Charter** — The road authority and governing charter for the road section east of the Pavers' Country approaching Asterhall. A different road authority and enforcement jurisdiction from the Wagon Road East. Per Pip, the Weavers' Guild enforcement wing keeps fewer assets stationed on Four Counties roads — different coverage model from the Wagon Road.

**The branch-road junction checkpoint (day 15)** — A guild checkpoint on a southern branch road that diverges from the main east road at approximately mid-morning position on day 15 of the journey. Passes through an unnamed village with an inn; runs under the Four Counties road authority seal. Cuts approximately two hours off the journey to Asterhall. Pip declined to use this route because he knows (or knew) people at this checkpoint who might not be glad to see him. The party took the main east road instead. Thread 4 node.

**Pip's candied ginger** — A supply of candied ginger Pip acquired in or near the Pavers' Country, rationed for three days before distributing one piece each to the party on the evening of day 15. A minor comfort item in otherwise trail-level camp provisions.

**The ford (day 17, east of the downed bridge)** — A river ford approximately a quarter-mile east of the main road bridge (which was found out on day 17). Near bank has a large elm; far bank has a birch. Farmers use this ford in dry summer. The party crossed here under rain-swollen, waist-deep conditions. The crossing line ran elm to birch. The crossing went wrong: Tess went under and the rope fouled. A dry folded note was found in Pip's pocket after the crossing, placed by an unknown party during the chaos.

**The shepherd's hut (east bank, ford)** — A stone-walled shepherd's hut approximately fifty yards east of the ford through the trees. Solid low roof, interior-latching door, stone-ringed firepit with cold ash, straw stacked against the back wall, one south-facing window stuffed with rush. Empty. The party sheltered here on the afternoon/evening of day 17 after the ford crossing disaster.

**The note (placed in Pip's pocket at the ford)** — A small, square-folded piece of paper found in Pip's inner coat pocket after the day 17 ford crossing. It was dry (oilskin-protected). Not placed there by any party member. Placed by someone physically present at the ford who knew Pip's route and identity and chose to communicate with him specifically. Content is revealed in ch 10; it is connected to Thread 4 (Pip's hidden Asterhall history).

**Pip's inner document pocket (coat)** — The inner pocket of Pip's coat where he habitually wraps his documents in oilskin. Established in ch 9 when a dry folded note was found there after the ford crossing. The oilskin habit is his own; the note was not his document.

**V** — A contact in Asterhall who left a warning note in Pip's inner coat pocket at the day 17 ford crossing and is identified by Pip as 'someone I knew in the city.' V's note (ch 10) warned that Sable Orr's Advocate's Row office is under enforcement-wing surveillance and provided the secondary Low Streets approach route. V is still in Asterhall. Pip recognized the handwriting. V's full identity and relationship to Pip is not revealed in ch 10; it is resolved in ch 12 (V is alive, the obligation discharged, Pip did not die returning). Thread 4 primary contact.

**Low Streets shorthand** — An Asterhall street-dialect writing system, mixing city dialect notation with older script forms. Specific to people who grew up in or near the Low Streets district. Pip reads it naturally; no other party member can. The language V used to write the warning note.

**Advocate's Row** — A street in Asterhall where Sable Orr's public-facing office is located — first floor, name on the door. Under Weavers' Guild enforcement-wing surveillance as of day 18 (the party's arrival). Not the route the party uses to deliver the bag.

**The inner gate checkpoint** — A secondary enforcement checkpoint inside Asterhall's main gate tunnel, positioned against the tunnel wall with a folding table and large leather ledger. Staffed by two officers in full enforcement blue tabards (not road-guild blue-gray). Specifically targets sealed-goods couriers. Not standard procedure when the party departed Drey; it is new infrastructure in place for the party's arrival. Thread 5's city node. The older officer at this checkpoint places his hand on the sealed bag and demands to inspect the contents at the end of ch 10.

**The older enforcement officer (inner checkpoint)** — An unnamed senior Weavers' Guild enforcement officer stationed at the inner gate checkpoint in Asterhall's main gate tunnel. Heavy through the shoulders, patient and deliberate manner. He read the party's contract papers past the transit stamp to the fourth page (contract specifics), looked at the bag, looked at Tess, signaled his colleague, and placed his hand on the sealed bag demanding to inspect the contents. Ch 10 ends on this moment.

**The secondary route (Low Streets)** — The alternate approach to Sable Orr's office through Asterhall's Low Streets district, bypassing the watched Advocate's Row entrance. Pip knows this route (walked it before); V's note confirmed it was still viable. Approximately 20–30 minutes longer than the direct approach. The route the party intends to use in ch 11.

**Pip's pre-prepared letter (the advocate's letter)** — A letter bearing Sable Orr's advocate's court dark-wax device, prepared by the reformist faction before the party's departure from Drey. Delivered to Pip the morning the party assembled. Named all four party members and stated their delivery was a council-ordered proceeding at the afternoon bells. Pip carried it in his inner coat pocket for 18 days and produced it at the inner gate checkpoint on day 18. It prevented the enforcement officer from opening the sealed bag.

**Sable Orr's office** — Second floor, left passage, last door of the Advocate's Court building, accessed via the back entrance. Smaller than expected. Shelves floor to ceiling on three walls (document cases, each labeled in a small exact hand). Single window facing an interior courtyard. Desk completely covered in organized paper. The room where the sealed bag was opened and its contents revealed in chapter 11.

**Advocate's Court back entrance** — The rear entry to the Advocate's Court building in Asterhall, under the city-council seal. One guard, one clerk with a scheduled-arrivals list. The party's approach route, navigated by Pip through the Low Streets. The public front entrance on Advocate's Row is under enforcement-wing surveillance.

**The fifth deponent (unnamed)** — A hedge-witch from a village two days east of Drey. The fifth of seven deponents in the sealed bag. She and Mara Weft argued about cattle ward geometry thirty years before the story. Tess half-remembers her name from a passing reference Mara made once. She is one of the seven people whose sworn testimony documents Weavers' Guild enforcement wing extortion.

**The Low Streets watcher** — An unidentified person standing in the shadow of a right-hand alley at a five-road junction in the Low Streets of Asterhall, facing the party's direction when Pip navigated them to the Advocate's Court on day 18. Pip saw the watcher and chose to pass without approaching, saying nothing to the party. Identity and affiliation unresolved.

**Low Streets lodging house** — An unnamed lodging in Asterhall's Low Streets district where Pip arranged the party's stay on Day 18–19. Narrow building between two taller ones, gray-bearded proprietor of approximately sixty who asks no names. Two upstairs rooms: one with a bed, table, chair, and courtyard window; one with a wall-cot built in. Pre-arranged pork stew was waiting on arrival. Proprietor owes Pip an unspecified debt.

**Tess's letter to Mara** — A letter Tess wrote on the night of Day 18 at the Low Streets lodging, using paper and pen Pip brought her. The letter covers most of one page and addresses Tess's understanding of what Mara knew and did. Not reproduced in the narrative. Folded twice, placed in Tess's kit case on top of the cord and salve tin. Not sent as of the book's end. Constitutes one half of the Tess/Mara emotional thread's close.

**Brann's five words** — Informal reference to Brann's last spoken line to Tess in the book: 'Whatever you decide, I'll be there when you do it.' Said the night of Day 18 after Pip was asleep, without prompting, without waiting for a response. Covers the Thread 1 (Tess/Brann) romantic and alliance threads without naming them. Left open as a sequel thread.
